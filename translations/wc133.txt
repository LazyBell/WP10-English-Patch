3070=A great mare who founded one of Japan's leading female lines. With half-sisters Tetsuno Homare and Kamiyamato she forms the Craig Dhu Lochi family; the branch through prolific Star Roch is called the "Star Roch line." Her own foals were poor, but later generations include Hard Barge, Sakura Yutaka O, Sakura Star O and Winning Ticket. Unsold at auction, allotted as a lottery horse, she drew little attention, but defied that: 3rd in the Oka Sho, she won the Oaks as 9th choice with a brilliant late run - the first lottery horse to win a Big Eight race. Without further graded wins, she won open and handicap races and was invited into the Arima Kinen; against 2-classic winner Kodama and other Big Eight winners, she led all the way to win as 9th choice. Perhaps paying for it, she lost 5 straight at 5, then won the Keio Hai Autumn H.; dislocating a joint in the Mainichi Okan, she was pulled up and retired.

3084=Winning on debut at 3, she then kept missing, taking a 1-win race only in her 11th start; at 4 she won 2 more classes, reaching open class at 5. 3085=The 2022 Kikuka Sho winner, a star from Deep Impact's second-to-last crop, giving his sire a record 5th Kikuka Sho and 24th classic G1. 3rd on debut behind later Satsuki Sho winner Geoglyph, he won a maiden, then at 3 a 1-win race and the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, beating favorite Do Deuce by a neck. Not yet top-rated, he was 5th in the Satsuki Sho and 3rd in the Derby. 2nd in the St. Lite Kinen, he faced St. Lite winner Gaia Force in the Kikuka Sho with the spring leaders absent; as Gaia Force faded, he held on from the front by a nose - a classic, and Deep Impact's 12th straight crop with a classic winner. At 4 he slumped: 9th as Nikkei Sho favorite, 11th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Takarazuka Kinen. Resting that summer, he died of multiple organ failure from heatstroke.
