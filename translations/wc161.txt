3522=A late-blooming speedster who finally won a graded race, the Victoria Mile (G1), at 6 in 2024, always racing at short trips. Winning her debut in August at 2, 3rd in her first stakes, the Artemis S. (G3), she was talented early, but then spent ages in allowances, passing the 3-win class only in spring at 4. In open class her best from 7 was 2nd in the Shin'etsu S. (L); she won the Toki S. (L) in summer at 5. After 6 months off, 7th in the Kyoto Himba S. and 6th in the Hanshin Himba S. at 6, she was 14th choice at 208-1 in the Victoria Mile, then flew from midfield to win - a stunner, and a first G1 for jockey Tsumura too. 7th in the Centaur S., she was a creditable 4th in America's BC Mile.
3523=The 2024 Derby winner. Debuting in October at 2, he won his 2nd start, then 4th to Shin Emperor in the Kyoto 2yo S. (G3). At 3 he won the Keisei Hai (G3) from a handy spot. Just before the Satsuki Sho start a leg problem was found and he was withdrawn. He made up for it in the Derby: 9th choice behind Satsuki Sho placers Justin Milano and Regaleira, he raced handily, broke clear and beat Justin Milano by 2 lengths - jockey Norihiro Yokoyama became the oldest winner of the Derby and of any JRA G1. Straight to the Kikuka Sho after a break, he faded on the far turn, 6th. In the Arima Kinen he led at a slow pace and held 3rd. At 4 he won the AJCC (G2), then the Dubai Sheema Classic (G1); 5th of 6 in England's International S., back home he was 3rd in both the Japan Cup and Arima Kinen - still a force.
3524=A dogged middle/long-distance horse who won the 2024 Takarazuka Kinen (G1) at 5. Slow to win, he took a maiden in his 9th start in June at 3, then climbed 3 classes by spring at 4. 3rd in his first stakes, the Hakodate Kinen (G3), he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai (G2) at 5 as favorite. 3rd in the Hanshin Daishoten, in his first G1, the Tenno Sho (Spring), he closed sharply for 2nd to T O Royal. In the Takarazuka Kinen he waited at the back again and swept wide in the straight for his first G1. 11th as Kyoto Daishoten favorite, 12th in the Japan Cup.
3525=Winner of the 2024 Sprinters S. (G1) - also the first G1 for jockey Junya Nishimura. Starting on dirt, he won a maiden in January at 3 in his 4th start, then on turf sprints won the Tachibana S. (L) and was 2nd by half a length to Mozu Meimei in the Aoi S. (G3). Near-misses followed (4th Swan S., 2nd Keihan Hai), then the Silk Road S. (G3) at 4. Favorite in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, he was 10th, then found to have a fracture. Returning straight in the Sprinters S. as 9th choice, he won strongly from up front - a G1 at last. Then 11th Hong Kong Sprint, 7th Takamatsunomiya Kinen at 5, 5th in Hong Kong's Chairman's Sprint Prize, 12th Sprinters S.; still, 2nd in the Keihan Hai and a win in the Hanshin C. (G2) keep him among the top sprinters.
3526=The 2024 Kikuka Sho winner. Ordinary in spring, he blossomed over the summer and became a classic winner in autumn. Winning his debut in August at 2 and the Hyakunichiso Tokubetsu (1-win), he was 2nd to later Derby winner Danon Decile in the Keisei Hai at 3, 4th in the Satsuki Sho from the back, but 11th in the Derby. After a break he won the St. Lite Kinen (G2), closing sharply from midfield. Co-favorite with Danon Decile in the Kikuka Sho, he broke clear from midfield and won by 2.5 lengths. In the Arima Kinen the two 3yos were the top 2 choices; slow away, he was 6th. At 4, 3rd in the Nikkei Sho, then 14th Takarazuka Kinen, 5th Tenno Sho (Autumn), 10th Hong Kong Vase.
3527=Champion 2yo filly of 2024, central to the 2025 3yo fillies' races. Winning her debut in August at 2, she was 2nd by a nose to Magic Sands as 6th choice in the Sapporo 2yo S. (G3). In the Hanshin JF she moved wide at the 4th turn and swept past with a powerful finish. A classic favorite at 3, she went straight to the Oka Sho as narrow 2nd choice behind Erika Express; closing from the back, she was a neck short of Embroidery, 2nd. 2nd choice behind Embroidery in the Oaks, she broke clear from a handy spot, but outsider Come Nyack caught her by a head - 2nd again. Preparing for the Shuka Sho, she developed tendon trouble and faces a long rest.
3528=Debuting in October at 2, he won a maiden in February at 3, then was 4th in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai (G2). He won a 1-win race, then 2 more classes at 4, and the Nakayama Kimpai (G3) at 5 as 5th choice, breaking clear from a handy spot by half a length. Last (16th) in the Osaka Hai, but 3rd in the All Comers (G2).
3529=Quickly through the 1-win class at 2, at 3 he was 7th in the Kyodo News Hai and 5th in the Radio Nikkei Sho (G3s). Back in allowances, he won the 3-win class late at 3, then the Kyoto Kimpai (G3) at 4 as 8th choice, with a razor-sharp finish from midfield. Unplaced since in mile graded races.
3530=Debuting in October at 2, she won her 2nd start, then the Fairy S. (G3) at 3 as 5th choice in a field of graded maidens, edging a 4-way blanket finish by a neck. 11th in the Oka Sho, but 5th as 13th choice in the NHK Mile C.
3531=Winning his debut easily, he took on the Shinzan Kinen (G3) as 3rd choice, closing sharply from midfield to beat favorite Ecoro Bloom by 1 1/4 lengths. Favorite in the Mainichi Hai (G3), he was 2nd to front-running outsider Meisho Tabaru. 12th in the NHK Mile C.; in autumn, 4th in the Swan S. (G2), holding on from up front.
3532=Debuting in February at 3, she won 2 straight, then 14th in the Oaks (G1). Back in allowances, she won 2 classes, then the Aichi Hai (G3) at 4 as favorite, with a brilliant late run from the back. Then 14th in the Osaka Hai and 13th in the Mermaid S. (G3).
3533=After a long spell in allowances, he won his first graded race, the 2024 AJCC (G2), at 6. Debuting at 3, he won a maiden and a 1-win race from 6; winless at 4, he won 2 classes at 5. 3rd in his first open race, the Argentine Republic Hai (G2), he won the AJCC at 6. Then poor (14th Tenno Sho (Spring), 6th Hakodate Kinen, 8th Sapporo Kinen), but 2nd in the Nikkei Sho (G2) at 7 - still capable.
3534=5th on a turf debut, he switched to dirt and was remarkably steady: 4 wins and a 2nd from 5 through the allowances, then the Negishi S. (G3) early at 4, breaking clear from a handy spot by 2.5 lengths. 2nd in the Keyaki S. (OP), he won the Enif S. (L), then the Musashino S. (G3) as 1.7-1 favorite, holding on from up front.
3535=A miler/10f horse who finally won a graded race, the Tokyo Shimbun Hai (G3), at 7. Winning his debut in October at 2, he reached the 2-win class at 3, then struggled, passing the 3-win class in summer at 5, and was unplaced in graded races. 7th choice in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai at 7, he broke clear from a handy spot to win. Racing on at 8, he won the Kyoto Kimpai.
3536=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Kisaragi Sho (G3), winning a 4-way blanket finish by a nose as favorite. 13th in the Satsuki Sho and 17th in the Derby, finding nothing from the back, he improved to 5th in the Kikuka Sho after a 6th in the Kobe Shimbun Hai. At 4 he won Saudi Arabia's Red Sea Turf H. (G2), was 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring), then in France won the Prix Foy (G2) and was 5th in the Arc, best of the Japanese - a fruitful trip.
3537=Debuting at 3, she was soon 2nd in the Flora S. (G2), but 8th in the Oaks and Rose S. After the 3-win class, she was 4th in the Turquoise S. (G3), then won the Kyoto Himba S. (G3) at 4 as 2nd choice, beating favorite Namura Clair by a neck.
