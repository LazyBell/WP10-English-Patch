1366=Half-brother of fillies' Triple Crown winner Still in Love. A bold front-runner, he won the Hosenka Sho by 6 lengths and led all the way in the Radio Tampa Sho. In the Kobe Shimbun Hai he led but broke down in the straight, 5th, and retired.
1404=His dam was a Japan Cup runner-up. His temper kept him winless at 3; gelded, he first won in a JRA-local dirt race at Morioka. Back on turf he blossomed, winning the Sekiya Kinen at 5. The next year he won the NST Open in a Japanese 1400m record, then the Sekiya Kinen and Mainichi Okan. He won the next American JCC, but injury cost him 6 months; after his return he did little and retired after a training injury.
1405=Highly rated before his debut, he underperformed at 2-3, graded-winless. Maturing at 4, he won the American JCC and Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. Most of his good graded runs came at 2200m+ - a stayer at heart.
1406=A very late bloomer, he reached open class at 6, then beat local star Mitsuaki Silence by 3 lengths in the Oguri Cap Kinen and won the Breeders' Gold C. by 9. He then slumped badly and retired at 8.
1407=After 3 dirt wins he tried the classics, was beaten badly, and returned to dirt, winning the Nagoya Yushun and the Derby Grand Prix, a G1. Injury-prone after, he switched to jumps at 7.
1408=Needing 6 starts to win, he won 2 open races and within 2 months was 6th choice for the Derby, a fine 5th. After a break he won the St. Lite Kinen, but was 14th in the Kikuka Sho. Injured, he ran poorly and retired to stud, siring Saga Kinen winner Chance Torai (Hyogo) and Kanto Oaks winner Asuka Libre (Funabashi).
1409=Perhaps from broodmare sire Real Shadai, he had stamina to burn. He won the 2400m Aoba Sho, was 7th in the Derby and Kikuka Sho, then in December's Stayers S. beat T.M. Opera O, who had come to win. He stood at stud in New Zealand, siring graded winners.
1410=Hot-tempered, he finished last in his first 2 races and was gelded at 2. He improved, but as a stayer had few races to choose from and rose slowly. At 4 he beat crop-mate Narita Top Road in the Stayers S., then won the next Meguro Kinen by 3.5 lengths and was 3rd in the Takarazuka Kinen. He tried the Dubai Sheema Classic (7th), won the Stayers S. again at 6 and raced until 8.
1411=Based in Hokkaido. Not dominant but very consistent, he won the JRA-local Breeders' Gold C. at 4.
1412=He won his debut and the Niigata Sansai S., then struggled, doing little in the classics. After the Kikuka Sho he beat older horses in the Keihan Hai, his first win since the Niigata race. Older, he starred at middle distances, often closing too late for 2nd or 3rd, but won the Kokura Kinen at 5, the All Comers in record time at 6 and the Kokura Kinen again at 7 - 5 of his 6 wins graded races. At stud he had few stars, but his last crop produced Crown Rose, who won her debut as 10th choice and the Fairy S. as 10th choice too - surely stunning even Rosado's fans.
1413=Steady from his debut, he bloomed once he tried JRA-local races when older: from the Kurofune Sho at 6 he won 6 straight dirt graded races, JRA and local. After a 2nd in the Tokyo Hai, he won the JBC Sprint, his long-sought G1, and retired. At stud, true to type, he sired many dirt sprinters like Toho Dolce, Love Me Chan and Namura Titan.
1414=Coming good in summer at 4, he won the Kokura Kinen and Asahi Challenge C., and the Kinko Sho the next year, starring in middle-distance turf graded races, then plateaued. In his 34th start, his first dirt race in a while, he broke down and was put down.
1415=In the Radio Tampa Sho he closed sharply from a hopeless 12th at the final turn for a neck 2nd. A graded win seemed a matter of time, but he slumped, finally winning the Epsom C. at 5.
1416=Outclassed in the Oka Sho, she then focused on sprints, running well in graded and open races until she won the '08 Ibis Summer Dash, then the Centaur S. She was 7th in the Sprinters S. In '09, after a big loss in the CBC Sho, she won the Ibis Summer Dash again and ran well in the Kita-Kyushu Kinen and Centaur S., winning the Summer Sprint Series a 2nd time. 3rd in the Sprinters S., she broke down going for a 3rd Ibis Summer Dash and was put down.
1417=Raced only on dirt, he was stuck in the lower classes. Then at 6 he won his first turf race, and next the Lord Derby Challenge T. He raced until 9 without another win.
1418=Debuting in February at 3, he won his 3rd start and 4 straight to open class. 2nd in the Osaka Hai, he won the Hakodate Kinen at 5, then injury cost him 2 years 8 months; back, he was always beaten badly.
1419=In his first graded race, the Kobe Shimbun Hai, he beat Satsuki Sho winner Air Shakur and Derby winner Agnes Flight - a summer improver in the spotlight. A tendon injury then ended his career before he could show his talent. At stud his best was open-class Dream Flight, 3rd in the Diamond S.
1420=Debuting late, he won a maiden in August at 3. 2nd in his first graded race, the Tokai S., in spring at 4, he raced eagerly at local tracks, winning the Mercury C. and Hakusan Daishoten, and was 3rd in the Japan Cup Dirt. Injury forced a long break; back the next year he was 2nd to Kanetsu Fleuve in the Teio Sho, then rested again. In his next comeback, the Heian S., he broke down and was put down - Kronos City and Monopolizer were also injured in that race.
1421=By double classic winner Vega, full brother of Derby winner Admire Vega. Naturally much hoped for, but leg trouble delayed his debut to summer at 3. He won it, was 2nd in the Shiretoko Tokubetsu, then ran above his class to win the St. Lite Kinen from midfield - a graded winner in 3 starts. He was 5th in the Arima Kinen and Tenno Sho (Spring) and 6th in the Takarazuka Kinen, but leg trouble forced his retirement. With sound legs he could have matched his brother. At stud he sired dirt runners like Admire Subaru (3rd, Japan Cup Dirt) and Kurino Star O (3 dirt graded wins incl. the Antares S.), and Mainichi Hai winner Iron Look.
1422=Debuting late, in April at 3, he missed the classics entirely. A typical late-blooming stayer, he improved as an older horse and beat Tap Dance City in the Nikkei Sho in spring at 5. A fine supporting player at middle-to-long trips, he won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai at 6. He switched to jumps in autumn '04, won once, returned to the flat in '05 and was 17th in the Tenno Sho (Spring). He later served as a lead pony at Hanshin Racecourse.
1423=4th in the Satsuki Sho, 6th in the Derby and 8th in the Kikuka Sho, he then switched to miles and shone: he won the Keihan Hai, was 2nd in the Nakayama Kinen and won the Milers C. in a record 1:32.8. He won the Epsom C. at 5, but leg trouble forced a long break; back after 16 months, he had declined and never won again. Fast, but held back by temper and fragility.
1424=Half-brother of Tokai Teio. At 4, still in allowances, he was 2nd in the Kokura Kinen and 4th in the Asahi Challenge C., then won the Hiei S. and the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. All his wins came at 2000-2500m.
1425=Full brother of double classic winner Vega. Fragile and often below par, in his first graded race, the Kyoto Kinen at 4, he beat Agnes Flight and Narita Top Road. He ran well in the Tenno Sho (Spring), but still fragile, he retired early to stud.
1426=3rd to Air Shakur in the Kikuka Sho, he showed great staying power, and at 4 won the Doncaster S. and Stayers S., both over 3000m, easily. But in G1s he fell short, beaten badly in 2 Tenno Sho (Spring) tries.
1427=Maturing late at 3, he rose quickly from the 5-million class to open. 5th in his first graded race, the Kyoto Kinen, he beat T.M. Opera O in the Osaka Hai. He won just once more and moved to Iwate at 6.
1428=Leg trouble cost him the classics, but at 4, running above his class, he won the American JCC, proving his potential. 2nd in the Diamond S., he then slumped and raced until 7 without another win.
1429=His half-brothers Yamanin Miracle and Yamanin Ability were graded winners, so hopes were high. Leg trouble at 4 cost him over a year, but back he won the Hakodate Kinen.
1430=Unbeaten in 5 up to the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun, she was local racing's 2009 Horse of the Year - the first 2yo ever. At 3 she aimed for the JRA's Oka Sho, but was 12th on turf in the Fillies' Revue and returned to dirt. Winning steadily every year, at 5 she beat strong JRA horses in the Tokyo Hai, and at 6 in 2013 finished with 4 straight wins incl. the Tokyo Sprint and Cluster C.
1431=His explosive late kick drew notice early. Running above his class, he was 2nd in the Kobe Shimbun Hai. Older, he won the Osakajo S., then swept past double classic winner Air Shakur in the Osaka Hai. 5th and 3rd in the spring and autumn Tenno Sho, he seemed to be coming good, but injury sidelined him. Back after a year for 2 races, he rested long again, but returning in '05 he won the Osaka Hai and Mainichi Okan, showing his power.
1432=Half-brother of Daitaku Riva, 2nd in the Satsuki Sho and Mile CS. Unlike his speedy brother, by Kikuka Sho winner Dance in the Dark he was a pure stayer. Over 3000m+ he had 3 wins, a 2nd and two 3rds from 6, winning the Hanshin Daishoten and Stayers S. He was 4th in the '04 Arima Kinen, but ran poorly in 2 more starts.
1433=Never consistent, he thrilled fans with rare, brilliant sweeps around the 3rd turn. The perfect example: the '01 Derby Grand Prix. Off poor runs, he was 9th choice of 14, the least fancied JRA horse, but as if stung, he swept to the lead past the 3rd turn and ran away in the straight, winning by 9 lengths for his first title.
1434=A familiar supporting player in sprints. At 3 he won the CBC Sho, coming from the back off a fast pace to sweep past strong older horses. Older, his form was erratic and his kick often dulled, but two G1 3rds proved his class, and longshot punters loved him. He moved to local racing at the end of '08 and retired at 12.
1435=He won the Cyclamen S. at the end of 2, then the Crystal C. as 13th choice. 3rd in the Japan Dirt Derby, he switched to dirt and was 2nd in the Thoroughbred Challenge C. and Derby Grand Prix, drawing notice. Then an accident sidelined him; back after about 6 months, he never got back on track and won just once more.
1436=A Bubble Gum Fellow filly based at Monbetsu. Debuting in May at 2 over 1200m on dirt, she won 3 straight. 9th in her first JRA race, the Lavender Sho, she then had a win and two 2nds, and in the JRA-local Edelweiss Sho, ridden for the first time by Yutaka Take, she won. Moving to Kawasaki, she was 2nd in the Tokyo 2yo Yushun Himba.
1437=Real Shadai's last great stayer. Improving from summer at 4, he won 2 10-million specials in autumn, then ran above his class in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, beating Tap Dance City and graded regulars with a performance far beyond an allowance horse. The next year he closed to half a length of Hishi Miracle in the Tenno Sho (Spring). Frustrating runs followed, and in the '06 Kokura Daishoten he dislocated a joint and was put down.
1438=A slow maturer who came good when older. He won the Milers C. in spring at 4 and was 3rd in the Yasuda Kinen against strong rivals. After about a year off, he won the Kita-Kyushu Kinen and Fuji S. Retired to stud, he was later exported to France.
1439=Unbeaten in 4 to the Spring S. Fans looked forward to an "Agnes showdown" in the Satsuki Sho with Agnes Tachyon, unbeaten in 3 to the Yayoi Sho, but injury just before the race made it a dream. Back after 6 months in the Kobe Shimbun Hai he was 8th, far from his best, then 8th in the Kikuka Sho. No longer the elite colt, he retired winless after 3 comeback starts.
1440=He chased favorite Agnes Gold for 2nd in the Spring S., then was 4th in the Satsuki Sho and 12th in the Derby. Improving in autumn, he won the St. Lite Kinen, but a stone bruise ruled out the Kikuka Sho. Back in the Arima Kinen (7th), he then had a long break with leg trouble. Never the same, he left the JRA after 3 starts for Southern Kanto, where after another year off he retired.
1441=With 1 win, he beat Daitaku Bertram, Millennium Bio and others in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai. 6th in the Derby and 9th in the Kikuka Sho, he then won the Keihan Hai. Older, he ran well but just couldn't win. Thought retired at the end of 6, he returned at local Kanazawa in '06 and won twice.
1442=Named "Born King" in hope, he was 5th to Agnes Tachyon on debut. He won the Keisei Hai at 3, was 2nd to Agnes Tachyon again in the Yayoi Sho, then 16th in the Satsuki Sho. Winless after, he rested long after the '02 Tenno Sho (Spring). He returned in June '05 after 3 years 2 months, but retired without a win.
1443=From local Oi, he was 3rd in the Japan Dirt Derby, 5th in the Teio Sho and 3rd in the JBC Classic there, then won the Teio Sho after moving to the JRA. He ran in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) on turf without success, then focused on dirt, winning the Kashiwa Kinen and more at the top level. Back at Oi at 8 in 2010, he placed in the Teio Sho 4 years running, a long-lasting partnership with jockey Fumio Matoba. He retired after the 2012 Tokyo Daishoten, sharing a retirement ceremony with Furioso, and became a lead pony at Oi.
1444=Fragile, and hurt below the hock as a foal, he debuted in a maiden in September at 3. Winning, he kept adding wins and, in his first graded race, the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai in autumn at 4, closed sharply for 2nd to Screen Hero, later Japan Cup winner. Racing only in graded races after, at 6 he won the Tenno Sho (Spring), his first graded win a G1. He was 4th in the Japan Cup and Hong Kong Vase. At 7, 4th as 2nd choice in the Kyoto Daishoten after a year off and 9th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he was thought past it, down to 14th choice in the Japan Cup, but closed in 34.0 for 3rd, stunning everyone. Steady in long races - 5th in the Hanshin Daishoten, 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) - he was 10th in the Japan Cup. "At 8, this must be it," people thought, but in his 4th Hong Kong Vase he ran on strongly for a close 2nd, surprising everyone again.
