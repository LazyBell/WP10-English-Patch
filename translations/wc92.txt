2223=A 2013 sprint star. In open class late at 3, he was 2nd in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai and Lord Derby CT at 4, then won the Keio Hai SC as favorite. Beaten badly in the Yasuda Kinen, he was 2nd in the Swan S. and 2nd to Tosen Ra by a length in the Mile CS. 2224=US-bred, a 2014-2015 dirt star, all-American dirt blood: by Majestic Warrior out of a Mr. Greeley mare. 2nd in the Hyogo Championship (Sonoda), he won the Unicorn S., top 3yo dirt horse, then was 5th in the Japan Dirt Derby and 3rd in the Musashino S. In 2014 he won the Subaru S. (OP), and was 3rd choice in the February S. but 13th. He won the Oasis S. (OP) as heavy favorite, was 2nd in the Akhal-Teke S. (OP), and won the Procyon S. At 1.2 in the Nambu Hai, he raced boldly and won by 4 lengths, his first G1. The next year he was 3rd in the February S., 2nd in the Kashiwa Kinen, and won the Nambu Hai again. At 6, winless, he was 3rd in the Kashiwa Kinen, 2nd in the Nambu Hai and 2nd in the JBC Sprint.
2225=Winner of the 2013 Radio Nikkei Sho. Improving late in spring, he stepped up to win it as a longshot (trifecta 910,000 yen). 5th in the St. Lite Kinen, he ran in the Kikuka Sho, won the 2 previous years by Stay Gold sons Orfevre and Gold Ship; this Stay Gold son was 5th.
2226=A 6-time dirt graded winner. He won the Leopard S. at 3 easily as favorite, fresh into open class. At 4 he won the BSN Sho and Radio Nippon Sho (both OP), then the Miyako S., sweeping from the back. At 5 he was 3rd in the Tokai S., 2nd in the February S. and won the Heian S., but a fracture before the Teio Sho cost a long break. At 6, 11th in the Tokai S., another fracture before the February S. followed. At 7 he won the March S., was 2nd to Copano Ricky in the Kashiwa Kinen, then won the Hakusan Daishoten and Musashino S. for a 6th graded win. At 8, winless, he was 3rd in the February S. and Kashiwa Kinen.
2227=A rare Heart's Cry sprinter who won the 2013 Kitakyushu Kinen, closing strongly as a longshot. Leg trouble followed; he retired after the 2014 Kitakyushu Kinen (10th), a year later.
2228=Winner of the 1991 Nambu Hai. Debuting in the JRA, after 2 maidens he moved to Niigata (local). At 5 he beat Iwate star Great Hope in the Nambu Hai: 27 wins from 57.
2229=A Southern Kanto mare who won the 1990 Diolite Kinen and Kashiwa Kinen, best foal of Oguri Cap's sire Dancing Cap. At 3 she won the Tokyo Princess Sho, a Southern Kanto fillies' classic. She won the new Kashiwa Kinen at 4 and was 3rd at 5.
2230=A Southern Kanto star who won the 1994 Kawasaki Kinen. JRA-based but stuck in the 15-million class, he moved to local racing at 5, won his first 2 and was 2nd as favorite in the Kashiwa Kinen. At 6 he won the Kawasaki Kinen as 9th choice, then the Tokyo Hai twice and the Grand Champion 2000.
2231=A Southern Kanto star who won the 1992 Kawasaki Kinen. Winning 6 of 11 from 3, he beat heavy favorite Daiko Garudan in the Kawasaki Kinen at 4. Fading after, he slumped in the JRA at 5, then moved to Kochi, racing until 9. He ran in the Kawasaki Kinen 4 years running.
2232=Winner of the 1993 Tokyo Daishoten in Southern Kanto. Hot-tempered, he barely won until 3, improving from 4. Invited to the JRA's All Comers in autumn at 5, he was beaten badly, then ran as if transformed: he beat top older horses in the Grand Champion 2000 and Tokyo Kinen, then won the Tokyo Daishoten easily.
2233=A Hokkaido local who won the 1991 Derby Grand Prix, a local 3yo championship (6th running), by a nose after a straight duel with favorite Keiwan Heart. Winless after, retired at 6.
2234=A Hokkaido local with 96 starts. Debuting at Obihiro, he moved to Southern Kanto at 4 and won the 1995 Kashiwa Kinen, then returned to Hokkaido, racing until 9: 25 wins from 96.
2235=A Southern Kanto star who won the 1995 Haneda Hai and 1996 Kashiwa Kinen. Unbeaten in 5 from debut, one of a Big Three with George Taisei and Concert Boy, he won the Haneda Hai, first leg of the local Triple Crown. Steady, he won the Kashiwa Kinen at 4. In his only JRA race he was 12th to Hokuto Vega in the February S.
2236=A Nagoya horse who also won the JRA's Tokai S. Winning 8 of 9 until early 4, he was 3rd in his first G1, the Kawasaki Kinen, 2nd in the All-Japan Thoroughbred C. and Nagoya Daishoten. At 8 he raced handy to win the JRA's Tokai S., proving himself there. Racing until 9 in 2004: 16 wins from 54.
2237=A 2007 2yo hope: a debut win as favorite, a close 2nd as favorite in the Sapporo 2yo S., 13th in the Asahi Hai FS, then 2 weeks later the Radio Nikkei Hai 2yo S. Out of form at 3, he switched to dirt and was 3rd at Kawasaki at 5.
2238=A 1980 Tenno Sho winner. A jinx then said a mare won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) every 9 years; 9 years after Tomei in 1971, she kept it alive. Tricky and erratic, she needed 8 starts to win, yet stepped up for 4th in the QEII Cup in autumn at 4 (old age system). At 5 she lost badly in open races, nearly retiring, then suddenly ran well in allowances. At 6 came her day: in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), with Derby winner Katsurano Haiseiko, Tenno Sho winner Hoyo Boy and mare Silk Ski fancied, she was 8th choice. As the fancies watched each other, she raced far clear and nobody could catch her - an astonishing wire-to-wire win. After a heavy defeat in the Arima Kinen she retired. Her daughter Steel Cast thrilled fans with a runaway lead, like her dam, in the Kikuka Sho Narita Brian won for the Triple Crown.
2239=A 2004-2009 star on turf and dirt. On turf until 4, he won the Crystal C., was 3rd in the Hakodate Sprint S. and 2nd in the Yodo Tankyori S. Switched to dirt at 5 in 2007, he was at once 3rd in Funabashi's Kashiwa Kinen, then won the Funabashi Kinen and Oi's After 5 Star Sho, racing until 7.
2240=The best of Fuji Kiseki's first crop, his only JRA graded winner. Never better than 5th after, he moved to Iwate at 7 and Arao at 8.
2241=A speedy 2002 2yo flash. She won her debut by 3 lengths, then the Niigata 2yo S. from last in record time. 4th in the Fantasy S. and 10th in the Hanshin JF, she was far back in the Fillies' Revue and Oka Sho, retiring at 4 after 9 starts.
