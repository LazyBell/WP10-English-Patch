3101=Debuting at 3 and winning his 4th start, he climbed 3 classes by the end of 4. At 5, 9th in the AJCC and 7th in the Kyoto Kinen, then after 5 months off he won the Tanabata Sho as 6th choice, breaking clear early from midfield by 2.5 lengths. 14th in the Niigata Kinen, 3rd in the Challenge C., 2nd in the AJCC at 6, then he won the Kokura Kinen in commanding style.
3102=Winning a maiden at 3, he passed the 2-win class by January at 4, then needed 12 tries until the Shirakawago S. in autumn at 5 for open class. 2nd in his first open race, the Brazil C. (L); at 6 he won the Procyon S. as 4th choice, breaking clear from a handy spot and holding on by half a length as the fancies collapsed.
3103=Struggling on dirt at 2, he switched to turf at 3 and quickly won 2 classes; the 3-win class took longer, but from summer at 4 he raced in graded and listed races. At 5, 4th in the Kochi S. (L), 2nd in the Tanigawadake S. (L), he won the Chukyo Kinen as 6th choice, leading as he likes and holding off outsider Cathedral by half a length. 5th in the Keisei Hai AH.
3104=A dirt miler who won the Elm S. in summer at 5. Nearly always among the top 3 choices, he never missed the top 5. Reaching open class in autumn at 4, he won the Marine S. (OP) at 5, then the Elm S. in his first graded race with a superb late run. Before the Nippon TV Hai he thrashed in the gate, was hurt and scratched; then 15th in the Musashino S. and 13th in the Antares S. at 6, and he retired.
3105=Winning his 2nd start, he won a 1-win race early at 3 and the 2-win class in spring, then the Leopard S. as 7th choice, waiting in midfield and winning a 3-way duel with fancied Taisei Drefong and Hapi. 3rd to K. I. Purple in the Hakusan Daishoten, 7th as 5th choice in the JBC Classic (G1).
3106=A late-blooming sprinter who won the 2025 Sprinters S. (G1) at 8. At 3 he was 4th to Contrail as 17th choice in the Satsuki Sho, 17th in the Derby. Climbing steadily, he reached the 3-win class at 4; at 5 he won the Tanigawadake S. (L), Yonago S. (L) and Sekiya Kinen (G3) - 3 straight - sharing favoritism with younger G1 winner Danon the Kid and beating him and outsider Shuri by 3/4 length. 12th in the Mile CS. At 6 he won the Tokyo Shimbun Hai (G3), then went abroad: 6th in Dubai's Godolphin Mile, 5th in the Mainichi Okan, 11th in the BC Mile. Turning to sprints at 7: 4th Takamatsunomiya Kinen, 2nd Keihan Hai. At 8, 2nd in the UAE's Al Quoz Sprint (G1), then he won the Sprinters S. - a G1 at last.
3107=Winning a maiden late at 2, 7th in the Shinzan Kinen and 4th in the Elfin S. (L), she skipped the classics, quickly won 2 classes and took the Niigata Himba S. (OP) in autumn. At 4, 2nd to Ruby Casablanca in the Aichi Hai and to Win Mighty in the Mermaid S., she won the Kokura Kinen in commanding style. 7th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Winless at 5 but often top 5 (3rd Aichi Hai, 5th Osaka Hai and Naruo Kinen, 4th Kokura Kinen and All Comers).
3108=Winning her 2nd start, she was 10th to Resistencia in the Hanshin JF. Turning to sprints, she won a 1-win race at 3 and 2 classes at 4. 10th in the Kitakyushu Kinen and 12th in the Centaur S., at 5 she pulled off a huge upset in the Kitakyushu Kinen: 16th choice at 164-1, she broke clear easily from a handy spot and held off fancied Namura Clair and Taisei Vision.
3109=Debuting at 3, he won a newcomer race and 2 classes, then rested about a year; back in summer at 4 he won the 3-win class into open class. At 5 he won the Shunrai S. (L), then 7th in the Hakodate Sprint S., 9th in the Ibis Summer Dash, before winning the Keeneland C. as 6th choice with a brilliant late run. 11th in the Sprinters S. At 6 he won the Ocean S., then 8th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen.
3110=A noted 3yo filly of 2022. After an easy win in the Wasurenagusa Sho (L), she was 2nd choice behind Circle of Life in the Oaks, but 7th. In autumn she won the Rose S. as favorite, holding off Sariela from a handy spot. 4th choice in the Shuka Sho, she was 5th. At 4 she won the Aichi Hai as favorite, but 4th as favorite in the Nakayama Himba S. and 13th in the QEII Cup, her last race; she retired to breed.
3111=2nd on debut in autumn at 2 to later Derby winner Do Deuce. After a fracture, back in March at 3 he won a maiden, then in autumn a 1-win race and the St. Lite Kinen (G2). 8th as Kikuka Sho favorite. Turning to the mile at 4: 2nd Milers C., 4th Yasuda Kinen, 5th All Comers, 5th Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 5 he tried dirt, 2nd in the February S. (G1), but after 15th in the Champions C. and 7th in the February S. at 6, he focused on turf, won the Fuji S. (G2), and was 2nd in both the Yasuda Kinen and Mile CS.
3112=Still a 1-win runner at 3, she ran well (4th Tulip Sho, 2nd Wasurenagusa Sho (L)). Climbing to the 2-win class, she was 5th as 12th choice in the QEII Cup in autumn at 4. At 5 she won the 3-win class, then the Fuchu Himba S. as 12th choice, flying past as if the rest were standing still and catching hot favorite Sodashi. 10th to Geraldina in her 2nd QEII Cup, 4th as 13th choice in the Arima Kinen. At 6 she struggled (10th Hanshin Himba S., 15th Victoria Mile, 5th Queen S., 7th Sapporo Kinen, 9th Fuchu Himba S., 8th QEII Cup), then was 3rd as 9th choice in her last race, the Challenge C., and retired to breed.
3113=Struggling on dirt, he switched to turf in spring at 5 and was transformed, rising to win the Japan Cup. Leg trouble delayed his debut to spring at 3; he won his 5th start and a 1-win race in January at 4, then hit a wall, never top 2 in 8 tries. On turf he won the Awaji Tokubetsu (2-win) at once and the 3-win class, then in his first graded race, the Kyoto Daishoten, as 2nd choice, caught favorite Bocchérini. In the Japan Cup, 3rd choice behind previous Derby winner Shahryar and 3yo Danon Beluga, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he came out best in the straight duel - a G1. 10th as 4th choice in the Arima Kinen; at 6, 13th in the Dubai World Cup, 8th Takarazuka Kinen, 7th defending the Japan Cup. Meant to race on, he was found to have tendon trouble and retired to stud.
3114=Beaten badly twice, he improved and won a maiden in spring at 3 in his 5th start, then a 1-win race that autumn and the 2-win class in January at 4; slower in the 3-win class, he reached open class that autumn. In the Argentine Republic Hai, 6th choice, he broke clear from up front and held off favorite T O Royal to win easily. At 5, 3rd in the Hanshin Daishoten, 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), 12th in the Takarazuka Kinen; in Australia, 8th in the Caulfield Cup and 16th in the Melbourne Cup.
3115=He won 3 straight from spring to autumn at 3, then needed 1.5 years in the 3-win class, reaching open class in spring at 5. 3rd in his first graded race, the Stayers S., and 3rd in the Hanshin Daishoten at 6. In the Tenno Sho (Spring) he unseated his rider at the start but ran the full 3200m riderless, crossing 2nd behind Titleholder - a talking point. That autumn he won the Stayers S., breaking clear coolly from midfield by 3/4 length. At 7 he won Saudi Arabia's 3000m Red Sea Turf H., then was 3rd to Justin Palace in the Tenno Sho (Spring), making amends for the year before.
3116=A dirt star from 2022 to 2025, winning the Kashiwa Kinen (JpnI) twice. Debuting in March at 3, he reached open class in about a year, then won the Tokyo Sprint (JpnIII), Summer Champion (JpnIII) and Oval Sprint (JpnIII); 3rd in the Mile CS Nambu Hai (JpnI) and 5th in the Champions C. (G1). At 5 he won the Kurofune Sho (JpnIII), 4th in the Kashiwa Kinen. After a winless spell, at 6 he won the Kurofune Sho again and the Kashiwa Kinen - a G1 horse. At 7 he won both again, then the Sakitama Hai (JpnI) - better than ever.
3117=Debuting at 3, he won a newcomer race easily as hot favorite. 9th in the Hyacinth S. (L), he won a 1-win race easily, then in his first graded race, the Hyogo Championship, as 3rd choice, broke clear from a handy spot and drew away by 8 lengths - in a class of his own. 3rd as Japan Dirt Derby favorite, then 5th in the Hakusan Daishoten and 12th in the Miyako S.
3118=Winner of the 2022 Japan Dirt Derby, champion 3yo on dirt. Winning his 3rd start and a 1-win race, he was 2nd to Delicada in the Fukuryu S. (OP) and 2nd, 8 lengths behind Blitz Fang, in the Hyogo Championship. In the Japan Dirt Derby he beat Blitz Fang and Unicorn S. winner Peisha Es - top of the 3yo dirt world. Mostly in G1-level races, 2nd in the Tokyo Daishoten. At 4, mostly unplaced, but 2nd in the JBC Classic and 4th in the Tokyo Daishoten. At 5, poor in G1-level races, he won the Saga Kinen and Nagoya GP.
