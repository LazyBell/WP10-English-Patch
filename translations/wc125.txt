2925=The 1978 Satsuki Sho winner. A debut winner, pulled up after his rider fell in the Hakodate 3yo S. (old age system), 2nd in the Keisei Hai 3yo S. and 4th in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. At 4, 2nd in the Tokyo 4yo S. (now Kyodo News Hai), he won the Yayoi Sho and the Satsuki Sho, holding off Inter Gushiken and Sakura Shori. 10th in the Derby, perhaps too far, he was 3rd in the Hakodate Kinen, then died of a twisted gut 10 days later.
2941=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the Tokyo Shimbun Hai and All Comers; 2nd to Green Grass in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 6 (old age system). Tough: 56 starts until 8, 17 of them at 5. In open class from late at 4, he won the Tokyo Shimbun Hai at 5, then after a 2nd in the Mainichi Okan, the All Comers. At 6, after the Diamond S., he was 2nd to Green Grass in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and 4th to Erimo George in the Takarazuka Kinen. Winless after, he retired in autumn at 8.
2942=Unbeaten in 4 incl. the Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system): his debut by 6 lengths, the Rindo Sho by 2.5, an open race by 7, then the Asahi Hai 3yo S. as 6th choice without graded form, beating favored Spirit Swaps and others. At 4 he was 4th to Tosho Boy in the Satsuki Sho, 18th in the Derby, then turned to sprints, winning an open race and the UHB Hai, 2nd in the Sprinters S. at 5. At stud for 8 years; no notable foals.
2943=Steady, never out of the top five from his debut, he won 5 straight at 4 (old age system) from allowances to the Kitakyushu Kinen and Kokura Kinen, then was 3rd to Tosho Boy in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai and 16th in the Kikuka Sho. At a mile to middle trips he was 2nd in the Kokura Daishoten, Swan S. and Hankyu Hai at 5, 2nd in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and Chukyo Kinen at 6, and won the Kokura Daishoten - a 3rd graded win. He retired at 7; his only unplaced runs came at 3000m, in the Kikuka Sho and Hanshin Daishoten.
2944=Debuting at 4 (old age system), he won allowances in summer, then was 5th in the Hakodate Kinen and 3rd in the Fukushima Kinen, and won the Nakayama Kinen in March at 5. In autumn, 2nd in the All Comers and 3rd in the Meguro Kinen, he was 4th to Kane Minobu in the Arima Kinen. At 6 he won 3 straight - Nakayama Kinen, an open race, Argentina Kyowakoku Hai - and at 7 the AJCC. No notable foals.
2945=Unbeaten in 2 at 3 (old age system), she then struggled in allowances, until a 2nd in the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu late at 4. At 5 she won the Hankyu Hai and Kinko Sho, at 6 the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and Kitakyushu Kinen. 2nd in the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu and 3rd in the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu, she retired in spring at 7. Her best foal was Flight Suzuka, 2nd in the Spring S., who ran in the Satsuki Sho and Derby.
2946=No Big Eight win, but he won the Yasuda Kinen and All Comers and was champion sprinter in 1966 at 5 (old age system). His sire Hishi Masaru (1955) left only 2 crops; he was the only one to succeed him. In allowances through 4, he bloomed older: 2 open wins, then the Yasuda Kinen, and that autumn the All Comers. 8th in the Arima Kinen, perhaps too far. At 6 he won the Keio Hai SH, 2nd in the Yasuda Kinen and Nakayama Kinen. With heavier weights, he won only 2 open races after, retiring in autumn at 7. At stud he sired Hishi Speed, 3-time graded winner incl. the Tokyo 4yo S. (now Kyodo News Hai) - 3 generations of home-bred sires - but Hishi Speed failed at stud and the line ended.
2947=A 1977 classic fancy. At 4 (old age system) he won the Keisei Hai and Tokyo 4yo S. (now Kyodo News Hai) and was Satsuki Sho favorite, but was 7th, then 13th in the Derby. His real misfortune was crop-mate Maruzensky, "the monster," who overshadowed the classic horses: in the Fuchu 3yo S., Asahi Hai 3yo S. and Tanchori S. at 4, he was 2nd to Maruzensky each time, crushed. He retired early, in autumn at 4, and had little success at stud.
2948=Debuting at 4 (old age system), she was 3rd in the Flower C. and 4th in the 4yo Himba Tokubetsu, then 23rd in the Oaks. Then she won 4 straight to the Sprinters S. - a gifted sprinter - and won it again at 5. 4th going for a 3rd win at 6, she retired. Her foals incl. Yasuda Kinen runner Main Dish.
2949=Unbeaten in 2, 7th in the Hanshin 3yo S. (old age system), he won the Cyclamen Sho to reach open class. At 4 he won an open race and the CBC Sho; at 5 the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, Swan S. and CBC Sho again. He retired after a 12th in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai at 6. No notable foals.
2950=The year Maruzensky won the Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system) by a street, he won the Hanshin 3yo S., western 3yo champion. At 4 he won the Kisaragi Sho, a classic name, but was 16th in the Satsuki Sho, 5th in the Derby and 6th in the Kikuka Sho. At 5 he won the Kyoto Daishoten and Meguro Kinen (Autumn), then was 4th as favorite in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) - a strong stayer. Winless after, he retired in January at 8.
2951=Winning allowances at 4 (old age system), 12th in the Kikuka Sho, he grew at 5 and won the Kyoto Kinen in autumn, then the Aichi Hai and Kimpai (West) at 6. In autumn he won the Kyoto Kinen again, then closed from the back for 5th as Pretty Cast ran away with the Tenno Sho (Autumn). He collapsed of a heart attack in training at 7 and died.
2952=Winning the Wakaba Sho in spring at 4 (old age system), she was last of 21 in the Oaks, then improved in allowances, blooming late at 4. After an open win, she won the BSN Hai at 5 and was 2nd to Sunny Flower in the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (now Fuchu Himba S.). At 6, 2nd in the Nakayama Himba S. and Yasuda Kinen, she won the Sprinters S. and retired at 7. She produced foals for 16 years; no stars, but MG White raced steadily at Kasamatsu.
2953=Debuting late at 3 (old age system), she won 3 straight allowances at 4 and was 6th of 21 in the Oka Sho. After about a year off, back in spring at 5, she ran 1st, 2nd, 1st, then won the Sprinters S. and the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (now Fuchu Himba S.) - champion older mare. She raced until autumn at 6. A modest broodmare, but a fast mare: 6 wins and 2 2nds from 11 at a mile or less.
2954=Winner of the 1979 Tenno Sho (Autumn); half-brother of Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Takarazuka Kinen winner Fujino Perseus. Winning late at 3 (old age system), he struggled in allowances until spring at 5, then stepped up to win the Diamond S. and bloomed: 3rd in the Mainichi Okan and Meguro Kinen, then the Tenno Sho (Autumn) after a tight finish with Mejiro Phantom. Injured soon after, he never returned, and was put down 2 years later at 7.
2955=The 1978 Oaks winner. Unbeaten in 2 at 3 (old age system), she then kept finishing unplaced, losing favor, until spring at 4: 2nd in the Flower C., 3rd in the 4yo Himba Tokubetsu, and into the Oaks. 5th choice, she held off favorites Hirono Ski and Sanem Joo in the straight. Unplaced in every race after, she retired in spring at 5. No notable foals, but 16 of them carry her blood.
2956=Of the Flory Scup line that shaped Japanese racing, she produced 2-classic winner Kitano Kachidoki, QEII Cup winner Lead Swallow, and Nihon Pillow Evert, dam of Nihon Pillow Winner - a great broodmare. Badly injured as a youngster, she never raced and went straight to breeding. Her first foal died soon after, and she stopped conceiving; near culling, she was sent to her owner's son's farm and turned out wild on the hills to regain her vigor - it worked. Her 3rd foal Kitano Kachidoki won the 1974 Satsuki Sho and Kikuka Sho, her 5th Nihon Pillow Evert later produced Nihon Pillow Winner, and her 6th Lead Swallow won the 1978 QEII Cup - a great dynasty. Her line lives on: 5 generations from her 4th foal Tokuno Perseus came Meisho Muramumo, 2021 Leopard S. winner.
2957=Winner of the 1978 QEII Cup. A debut winner at 3 (old age system), she struggled in allowances until the Nanohana Sho at 4, then was 4th to Oyama Tesco in the Oka Sho and 5th to Five Hope in the Oaks. After the Katsuragawa Tokubetsu, she was QEII Cup favorite and closed sharply from midfield to win. Older she won the Hankyu Hai, 2nd in the Kokura Daishoten and 3rd in the Swan S. Few foals, none notable.
2958=Beaten in his debut and a maiden at 3 (old age system), he was 2nd in the Kokura 3yo S. while still a maiden, then won one, was 3rd to later Tenno Sho winner Nichido Taro in the Momiji Sho, and won the Kyoto 3yo S. 2nd choice in the Hanshin 3yo S., he won as unbeaten hot favorite Bold Acan sank - western 3yo champion. He retired after a 2nd in an open race early at 4.
