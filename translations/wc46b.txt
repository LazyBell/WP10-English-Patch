630=From a top family, full sister of Scarlet Bouquet. She beat Arahoutoku in the Yonsai Himba Tokubetsu (West) and was Oka Sho favorite, but finished 12th, and injury cost her the classics. She later ran well over sprints and miles without winning.
640=3 wins from 42. At 3 she was 2nd to Mejiro Ramonu by two and a half lengths in the Oaks, but did little after. In autumn at 4 she finally won a graded race, the Kabutoyama Kinen, then retired. She produced top jumper Gokai (93: Judge Angelucci).
646=She won her debut by 9 lengths, then a 4-million special and the Tulip Sho by 2, 3 straight. 4th choice for the Oka Sho, she broke clear from a good spot, but stablemate Arahoutoku's sharp finish beat her into 2nd. After a 5th in the Oaks a fracture was found, but she came back to win the Rose S. Favorite for the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, she lost by a nose to outsider Miyama Poppy's late run. G1 class, short only on luck.
647=Breaking her maiden in her 3rd start, she won once more before the Oaks (7th). After summer she won the Queen S. and met Max Beauty in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, 5th behind Talented Girl, and became a regular in fillies' graded races.
651=Unusually for a Sakura Yutaka O runner, she rose on dirt, 5 of her 8 wins coming there. Late on, on turf, she was 3rd in the Epsom C. and Tanabata Sho and 2nd in the Sekiya Kinen. In her farewell, the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu, she won as favorite, her first graded win. As a dam she produced '02 Nakayama Kimpai runner-up Tough Grace (96: Tony Bin).
649=Half-sister of Satsuki Sho winner Narita Taishin. After 3 allowance wins she was 5th in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, won the Golden Whip T., and placed in the Hanshin and Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu. After a slump she won the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu at 5.
653=She won her debut and the Kokura Sansai S., then went winless until 5. Switched to front-running, typical of "A Shin" horses, she won 2 allowance races in a row and led for 3rd in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup.
654=6th in the Oka Sho and 11th in the Oaks, she bloomed in winter, winning the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu, Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu and Nakayama Himba S. in a row, then the Kyoto race again the next year - transformed against fillies.
