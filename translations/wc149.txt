3343=A great miler, Hong Kong Horse of the Year in 1999/2000 and 2000/01, and the first Hong Kong-trained winner of the Yasuda Kinen. He ran once at 2, winning on debut. At 3 in 1998/99 he won 4 of 8 incl. the Chairman's Prize (later Chairman's Sprint Prize), his first G1. At 4 in 1999/2000 he blossomed, winning the Hong Kong Sprint, then stunned the crowd winning the Yasuda Kinen in Japan as 10th choice. Just those 2 wins that season, but Horse of the Year. At 5, 2nd to 7-time Australian G1 winner Sunline in the Hong Kong Mile, he won 3 Hong Kong G1s - Stewards' C., Bauhinia Sprint T. and Chairman's Sprint Prize; 9th defending the Yasuda Kinen, he was Horse of the Year again. At 6 he won his opener, then was injured and retired. His name means "fairy" (lively) + "king prawn" (big shrimp), unrelated to Irish sire Fairy King.
3344=A globetrotter who won 6 G1s in England, Ireland, the US and Hong Kong, plus the Dubai Sheema Classic, and was 3rd in the Japan Cup. Unbeaten in 2 from August at 2, he won the Classic Trial S. (G3) at 3, but a heavy loss in the Derby Trial S. ended classic plans. 2 more wins encouraged his camp to try the Arc; 11th, far behind the Montjeu-El Condor Pasa duel. At 4 he blossomed: he won the Dubai Sheema Classic (G3), was 2nd in the Coronation C. and King George, won his first G1, the Man o' War S., in the US, 5th in the BC Turf, 3rd to T M Opera O in the Japan Cup, and won the Hong Kong Cup. At 5 he won the Tattersalls Gold C. and Prince of Wales's S.; 2nd to Galileo in the King George, he beat Galileo in the Irish Champion S., then won the BC Turf and retired. At stud in England, then Japan from his 5th season, he sired only 2 local G1 winners and was pensioned with little success.
3345=A US winner of 5 G1s incl. the 1999 Belmont S. As a sire he produced G1 winners Lemons Forever and Citronnade, and in Japan February S. winner Lemon Pop. Debuting in July at 2 and winning his 2nd start, he won the Futurity S. (G1); 5th in the BC Juvenile. At 3, 9th in the Kentucky Derby, he skipped the Preakness; after a G2 3rd he won the Belmont, beating hot favorite and dual classic winner Charismatic. He won the Travers S.; 6th in the BC Classic. At 4 he won the Brooklyn H. and Suburban H. (G2s), then the Whitney H. and Woodward S. (G1s) - 4 straight; 5th in the Jockey Club Gold C. and BC Classic, but Eclipse champion older male. Retired that year, his 2nd crop had Kentucky Oaks winner Lemons Forever and Gamely S. winner Citronnade; a steady sire, a pillar of the Mr. Prospector line.
3346=A US winner of 5 G1s incl. the 1999 Kentucky Oaks and Alabama S., one of Silver Deputy's best. Debuting in June at 2, she won 3 straight, a G2, then the BC Juvenile Fillies (G1) and a G3 - Eclipse champion 2yo filly. At 3 she kept winning: the Ashland S. (G1) easily, the Kentucky Oaks, and the Black-Eyed Susan S. (G2) for 8 straight. Against colts in the Belmont she was 7th, ending the run. She then won the Alabama S. and Gazelle H. for 5 G1s; 6th in the BC Distaff, but Eclipse champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won a listed race, then lost 4 and retired. No notable foals.
3347=A leading Danehill sire son, who spread the line with Arc winner Rail Link, King George winner Harbinger and many more. Not highly rated as a racehorse, he raced mainly at a mile and just missed at G1 level. Unbeaten in 2, 2nd in the Poulains, then 3rd in the Jacques le Marois and Moulin, 2nd in the Sussex S. and Prix de la Foret - G1 near-misses; he retired with a G2 and 2 G3s. At stud he was transformed: his 2nd crop had Arc winner Rail Link, then G1 winners Zambezi Sun, Harbinger and Dank. French champion sire in 2006, a key builder of the Danehill line with Danehill Dancer.
3348=Winner of the 2000 Derby, Irish Derby and Arc - the first to win both Derbys and the Arc. Debuting in September at 2, he won 2 straight incl. the National S. (G1). At 3, 2nd in a listed race - his only defeat - he won the Derby Trial S. (G3), then the Derby over later Arc winner Sakhee, and the Irish Derby by 9 lengths. After a rest he won the Prix Niel (G2) by 8, then the Arc, beating favored previous winner Montjeu and breaking the jinx that winners of both Derbys can't win the Arc. Cartier Horse of the Year went to crop-mate Giant's Causeway, but he beat him for champion 3yo colt, and retired. At stud he steadily sired stars like Irish Oaks winner Shawanda and Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud winner Youmzain, 3 times Arc runner-up. He died in France in 2018.
3349=In the 2001 US classics he was beaten in the Kentucky Derby, caught in a hot pace, but won the Preakness and Belmont. Debuting in August at 2 and winning his 2nd start, he won the Kentucky C. Juvenile S. (G3); in 3 straight G1s he was 2nd in the Champagne S. and BC Juvenile, then won the Hollywood Futurity. At 3 he won the San Felipe S. and Santa Anita Derby; in the Kentucky Derby he raced forward in a record-fast pace and faded to 5th. He bounced back in the Preakness, won the Belmont by 12 1/4 lengths, then the Haskell Invitational H. and Travers S. - 6 G1s. A BC Classic clash with Europe's best, Galileo, was hoped for, but tendon trouble ended his career. Eclipse Horse of the Year. At stud: Go Between, Coil and Canada's first filly Triple Crown winner Sealy Hill, but below expectations.
3350=A US sprinter who, despite long injury breaks, blossomed at 6 and won the 2004 BC Sprint. At stud he sired many G1 winners incl. Haynesfield, Tamarkuz and Japan's Mozu Superflare. Winning a maiden early at 3 in his 2nd start, he won 3 straight in spring, then a fracture kept him out nearly 2 years. Back in spring at 5, he won an allowance in 2 starts and rested again. At 6 he won a listed race and 3 straight G2s; 3rd in the Vosburgh S., he won the BC Sprint at last - Eclipse champion sprinter - and retired. Like him, his foals are mostly sprinters; in Japan Mozu Superflare and Rieno Tesoro made his name.
3351=Winner of the 2002 Dubai World Cup. 5 wins, 6 seconds and a third from 12 - remarkably consistent - and a great sire of Street Sense and Zenyatta. At 2 he won just a maiden from 5, 2nd in 2 G2s and 3rd in the BC Juvenile. Moving to Dubai at 3, he won the listed UAE 2000 Guineas. At 4 he hit his prime: the Maktoum Challenge R3 (G2), then the Dubai World Cup for his first G1, and the Stephen Foster H. 2nd in the Whitney H., he retired injured. His first crop had Kentucky Derby winner Street Sense and 13-time G1 winner Zenyatta - top first-season sire in North America. Shuttled to Australia, he sired Winx and many G1 winners - a world-class sire. He died at 16 at his peak, but Street Sense carries on his line.
3352=A US sprint filly of 2000-2003 with 26 wins from 35, Eclipse champion 3yo filly with just 1 G1. Debuting in June at 2, she won 6 straight incl. a stakes; 10th in the BC Juvenile Fillies, too far, she won 6 more into 3. In July at 3 she won the Prioress S., then G1; 2nd in the Test S. and BC Sprint - champion 3yo filly. At 4, no G1 but 7 wins incl. 5 stakes. At 5 she won another G2, then retired injured. 25 of her 26 wins were stakes - a North American record for a filly. As a broodmare, 2 graded-placed foals, nothing more.
3353=The 2003 Arc winner, 8 wins from 9, beaten only into 2nd in the Irish Derby - a precious carrier of the now-rare Mill Reef line. Winning his debut in August at 2, he won the Prix des Chenes and Criterium International (G1) - 3 straight. At 3 he won the Prix Greffulhe, Prix Lupin and French Derby unbeaten, then lost a duel with Alamshar in the Irish Derby, his only defeat. After a rest he won the Prix Niel, then the Arc, beating favorite High Chaparral, previous Derby and Irish Derby winner and BC Turf winner - Cartier Horse of the Year - and retired. His first crop had 4 G1 winners incl. 2-time BC Turf winner Conduit and Irish Oaks winner Moonstone; later French Derby winner Reliable Man and Grand Prix de Paris winner Shakeel. An undiagnosed illness stopped him covering in 2016, and he was pensioned.
3354=The 2004 Arc winner, known in Japan as the sire of Kikuka Sho winner Big Week and 3-time Grand Prix winner Chrono Genesis. Unbeaten in 4 at 2 incl. the Criterium International (G1), he won the Prix Jean Prat and Grand Prix de Paris at 3 - 6 straight. Stepping up in trip for the Arc, he was 3rd in the International S. and Prix Niel, then won the Arc as 5th choice - Cartier champion 3yo colt. At 4 he won only the Prix Ganay, but was 2nd in the Tattersalls Gold C. and 3rd in the King George and in the Arc again; 4th in the BC Turf and 8th in the Japan Cup, he retired to stud in Japan. His first crop had Kikuka Sho winner Big Week and Oka Sho runner-up Oken Sakura; later Chrono Genesis cemented his standing.
3355=A European sprint/mile filly of 2003-2005, the first to win both the English and Irish 1000 Guineas, unbeaten at the time. A brilliant front-runner, she won her first 5 at 2 incl. 2 stakes; injured after July, she still stunned enough to be Cartier champion 2yo filly without a G1. At 3 she returned in the 1000 Guineas, her first mile and first G1, and won, then the Irish 1000 Guineas and Coronation S. - 3 straight G1s. She lost 3 against older horses and colts, then won the Sun Chariot S. for a 4th G1. A stellar year, but crop-mate Ouija Board, winner of the Oaks, Irish Oaks and BC Filly & Mare Turf, took the headlines and the Cartier Award. At 4 she won the Matron S., a 5th G1, in 3 starts and retired. As a broodmare, one stakes-winning colt, nothing like her.
