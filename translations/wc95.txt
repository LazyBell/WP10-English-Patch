2313=Classic-class, yet in autumn at 3 he turned to the mile Fuji S. and won. In spring, still in the 5-million class, he was 3rd in the Mainichi Hai and 5th in the Satsuki Sho as 15th choice, but missed the Derby; 4th in the St. Lite Kinen, he passed on the Kikuka Sho. In the Fuji S. he closed strongly from the back to beat older horses. At 4 he was 2nd in Hong Kong's QEII Cup and, as 10th choice, closed fastest for 2nd to Lovely Day in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 5, 2nd in the Naruo Kinen, top five in the Takarazuka Kinen and Mainichi Okan, he was 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 6 he was 2nd in the Osaka Hai, newly a G1, and 2nd in the All Comers, but 10th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn); at 7, 4th in the Mainichi Okan and 9th of 12 in the Tenno Sho (Autumn).
2314=A 2015 middle-distance star. Debuting late in spring at 3, he won 5 straight to open class and was favorite at 1.9 in his first graded race, the Challenge C. At 4 he won the Epsom C. and Mainichi Okan as favorite; 9th as 2nd choice in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he went to Hong Kong and won the Hong Kong Cup, his first G1. At 5 in Europe he won France's Prix d'Ispahan by a huge margin, cruising clear on heavy ground. Back home, 2nd choice again in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he led from a fine start but faded to 12th.
2315=The 2014 Oaks winner. Harp Star drew all the attention, and she was little noticed in spring at 3. 2nd in the Tulip Sho though well beaten by Harp Star, she was 3rd as 5th choice in the Oka Sho, a length behind. Seen as needing more distance, she was 2nd choice in the Oaks, went clear early and held off Harp Star's kick for a G1. With Harp Star at the Arc, she was the autumn star: she won the Rose S. and, as heavy favorite in the Shuka Sho, closed but just missed, 2nd. Favorite in the QEII Cup, she was a close 2nd against older mares. At 4 she won the Nakayama Kinen and was 5th in the Takarazuka Kinen, among the top older horses; 2nd in the All Comers and QEII Cup, she was 2nd in the Hong Kong Cup, unable to catch leader A Shin Hikari. At 5 she was 6th in Hong Kong's QEII Cup and won America's Red Carpet H. - a globetrotter.
2316=Winner of the 2014 Fairy S. Highly rated, she went straight from her debut to the Fairy S. as favorite and edged clear from midfield in a 7-horse blanket finish. A fracture meant a long break; rated near Harp Star when sound, she has lost badly since returning.
2317=Winner of 3 fillies' graded races. After the Flower C., she skipped the Oka Sho for the Satsuki Sho, middle trips suiting her (11th). 3rd choice in the Oaks, she was a close 3rd to Nuovo Record and Harp Star. At 4 she won the Nakayama Himba S. Beaten badly after, she won the Aichi Hai at 5.
2318=A great mare who won the 2014 Shuka Sho and 2015 Japan Cup. Winning her 2nd start, she was favorite every time in open races but kept falling short, missing both the Oka Sho and Oaks. Restarting in allowances, she won in August and was a close 2nd in the Shion S., earning a Shuka Sho spot. 3rd choice behind heavy favorite Nuovo Record, she slipped through on the inside and held on - a perfect ride by Hamanaka, her jockey from the start. 6th against older mares in the QEII Cup, at 4 she matched the males, 3rd to Lovely Day in the Takarazuka Kinen. She won the All Comers and was 4th to Lovely Day from the back in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), then beat him in the Japan Cup - the best of all, mare or not. At 5 she was 3rd in the Osaka Hai and Victoria Mile, then retired injured while preparing for the Takarazuka Kinen.
2319=Winner of the 2014 Unicorn S., a leading son of dirt sire Gold Allure. Struggling on turf, he soared on dirt, winning his way up; 3rd choice in his first graded race, the Unicorn S., he went early from midfield and pulled away. He won 2 open races incl. the Subaru S., but no more graded races.
2320=Winner of the 2014 Japan Dirt Derby. Struggling as a maiden, he was transformed on dirt at 3, winning a 5-million and open race, then beat favorite Happy Sprint in Oi's Japan Dirt Derby. A dirt G1 regular, he was 2nd to Hokko Tarumae in the Kawasaki Kinen, later 2nd in the Miyako S. and 3rd in the Nagoya GP, but didn't win again, retiring at the end of 8.
2321=A super blueblood by Galileo out of 7-G1 mare Ouija Board. His owner, with farms in Australia, saved the name for a horse who could be Australia's leading sire. Debuting in June at 2, he won his 2nd start and a G3, then rested and went straight to the 2000 Guineas, a close 3rd. Hot favorite in the Derby, he won from midfield with a perfect run, then the Irish Derby at 1.125 - "like a morning canter." He beat older horses in the International S. easily, but was caught by The Grey Gatsby, beaten last time, in the Irish Champion S., 2nd, and retired. At stud he soon sired BC Mile winner Order of Australia.
2322=He ruled European miles in 2014 with 4 G1s incl. the Sussex S. and Prix Jacques le Marois: 7 wins from 8, his only loss a 2nd in the 2000 Guineas. After an easy debut and 2 graded wins, he was caught near the line in the Guineas by Night of Thunder, beaten last time. Taking the mile route, he won the Irish 2000 Guineas easily and ran down Night of Thunder in the St James's Palace S., then beat older horses incl. defending champion Toronado in the Sussex S. and won the Jacques le Marois - 4 straight G1s. An infection forced his retirement. At stud from 2015, his first crop gave 3-G1 winner Persian King, and in Japan Schnell Meister won the NHK Mile C.
2323=Winner of 7 G1s incl. 2 US classics and the Dubai World Cup, US Horse of the Year at 3 in 2014 and at 5 in 2016. California-bred, rare in the US, a standout by sire Lucky Pulpit. Emerging at home, he won the Santa Anita Derby, then the Kentucky Derby from a handy spot - the first California-bred winner in 52 years - and the Preakness, the first for a California-bred. 4th in the Belmont, he won the turf G1 Hollywood Derby, Horse of the Year. At 4 he was 2nd in the Dubai World Cup, then injured; at 5 he won it. Back home he won the Pacific Classic and Awesome Again S. for 7 G1s, then lost the BC Classic by half a length to younger star Arrogate, still Horse of the Year. Retiring at 6, he was later imported to Japan; his first foals debuted in 2023.
2324=Winner of 7 G1s incl. the King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. and BC Turf, a flagship son of Galileo. At 3, 6th in the French 2000 Guineas, 2nd in the French Derby and 5th in the Irish Derby, he won the Secretariat S. in America and the Hong Kong Vase. At 4 he won the King George, was 2nd to Found in the Arc, then led all the way to beat her in the BC Turf. Going for a 2nd Hong Kong Vase, he led but was caught by Satono Crown, 2nd. At 5 he won the Coronation C. and Prince of Wales's S., then a 3rd Hong Kong Vase and retired. At stud from 2018; first foals in 2021.
2325=In 2015 he won the US Triple Crown, the first since Affirmed 37 years earlier, then the BC Classic - America's first "Grand Slam." 5th on debut, still a maiden he won the G1 Del Mar Futurity easily, then the FrontRunner S., champion 2yo colt. At 3 he won the Rebel S. by 6 1/4 lengths and Arkansas Derby by 8. A narrow Kentucky Derby win was followed by easy wins in the Preakness and Belmont - the 12th Triple Crown winner. Then, at 1.7 in the BC Classic, he won by 6.5 lengths, retired, and was Horse of the Year. He sired G1 winners American Theorem and Harvey's Lil Goil, and in Japan Cafe Pharoah and Danon Pharaoh won G1s.
2326=At 5 in 2014, he won a Tenno Sho (Autumn) packed with stars like Isla Bonita and Gentildonna, as 5th choice. With no graded win before, his best was 3rd in the Kyodo News Hai and Mainichi Hai at 3; 14th in the Derby. After over a year off with leg trouble, he restarted in autumn at 4, stronger with age, and won 3 straight. 3rd in the Mainichi Okan with a sharp late charge, he unleashed it in the Tenno Sho (Autumn): as Isla Bonita and Gentildonna dueled, he flew past on the outside. 3rd to Epiphaneia in the Japan Cup, closing again, he proved it no fluke. At 6, 4th in the Osaka Hai, he was 6th in England's Prince of Wales's S.
2327=The 2014 Kikuka Sho winner. Colitis at 2 delayed his debut until the week after the Derby - then the fastest-ever rise to a Kikuka Sho win, a miracle horse. Winning his 3rd start and a 5-million race, he got into the Kobe Shimbun Hai by ballot; 9th choice and blocked in the straight, he was 3rd on the same time as the winner. Not entered for the classics, his camp paid a late fee. 3rd choice in the Kikuka Sho, he raced handy, led early in the straight and won a fierce duel with Sounds of Earth in record time, far clear of 3rd. At 4, delayed, he was 4th as 7th choice in the Takarazuka Kinen, ran in the Sapporo Kinen and rested. At 5, from the Hanshin Daishoten, he was 5th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and 11th in the Takarazuka Kinen.
2328=In 2015 he won 3 straight to his first graded win, then the Arima Kinen as 8th choice - no fluke, a mainstay of older middle-to-long races since. At 3, after 2 wins, he was 3rd to Toho Jackal in the Kikuka Sho. Resting until summer at 4, he won 3 straight to the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. Overshadowed by stars as 8th choice in the Arima Kinen, he raced handy and won - a Grand Prix winner. In 2016 he won the Nikkei Sho and All Comers, but was 12th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and 4th as 3rd choice in the Japan Cup. Defending the Arima Kinen, he was 3rd after a fierce duel with Satono Diamond and Kitasan Black.
2329=Winner of the Nikkei Shinshun Hai and Nikkei Sho, strong at long trips. 9th in the Satsuki Sho and 7th in the Derby, after a long break he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai as 6th choice from up front as favorites like Satono Noblesse faltered. He won the Nikkei Sho dominantly and was a Tenno Sho (Spring) fancy, but lacked his kick, 15th; that autumn his best was 7th in the Arima Kinen. At 5, winless, he was 3rd in the Kyoto Kinen and Hanshin Daishoten and 2nd in the Kyoto Daishoten. At 6 he was 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), steady to the end. Moving to Australia after retiring, he injured a ligament in training.
2330=Winner of the 2015 Kokura Kinen, on his 10th graded try. 2nd to One and Only in the Radio Nikkei Hai at 2, he then bounced between open and 16-million races. At 4 he stepped up for the Kokura Kinen under 2nd-year jockey Matsuwaka and closed from behind midfield to win by 1 1/4 lengths.
2331=Often placing in fillies' graded races, she finally won the Mermaid S. at 5. At 3 she was 3rd in the Tulip Sho and Rose S., at 4 2nd in the Fukushima Himba S. In the Mermaid S. at 6th choice, she went clear early from the leaders, was passed, but fought back to win.
2332=She ran in the Oka Sho at 3, then turned to dirt, 2nd in the Unicorn S. Improving at 4, she won 2 straight incl. the Kakitsubata Kinen, then was 2nd in the Procyon S. and 3rd in the Tokyo Hai. In the JBC Sprint she led from the start and held on by 3/4 length. The next year she won the Tokyo Sprint, 2nd in the Tokyo Hai and 3rd in the JBC Sprint.
2333=Bought for 75 million yen as a yearling, he won his debut with a sharp kick, then as 3rd choice held off hyped Deep Impact colt Avenir Marche in the Niigata 2yo S. in a course-record 1:33.4. A fracture found after the race dimmed his rise, but in his 2nd race back he was 3rd in the NHK Mile C.
2334=His grand-dam was Crystal C. winner Taiki Diamond and his half-brother graded placer Clarity City. Winning a maiden in his 3rd start, he led to win the new Icho S. by 2 lengths in record time. 3rd choice in the Asahi Hai FS, moved to Hanshin, he was 3rd to Danon Platina. Growing at 3, he was 5th as a longshot in the Satsuki Sho, then won the NHK Mile C. as 3rd choice, breaking clear from up front. He then slumped, 12th in the Mile CS; at 4 his best was 3rd in the Ireland T. (OP), but at 5 he was 2nd in the Nakayama Kimpai and 3rd in the Kokura Daishoten.
2335=His dam Black Emblem won the Shuka Sho as 11th choice, sparking a 10-million-yen trifecta. Winning a Tokyo debut in June at 2, he closed powerfully from the back like a Neo Universe in the Sapporo 2yo S. on tiring ground. Straight into the Asahi Hai FS as 2nd choice, he couldn't quicken, 7th. At 3 he was 2nd in the Yayoi Sho and 4th in the Satsuki Sho, but faded from autumn.
2336=He led all the way in the Hakodate 2yo S., unbeaten in 2. 3rd choice in the Keio Hai 2yo S. after 3.5 months off, he couldn't lead and was 6th. 9th choice in the Asahi Hai FS, he led and held 5th as the pace-setters collapsed. A sprinter at 3, he won the Centaur S. as 10th choice, then was 9th in the Sprinters S. - dangerous if allowed to lead.
2337=A 2014 debut winner who won the Kokura 2yo S. too. 15th choice and last into the straight, she charged wide with by far the fastest finish to pass the field. Straight into the Hanshin JF after a rest, she closed from the back again but only to 9th.
