3760=The 2025 Kikuka Sho winner. Winning his October debut at 2 and the Saintpaulia Sho (1-win) at 3, he won his first stakes, the Aoba Sho (G2), as favorite with a powerful late run from the back - unbeaten in 3, a Derby contender. Unwell, he skipped the Derby; against older horses in the Niigata Kinen he was 2nd to Shirankedo. Straight to the Kikuka Sho, with both the Satsuki Sho winner Museum Mile and Derby winner Croix du Nord absent, he was narrow favorite over Kobe Shimbun Hai winner Eri King; moving early from the back on the 3rd turn, he took the lead and won decisively - a G1.
3761=A leader of the 2025 3yo crop, winner of the Tenno Sho (Autumn) (G1). Unbeaten in 2, 11th as 4th choice in the Hopeful S. at 2, he won the Kyodo News Hai (G3) at 3 as favorite. 3rd to Museum Mile in the Satsuki Sho, charging from the back; 2nd in the Derby, 3/4 length behind Croix du Nord. Skipping the Kikuka Sho, he was favorite over older horses in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), and beat Satsuki Sho winner Museum Mile by 3/4 length, both closing from midfield. Favorite in the Japan Cup, he lost a fierce duel with French star Calandagan by a head.
3762=Winner of the 2025 Champions C. (G1), only the 2nd filly or mare to win it, after Sanbista in 2015. Debuting late, in summer at 3, she won 4 straight from a maiden to the 3-win class, then her first open race, the Sannomiya S. (OP) - 5 straight. 2nd in her first stakes, the Breeders' Gold C., her first defeat, she won the Miyako S. (G3) as 2nd choice, leading early and holding off Simon Zanadu by a neck. In the Champions C., with top 3yo Narukami hot favorite, she was 3rd choice; saving ground on the rail, she kicked in the straight and beat 2-time runner-up Wilson Tesoro by a nose - a G1.
3763=Champion 2yo filly of 2025. 5th on debut closing from the back, she then raced forward and won a maiden by 7 lengths. Hot 1.7-1 favorite in the Chukyo 2yo S. The Hanshin JF had no graded winners; 2nd choice behind unbeaten Alankar, she waited in midfield and held off fast-closing Gallaborg by 1 1/4 lengths - her first stakes win a G1.
