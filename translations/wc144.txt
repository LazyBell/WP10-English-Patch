3273=Sir Gaylord's best son, a great European miler who sired many speedsters like himself, founding the Habitat line. Timid, he was slow to train and first raced in April at 3. Winning his 3rd start, he went straight into Britain's top mile race, the Lockinge S., and beat the stars as an outsider. 2nd in the St. James's Palace S., he won the Prix Quincey and Wills Mile, then the Prix du Moulin de Longchamp easily in his final race. At stud his first crop included Habat, making him top first-season sire in Britain and Ireland; 3 times champion 2yo sire, he produced many precocious sprinters. Steel Heart, from his 2nd crop, was imported to Japan and sired Nihon Pillow Winner, continuing to Yamanin Zephyr.
3274=Gallant Man's best foal, US champion 3yo filly of 1969, unbeaten in 8 that year, ahead of rival Shuvee. Inconsistent early, she found form in autumn at 2, winning 4 of 5 to year-end incl. the Matron S. and Gardenia S. At 3 she was even better, winning 8 straight from the spring Liberty Belle H., beating Shuvee in the Delaware Oaks and Gazelle H. and older star Gamely in the Matchmaker S. - undisputed champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won her first 2, then was 3rd against males in the Nassau County H. and 7th in the Suburban H., and retired. Of her 6 foals, none stood out.
3275=The strongest branch of Grey Sovereign's Fortino line comes through Caro. Racing mainly in France, he won the Poule d'Essai des Poulains, Prix d'Ispahan and Prix Ganay, and was 4th to Mill Reef in the Arc. His 2nd crop included French Derby winner Crystal Palace and French Oaks winner Madelia, making him French champion sire. Cozzene, Golden Pheasant, Winning Colors and more followed - 77 stakes winners in all. Crystal Palace, Cozzene and Golden Pheasant succeeded at stud, carrying his line today. A fine broodmare sire too: Tayasu Tsuyoshi, Maria's Mon, Mendez and others.
3276=The first US mare to earn $1 million. Graded races began in 1973, when she was 4, and she won 8 G1s through 6. She blossomed late at 2, winning the Villager S. by 8 lengths, then 6 straight at 3 to the Kentucky Oaks. In the New York Filly Triple Crown she won the Acorn S. but was 2nd in the Mother Goose S. and 3rd in the CCA Oaks. 3 more wins made her champion 3yo filly. At 4, with grading introduced, she won 4 G1s incl. the Santa Margarita Invitational H. and Santa Barbara H. - Eclipse champion older mare. A fracture at 5 threatened her career, but famed vet Dr. Copelan operated and she returned; at 6 she won 4 more G1s incl. the Matchmaker S. and Beldame S., champion older mare again, and retired. Of her 11 foals, a colt named Copelan after the vet won the Hopeful S. and went to stud.
3277=The 1972 US dual classic winner. He won the Kentucky Derby as favorite, struggled on sloppy ground for 4th in the Preakness, then won the Belmont easily. Winning his 2nd start, he took 5 straight incl. the Flash S. and Futurity S., the first Eclipse champion 2yo colt. At 3 he won the Hibiscus S. and Blue Grass S., then 2 classics, and the Hollywood Derby - best of his crop. Then 5 losses against older horses; champion 3yo went to crop-mate Key to the Mint. At 4 he beat Key to the Mint in the Brooklyn H. and met that year's Triple Crown winner, stablemate Secretariat, in the Marlboro C. Invitational H.; 2nd, but strong in defeat. He retired that year; at stud, 29 stakes winners incl. Hopeful S. winner Tap Shoes.
3278=A great sire carrying a main Never Bend branch, founding the Riverman line through Irish River, Riverlea, Rousillon and more. Winning his debut at 2, at 3 he won the Prix de Montenica, the Poule d'Essai des Poulains, the Prix Jean Prat and Prix d'Ispahan - 4 straight - then 3rd in the King George and 2nd in the Champion S., and retired. At stud he quickly sired 7-time G1 winner Irish River and Arc winner Gold River, French champion sire in 1980 and 1981. Rousillon, Riverlea and 9-time G1 winner Triptych, the "Iron Lady," followed. As broodmare sire: El-Harb, Erimo Excel and more.
3279=The leading horse of the Native Dancer branch through Etan, conceived during Etan's one year at stud in Ireland before export to Japan. Nearly all of Etan's line runs through him. Racing in England, he won his first 5 at 2, but lost 3 at 3 and retired. At stud he soon sired G1 winners Kris and Sharpo, champion 2yo sire in Britain and Ireland in 1978; then Diesis and Pebbles, and in the US, Trempolino and Selkirk. Kris, Diesis and Selkirk carried the Etan line on to today.
3280=A dominant US filly of 1973-1974, Eclipse champion 3yo filly and champion older mare, known as the dam of Real Shadai. Modest at 2 (a maiden win from 5), she blossomed at 3, winning 6 straight G1s: Monmouth Oaks, Delaware Oaks, Test S., Alabama S., Gazelle H. and Beldame S. - champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won the Beldame S. and Matchmaker S., and on her first turf start was 2nd in the Washington DC International, ahead of great mare Dahlia - champion older mare. She ran once at 5 and retired. She died at just 12, but her 2nd foal, Real Shadai, was a great sire, raising her name.
3281=Zeddaan's best son and lead sire heir. His son Kampala sired Tony Bin, building the line in Japan. Racing in France, he won the Poule d'Essai des Poulains, Prix Lupin and Prix Jacques le Marois. His first crop included Jacques le Marois winner Kenmare, then G1 winners Shakapour, Bikala and Kalaglow; he sired 21 stakes winners before dying at just 9. Kenmare sired Highest Honor and Kendor. Kampala, from his 2nd crop, was ordinary on the track but sired Tony Bin, the key to the line: imported to Japan, Tony Bin sired Winning Ticket, Sakura Chitose O, Jungle Pocket and more, spreading Kalamoun's blood.
3282=A filly who won her first 12 races at 2 in 1972 - Eclipse champion 2yo filly, and Horse of the Year rival of fellow 2yo Secretariat. Debuting at Canada's Woodbine, she won the My Dear S. in her 3rd start, then the Fleur de Lis S., Colin S. and Schuylerville S. easily, and the Selima S. on sloppy ground by 14 lengths. Unbeaten in 12, she split the Horse of the Year vote with Secretariat, who won the Eclipse; she was Canada's Sovereign Award Horse of the Year. At 3 she faded, winning 2 stakes from 14; at 4, only 5 allowances. After an 8th in the Miss Florida H. she fell ill and died. Still, she was Sovereign champion older mare at 4 and later entered both the Canadian and US Halls of Fame.
3283=The 1975 Kentucky Derby winner, best known for his match race with great filly Ruffian, who broke down fatally in it. Emerging from debut, he won the Dover S. by 10 lengths, then the Sapling S., Hopeful S. and Champagne S. - 3 G1s; unbeaten in 7, undisputed champion 2yo. At 3 he won the Flamingo S. and Wood Memorial, then the Kentucky Derby as favorite; 2nd in the Preakness and Belmont, he was still top of his crop. Meanwhile Ruffian swept the New York Filly Triple Crown, and a match race was arranged; Ruffian broke down and was put down, so he is remembered as her last opponent. Winless after, champion 3yo went to Wajima. At 4 he won 4 incl. the Suburban H. and retired. A fair sire with Marfa, Prayers'n Promises and more; his male line has since declined.
3284=A sire son of Nijinsky who sired Arc winner Suave Dancer and many more, and won the Poule d'Essai des Poulains and Prix Lupin himself. At 2 he won his debut, was 2nd in the Prix de Chenes, then won England's G1 Observer Gold C. At 3 he won the Poule d'Essai des Poulains and Prix Lupin - 3 straight G1s - but was 6th in the Derby and 8th in the Arc, less effective over further, and retired. His first crop included French 1000 Guineas winner Aryenne; with Maximova and Suave Dancer he was French champion sire in 1991. His 2nd-crop son No Attention, in Japan, sired Super Creek, and late son A.P. Preston won Japan's Asahi Hai 3yo S.
3285=A German-bred by German sire Literat out of German mare Surama, the 1977 German Derby winner. Winning his 3rd start and the Union-Rennen (G2), he won the German Derby easily as favorite; also 2nd in the Aral-Pokal (G1). He retired after the Derby with 3 wins from 9. A great sire, with 3-time West German Horse of the Year Acatenango, 2-time Horse of the Year Mondrian, and Platini, 4th to Legacy World in the 13th Japan Cup; 6 times German champion sire. Acatenango, siring Lando, Borgia and more, was champion sire 5 times - a heir every bit his equal.
3286=A filly who won the 1979 Arc. Her International Classification rating of 137 remains the highest ever for a filly even under today's World Thoroughbred Rankings (revised to 132 in 2013). Winning her only start at 2, at 3 she won the Prix Vanteaux, Poule d'Essai des Pouliches and Prix Saint-Alary; a close 2nd in the Prix de Diane, she won the Prix Vermeille, then the Arc, beating hot favorite Troy - rated 137 to his 136. At 4 she won the Prix d'Harcourt, was 2nd in the Prix du Prince d'Orange and 4th in the Arc, then 8th in America's Turf Classic S. She retired to breed in the US; of 7 foals, her 4th, the colt Oregon, went to stud.
