3478=Winner of the 2008 Kentucky Derby and Preakness unbeaten. Hoped to be the first unbeaten Triple Crown winner since Seattle Slew, he was inexplicably pulled up in the Belmont. Brilliant from the start, he won his September debut at 2 by 11 1/4 lengths, an allowance at 3 by 12 3/4, the Florida Derby by 5 and the Kentucky Derby by 4 3/4 - no one could get near him. He won the Preakness by 5 1/4. A Triple Crown seemed certain; he was 1.3-1 Belmont favorite, but faded at the crucial point and was pulled up, a crushing letdown for fans. He won the Haskell Invitational S. (G1) and the turf Monmouth S. (L), then retired as Eclipse champion 3yo. At stud: G1 winner Dortmund and scattered stakes winners - modest.
3479=A US winner of 7 G1s from 2007 to 2011, Eclipse champion turf male in 2009 and 2010 - 12 wins and 10 seconds from 29, only twice unplaced. Unbeaten in 2 from debut, 2 of 3 at 2; at 3 he won 3 stakes incl. the Virginia Derby (G2). At 4 he blossomed, winning 4 straight G1s - Frank E. Kilroe Mile H., Manhattan H., Man o' War S., Arlington Million - and was 2nd to Zenyatta in the BC Classic; champion older male and turf male. At 5 he won the Man o' War S. and Shadwell Turf Mile S., champion turf male again. In 2011 at 6 he won only the Shadwell Turf Mile from 6 G1 tries and retired. His best foal is BC Sprint winner Drefong.
3480=An Australian sprint gelding of 2010-2012 who repeatedly met Black Caviar, unbeaten in 25, finishing 2nd to her 4 times. Debuting at 3 in 2008/09, he won 8 straight into 2009/10, then 3 more in 2010/11 incl. the Manikato S., his first G1. That season his clashes with Black Caviar began: 2nd in the Lightning S., T J Smith S., BTC Cup and the next season's Lightning S. In 2011/12 he won the All Aged S. and Newmarket H. - 3 G1s. After about a year off, he returned in March 2013, winless in 5, then 11th in The Shorts (G2) and 14th in the Premiere S. (G2) at 8, and retired.
3481=An invincible Singapore sprinter of 2008-2012 who also won 2 international G1s, the Dubai Golden Shaheen and KrisFlyer International Sprint, and was 4th to Curren Chan in Japan's Sprinters S. Debuting in October 2008 at 3, he won his first 7, mostly easily. From 2010 he tried international G1s: 2nd in the Golden Shaheen, KrisFlyer and Hong Kong Sprint. In 2011 he won the Golden Shaheen and KrisFlyer, then 4th in the Sprinters S., his first finish outside the top 3. 2nd defending the Golden Shaheen in 2012, he won once at home, then leg trouble kept him out; his retirement was announced at the end of 2016, at 11.
3482=Winner of the 2010 BC Classic (G1), handing 19-race unbeaten favorite Zenyatta her only defeat. Debuting in September at 2, he won his 2nd start, rested 8 months, then won 2 straight, the Fayette S. (G2), Clark H. (G2), and at 4 the William Donald Schaefer S. (G3), Stephen Foster H. and Whitney H. (G1s) - 5 straight. 2nd to Haynesfield in the Jockey Club Gold C., he then beat Zenyatta in the BC Classic, breaking clear and holding off her furious run from wide by a head. He retired after it; Horse of the Year went to Zenyatta, but he was champion older male. At stud: G1 winners such as French Oaks winner Senga and CCA Oaks winner Wet Paint.
3483=A filly who won 3 G1s incl. the Woodward S. in 2011, Eclipse Horse of the Year - the only star, and masterpiece, of Saint Liam, who died after one crop. 1 win from 2 at 2; back from a long rest in May at 3, she won the Cotillion S. (G2), but was 2nd in the Alabama S. and 3rd in the BC Ladies' Classic. At 4 she improved, winning the Azeri S. (G3), Apple Blossom H. (G1) and Obeah S. (G3), then beat the males in the Woodward S. and won the Beldame Invitational S. 4th in the BC Classic, she was still Horse of the Year. At 5 she won a listed race easily, then retired injured. Bred to Tapit and others, her 2017 foal Marvelous Guy was imported to Japan.
3484=A French mare who starred in European sprints and miles from 2011 to 2013, winning 6 G1s incl. 3 straight Prix Maurice de Gheest. Unbeaten in 2 from August at 2, she won the Prix Imprudence (G3) at 3; 7th in England's 1000 Guineas, she won the Maurice de Gheest at home. At 4 she won it again and the Prix du Moulin; 8th of 9 in America's BC Mile. At 5 she won the Maurice de Gheest, Jacques le Marois and Prix de la Foret - 3 straight G1s; 6th in the Hong Kong Mile, but Cartier champion older horse, and she retired. Unbeatable in France with 6 G1s, she lost all 5 starts abroad - yet in one, the Diamond Jubilee S., she was 2nd by a head to Australia's unbeaten Black Caviar, the only horse ever to truly threaten her.
3485=The 2012 Arc winner, famous in Japan for catching hope Orfevre near the line to win by a neck, crushing Japanese fans. To 3 she was unremarkable: 2 wins from 7 and a stakes 2nd. At 4 she won a listed race and the Prix Corrida (G2), then 4th in the Prix de Pomone and 3rd in the Prix Vermeille. Her camp took a bold shot at the Arc, where unbeaten Derby winner Camelot and Japanese Triple Crown winner Orfevre were fancied; she was 12th choice. In the straight Orfevre looked the winner but veered badly, and she pounced to win by a neck - shattering Japan's dream and making her name famous there. 13th in the Japan Cup, far behind the Gentildonna-Orfevre battle, she retired.
3486=Winner of the BC Filly & Mare Sprint (G1) in 2012 and 2013, Eclipse champion female sprinter both years. Debuting late in June at 3, she won 3 straight to the Gardenia S. (G3). At 4 she won the Madison S. (G1), the Humana Distaff S. (G1) by 7 1/4 lengths in record time, and the BC Filly & Mare Sprint by 4.5 - 5 straight. At 5 she won the Masters S. (G2) and the BC Filly & Mare Sprint again, holding the lead to the line by half a length. 4th in the Cigar Mile H. (G1), she won the Hurricane Bertie S. (G3) at 6 and retired.
3487=A gelding who won 5 straight G1s in 2015 incl. the Dubai Turf and QEII S., a miler and Cartier champion older horse. Debuting over a mile in September at 2, he kept losing; stepped up at 3, he won his 5th start at 2400m. After nearly a year off, 6th of 8 in his first stakes, the 3100m Prix Vicomtesse Vigier (G2) at 4, he returned to the mile and was transformed: 5 straight into 5 to the Dubai Turf (G1), then the Prix d'Ispahan, Queen Anne S., Sussex S. and QEII S. Unbeaten in 6 that year, Cartier champion older horse; one more win at 6 made 10 straight, and he retired.
3488=A filly who won the 2014 Oaks (G1) and King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. (G1) - only the 2nd 3yo filly to win the King George, after Pawneese 38 years earlier. Winning her debut in September at 2, she rested long. Aimed at middle distances at 3, she won the Pretty Polly S. (L) and the Oaks easily. Taking on older horses and colts in the King George, she beat Telescope, Magician and others by 3 lengths. Hot favorite in the Yorkshire Oaks, she lost a duel with Tapestry by half a length; favorite in the Arc too, she was 3rd as Treve won again, and retired to breed.
3489=An Irish mare who won the 2016 Arc (G1). With just 1 G1 that year but 6 G1 seconds, she was Cartier champion older horse. Debuting in August at 2, she won 2 of 3 incl. the Prix Marcel Boussac (G1). At 3 she was 2nd in the Irish 1000 Guineas, Coronation S., Irish Champion S. and Champion S. - so close - then won the BC Turf (G1) in America. At 4 she was 2nd in 5 straight G1s: Tattersalls Gold C., Coronation C., Prince of Wales's S., Yorkshire Oaks and Irish Champion S. 5th choice in the Arc, she won in record time, making up for it all. 2nd in the Champion S. and 3rd in the BC Turf, she retired - 10 G1 seconds in 2 years, a remarkable record. Her foal by War Front, Battleground, won the Vintage S. (G2).
3490=A filly who dominated the 2016 English fillies' classics, from a blueblood family with full sisters Empress Josephine (Irish 1000 Guineas) and Tuesday (Oaks). Debuting early, in June at 2, she won her 2nd start, then the Moyglare Stud S. and Fillies' Mile (G1s) - Cartier champion 2yo filly. At 3 she won the 1000 Guineas easily on her return, lost the Irish 1000 Guineas by a head, and won the Oaks as favorite - best of her crop. She beat older horses in the Pretty Polly S. and Nassau S. (G1s); 3rd in the Irish Champion S., she won the QEII S. against colts. Cartier Horse of the Year and champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won the Mooresbridge S. (G2) easily, then retired injured. Her son Henry Longfellow won the Vincent O'Brien S., his first G1.
3491=Winner of the 2017 BC Classic (G1), Woodward S. (G1) and more, Eclipse Horse of the Year. A fine start at stud too: his first crop includes Preakness winner Early Voting and many others. Unbeaten in 2 from September at 2, he won the Risen Star S. (G2) at 3; 3rd in the Kentucky Derby, 2nd in the BC Dirt Mile, he won the Clark H. (G1) and blossomed. At 4 he won the Razorback H. (G3) easily; 2nd in the Dubai World Cup, he won the Stephen Foster H., Whitney S., Woodward S. and BC Classic - 4 straight G1s - Horse of the Year. He won the Pegasus World Cup (G1) at 5 and retired to stud.
