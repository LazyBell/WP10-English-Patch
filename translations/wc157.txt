3464=An Australian sprinter of 2002-2003 who, at Royal Ascot in 2003, became the first Southern Hemisphere-trained winner of both the King's Stand S. (G2) and Golden Jubilee S. (G1). Debuting in 2001/02 at 2, he won easily, then the Skyline S. (G3) in his 5th start. In G1s he was 3rd, 2nd, 3rd. At 3 he missed 6 more times, then won the Emirates Classic (G2) and Lightning S. (G1). Late that season in England he won the King's Stand S. as 13th choice, and 4 days later the Golden Jubilee S. as 2nd choice. Favorite in the July C. (G1), he was 2nd, then retired to stud. A fine sire, with Golden Jubilee winner Starspangledbanner and many G1 winners.
3465=A durable Hong Kong horse who raced until 9 and won the Yasuda Kinen (G1) at 7 in 2005/06, Hong Kong Horse of the Year. Debuting in England, he won his first 2, then nothing to the end of 3, and moved to Hong Kong. Just 1 win from 7 in 2002/03; in 2003/04 he won the Hong Kong Kimpai, a first sign of progress. From 2004/05 he raced at the top, winning the Stewards' C. and Champions Mile. In 2005/06 he won the Champions Mile again, then in a wide-open Yasuda Kinen beat Asakusa Den'en and others for his first G1 - Horse of the Year. Declining from 2006/07 (winless in 9, then 1 win from 7), he raced until the Hong Kong Cup in December 2008 and retired.
3466=Winner of 4 G1s in 2005 incl. the BC Classic, US Eclipse Horse of the Year. Debuting at 3, he won just a maiden and an allowance from 9. At 4 he improved, placing in stakes, then 2nd by a neck to that year's Horse of the Year Ghostzapper in his first G1, the Woodward S. At 5 he won the Donn H., his first G1, then the Stephen Foster H. and Woodward S., and the BC Classic as favorite - top of the tree, Horse of the Year - and retired. He died after just one crop, which included Woodward S. winner Havre de Grace, a filly.
3467=An Australian sprint mare who won 5 G1s in 2006-2007. Debuting at 3 in 2004/05, she won easily, then after a 2nd won 5 straight. In 2005/06 she won the Winterbottom S. (G2), but was 6th and 11th in G1s. In 2006/07 she won the Manikato S., then the Lightning S., Australia S. and Newmarket H. - top of Australian sprinting. At Royal Ascot she won the King's Stand S. (G2) easily; 15th in the Golden Jubilee S., but Australian Horse of the Year. In 2007/08 she won The Age Classic for a 5th G1, and retired after a 10th in the Hong Kong Sprint.
3468=Winner of the 2005 Poulains and French Derby, now better known as a sire: dual French classic winner Lope de Vega, BC Turf and Prix Vermeille winner Tarnawa and many more G1 winners; in Japan, graded winner Light On Cue. Debuting in July at 2, he won 3 straight to the Dewhurst S. (G1), Cartier champion 2yo. At 3, 9th in the UAE Derby, he won the Poulains, French Derby and St. James's Palace S. - 3 straight G1s - then a fracture ended his career. His 2nd crop had Lope de Vega; then Hong Kong stars Dan Excel and Able Friend, French 1000 Guineas winner Castle Lady and others - over 20 G1 winners. Lope de Vega has succeeded at stud too.
3469=A French filly who won the 2005 Pouliches and French Oaks unbeaten; Cartier champion 2yo filly, then champion 3yo filly. Debuting in May at 2 under Christophe Lemaire, then based in France, she won all 5 incl. 2 G1s, the Prix Morny and Prix Marcel Boussac. At 3 she won the Prix de la Grotte (G3) easily, the Pouliches by 5 lengths, the French Oaks by 3, and the Prix d'Astarte (G1) against older mares. Against colts in the Jacques le Marois, she was 4th to Dubawi - her first defeat - and retired. Her first foal, Eightfold Path, won a US G3.
3470=Winner of the 2005 Preakness and Belmont S. At the time, a girl also named Alex died of childhood cancer at 8; his owners donated to and raised money for a childhood cancer fund, earning a special Eclipse Award. Debuting in June at 2, he won 4 straight to the Hopeful S. (G1). At 3 he won the Arkansas Derby (G2), then was caught by 2 outsiders in the Kentucky Derby, 3rd. He made amends in the Preakness by 4 3/4 lengths and won the Belmont by 7. An injury then ended his career; with only a spring campaign, he was still Eclipse champion 3yo. At stud he sired Texas Red, Afleet Express and other G1 winners.
3471=A US turf horse of 2005-2007 with 6 G1s incl. the BC Turf, which he won on his 3rd try by a record 7 lengths. Always on turf, he won the Virginia Derby (G3) in summer at 3, but was beaten in the Secretariat S., Turf Classic Invitational and BC Turf. At 4 he won the Turf Classic S., United Nations S. and Turf Classic Invitational, then 3rd to Red Rocks in the BC Turf. Unsatisfied, he raced on at 5, winning the United Nations S. and Turf Classic Invitational again, then, on soft ground for the first time, broke clear from a handy spot and drew away in the BC Turf. Eclipse champion turf male, he retired. At stud he sired Arlington Million winner The Pizza Man and more.
3472=Winner of both the 2007 King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. and Arc; with 4 G1s that year, Cartier Horse of the Year. At 2 he won 2 of 4. At 3, 3rd in the Derby, he won the Irish Derby and Irish Champion S.; on dirt in America he was last, 32 lengths back, in the Jockey Club Gold C., and came home. At 4 he won the Prix Ganay, the King George easily, the Irish Champion S. again, and the Arc, holding off Youmzain by a head. Beaten in the BC Turf and Hong Kong Vase, he retired. A steady sire with multiple stakes winners from his first crop.
3473=At 4 in 2007 she won 3 G1s incl. the BC Distaff, Eclipse champion older mare. Debuting in January at 3, she struggled, first winning in her 4th start; 2 wins from 6. At 4 she won the First Flight H. (G2), then the Go for Wand H. and Ruffian H., and outfought Hystericalady in the BC Distaff - champion older mare. At 5 she won the Ogden Phipps H., Go for Wand H. and Personal Ensign H.; 6th to Zenyatta in the renamed BC Ladies' Classic, she retired. Imported to Japan as a broodmare, she produced stars Rouge Buck and Potager.
3474=A Hong Kong sprint gelding who won the Hong Kong Sprint (G1) in 2007/08 and 2009/10. Debuting at 3 in 2006/07, he won 5 straight; in 2007/08 he won 3 straight to the Hong Kong Sprint. Aimed at Japan's Sprinters S. in 2008/09, he was injured; back, he won Singapore's KrisFlyer International Sprint (G3). In 2009/10 he won a 2nd Hong Kong Sprint, then the Centenary Sprint C. and Chairman's Sprint Prize - Hong Kong Horse of the Year. In 2010/11, 3rd in the Hong Kong Sprint, he won 2 stakes; in 2011/12 at 8, after heavy defeats, he retired.
3475=Winner of the 2006 BC Juvenile (G1) and 2007 Kentucky Derby (G1), and a successful sire in the US and Japan. Debuting in July at 2 and winning his 2nd start, he was 3rd in the Arlington-Washington BC Futurity (G3) and Breeders' Futurity (G1), then won the BC Juvenile by a record 10 lengths - Eclipse champion 2yo. At 3, after 2 preps, he broke clear powerfully in a 20-runner Kentucky Derby. He won the Travers S. for a 3rd G1; 4th in the BC Classic, he retired. His foals include stakes winners every year; in Japan, in-utero import Fleet Street won the Elm S. (G3), and during one season in Japan he sired local graded winners Fashionista and Field Sense.
3476=Winner of the 2008 Derby, Irish Champion S. and Champion S.; Cartier champion 2yo, then champion 3yo colt. Unbeaten in 5 at 2 incl. the National S. and Dewhurst S. (G1s), he was hailed as next year's classic star. At 3, 2nd in the 2000 Guineas and Irish 2000 Guineas, his camp stepped him up in trip 1 week later in the Derby, where he outfought Tartan Bearer. 3rd against older horses in the International S., he won the Irish Champion S. and Champion S., and was champion 3yo colt over 4-time G1 winner Henrythenavigator. At stud he sired Dawn Approach and many more.
3477=A great modern sire, North American champion sire every year since 2019, the best son and heir of Harlan's Holiday. In 2020 his progeny earned a North American record $21,725,335, over double runner-up Uncle Mo. On the track he won 3 of 6 incl. the CashCall Futurity (G1) and drew little attention at stud. But his first crop produced 2-time BC Dirt Mile winner Goldencents, then G1 winners Practical Joke, Authentic and Gamine. His value soared; his fee reached $150,000 when he first topped the list in 2019, yet mares kept flooding in, and his runners dominate stakes. In 2025 2yo stars like Hopeful (G1) winner Ted Noffey and Spinaway S. (G1) winner Tommy Jo kept coming - his reign looks set to continue.
