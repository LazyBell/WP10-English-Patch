2161=A mare who won graded races incl. the Mainichi Okan and Niigata Daishoten. At 4 (old age system) she won the Flower C., was 2nd in the 4yo Himba Tokubetsu and 4th in the Oaks; in autumn she won the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (now Fuchu Himba S.), 4th in the QEII Cup. At 5 she won the Niigata Daishoten and Mainichi Okan, unplaced in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen. She retired after the Kabutoyama Kinen at 6; her son Tosho Arrow won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai.
2187=5th in the Satsuki Sho, 11th in the Derby and 5th in the Kikuka Sho at 4 (old age system), he won the Kumohata Kinen (a December Nakayama 1800m handicap until 1980), then the Kimpai (East) and Nakayama Kinen - strong at middle trips. 2nd in the Nikkei Sho, he was 12th in the Tenno Sho (Spring). His best foals: Sprinters S. runner-up I'll Be There and Kokura Daishoten runner-up Banri Ryuho.
2188=She won the 2008 Tulip Sho from the front, holding off favorites incl. later Oaks winner Tall Poppy. 9th in the Oka Sho and 17th in the Oaks, she never placed again as a sprinter. A broodmare from 2010.
2189=Winning early, he was 24th in the Derby at 4 (old age system). Winless for a while, he rose from autumn at 5, led all the way in the Nihon Keizai Sho at 6 as 10th choice, then won the Mainichi Okan as 7th choice. Last in the Arima Kinen, he won the AJCC and Nihon Keizai Sho at 7 - no fluke.
2190=Winner of the 2004 Falcon S. Often fancied but beaten, she won a maiden in her 7th start, then a 5-million race, and the Falcon S. as 10th of 16 in her 3rd open race. Far back in sprint graded races and the Takamatsunomiya Kinen after, she retired to breed in 2006.
2191=Winner of the 2000 Queen C. After a debut win at 3, she stepped up as 10th choice and closed sharply to win by 1.5 lengths. 15th in the Oka Sho, 9th in the Oaks, 14th in the Shuka Sho, she later won only a 16-million race, but at 6 ran 13 times, 3rd in Kawasaki's Sparking Lady C. - tough.
2192=A 2004 classic hope. He beat Heart's Cry and Black Tide in the Kisaragi Sho, then was 8th in the Satsuki Sho, and fractured a leg in the Derby and was put down. Having beaten later Arima Kinen winner Heart's Cry, many rate his talent highly.
2193=A late bloomer at 6: winning a maiden at 3, then a race each year, he stepped up to win the Ibis Summer Dash at 6, blazing to the front. A pure sprinter (all races at 1200m or less) who loved Niigata: 4 of his 5 wins.
2194=Best foal of French great Sea-Bird, she won 8 G1s incl. the French fillies' Triple Crown and the Arc - the first mare to earn $1 million. At 3 she beat rival Dahlia to the Triple Crown, then was 2nd in the Arc and Champion S., top-rated French 3yo. At 4 she won the Prix Ganay and Prix d'Ispahan among 4 straight, then the Arc as favorite, French Horse of the Year. At 5 she won the Ganay again for an 8th G1 and retired. She beat Dahlia, 2-time King George winner, in all 8 meetings - but as a broodmare had only 1 graded winner to Dahlia's 4 G1 winners.
2195=Winner of the 1970 Tenno Sho (Autumn); with son Mejiro Titan and grandson Mejiro McQueen, 3 generations of Tenno Sho winners. Emerging at 5 (old age system), he won the Yasuda Kinen and Hakodate Kinen, then after a 2nd in the Meguro Kinen (Autumn) won the Tenno Sho (Autumn), beating doubts about a Partholon son's stamina. At 6 he won the Argentina JCC and Hollywood Turf Club Sho (now Kyoto Daishoten), 2nd in the Meguro Kinen (Spring) and Takarazuka Kinen. At 7 he won the AJCC and was 2nd to Ishino Hikaru in the Arima Kinen. Perhaps due to flu antibiotics, his fertility was very low - only 19 foals, yet they incl. Tenno Sho winner Mejiro Titan and Kyoto Daishoten winner Mejiro Cara.
2196=Winner of the 2000 Radio Tampa Sho. Debuting at 3, he led all the way, then won in May and June and went straight to the Radio Tampa Sho, run at Tokyo 1800m that year - 2 for 2 there. He beat the fancies to win. Little after; he won once over jumps at 6.
2197=A 2006 2yo star: debut by 5 lengths, an open race as favorite, then the Daily Hai 2yo S. as heavy favorite, unbeaten in 3. In the Asahi Hai FS he lacked his usual kick, 3rd. Troubled by leg injuries, he raced 22 times until 7.
2198=She won the 1975 Swan S. and Takamatsunomiya Hai at 5 (old age system). As a broodmare she produced 2-classic filly Hagino Top Lady and Takarazuka Kinen winner Hagino Kamui O. Hagino Top Lady produced 2-G1 mare Dai Ichi Ruby - the "Glorious Family," Itto to Hagino Top Lady to Dai Ichi Ruby.
2199=A Southern Kanto mare with many wins incl. the 1982 Teio Sho - its only female winner until Hokuto Vega in 1996. 12 wins from 24, retiring at 4; her foals incl. local star Tsuki no Maruzen.
2200=A Southern Kanto star who won the 1984 Teio Sho and 1985 Tokyo Daishoten. Tough: Teio Sho 4 years running from 4, 70 starts. In his only JRA race, the Local Invitational, he was 4th.
2201=Winner of the 1982 and 1984 Kawasaki Kinen in Southern Kanto. In 1983 he was the first local horse in the Japan Cup (10th). 2 years later local Rocky Tiger was 2nd, proving local strength.
2202=A Southern Kanto star who won the 1982 Tokyo Kinen and Tokyo Daishoten and 1983 Teio Sho: 11 wins from 26, retiring at 7. No notable foals.
2203=Winner of the 1981 Teio Sho and Tokyo Daishoten, a long-striding stayer; he beat Toukei Hope in the Teio Sho in record time. He moved to the JRA with crop-mate Hikari Duel but disliked turf; while Hikari Duel became Horse of the Year, he retired winless there.
2204=A dirt force who won the 2013 Procyon S. Struggling on turf until 3, he was transformed on dirt at 4, rising quickly: 3rd in the Musashino S., 2nd in the Procyon S. At 6 he won the Procyon S. in record time. He ran in Morioka's Mile CS Nambu Hai 4 years running.
2205=At 5 in 2013 he stepped up to win the Kokura Kinen on a light weight, leading at the 4th turn in record time, then was 2nd in the All Comers. But as favorite he was 14th in both the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai and Kinko Sho. In 2014, racing mainly locally, he led all the way in the Tanabata Sho.
2206=Winner of the 2013 Elm S. Unbeaten in 3 on dirt, he flopped in the Japan Dirt Derby and Leopard S. at 3 and restarted in allowances at 4. Winning 2 to return to open class, he won the Elm S. as 3rd choice in record time. 4th in the Hakusan Daishoten (Kanazawa), he was scratched before the Galaxy S.
2207=A long-lasting mare: no G1, but 2 Hanshin Himba S. and, at 7, the Kyoto Daishoten. Debuting late, in April at 3, she won her first 2; after a 10-million win she was 2nd to Meisho Mambo in the Shuka Sho. In 2014 she won the Osakajo S. (OP) and Hanshin Himba S., closing powerfully after a slow start. At 5 she won only the Yonago S. (OP); at 6 she won the Hanshin Himba S. again and was 4th in the Victoria Mile, her best. At 7 she beat males incl. favored Cheval Grand in the Kyoto Daishoten, then was 5th in the Hong Kong Cup. At 8 she fell away.
2208=A Neo Universe stayer who won the 2013 Stayers S. 3rd in it at 4, 2nd to Gold Ship in the Hanshin Daishoten at 5, he then lost several before winning the Stayers S. easily as favorite. In 2014 he raced boldly to win the Kyoto Kinen over Gentildonna, Tosen Ra and Lachesis. 5th choice in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he was far back; after more defeats, he won the Stayers S. again under Norihiro Yokoyama - an Arima Kinen dark horse - but retired injured to stud.
2209=Winner of the 2014 QEII Cup. At 3 she was 2nd to Meisho Mambo in it, her first G1. At 4, 2nd in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and All Comers, she beat Oaks winner Nuovo Record and defending champion Meisho Mambo to win the QEII Cup, then was 6th as 11th choice in the Arima Kinen. At 5 she swept past Kizuna in the Osaka Hai. 2nd choice in a 3rd QEII Cup, she was 11th, and retired to breed.
2210=Winner of the 2013 Keeneland C. Unplaced in G1s like the Hanshin JF and Oka Sho, she switched to sprints at 4. At 5 she was 3rd in the Hakodate Sprint S. and 2nd in the Ibis Summer Dash, then led all the way to win the Keeneland C. over favorite Straight Girl. 16th to Lord Kanaloa in the Sprinters S.
2211=Winner of the 2013 Keisei Hai Autumn H. Highly rated but slow to win, he came good at 4, winning 4 straight from allowances to the Keisei Hai AH. A fracture then sidelined him for 2013, and he never won again.
2212=Winner of the 2013 St. Lite Kinen. Fresh from a 5-million win, he was 3rd choice and split the field to win by a nose. Fancied in the Kikuka Sho, he was 15th on heavy ground. He won the 2014 Niigata Daishoten.
2213=A romantic pedigree: sire and broodmare sire won the Derby, grand-dam was Oaks winner Umeno Fiber. Long stuck in allowances, he won 3 straight at 5 to reach open class and won the All Comers. 8th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 10th in the Arima Kinen, he won the 2014 AJCC over the same course, but after finishing last in the Takarazuka Kinen died of cancer.
2214=Winner of the 2015 Fukushima Himba S. 2nd in the Queen C. at 3, she ran in the Oaks, then was 3rd in the Fuchu Himba S. and Aichi Hai. In the Fukushima Himba S. at 5, as the fancies collapsed, she alone of the top 3 choices made the top five, and won. She retired at the end of 5.
2215=With only 3 wins by the end of 4, he blossomed in 2015, winning the Takarazuka Kinen and Tenno Sho (Autumn) to top the older ranks. 4th choice in the Asahi Hai FS after 2 wins, he then slumped: 15th in the Satsuki Sho and 7th in the Derby, both as 17th choice. He came good winning the Metropolitan S. at 4, then placed in graded races. At 5 he won the Nakayama Kimpai and Kyoto Kinen; 8th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), doubtful at the trip, he won the Naruo Kinen and Takarazuka Kinen. In autumn he won the Kyoto Daishoten, then the Tenno Sho (Autumn) as favorite, alone holding on from the front as the pace-setters collapsed. 3rd in the Japan Cup and 5th in the Arima Kinen, he was champion older colt. At 6 he was 4th in Hong Kong's QEII Cup, 4th in the Takarazuka Kinen, 3rd in the Kyoto Daishoten and 9th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn).
2216=Winner of the 2015 Hakodate Kinen. In graded races early, 2nd in the St. Lite Kinen at 3, he then stayed in allowances, returning to open class at 5. 6th as 2nd choice in the Meguro Kinen, he won the Hakodate Kinen from midfield, holding off Hagino Hybrid by a head. Unplaced in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen.
2217=Winner of the 2013 Meguro Kinen. In open class late at 4, he was 2nd in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai and Nikkei Shinshun Hai, 3rd in the Nikkei Sho. Outclassed in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he split the field to win the Meguro Kinen in a record 2:29.6. Winless after, he tried dirt in the 2014 Kawasaki Kinen, 2nd by half a length to Hokko Tarumae. Racing on turf and sometimes dirt (Teio Sho, Procyon S.), he retired in May at 9 after 39 races.
2218=A 2013 classic runner who won the Aoba Sho and ran in the Derby, 5th choice behind Kizuna, Logotype, Epiphaneia and Kodino. 13th, but only about 4 lengths behind Kizuna. Far back in the St. Lite Kinen and Kikuka Sho, often injured, he won the Tancho S. (OP) in autumn at 5, and retired to stud at 7.
2219=Winner of the 2014 Kyoto Himba S. Slow to win at 3, she improved after a 3rd in the Rose S., won a 10-million race as favorite, then the Kyoto Himba S. at 4 and 2nd in the Hanshin Himba S. 16th in the Victoria Mile, she won the CBC Sho at 5, then was 2nd in the Centaur S. and 5th in the Sprinters S.
2220=A long-lasting middle-to-long distance horse with 4 graded wins. 3rd in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and 2nd in the Kikuka Sho, he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai and Kokura Kinen at 4, then the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and Naruo Kinen at 6. Unplaced in G1s like the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Arima Kinen.
2221=He blossomed at 4 in 2014: a 16-million win, the Kokura Daishoten by 2 lengths as favorite, the Kyoto Daishoten in a tight finish, and the Kinko Sho in record time. At 5, 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he was 7th choice in the Japan Cup but, with Ryan Moore saving ground, lost by a neck to Shonan Pandora. At 6 he was 3rd in the Dubai Sheema Classic, behind Duramente's 2nd.
2222=A Niigata specialist: all 3 open wins were Niigata graded races. A handsome blueblood bought for 94.5 million yen as a foal, he reached open class at 3, but leg trouble cost a year. Back at 5 he placed often, won the Niigata Daishoten, then the Niigata Kinen at 7 and Niigata Daishoten at 8.
