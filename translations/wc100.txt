2436=A super horse named by the University of Tokyo Horsemen Club, introduced in Winning Post 8 2016. Heir to his sire Durandal, whose kick was likened to Shinzan's "hatchet." His dam 1 Carat adds more sparkle. Late-normal maturity with lasting growth like his sire, he always makes players fear a charge from off the screen. His name is a 16th-century German greatsword (inspired by Durandal, a French holy sword).
2437=A super horse named by the University of Tokyo Horsemen Club, from Winning Post 8 2016. A dirt horse of North American blood, inbred 3x4 to Deputy Minister, he races forward and keeps going. After ruling Japan's dirt races he aims for the BC Classic his sire Casino Drive never won. His name means "Watch me, Father."
2446=Half-brother of Rose S. winner Animate Bio (07: Zenno Rob Roy). He won the Arlington C. and was 3rd in the NHK Mile C., then tried longer races: the Derby, and the Sapporo Kinen, a neck behind favorite Maurice in 3rd. In the Kikuka Sho he was 2nd to Satono Diamond as 9th choice, handling 3000m, then 6th in the Japan Cup, best of the 3yos. Older, he was 5th in the Takarazuka Kinen and 3rd as 13th choice in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 5 he won the Hanshin Daishoten, then the Tenno Sho (Spring) as 2nd choice, closing superbly from midfield for a G1. His jockey dismounted after the line - he was injured and retired to stud.
2447=Racing in local middle-distance graded races, he won the Kokura Kinen at 7. Always fancied, he won a 16-million race in summer at 5, then was 2nd in the Niigata Kinen in his first open race. Unplaced for a while, at 7, next-to-last choice in the Kokura Kinen, he led and held on - a big upset.
2448=Of Sakura Bakushin O's blood, a sprinter like him. Debuting in spring at 3 and slow to win, he came good at 5, winning 3 classes quickly, then raced in sprint graded races. Always overlooked, he closed brilliantly from last to win the Kitakyushu Kinen at 7. Little after; retired in August at 8.
2449=A turf G1 winner from Nikiya's famed dirt family. Winning with a sharp finish, he won the Arlington C. in his 5th start. 4th choice in the Satsuki Sho, he joined the leaders at the 4th turn and lost a duel with Al Ain, 2nd. 7th in the Derby, he turned to miles: 5th in the Fuji S., then 4th choice in the Mile CS, he split the field at the 4th turn and swept past all - the first 3yo winner since Agnes Digital in 2000, one of 3 G1 winners from his sire Harbinger's crop with Deirdre and Mozu Katchan. Older he was 2nd to Suave Richard in the Osaka Hai and a head 2nd defending the Mile CS, then 3rd to Indy Champ in it the next year. At 6, 2nd in the Sapporo Kinen, he was 7th in a 4th Mile CS, then 7th in the Arima Kinen, his first 2500m race. At 7, 10th in the Osaka Hai and 7th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), his best were 3rds in the Sapporo Kinen and Challenge C. He retired after the Arima Kinen and became a lead pony.
2450=Winner of the 2016 Hakodate Kinen on the middle-distance G3 circuit. Winning 2 allowances at 4, he placed in G3s (5th Kokura Daishoten, 3rd Chunichi Shimbun Hai) and at 6 won the Hakodate Kinen as 3rd choice, leading throughout and winning by 2 lengths.
2451=A dirt sprinter who won open races like the Green Channel C. and Polaris S., then Saga's Summer Champion at 6 as hot favorite at 1.8, leading all the way. After a dry spell he won Korea's G1 Korea Sprint, then the Hyogo Gold T. At 8 he led all the way in the Tokyo Sprint, was 2nd in the Sakitama Hai and Tokyo Hai, then won the JBC Sprint as 4th choice - Christophe Lemaire, on a run of 3 straight weekly G1 wins, delivered again with a perfect ride from up front.
2452=2nd in the Mainichi Hai at 3, he flopped in the NHK Mile C. and turned to sprints, winning the Yodo Tankyori S. (OP) at 4 and the Ocean S. at 5 as 5th choice, closing sharply from behind midfield to catch leader Haku San Moon. In the Takamatsunomiya Kinen he closed for 5th.
2453=A speedster who bloomed at 5 and won the Sprinters S. twice. Disliking right-handed tracks, he raced mainly at Tokyo and Chukyo, switching between dirt and turf, and reached open class in autumn at 4. 10th in the Negishi S., he was 2nd and 1st in dirt open races, seemingly a dirt horse, but then won the turf CBC Sho from the back with the fastest finish. In the Sprinters S. off a break, 3rd choice behind hot favorite Big Arthur on a right-handed track he disliked, he charged from midfield past Mikki Isle and the leaders for his first G1. At 6 he was 3rd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, won the Keio Hai SC, 3rd in the Yasuda Kinen, then won the Sprinters S. again as favorite off a break, closing from the back.
2454=3rd in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai at 3 with only 1 win, he restarted in the 5-million class. After many placings he won 3 straight at 4 to reach open class. Fancied but only top five in open races, he won the Chukyo Kinen at 5, closing fastest from last.
2455=He upset mile king Maurice in the Sapporo Kinen, and was 3rd in a wide-open Mile CS that autumn - real class. In allowances until the end of 4, he raced in graded races from 5. In the Sapporo Kinen, switching from waiting to leading, he beat favorite Maurice by 2 lengths. He tracked in 2nd for 2nd in the Mile CS, now a front-runner, and went to the Hong Kong Mile. The next year, after the Nakayama Kinen, he won Hong Kong's QEII Cup - a big title abroad - and was 3rd in the Hong Kong Cup, best of the Japanese.
2456=He won 3 straight at 5 from allowances to the G3 Sirius S. Winning a maiden early, a weak left foreleg limited him to 3-4 races a year, and it took 3 years to reach open class. After a 16-million race and the July S. (OP), he led from the start in the Sirius S. and won a 3-way duel with Pionero and Apollo Kentucky.
2457=A sprinter who won the Keihan Hai twice. Blooming mid-4, he won the Kyoraku S. and Opal S. (both OP); at 5, 2nd in the Ibis Summer Dash and Centaur S., he won the Keihan Hai as 2nd choice, leading from the start and pulling away. At 6 he suddenly lost form, then was 4th on dirt in the JBC Sprint and led all the way to win the Keihan Hai again as 9th choice. At 7, mainly on dirt, he was 3rd in the Tokyo Sprint and 2nd in the Cluster C. and Tokyo Hai.
2458=3rd in the Keisei Hai at 3, a classic hope, he fractured and missed the classics. Restarting that summer, he reached open class the next year, was 6th in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, then won the Milers C. in his first mile race, closing fastest from midfield past favorite Fiero. At 7 he raced 4 times in Australia, 2nd to great mare Winx in the G1 Queen Elizabeth S. At 8 he won the Lord Derby CT, his first win in 4 years.
2459=Won 3 straight: a 5-million race, an open race and the Falcon S., closing from behind midfield to win by 1 3/4 lengths. 5th as 5th choice in the NHK Mile C., he was unplaced in the Chukyo Kinen and Keisei Hai AH, then won the Shin'etsu S. (OP) in autumn.
2460=He lost a maiden by a neck to later Satsuki Sho winner Dee Majesty, then won 3 straight incl. the Spring S., passing leader Meiner Honey from a handy spot. But in the G1s - 6th in the Satsuki Sho, 7th in the Derby and Kikuka Sho - he couldn't break into the Big Three of Dee Majesty, Makahiki and Satono Diamond.
2461=From the late crops of great sire Brian's Time, noted as his last graded winner. 1st in the Junior C. (OP) and 2nd in the Arlington C., he won the NZT as 2nd choice, settling despite pulling and holding on with something in hand. 4th in the NHK Mile C., then unplaced in 2 open races.
2462=By Deep Impact out of a Galileo mare, a stout pedigree suited to long trips. Winning a 2000m maiden and a 2400m 5-million race, he won the Aoba Sho, leading in the straight from a handy spot. 13th in the Derby, perhaps worn out by long races, he suffered a tendon injury; preparing a return, it recurred the next July, and he retired to stud.
2463=A sharp closer, 3rd in his first open race, the Principal S., then winner of the Radio Nikkei Sho as favorite. 2nd in the St. Lite Kinen, he skipped the Kikuka Sho for the Fukushima Kinen at 2.1, but couldn't catch leader Maltese Apogee, 2nd. 2nd again in the AJCC at 4, he won the Tanabata Sho that summer, proving his strength at 2000m.
2464=Winning a 5-million race at 3, he won the Hosu S. (OP), then the Japan Dirt Derby, 3yo dirt champion. Billed as Gold Dream vs. Strong Barows, he burst clear from a handy spot and pulled away. Little in the Sirius S. and Miyako S.
2465=A 2016 3yo dirt star who won the Leopard S. Beaten on turf on debut, he won steadily on dirt and was 3rd as 3rd choice in the Unicorn S. In the Leopard S., joint favorite with Katy Brave, he spurted from a handy spot and caught the leader near the line. At 4 he won the Tokai S., then was 5th in the JBC Classic and 10th in the Champions C.
2466=2nd to Satono Diamond on the same time in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and 5th in the Kikuka Sho, he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai as favorite, beating crop-mate Shaketra in a duel. 4th in the Kyoto Kinen and Kyoto Daishoten, 2nd in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, he was 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 5. In the Takarazuka Kinen, with Derby winner Rey de Oro and Osaka Hai winner Suave Richard absent, he was 7th choice in a wide-open race, raced boldly, led early in the straight and held off longshot Waarther by a neck for a G1. Jockey Wada's first JRA G1 in 17 years, since T.M. Opera O, who had died that May; his words, "Opera O pushed me," moved many fans.
2467=Winning the Saga Kinen easily at 4, he was 4th in the March S., 2nd in the Antares S., then won the Elm S. as 4th choice in record time, catching favorite T M Jinsoku. In Korea he led all the way to win the Korea Cup (Korean G1), with Chrysolite 2nd - a Japanese 1-2. He won it again the next year.
2468=Emerging early, he won the Saudi Arabia Royal C. as 3rd choice, charging wide from the back past Climb Major and others, then the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. by a neck in a 3-way duel with Move the World and Suave Richard as the leaders collapsed. A fracture meant a long break - cruel luck for a colt who had beaten later classic stars. Back in autumn at 3 straight into the Kikuka Sho, he was 12th, then 3rd in the Challenge C.
2469=A rare Japanese runner by US sire Pioneerof the Nile, sire of Triple Crown winner American Pharoah, and his first graded winner in Japan - the 2016 Hakodate 2yo S., breaking clear early from a handy spot and holding off favorite Mondo Cannon. Beaten badly in the Asahi Hai FS, he has struggled in sprints since.
2470=After winning a maiden, he was top three in 16 of 20 allowance races. Finally winning a 16-million race in summer at 5, he won his first graded race, the Elm S., as 7th choice, moving early from a handy spot past favorite Mondo Classe and holding off Kurino Star O.
2471=A Shuka Sho runner at 3, she slumped and restarted in the 16-million class. In open class the next year, she was 4th in the Kyoto Himba S. and 3rd in 2 open races. At 6 she suddenly won the Fukushima Himba S. as 15th choice, then the Queen S. as 9th choice - stunning back-to-back wins. Injured after, she retired.
2472=In open class in autumn at 4, she was 7th in the QEII Cup and 8th in the Aichi Hai, then won the Nakayama Himba S. as 4th choice, running down favorite Rouge Buck from midfield. 4th as favorite in the Mermaid S., she was 3rd against males in the Kinko Sho.
2473=Starting on turf and running in the Flower C. at 3, she switched to dirt at 4 and was steady. 2nd in the Empress Hai at 5, she won the Marine C. - hot favorite Buchiko was scratched at the gate; tracking in 2nd, she drew level 200m out and won by half a length.
2474=3rd in the Leopard S. and TCK Jo-o Hai, she won the Ladies' Prelude in autumn at 4, moving up from behind midfield at the 4th turn and charging wide past favorite White Fugue to win by 1.5 lengths. White Fugue turned the tables in the JBC Ladies' Classic (4th).
