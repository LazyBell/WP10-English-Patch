3694=He beat Arrow Express to win the 1971 Sprinters S. Winning his first 2 from July at 3 (old age system), he then spent ages in allowances until spring at 6. 4th in the Yasuda Kinen, he won the Sprinters S. that autumn - his only graded win, but beating hot favorite Arrow Express left a lasting impression. He won an open race at 7 and retired. No notable foals.
3695=Winner of the 1971 Victoria C. (forerunner of the QEII Cup). Debuting in July at 3 (old age system), she won 2 of 6. At 4 she was a fine 9th as 20th choice in the Oka Sho. After an allowance win that autumn she won the Victoria C., then the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu - 3 straight. At 5, in middle-distance stakes against males, 3rd in the Sankei Osaka Hai and 2nd in the Takarazuka Kinen. Retired that year; her 2 colts did nothing notable.
3696=A filly who won 4 graded races incl. the 1971 Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system). Winning 3 straight to the Keisei Hai 3yo S., then the Asahi Hai 3yo S., she was 4th in the Oka Sho at 4, then won the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (forerunner of the Fuchu Himba S.) and Lord Derby CT in autumn. 14th in the Arima Kinen; winless as an older mare, she retired in spring at 6. 7 foals, some family growth, but her line has since died out.
3697=Winner of the 1971 Hanshin 3yo S. (old age system). Winning his debut easily, then 2 straight to the Hanshin 3yo S., then an open race, the Kisaragi Sho and Keisei Hai at 4 - 5 straight, classic favorite. 2nd in the Spring S. with leg trouble, he was sidelined; back in spring at 6, he ran once and retired. At stud he sired local stars like Zen-Nihon 3yo Yushun winner Shigeno Kamada and Kashiwa Kinen winner Kisarazu Boy.
3698=Winner of 4 graded races incl. the 1972 Sankei Osaka Hai. Winning 3 straight from autumn at 3 (old age system) to the Shinzan Kinen, 3rd in the Mainichi Hai and Spring S., he joined the classics; among the famed "Class of '72" - Land Prince, Long Ace, Taite M - he was a creditable 3rd in the Derby. Older, he won the Kimpai (West), Sankei Osaka Hai and Naruo Kinen. Then just one open win, and he retired in spring at 7.
3699=Winner of the 1972 NHK Hai. Winning his first 3 from late at 3 (old age system), 2nd in his first stakes, the Shinzan Kinen, steady in open races, he won the NHK Hai. In the Derby, no match for the big three Long Ace, Land Prince and Taite M, he was 5th; 6th of 19 in the Kikuka Sho, he retired that year. Nothing notable at stud.
3700=A mare who at 5 (old age system) in 1971 won 3 straight graded races - Tanabata Sho, Fukushima Daishoten, Lord Derby CT - 5 graded wins in all. Debuting in January at 4, she climbed allowances by autumn, 3rd in the Victoria C. (forerunner of the QEII Cup) and 5th in the Himba Tokyo Times Hai. At 5 she blossomed with those 3 wins; at 6 she added the spring Meguro Kinen and Takamatsunomiya Hai - 5 graded wins against males. Of 6 foals, none stood out.
3701=A mare who won the 1972 Yasuda Kinen. At 3 (old age system) she won a newcomer race and an allowance, then 4th as 12th choice in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. Only 2 allowance wins at 4, she blossomed at 5: 2nd in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai and spring Meguro Kinen, then the Yasuda Kinen - all 3 on bad ground, earning the nickname "Mud Demon." At 6 she won the Keio Hai Autumn H.; switching to jumps in autumn at 7, she won 1 of 4. Just 2 foals.
3702=Winner of the 1972 Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system). Winning his October debut at 3 easily and an allowance, he won his first stakes, the Asahi Hai 3yo S., as 8th choice of 12; with hot favorite Mammy Blue and the other fancies flopping, 8th and 9th choices (him and Cape Cod) went 1-2 - a big upset. He retired after it, unbeaten in 3. Not a success at stud.
3703=A filly unbeaten in 4 to the 1972 Hanshin 3yo S. (old age system). She won her debut by a huge margin and the Daily Hai 3yo S. by 8 lengths in record time, then the Hanshin 3yo S. as favorite by 5 - champion 3yo filly. Classic favorite at 4, she was 2nd to Nittou Chidori in both the Hanshin 4yo Himba Tokubetsu and Oka Sho. Turning to sprints, 2nd in the Centaur S. At 5 she won 2 open races; set to retire after the autumn Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu, she fell in the race and was put down.
3704=Winner of the 1972 Sapporo Kinen. First winning late at 3 (old age system), he climbed allowances and won an open race at 4, 3rd in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai. At 5, after a 5th in the spring Meguro Kinen, he won the Sapporo Kinen as favorite, beating 4-time graded winner Long One. Only 2 allowance wins at 6, and he retired that year.
3705=An Oi star until 5 (old age system) who moved to the JRA at 6 in 1971 and won that year's Sapporo Kinen. At Oi he was 4th in the Haneda Hai and 5th in the Tokyo Derby; blossoming at 5, he won 6 of 8 incl. the Dioright Kinen and Oi Kinen. With the JRA at 6 he ran 2nd, 1st, 2nd in open races, then won the Sapporo Kinen as favorite. 2nd in the autumn Meguro Kinen, 7th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he retired.
3706=Winner of the 1970 Sapporo Kinen, full brother to the next year's Hanshin 3yo S. winner Hide Hayate. Debuting in August at 3 (old age system), he won his 3rd start, climbed allowances by spring at 4 and won 2 open races. 2nd choice against older horses in the Sapporo Kinen, he won by 3 lengths. Rarely racing after, one open race each at 5 and 6, he retired.
3707=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1972 Nakayama Kinen, a half-brother 6 years older than Tosho Boy. Debuting in October at 3 (old age system), he passed allowances in spring at 4; in autumn, 2nd in the Sekiya Kinen and 3rd in the Niigata Kinen. At 5 he won 3 straight to the Sekiya Kinen, then the Kumohata Kinen; at 6 the Nakayama Kinen. 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), 6th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he retired that year. Over 10 years at stud, no stars.
3708=Winner of the 1971 Nakayama Kinen. Winning his July debut at 3 (old age system) and the Niigata 3yo S., he was talented early, but his crop had strong rivals like Arrow Express and Tanino Moutiers; winless at 4, best 2nd in the Tokyo 4yo S. (forerunner of the Kyodo News Hai). After allowances at 5, he won the Nakayama Kinen; 2nd in the Argentine JCC (forerunner of the Argentine Republic Hai), he left the turf.
3709=Winner of the 1970 Nakayama Kinen. Winning his November debut at 3 (old age system), 7th in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. and 18th in the Derby at 4, he was 2nd in the Kabutoyama Kinen that autumn, then raced in stakes and won the Nakayama Kinen at 5 as 7th choice. His only later wins were 3 straight allowances at 7, and he retired in spring at 8.
3710=The best Grey Sovereign son to race in Japan, winner of the 1969 Nakayama Kinen. Winning his September debut at 3 (old age system), he won an open race in spring at 4, 13th in the Derby, then improved to 2nd in the Kumohata Kinen. At 5, 2nd in the Keio Hai Spring H., 3rd in the spring Meguro Kinen, he won the Nakayama Kinen. Winless after, he retired in spring at 6. Hoped to carry Grey Sovereign's line, he sired no stars.
3711=Winner of the 1972 Kinko Sho and Asahi Challenge C. At 3 (old age system) he won his 6th start and an allowance. At 4 he won 2 open races but was outclassed in stakes. Often unplaced at 5, he used the lightest weight to win a 7-runner Kinko Sho, then the Asahi Challenge C., another 7-runner field, as 5th choice. At 6, 2nd in the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai and Chukyo Kinen, and he retired that year.
3712=Winner of the 1971 Kinko Sho and Hanshin Daishoten. First winning at 4 (old age system), he won 5 straight allowance and open races, then was 4th as 12th choice of 28 in the Derby. He won the Kinko Sho that summer; 2nd in the Kobe Hai, 6th in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai, 2nd in the Kikuka Sho, then after an open win he won the Hanshin Daishoten as favorite. At 5, 3rd in the Swan S., 2nd in the spring Kyoto Kinen; he raced until autumn at 6.
3713=Winner of the 1970 Hakata S. (that year only, the Kinko Sho was run under this name at Kokura). First winning late at 3 (old age system), he won 3 allowances at 4, then the Hakata S. at 5 as 7th choice. Besides an open win, his best in stakes that year was 4th in the Kokura Daishoten. At 6 he won an allowance and the Hakata S. (an open race again), and retired that summer.
