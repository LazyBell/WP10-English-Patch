2920=A 1975 classic fancy: 2nd choice each time, he was 2nd in the Satsuki Sho, 6th in the Derby and 5th in the Kikuka Sho - no crown, but wins in the Spring S., Asahi Challenge C. and Hanshin Daishoten showed his class. From late at 4 (old age system) to spring at 5 he won 6 straight incl. the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai and Sankei Osaka Hai, then was 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Takarazuka Kinen, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 2nd in the Hanshin Daishoten. From 6, 1 open win; 11th in his farewell Tenno Sho. His best foal: Shinshun 4yo Himba S. winner Juji Lady.
2923=A foreign-bred blueblood (Gallant Man x Northern Dancer) who won the 1979 Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system) unbeaten. Favorite every time, he beat Takeden and Sakura Shori easily. Foreign-bred horses then couldn't run in Japan's classics, so at 4 he aimed at Europe's, but fell ill and never raced there; he came home and rested long. Back in spring at 5, after an open race he won the New Zealand T. (an open race then, unlike today's), but was injured soon after and died, leaving no line.
2924=Unbeaten in 4, 2nd in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system), he won the Keisei Hai and Spring S. at 4 and was Satsuki Sho favorite, but 9th, then 13th in the Derby and 6th in the Kikuka Sho. Slumping at 5, winning only the Tanigawadake S. at 6, he won an open race and the Yasuda Kinen at 7. At stud he sired steady open-class horses like March S. winner Takeden Victory and Hopeful S. winner Takeden Junior.
2925=The 1978 Satsuki Sho winner. A debut winner, he was pulled up after his rider fell in the Hakodate 3yo S. (old age system), then 2nd in the Keisei Hai 3yo S. and 4th in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. At 4, 2nd in the Tokyo 4yo S. (now Kyodo News Hai), he won the Yayoi Sho, then the Satsuki Sho, holding off Inter Gushiken and Sakura Shori. 10th in the Derby, perhaps beyond his trip, he was 3rd in the Hakodate Kinen, then died of a twisted intestine 10 days later.
2926=A 1-win horse struggling through 4 (old age system), he was transformed at 5: 3 straight wins, a 2nd, then 2 more, straight into open class. He won the Aichi Hai late that year, then the Kimpai (West) and Chunichi Shimbun Hai at 6, and was a head 2nd to Katsurano Haiseiko in a strong Milers C. with Nichido Arashi. Retired that autumn; no notable foals.
2927=Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Fuchu 3yo S. (old age system), 3rd to Bingo Garoo in the Asahi Hai 3yo S., she won the Queen C. and Hanshin 4yo Himba Tokubetsu, the classic favorite - but was crushed by longshot Horsemen Tesco in the Oka Sho, 2nd, then 11th in the Oaks. Turning to sprints, she was 2nd in the Sekiya Kinen and Sprinters S., then at 5 won the Keio Hai SH and Sekiya Kinen. A head 2nd to Sakura God in the Sprinters S. again. As a broodmare she produced Dynamic Bird, 3rd in the Nakayama Kinen.
2928=Winner of the 1980 Takarazuka Kinen; only twice out of the top three in 18 starts. Unbeaten in 2 at 3 (old age system), at 4 he was 2nd in the Kisaragi Sho, 3rd in the Mainichi Hai, won the NHK Hai and was 3rd to Katsurano Haiseiko in the Derby. Falling short after (4th Takamatsunomiya Hai and Kobe Shimbun Hai), he won an open race at 5, was 6th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), then won the Takarazuka Kinen as favorite. He died after 2 crops at stud, with no stars.
2929=Racing in allowances at 3 (old age system), top four in all 8, at 4 he was 3rd in the Shinzan Kinen and won the Kisaragi Sho over Teruten Ryu and others. A classic force, 3rd to Bingo Garoo in the Satsuki Sho and 5th to Katsurano Haiseiko in the Derby, he won the Takamatsunomiya Hai and Kobe Shimbun Hai, then was 2nd in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai and injured. Back after about 2 years, 6th - his first time out of the top five - he retired. No notable foals.
2930=One of Shinzan's best sons. Debuting at 3 (old age system), he won the Fukushima 3yo S., then was 5th to Tokuzakura in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. At 4, 23rd to Long Ace in the Derby, he won the Nihon Tanpa Sho (now Radio Nikkei Sho). 7th in the Kikuka Sho, he won the CBC Sho in record time. He won the Tokyo Shimbun Hai at 5 and Keio Hai AH at 6, racing until the Kabutoyama Kinen in November at 8. No notable foals.
2931=Winner of the 1967 Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen, better known for his inconsistency: losing when fancied, winning when not. At 4 (old age system) his jockey Yamaoka was banned for life over a race-fixing scandal, tarnishing his image. Unpopular, he won the Kabutoyama Kinen that autumn. At 5, 2nd in the AJCC, he was 4th as favorite in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai, then 3rd as 9th choice in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and 6th as favorite in the Yasuda Kinen - mocked as "the horse who reads the papers." At 6, unplaced as favorite in the Stayers S. and Meguro Kinen (Autumn), he won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) as 8th choice, then the Arima Kinen as 4th choice by 6 lengths. So erratic that, despite 2 Big Eight wins, he wasn't Horse of the Year. At 7 he won 5 open races in 14 starts but flopped as favorite in every graded race, mocking fans to the end. At stud he did steadily, siring Sankei Osaka Hai winner Gold Eagle.
2932=Winner of 5 graded races 1973-1977 incl. the Aichi Hai and Milers C. In the 1973 Aichi Hai he won easily in a record 1:59.9 - the first sub-2-minute 2000m. Leg trouble limited him to 27 starts until 8 (old age system), with 14 wins. No star foals, but his son Milky Way remarkably competed in Olympic equestrian events.
2933=Racing until 9 (old age system), he won the Chukyo Kinen, Nakayama Kinen and Keio Hai SH: 20 wins from 47 - no JRA horse has won 20+ since (as of 2021). In open class from 5, he won 3 straight to the Chukyo Kinen, the Lord Derby CT at 6, the Nakayama Kinen, Keio Hai SH and Kinko Sho at 7, and was 4th to Tosho Boy in the Arima Kinen. He won the Hakodate Kinen at 8 and retired after the Arima Kinen at 9. Few winners at stud.
2934=Needing 23 starts to win allowances through 4 (old age system), he bloomed at 5: 2nd in the Meguro Kinen and Diamond S., then the Keio Hai SH and Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, the All Comers and Meguro Kinen - Tenno Sho (Autumn) favorite, 4th to Fujino Perseus. Winless after, but often top five in graded races; he retired after a 7th in the Meguro Kinen in February at 7.
2935=A debut winner, 5th in the Hanshin 3yo S. (old age system), at 4 he was 7th in the Satsuki Sho, 4th in the Derby and 5th in the Kikuka Sho, winning the Asahi Challenge C. before it. Often fancied but beaten after, pulled up after a fall in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 5, he won the Kimpai (West) and Chunichi Shimbun Hai at 6 and was 2nd in the Chukyo Kinen. He retired after a 7th in the Keihan Hai.
2936=The 1975 Kikuka Sho winner. Struggling in allowances until spring at 4 (old age system), he bloomed by summer, winning 2 classes, then 3rd in the St. Lite Kinen and winner of the Kyoto Shimbun Hai. With 2-classic winner Kaburaya O absent, he won a wide-open Kikuka Sho. Winless as an older horse, he retired at 8 and did not go to stud.
2937=Tough, with 66 starts until 8 (old age system), he won the Naruo Kinen and Meguro Kinen. Winning 3 straight in spring at 4, he won 2 open races and was 2nd in the Kobe Shimbun Hai. At 5 he won the Naruo Kinen, 4th to Erimo George in the Tenno Sho (Spring), then the Meguro Kinen as 8th choice; beaten badly in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen. Slumping from 6, he won the Sapporo Kinen at 7 and retired in summer at 8 with 10 wins from 66.
2938=Owned by the president of Aichi Toyota and named after the "Crown Eight" car launched in her debut year, 1964. She won the 1966 Takarazuka Kinen, its first female winner. Unbeaten in 4 incl. the Hanshin 3yo S. at 3 (old age system), she was 4th as favorite in the Oka Sho, 16th in the Oaks and 17th as 15th choice in the Kikuka Sho. 2nd in the Aichi Hai, then steady, she won the Naruo Kinen at 5, then the Takarazuka Kinen as 4th of 8, leading early and winning by 3.5 lengths in record time. She retired in January at 6. Her first foal Naoki won the Takarazuka Kinen too - a mother-son double - and her 4th, Crown Pirado, was 2nd in both Tenno Sho - a great broodmare.
2939=Winning a maiden at 4 (old age system) and 3 straight in autumn, he was 6th in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and 4th in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai, 2nd to Erimo George in the Kyoto Kinen, then won the Keihan Hai in a blanket finish. He won the Kimpai (West) at 5, and at 6 an open race and the Kyoto Kinen. 17th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he retired.
2940=Winner of the 1976 QEII Cup. 4th in the Flower C. and 2nd in the 4yo Himba S. in spring at 4 (old age system), she missed the classics and bloomed in autumn: 3rd in the Queen S., 1st in the Keiyo Tokubetsu, then the QEII Cup, beating 2-classic filly Teitania - the first running under that name, formerly the Victoria C. Older she won the Fukushima Kinen, 3rd in the Nakayama Himba S. and Keio Hai AH. Of 11 foals, none starred.
