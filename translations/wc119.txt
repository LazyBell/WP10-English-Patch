2841=By Deep Impact's son Mikki Isle; from his first crop (debuting 2020), his first graded winner. She won her debut as favorite, the Kokura 2yo S. after a duel with Mont Rêve from midfield, and the Fantasy S. from up front - unbeaten in 3. 3rd choice in the Hanshin JF, she closed from the back for 4th. At 3 she dead-heated with Elizabeth Tower in the Tulip Sho at 1.6, but Yutaka Take flagged her temper, which left her last (18th) in the Oka Sho. Turning to sprints, she closed sharply for 4th in the Sprinters S. At 4 she won the Silk Road S. and Keio Hai SC, and the Centaur S. from up front by 2.5 lengths, but was 14th as favorite in the Sprinters S. and 5th as 2nd choice in the Hong Kong Sprint. At 5, pushed to favoritism in a wide-open Takamatsunomiya Kinen, she was 12th, then 15th in the Yasuda Kinen; 5th in the Sprinters S., she was last in the BC Filly & Mare Sprint.
2842=A 2020 debut crop colt who won the Keio Hai 2yo S. Beaten a head as favorite on debut, he won a maiden by a huge margin, then was hot favorite in the Kokura 2yo S., 2nd to Meikei Yell. In the Keio Hai 2yo S. he broke clear from up front and held on by a neck. He led far clear in the Asahi Hai FS but faded 200m out, 10th. At 3, 3rd in the Falcon S., 5th as favorite in the Aoi S., 12th in the Ibis Summer Dash. At 4, 16th on dirt in the Keiyo S.; apart from a 5th in the Kitakyushu Kinen, rarely top five.
2843=The first Kumamoto-bred to win a JRA graded race; her name means "it's fine, it's fine" in Kyushu dialect. Unbeaten in 3, 5th in the Fantasy S., she was 5th to Sodashi as 10th choice in the Hanshin JF. 2nd in the Fillies' Revue at 3, she was 17th in the Oka Sho, then 2nd in the Aoi S. and 5th in the CBC Sho before winning the Kitakyushu Kinen in her native Kyushu. A fracture in training before the Sprinters S. ended her career; she retired to breed.

2853=By Pas de Trois, a 3-time sprint graded winner - his first graded-winning foal. 2859=A Just a Way colt after Velox, sold for 100 million yen at the Select Sale. He won his debut impressively, then the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. at 1.7 from a handy spot - a precious graded win for his sire amid a battle of new sires like Maurice, Duramente and Kizuna. Favorite in the Hopeful S., he won unbeaten in 3 after Saturnalia (2018) and Contrail (2019) - trainer Takayuki Yasuda's first middle-distance turf G1 for a sprint-famed stable. Classic favorite, he was 3rd at short odds in the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen and 15th in the Satsuki Sho, then fractured. Back in autumn, 4th in the Fuji S., 3rd in the Mile CS behind Gran Alegria and Schnell Meister. At 4, 7th and 6th in spring, 3rd in the Sekiya Kinen and Mainichi Okan, 2nd to Serifos in the Mile CS and in the Hong Kong Cup, best of the Japanese. At 5, 11th in the Nakayama Kinen, 3rd as a longshot in the Osaka Hai, 5th of 7 in Hong Kong's QEII Cup, 13th in the Takarazuka Kinen, 5th in the Mile CS - erratic. After a 12th in the Hong Kong Mile he retired to stud.
