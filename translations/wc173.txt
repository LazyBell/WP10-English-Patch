3717=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1972 Nihon Keizaisho. Winning his debut at 3 (old age system), 3rd in the Hokkaido 3yo S. (forerunner of the Sapporo 2yo S.), 2nd in the Asahi Hai 3yo S., he was noticed early. Struggling at 4, he won 2 allowances and the Kabutoyama Kinen. At 5 he won the Nihon Keizaisho and autumn Meguro Kinen; in Big Eight races, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and, at 6, 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring). He retired after a 6th in the Takarazuka Kinen.
3736=Winner of the 1968 spring Kyoto Kinen. Climbing through allowances at 3 and 4 (old age system), he was 6th in his first stakes, the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai, early at 5, then after an open race won the spring Kyoto Kinen as 5th choice of 7, a length clear of Yama Pit. 2nd to Yama Pit in the Sankei Osaka Hai, 4th in the Takarazuka Kinen, he rarely raced after and retired at 7.
3737=Winner of the 1972 Stayers S. Debuting in October at 3 (old age system), he first won in his 12th start in May at 4, then spent ages in allowances until autumn at 5. 10th in his first open race, the Aichi Hai, he blossomed in the Stayers S., winning by 3.5 lengths as 4th choice. 8th in the AJCC at 6, he left the turf.
3738=Winner of the 1969 Stayers S. First winning at 4 (old age system), he raced long in allowances and stepped up to be 3rd in the Niigata Kinen in summer at 5, then won the Stayers S. in record time on soft ground. 11th in both the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen.
3739=Winner of the 1972 Keio Hai Spring H. Winning his first 2 from October at 3 (old age system), he struggled in allowances to the end of 4, then improved at 5: 3rd in his first stakes, the spring Meguro Kinen, he won the Keio Hai Spring H. 2nd in the Argentine JCC (forerunner of the Argentine Republic Hai), 4th in the Nihon Keizaisho. From 6 just 2 allowance wins; switching to jumps at 8, he was beaten badly and retired.
3740=Winner of the 1966 Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system), champion 3yo colt, and a big classic hope. Unwell, he was 8th in the Satsuki Sho and 10th in the Derby; he won the St. Lite Kinen in autumn but missed the Kikuka Sho, unwell again. At 5 he won 4 open races, 2nd in the Sapporo Kinen and All Comers, 4th in the Arima Kinen. At 6 he won the Keio Hai Spring H., a 3rd graded win, and retired in spring at 7.
3741=Winner of the 1968 Keio Hai Spring H. At 4 (old age system), 3rd in the St. Lite Kinen, he was 14th in the Kikuka Sho; 12th in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai at 5, he was overlooked in the Keio Hai Spring H. but beat favorite Hakusensho. 2nd in the spring Meguro Kinen, 4th in the Diamond S. Then just 2 open wins; switching to jumps at 7, he won 1 and was 2nd 3 times from 4, and retired.
3742=A filly who won the 1972 Swan S. At 4 (old age system) she was 5th in the Oka Sho, 8th in the Oaks, 18th in the Victoria C. (forerunner of the QEII Cup). Then 2 allowance wins late at 4, an open race early at 5, and the Swan S. - 4 straight. Unplaced after in the Sankei Osaka Hai, Milers C. and others, she retired at the end of 5.
3743=Winner of the 1971 Swan S. and 1972 Hankyu Hai, a homebred from Bamboo Farm's early days who helped build it. Reaching open class late at 4 (old age system), 4th in the Kimpai (West) at 5, he won the Swan S., then after 2 open wins and near-misses, the Hankyu Hai at 6. He won 3 straight open miles late at 6 into 7 - a standout around a mile.
3744=A local star at Nagoya and Kasamatsu in 1967 at 3 (old age system), he moved to the JRA at 4 and won 3 graded races. At 4 he won the Kobe Hai, 3rd in the autumn Kyoto Kinen, 2nd in the Hanshin Daishoten. At 5 he won the Swan S. and Centaur S., 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Spring). Winless in stakes from 6 but with 3 open wins, he switched to jumps in spring at 8, winning 1 of 2.
3745=Winner of 4 graded races incl. the 1966 Hanshin Daishoten, and a fair sire who stood out in an era unkind to homebreds. Always top 3 until autumn at 4 (old age system), first unplaced in the Kikuka Sho, he won the Hanshin Daishoten. At 5 he won the Sankei Osaka Hai, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 2nd in the Arima Kinen. At 6 he won the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai and Swan S. and retired. At stud: Hanshin 3yo S. winner Ryu Kiko and later sire Andre Amon among others.
3746=Winner of the 1972 Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai. Through allowances by the end of 4 (old age system), at 5 he was in the top 5 in all 7 graded races without winning. At 6 he won the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai as favorite; often unplaced after (15th Tenno Sho (Spring)), he retired at 7 without another win.
3747=Winner of the 1970 Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai at 5 (old age system). Ignored in the classics at 4, 16th choice or longer, he still ran 13th, 7th and 7th. 2nd in the autumn Kyoto Kinen and Kimpai (West), he won the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai. Only 2 allowance wins after, but he raced until spring at 8 - 51 starts, a tough career.
3748=Winner of the 1970 autumn Meguro Kinen. Debuting at 4 (old age system), he spent 34 races in allowances until autumn at 5, then still an allowance horse was 2nd in the autumn Meguro Kinen and 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 6 he won the autumn Meguro Kinen as 8th choice by half a length over Mejiro Asama, Makino Hope and others. 3 more runs to 7, and he retired.
3749=Winner of the 1972 Argentine JCC (forerunner of the Argentine Republic Hai) - his only graded win, but his razor-sharp finish thrilled fans. At 4 (old age system), as dual classic winner Hikaru Imai stole the headlines, he showed promise with a 5th in the Derby. At 5, after an allowance win, he produced the run of his life in the Argentine JCC, sweeping past Tosho Pit, Banrai and others to win in record time. Winless after and fragile, he rarely raced and retired at 7.
3750=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1970 Argentine JCC (forerunner of the Argentine Republic Hai). In allowances until summer at 4 (old age system), he won his first stakes, the Tanabata Sho, easily in record time, then the Fukushima Daishoten. At 5, 2nd in the Diamond S., 4th in the Nakayama Kinen, he beat fancied Speed Symboli by a nose in the Argentine JCC. Winless after, he retired at 6.
3751=A mare of 1970-1973 who raced mainly in the then-rare fillies' graded races. 3rd in her first stakes, the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu, at 4 (old age system), 2nd in the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu; at 5, 2nd in the Kyoto race in the same time as the winner, she won the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu. At 6, 4th in the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu and 2nd in the Kimpai (West), and she retired.
3752=Winner of fillies' stakes the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu and Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu in 1969. Debuting at 4 (old age system), she won her 6th start, climbed allowances by autumn, was 13th in the Keihan Hai, then won the Kyoto Himba Tokubetsu and beat Tomei in the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu. Winless at 5, but 2nd against males in the Naruo Kinen and autumn Kyoto Kinen; after a 2nd in the Hanshin Himba Tokubetsu she retired.
3753=A French horse, 2025 Cartier Horse of the Year, who also won the Japan Cup (G1) - the first foreign-trained winner in 20 years, since Alkaased in 2005. At 2 he won 1 of 2. At 3 he won the Prix Noailles (G3), Prix Hocquart (G3) and King Edward VII S. (G2), but was 2nd in the International S. and Champion S. At 4, after 2nds in the Dubai Sheema Classic and Coronation C., he won the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud at home, his first G1, then the King George and Champion S. in England - Cartier Horse of the Year. In Japan, as Europe's best, he drew huge interest and won the Japan Cup by a head after a fierce duel with Japan's leading Masquerade Ball.
3754=Hong Kong's dominant sprinter of 2024-2025: 4 sprint G1s in 2024/25 incl. the Hong Kong Sprint, Horse of the Year. Debuting at 3 in 2023/24, he won, ran 2nd twice, then won 4 straight to the Sha Tin Vase (G3). In 2024/25 he won all 8 incl. 4 G1s - Hong Kong Sprint, Centenary Sprint C., Queen's Silver Jubilee C., Chairman's Sprint Prize. In 2025/26 he won The Everest (G1) in Australia and the Jockey Club Sprint (G2) at home, then a 2nd Hong Kong Sprint in December, extending his winning run.
3755=A 2025 dirt star, winner of the February S. (G1). Debuting late at 2, he steadily climbed the classes, 4 wins from 6 by spring at 4, then won his first open race, the Keyaki S. (OP), as 2nd choice. 6th in the Cluster C., he won the Negishi S. (G3) at 5 by 4 lengths. In the February S., 2nd choice under visiting jockey Rachel King, he surged from a handy spot and held off Sunrise Zipang - the first JRA flat G1 won by a female jockey. 3rd as 1.5-1 Kashiwa Kinen favorite, 11th in the Sakitama Hai, he bounced back with a 2nd in the Musashino S.
3756=Central to the 2025 3yo fillies' races, winner of the Oka Sho (G1) and Shuka Sho (G1), from Admire Mars's first crop. At 2 she won 2 of 4. At 3 she won the Queen C. (G3) as 3rd choice; 3rd choice in the Oka Sho behind Erika Express and Arma Veloce, she beat Arma Veloce by a neck. Oaks favorite, she pulled hard and was 9th. Straight to the Shuka Sho, billed against Oaks winner Come Nyack; as Come Nyack faded, she dueled front-running Erika Express and caught her by half a length. 11th in the Hong Kong Mile, but champion 3yo filly.
3757=The 2025 Satsuki Sho winner. Active early, 2nd as 2nd choice in the Asahi Hai FS at 2. At 3, 4th as Yayoi Sho favorite, he was 3rd choice in the Satsuki Sho and swept past hot favorite Croix du Nord from midfield for his first G1. 2nd choice in the Derby, he closed from the back for 6th. He won the St. Lite Kinen, then skipped the Kikuka Sho for the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 3rd choice as fellow 3yo Masquerade Ball was favorite; both waited at the back and surged, and he was 2nd by 3/4 length - showing the 3yos' strength. In the Arima Kinen he beat veterans Danon Decile and Regaleira decisively - champion 3yo colt.
3758=Winner of the 2025 Tenno Sho (Spring) (G1). Racing mainly at long trips, he won a 1-win race by spring at 3, 8th in his first stakes, the Aoba Sho. He won 2 more classes, then was 2nd to Urban Chic in the Kikuka Sho, moving early from the back. At 4 he won the Diamond S. (G3) as 1.9-1 favorite, leading at the 4th turn and drawing away. Favorite in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he broke clear from a handy spot and held off Byzantine Dream's charge by a head - a G1, top of the stayers.
3759=The 2025 Oaks winner. Winning her August debut at 2, she was 6th as favorite in the Artemis S. At 3, 4th in the Elfin S. (L), she won the Flora S. as 7th choice with a sharp late run in race-record time. In the Oaks, 4th choice behind the big three - Oka Sho winner Embroidery, champion 2yo Arma Veloce and Oka Sho 3rd Links Tip - she closed powerfully from the back and caught Arma Veloce by a head. She won the Rose S. easily; Shuka Sho favorite, billed against Embroidery, both seeking a 2nd classic, she found nothing and faded to 16th.
