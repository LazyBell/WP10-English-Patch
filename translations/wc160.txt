3507=A European stayer of 2022-2024 who won 8 staying G1s incl. the 19f Ascot Gold C. and 4000m Prix du Cadran; Cartier champion stayer in 2022 and 2024. Unremarkable early - a maiden from 2 at 2, an allowance from 2 at 3 - he blossomed at 4, winning 3 straight to the Ascot Gold C., then the Goodwood C., Irish St Leger and Prix du Cadran - 4 straight staying G1s, champion stayer. Injured at 5, he ran twice, 2nd both times. At 6 he was back, winning the same 4 G1s - unbeaten in 7 - and champion stayer again.
3508=US Eclipse champion female sprinter in 2022 and 2023. A dirt 7f specialist, she won 4 G1s incl. 2 BC Filly & Mare Sprints. Debuting at 3, she first won that autumn, then kept winning: at 4, 5 straight to the Ballerina H., her first G1, then the BC Filly & Mare Sprint - champion female sprinter. At 5 she won the Madison S. for a 3rd G1; 3rd in the Derby City Distaff S. ended 7 straight, but she won the Bed o' Roses S. (G2), then the BC Filly & Mare Sprint again by 2 3/4 lengths, and retired: 9 wins from 12, beaten only twice into 2nd and once into 3rd.
3509=Winner of a 2nd straight BC Dirt Mile (G1) at 5 in 2023, Eclipse Horse of the Year. Named "Cody's Wish" for Cody, a boy in a wheelchair battling a genetic disease, whom the colt adored. Debuting late in June at 3, he needed 4 tries to win, then blossomed at 4: the Westchester S. (G3), the Forego S. for his first G1, and the BC Dirt Mile, outfighting Cyberknife. At 5 he won the Churchill Downs S. and Metropolitan H.; 3rd in the Whitney S., he won the Vosburgh S. (G2), then beat National Treasure by a nose in a fierce duel for a 2nd BC Dirt Mile, his last race. Cody watched it, and passed away soon after, as if at peace.
3510=America's sprint king of 2022-2023, Eclipse champion sprinter both years. Debuting late in September at 3, winless in 2, he first won in his 2nd start at 4, then soared: 4 straight to the Vosburgh S. (G2) easily, then the BC Sprint - champion sprinter. Into 5 he won the Riyadh Dirt Sprint (G3), True North S. (G2) and A G Vanderbilt H. (G1) - 8 straight. 2nd to Gunite in the Forego S., he beat Gunite in the BC Sprint - champion sprinter again - and retired.
3511=A US horse who won the 2024 Dubai World Cup (G1). 5th on debut in October at 2, after 6 months off he won in spring at 3, then just one allowance from 3 more. At 4 he won the Pat O'Brien S. (G2). Moving to Dubai at 5, 7th in the Al Shindagha Sprint (G3), he won the Burj Nahaar (G3). In the Dubai World Cup, with 4 Japanese runners incl. Ushba Tesoro and Derma Sotogake, he was 6th choice in JRA betting, but led from a fine start, quickened on the far turn and drew away, beating Ushba Tesoro by 8.5 lengths for his first G1.
3512=A Hong Kong gelding in short races from 2022 to 2024, winner of the Hong Kong Mile and Queen's Silver Jubilee C. (G1s), plus Dubai's Al Quoz Sprint (G1). Debuting at 3 in 2020/21, he won 2 straight; in 2021/22 he was 2nd in the Classic Mile, won the Classic C. and was 2nd in the Hong Kong Derby. On the mile path, he beat champion Golden Sixty in the 2022/23 Hong Kong Mile for his first G1. Dropping to sprints from 2023/24, he won the Queen's Silver Jubilee C., then the Al Quoz Sprint in Dubai in record time. 2nd in the Chairman's Sprint Prize (G1) and Premier C. (G3), he has been winless since.
3513=Winner of the 2022 Coronation S. and Jacques le Marois (G1s), Cartier champion 3yo filly. Brilliant from her June debut at 2, she won 3 straight to the May Hill S. (G2), then the Fillies' Mile (G1) - champion 2yo filly. At 3 she started late, in June's Coronation S., winning by 4 3/4 lengths; 2nd in the Falmouth S., she won the Jacques le Marois. 6th in the QEII S., still champion 3yo filly. At 4 she was even better, winning the Jacques le Marois, Sun Chariot S. and BC Filly & Mare Turf - 3 straight G1s. At 5, 3rd bidding for a 3rd Jacques le Marois, she has been winless this year.
3514=Winner of 3 G1s incl. the 2023 BC Distaff, Eclipse champion older mare. Winning her debut in April at 3, she kept missing after. At 4 she improved, winning 3 straight, the Shawnee S. (G3), the Delaware H. (G2), then the Personal Ensign S., Spinster S. and BC Distaff - straight to the top, champion older mare. At 5 she won the La Troienne S. (G1) - 4 straight G1s - then lost the Ogden Phipps S. by a head; she won the Molly Pitcher S. (G3), lost the Personal Ensign S. by a head again, and won the Spinster S. easily for a 5th G1.
3515=He swept French racing in 2023, unbeaten in 6 incl. the French Derby and Arc - Cartier Horse of the Year. From the first crop of Frankel's son Cracksman, he gave his sire a big title at once. Irish-bred, he debuted in France at 3 under Cristian Demuro, won easily, then an allowance and the Prix de Suresnes (L). 3rd choice in the French Derby, he swept past hot favorite Big Rock in the straight. Hot favorite in the Prix Guillaume d'Ornano (G2), he won by 3/4 length with ease. Arc favorite at last, he won by 1 3/4 lengths. A Japan Cup clash with Japan's champion Equinox was hoped for, but he retired after the Arc. Cartier Horse of the Year and champion 3yo colt, he went to stud in France.
3516=A filly who won the 2024 Arc (G1). Winning her debut in September at 2, she kept missing at 3: 2nd and 3rd, then 2nd by half a length to Savethelastdance in the Irish Oaks, 4th in the Yorkshire Oaks, 2nd in the Stand C. (L) and by a neck in the British Champions Fillies & Mares. At 4 she found her form, winning 2 straight to the Pretty Polly S. (G1), 2nd in the King George, and the Prix Vermeille. In the Arc she sat 2nd from a fine start, broke clear and never tired, winning by 1 1/4 lengths. She retired to breed after it.
3517=The 2024 Derby winner and Cartier Horse of the Year. Winning his debut in July at 2, he won the Superlative S. (G2) by 6.5 lengths and Dewhurst S. (G1) by 3.5 - in a class of his own; champion 2yo colt. 9th at 3 in the 2000 Guineas on unsuitable ground, he won the Derby easily, beat older horses in the Eclipse S., and won the International S. in record time - 3 straight G1s. For his farewell he tried the BC Classic in America, banking on dirt aptitude from his sire Justify; 2nd choice on his first dirt start, he never got going and was 8th. Still Cartier Horse of the Year, he retired to stud.
3518=A late bloomer who won the 2024 February S. (G1) at 6. Debuting in January at 3 and winning his 3rd start, he won 3 straight through 2 classes, then struggled, passing the 3-win class only in autumn at 4. At 5 he won the Onuma S. (L), but in graded races his best was 4th in the Miyako S. In the February S. at 6, as 11th choice, he edged clear from a handy spot and held on by 1 1/4 lengths - his first graded win a G1. 3rd as 3rd choice in the Kashiwa Kinen, 2nd to Lemon Pop in the Mile CS Nambu Hai and 5th to him in the Champions C. - among the best.
3519=A sprinter in G1s from 2023 to 2025 who won the Takamatsunomiya Kinen (G1) at 5 in 2024 - the first Japanese graded and G1 winner for popular Irish sire Dark Angel. Trying miles and dirt early at 3, he focused on sprints after his first win, climbing 3 classes in 3 straight; at 4 he was 3rd in his first open race, the Silk Road S. (G3). From autumn he ran in 5 straight sprint G1s: 2nd in the Sprinters S., 8th in the Hong Kong Sprint, then won the Takamatsunomiya Kinen at 5 - his first graded win a G1. 11th in Hong Kong's Chairman's Sprint Prize and 12th in the Sprinters S., he was 2nd in the Hanshin C. (G2). At 6, 6th defending the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 10th in the Yasuda Kinen at a mile; aiming at the Sprinters S., a leg problem ended his career.
3520=The 2024 Oka Sho winner, central to that year's fillies' classics. Winning her debut in July at 2, she needed 2 tries for the 1-win class. In a Hanshin JF where 6 were closely fancied, as 5th choice she had the fastest finish and lost by a neck to Ascoli Piceno. Straight to the Oka Sho at 3, she kicked first this time and held off Ascoli Piceno for the first classic. Oaks favorite, she lost a finishing duel with 2nd choice Cervinia by half a length. Straight to the Shuka Sho, co-favorite with Cervinia, she couldn't catch her and was also passed by outsider Bond Girl, 3rd. 3rd in the Hong Kong Vase. At 4, expected to lead the older mares, she was 13th in the Osaka Hai, 8th in the Victoria Mile and 10th as 6th choice in the QEII Cup - her sparkle gone.
3521=The 2024 Satsuki Sho winner, Kizuna's first G1-winning colt, carrying hopes for grandsire Deep Impact's line. Winning his debut in November at 2, after a break he won the Kyodo News Hai (G3) at 3 from a handy spot. 2nd choice behind G1-winning filly Regaleira in the Satsuki Sho, he broke clear from a handy spot and held on by a neck. Hot Derby favorite, a double seemingly certain, he couldn't catch outsider Danon Decile, who kicked first, 2nd. Aimed at the Tenno Sho (Autumn) instead of the Kikuka Sho, he developed tendon trouble; his retirement was announced suddenly, and he went to stud the next year.
