3287=Winner of the 1979 Derby and Irish Derby. He also won the King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S., but in the Arc was 3rd, unable to catch filly crop-mate Three Troikas. Debuting in England, he won 2 of 4 at 2. At 3 he won the Classic Trial S. and Predominate S., then as 2nd choice won the Derby by 7 lengths with a surge in the straight, the Irish Derby by 4, and the King George as hot favorite - straight to the top. In autumn he won the Benson & Hedges Gold C.; 3rd to Three Troikas in the Arc, he was British Horse of the Year and retired. Dying at just 7, he left only 4 crops, incl. Irish Oaks winner Helen Street and Prix Vermeille winner Walensee. As broodmare sire of Pilsudski, Fine Motion and Street Cry, his blood lives on widely.
3288=A star British miler of 1979-1980, winner of the Sussex S., with a remarkable 14 wins and 2 seconds from 16. Unbeaten in 4 at 2, he won the Greenham S. at 3 for 5 straight and was 2000 Guineas favorite, but 2nd to outsider Tap On Wood. 3 straight wins incl. the Sussex S. made him Britain's top miler; 3 more in autumn - 7 of 8, International Classification 131. At 4 he won his first 3 for 9 straight, then lost a fierce duel with that year's 2000 Guineas winner Known Fact by a neck in the QEII S., far clear of the rest, and retired. His first crop included Fillies' Triple Crown winner Oh So Sharp, his 2nd Irish 2000 Guineas winner Flash of Steel; champion sire in Britain and Ireland within 2 years. His male line didn't last, but he is in dam lines, e.g. broodmare sire of Neo Universe's dam.
3289=An Irish horse who won the 1980 Eclipse S. and King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S., the best foal left in Ireland by Pitcairn, later a sire in Japan. At 2 and 3 he battled Troy, losing in the Derby and King George - 1 win and 3 losses against him. At 4 in 1980 he won 3 straight to the Eclipse, then the King George - champion older horse. In the Arc he was 3rd, half a length behind 3yo filly Detroit, and retired. At stud he sired St Leger winner Snurge, Grand Prix de Paris winner Sumayr and more. Hoped, like Troy, to carry the then-dominant Fairway line, he had no lasting heirs, and his male line has faded.
3290=A German-bred 1979 German Derby winner, and 3 times German champion sire. Winning 1 of 2 at 2, he won 3 straight to the German 2000 Guineas at 3, lost the Union-Rennen by a short head to rival Nebos, then beat Nebos narrowly in the German Derby. He also won the Aral-Pokal (G1) against older horses and the German St Leger - German Horse of the Year. At 4 he won the Grosser Preis der Stadt Gelsenkirchen, then 4th, 2nd, 5th; Horse of the Year went to Nebos. Racing at 5, he won 3 incl. the Grosser Preis des Jockey Club and retired. Besides 3 sire titles, his best son Monsun spread his blood worldwide as a sire.
3291=A mare who raced in Canada, then the US, winning the Eclipse as champion older mare, and was a hugely successful broodmare, dam of Singspiel and Rahy. At 2 and 3 in Canada she won 4 stakes incl. the Bell Mahone S. and Maple Leaf S. Moving to the US at 4, she won the La Canada S. (G1) in her 3rd start, then the Santa Margarita Invitational H. and Top Flight H. - 3 straight G1s - and against males was a superb 2nd to great Spectacular Bid in the Amory L. Haskell H. Eclipse champion older mare, and in Canada Sovereign Horse of the Year and champion older mare. At 5 she won 3 (Santa Maria H., Dominion Day H., Spinster S.), becoming the first Canadian-bred to earn $1 million, champion older mare again, and retired. As a broodmare she produced world-class sires Singspiel and Rahy, and Grand Opera, sire of Meisei Opera.
3292=A sire son of Damascus keeping the Teddy line alive, sire of BC Distaff winner Personal Ensign, Private Terms, East of the Moon and more. Debuting in the US in May at 3, he won 3 straight; at 4 he won 2 G1s, the Widener H. and Gulfstream Park H. At stud his 2nd crop included 3 G1 winners such as Personal Flag, and his 3rd brought great mare Personal Ensign, unbeaten in 13 incl. the BC Distaff. Then came Inside Information, BC Distaff winner by 13 1/2 lengths, and East of the Moon, out of Miesque, winner of the French fillies' double. With mostly filly stars his male line thinned, but his blood lives on through dams.
3293=In 1979 she became the 5th winner of the New York Filly Triple Crown (Triple Tiara), winning the Mother Goose S. by 10 lengths and the CCA Oaks by 8. Unbeaten in 2 from autumn at 2, at 3 she lost her first 2, then won 3 straight to the Fantasy S., the Kentucky Oaks and Black-Eyed Susan S., then the Acorn S., Mother Goose S. and CCA Oaks, all easily. 3 losses followed, but she was Eclipse champion 3yo filly. Resting early at 4, she returned in July, won the Ballerina S., and retired after a 4th in the Maskette S. Of 12 foals, none stood out.
3294=A sire son of Mr. Prospector from his early, unknown days, who succeeded racing and at stud, raising his sire's name. Debuting in summer at 2, he won 5 straight to May at 3, mostly allowances. Winning the Discovery H. (G3) in autumn at 3, he won the Metropolitan H. at 4, his first G1, and retired that autumn. His first crop included BC Juvenile winner Tasso; his 2nd, G1 winners Cryptoclearance and Tappiano; then Aptostar and Quiet American, and in his 5th crop his biggest star, Unbridled. Many became sires, building a great dynasty from Fappiano.
3295=A filly who ruled European sprinting in 1981, sweeping the top sprint G1s of the day: King's Stand S., July C. and Prix de l'Abbaye de Longchamp. She won a stakes on debut, then 5 straight unbeaten to the Cheveley Park S. (G1) at 2. At 3, 4th in the 1000 Guineas, perhaps too far, she turned to sprints: after the Gus Demmy Memorial S. she won the King's Stand S. and July C. Twice 2nd in G2s, in the Abbaye she beat Sharpo, a year older, by a neck in a thriller, and retired. A fine broodmare: G1 winners Caerwent (Irish National S.) and Marling (Irish 1000 Guineas).
3296=A successful sire son of Northern Dancer; his son Storm Cat spread the line widely, founding the powerful Storm Bird line. Debuting in Ireland in July at 2, he won by 6 lengths as hot favorite, then the Anglesey S., Irish National S. (now Vincent O'Brien S.), Larkspur S. and England's Dewhurst S. (G1) - 5 straight; champion 2yo in Britain and Ireland. At 3 came misfortune: his mane and tail were hacked off in his stable, plus leg trouble and fever. Back in autumn for one race, 5th, he retired. Besides Storm Cat, he sired G1 winners Indian Skimmer, Summer Squall, Balanchine and more; in Japan his son Ski Captain won the Kisaragi Sho. Well served by sire sons, his line is now a main branch.
3297=A filly who made her name in Europe from 1982 to 1984, winning 4 G1s incl. the Oaks, Champion S. and King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. Winning her 2nd start, she won 2 of 5 at 2. At 3 she won the Masaka S. easily, 2nd in the 1000 Guineas, then the Oaks; in autumn the Sun Chariot S. and the Champion S. by 7 lengths - British champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won the King George over Diamond Shoal and others, then the Prix Foy, but was 4th to All Along, a Japan Cup runner the year before, in the Arc. British champion older mare, she showed, with French and Eclipse Horse of the Year All Along, the best of the fillies. At 5 she won the Coronation C.; without wins in the King George and Arc, she was champion older mare again and retired. Of 10 foals, Zinaad won an English G2 and went to stud.
3298=The core of the Vice Regent branch of Northern Dancer's line, siring Awesome Again, Dehere, Deputy Commander and more. Brilliant on the track, he won 8 of 9 at 2 incl. 2 G1s, the Laurel Futurity and Young America S. - Canada's Sovereign Horse of the Year and champion 2yo, and Eclipse champion 2yo. Hampered by injury from 3, he won just 2 G2s, but shone at stud: early G1 winners included New York Filly Triple Crown winner Open Mind and Eclipse champion 2yo filly Go for Wand, then Dehere, Touch Gold and Awesome Again; North American champion sire in 1997 and 1998. French Deputy, sire of Kurofune in Japan, and foreign-bred Tokyo Daishoten winner Toyo Seattle are also his. Well served by sire sons, his line thrives today.
3299=Raced in Australia until 4, then in France and America. His wins include the Grosser Preis von Baden and Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud, and Japanese fans know him for a 7th to Katsuragi Ace in the 4th Japan Cup. Emerging at 3 in Australia, he won G1s incl. the Rosehill Guineas and AJC Derby - Australian Horse of the Year. At 4 he won Australia's top race, the Cox Plate, and with 13 wins from 26 moved to France. He soon won Germany's Grosser Preis von Baden (G1), then raced the world: 3rd Washington DC International, 4th BC Turf, 7th Japan Cup. He won the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud for a 2nd G1, then moved to the US in 1986, late at 6, winning only a G2 but racing hard until 7. At stud he sired BC Turf winner Fraise.
3300=The 1984 Arc winner. Dying young, he left just 3 crops, but they included BC Classic winner Arcangues and Saganeca, dam of Arc winner Sagamix. Debuting in France in spring at 3, he won his 3rd start and the Prix Niel, then was 11th in the Arc. After that he only twice finished 2nd and won all the rest. At 4 he won the Prix Foy, then the Arc by 2 lengths. At 5 he won the Prix Ganay, Prix d'Ispahan and Prix Foy, then crossed the line first in a 3rd Arc but was demoted to 2nd for interference, and retired. Arcangues and Sagamix did little at stud, so his male line is fading.
