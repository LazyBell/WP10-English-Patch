698=A great sire of Oceania, champion sire 6 times in Australia and once in New Zealand in the 1980s. His 45 G1 winners were then a world record, earning him the name "the Northern Dancer of the Southern Hemisphere." He raced in France, winning just 2 allowance races from 19. But his build and pedigree caught the eye of New Zealand breeder Sir Patrick Hogan, and he went to stud there. His first crop produced G1 winner Sovereign Red, and G1 winners followed almost every year, making him Oceania's great sire. His son Zabeel became nearly as great a sire, and his heirs flourished. In Japan, son Yamanin Vital was 2nd in the Naruo Kinen and later sired 2 G1 winners back in New Zealand. A rare carrier of the now uncommon Sir Gaylord line.
