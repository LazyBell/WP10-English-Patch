527=She raced at around a mile on turf or dirt, so erratic she might finish in double figures then suddenly run well. After 5 JRA wins from 41, she moved at 7 to Southern Kanto, where she won the JRA-local Empress Hai.
532=She won 3 at 2, including the Fukushima 2yo S., and classic success was hoped for, but she was 12th in the Oka Sho. Skipping the Oaks, she targeted sprints of 1200m or less, finishing 3rd in the Ibis Summer Dash and winning the Keeneland C., her first graded race. Little followed but an open-special 2nd, and she retired in spring '09.
536=He won the Arlington C. but was crushed in the Satsuki Sho, then finished in double figures in the NHK Mile C. and Ibis Summer Dash. But as 10th choice in the Keisei Hai Autumn H., his stock fallen, he ran a stunning race for his second graded win. Troubles then kept him out nearly a year.
