2161=A mare who won graded races incl. the Mainichi Okan and Niigata Daishoten. At 4 (old age system) she won the Flower C., was 2nd in the 4yo Himba Tokubetsu and 4th in the Oaks; in autumn she won the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (now Fuchu Himba S.), 4th in the QEII Cup. At 5 she won the Niigata Daishoten and Mainichi Okan, unplaced in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen. Retired after the Kabutoyama Kinen at 6; son Tosho Arrow won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai.
2257=A dirt sprint star who became a G1 winner in the JBC Sprint at 6. Debuting at Mombetsu, he won 2 open races there, moved to the JRA and won a 5-million race and the Hyogo Junior GP at once. Without big wins for a while, he won the Polaris S. and Subaru S. (both OP) and was 4th in the Musashino S. Late at 5 he was 2nd in the Procyon S. and Capella S., then won the Hyogo Gold T. At 6 he was 5th as 14th choice in the G1 February S., then won the Hokkaido Sprint C. as favorite. In the JBC Sprint he raced forward and held off favored Copano Ricky by a head for his long-sought G1. Never out of the top five at 5 and 6, he declined sharply at 7; his only top-five finish was a 4th in the Hokkaido Sprint C.
2258=Bought for 262.5 million yen at the Select Sale, he won his first 3 incl. the Kisaragi Sho. A classic fancy, he was 11th as 3rd choice in the Satsuki Sho and 16th as 5th choice in the Derby, then 7th in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and 8th in the Kikuka Sho. In the Challenge C. he charged from the back to win. At 4 he went to Australia, 2nd in the G1 Ranvet S. Back home he won the Cassiopeia S. (OP) but struggled in graded races, and moved to Australia in April at 5, winning 2 G1s there at 6: the Toorak H. and Emirates S.
2259=Winner of the 2013 Asahi Hai FS, a US-bred son of Henny Hughes. After easy dirt wins in his debut and a 5-million race, he was balloted out of Kawasaki's Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun, so ran in the Asahi Hai FS - his first turf race - and won as 4th choice, champion 2yo colt. 2nd in the Spring S., he was 6th in the Satsuki Sho, and returned to dirt. Beaten in the Unicorn S., he won the Leopard S. easily. After a fracture, at 4 he was 2nd in the Nagoya Daishoten and Antares S.
2260=A 2014 fillies' classic fancy. A close 3rd behind Red Rivere and Harp Star in the Hanshin JF at 2, she won the Queen C. as favorite at 3. 3rd choice in the Oka Sho rematch, she was 8th, then 11th in the Oaks.
2261=A filly noted for an otherworldly finish recalling her sire Deep Impact. She won her debut and the Niigata 2yo S. with straight-long charges, the top 2yo filly of 2013. Heavy favorite in the Hanshin JF, she charged from the back but lost by a nose to Red Rivere. In 2014 she won the Tulip Sho at 1.1, the others seeming to stop, then caught Red Rivere by a neck in the Oka Sho for her G1. With Red Rivere going to the Derby, a 2nd classic looked sure in the Oaks; she closed in under 34s, the only one to do so, but failed by a neck to catch Oka Sho 3rd Nuovo Record under Iwata, 2nd. Following stable star Buena Vista, her camp aimed for the Arc. In the Sapporo Kinen she beat Gold Ship, Takarazuka Kinen winner with 5 G1s, by 3/4 length in a match race, albeit with a 5kg weight edge. In the Arc, the first by a Japanese 3yo filly, with a light 54.5kg like previous winner Treve, she joined world top-rated Just a Way and Gold Ship; racing near last, she closed like an arrow but was 6th. 5th as 2nd choice after interference in the Japan Cup, she ended 2014. At 4, aiming at the world, she was 5th as heavy favorite in the Kyoto Kinen and beaten badly in the Dubai Sheema Classic, then retired injured. Bred to King Kamehameha, her dream passes to her foals.
2262=A front-running 2013 2yo filly who won 3 straight: her debut, the Kokura 2yo S. and Daily Hai 2yo S. 2nd choice in the Hanshin JF against favorite Harp Star's fierce kick, she was 7th. At 3 she was 5th as favorite in the Fillies' Revue, 4th in the Oka Sho and 5th in the NHK Mile C., then last in the Mile CS against older horses after a long break.
2263=She won the 2013 Artemis S., a Hanshin JF fancy. Keen early, she learned to wait in midfield and won the Fuyo S. and Artemis S. 4th choice in the Hanshin JF, she won the tight battle for 5th. In 2014 she ran in all 3 fillies' classics, 7th, 6th and 16th, then was 2nd in the Turquoise S. (OP) in December.
2264=Unbeaten in 3 from debut, she won the 2013 Hanshin JF, all close finishes and never favorite - Stay Gold's first G1-winning filly. At 3 she went straight to the Oka Sho, 2nd by a neck to favorite Harp Star. Her camp then chose the Derby over the Oaks, the first filly since Vodka; 4th choice, perhaps hurt by weight loss, she finished far back for the first time. With Harp Star in France, she was expected to lead the autumn fillies, but was out of the top five in the Shuka Sho and QEII Cup. At 4 under Lemaire she was 4th in the Victoria Mile and 2nd in the Queen S., but faded after.
2265=She won her 2013 debut by 7 lengths in record time in a canter, then the Hakodate 2yo S. easily as favorite. A Hanshin JF fancy, she flopped in the Artemis S., then switched to waiting tactics in the Hanshin JF and charged to 4th. Winless in 2014, she was 3rd on the same time in the Hakodate Sprint S. on 50kg, closing fastest. Older, she won the UHB Sho (OP) at 5 and was 3rd in the Ocean S. at 6, then moved to Funabashi and retired after 1 race.
2266=She ran in the Oaks at 3, but a mile suited her best. Racing at 1400-1600m, 2nd in the Kyoto Himba S., she won the Lord Derby CT from midfield up the inside, catching leader Logotype - the only filly in the field. 3rd in the Sekiya Kinen and 2nd in the Fuchu Himba S., she has yet to win a G1.
2267=Out of Biwa Heidi, from a blue-blooded family incl. siblings Buena Vista, Joie de Vivre and Admire Aura. With only a debut win, she got into the 2014 Flora S. by ballot and won as 4th choice, earning an Oaks ticket. 7th in the Oaks after losing 14kg, she was 5th as a longshot in the Shuka Sho, then 14th in the QEII Cup. At 4, 8th in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and 10th in the Hanshin Himba S., she dropped to the 16-million class.
2268=Winner of the 2015 Kyoto Kimpai. 3rd in the Asahi Hai FS, a classic fancy, he was 3rd in the Satsuki Sho but scratched 2 days before the Derby with injury. Back in autumn, racing around a mile, he won the Cassiopeia S. (OP), then the Kyoto Kimpai at 4. Injured again, he has been out long.
2269=The star of 2014's Southern Kanto 3yos. From Hokkaido racing, at 2 he easily won the Hokkaido 2yo Yushun and Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun. Moving to Oi at 3, he won the Haneda Hai and Tokyo Derby easily, but lost the Triple Crown by a nose in the Japan Dirt Derby. Placing in dirt G1s like the Tokyo Daishoten and Teio Sho, he won the G2 Urawa Kinen in autumn at 4.
2270=A local horse who won the 2014 Keisei Hai. Unbeaten in 2, he tried the JRA's Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. as 4th choice and lost by a neck to Isla Bonita. 7th in the Asahi Hai FS, he won the Keisei Hai easily at 3 and aimed at the classics, but a leg injury that wouldn't heal ended his career.
2271=A French mare who won the 2013 and 2014 Arcs. In 2013 she crushed Japan's Orfevre, making her name in Japan as Europe's best mare. Small and modestly bred, she was little rated young, but won her first 3 incl. the Prix de Diane easily, then the Prix Vermeille, and in the Arc beat favorite Orfevre, 2nd the year before, by 5 lengths - unbeaten. Cartier Horse of the Year. At 4 she often fell short but won the Arc again. At 5 she won the Prix Corrida (G2), Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud and Prix Vermeille, going for a historic 3rd Arc, but lacked her usual burst and was 4th, unable to catch Derby winner Golden Horn. Her first foal Qous (2017) won in summer at 3.
2272=A German horse who won the 2013 King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S., following Danedream for a 2nd straight German-trained win. 4 G1s incl. the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud and Grosser Preis von Baden - a German great. Unbeaten in 4, he was hot favorite for the German Derby but lost by half a length, then was 4th to Danedream in the Grosser Preis von Baden. He won Italy's G1 Gran Premio del Jockey Club. At 4 he won the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud, then the King George by 5 lengths in 2:24.60, over 2 seconds under the record. After winning the Grosser Preis von Baden he aimed for the Arc, but a fever the day before ruled him out. Already bound for stud in Japan, he retired. His foals debuted in 2017; Last Draft won the Keisei Hai, his first graded winner, and in 2022 Breakup won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai.
2273=A speedster who won the NHK Mile C. at 3 and Mile CS at 5. 2nd on debut, he won 3 straight incl. the Shinzan Kinen, then the Arlington C. as favorite and the NHK Mile C. 16th in the Yasuda Kinen and 13th in the Mile CS, he slumped, but revived at 5 with the Hankyu Hai, 2nd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and Sprinters S., then led all the way in the Mile CS for a 2nd G1. At stud he sired temperamental 6-time graded winner Meikei Yell and 5-time graded sprinter Namura Clair; a first G1 winner is eagerly awaited.
2274=Raced only on dirt, he won the 2013 Japan Dirt Derby at Oi. Never out of the top two before, he won it by 7 lengths as favorite - like his sire Gold Allure. 5th in the JBC Classic at Kanazawa and 15th in the Japan Cup Dirt, he ended 2013. Beaten badly early at 4, he was 2nd from the front in the Mercury C. in July, then won the Nippon TV Hai by 7 lengths as heavy favorite. Favorite in the JBC Classic over Teio Sho winner Wonder Acute and February S./Kashiwa Kinen winner Copano Ricky, he couldn't quicken and let Copano Ricky lead all the way, 2nd. He let fans down in the Champions C. and Tokyo Daishoten too. His next G1 came at 6 in Korea: in the new Korea Cup, the country's richest race, he pulled far clear. At 7 he won the Diolite Kinen for a 3rd straight time, was 2nd in the Teio Sho and 2nd in the Korea Cup. At 8 he was 3rd in the Urawa Kinen.
2275=A gelding who won the 2013 Kinko Sho. A late bloomer in open class only in June at 5, he won his first graded race, the Kinko Sho, as 3rd choice, then was 6th choice in the Arima Kinen - like Kinko Sho longshot Ocean Blue, 2nd the year before. 6th behind Orfevre's crushing win, with 4th-7th close, he looked G1-capable. In 2014 he was 14th in the Nakayama Kinen, 4th in the Osaka Hai and Naruo Kinen, and 9th choice in the Takarazuka Kinen. Racing boldly, 3 lengths behind Gold Ship, he held off Win Variation, Gentildonna and others for 2nd. 5th in the Hong Kong Vase after 6 months off, he was 3rd to Gold Ship in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 7 and 2nd to Kitasan Black at 8 - gritty when he got a good early position.
2276=A mare who won the 2013 Aichi Hai as 12th choice. Stuck in 5-million dirt races until summer at 4, she was transformed on turf, winning 2 straight, then the Aichi Hai on a light 50kg. With 14th and 13th choices 2nd and 3rd, the trifecta paid a record 4.71 million yen. In 2014 she was 3rd against males in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai, won the Nakayama Himba S., and was 4th to Lachesis as 10th choice in the QEII Cup. 2nd in the next Nikkei Shinshun Hai, she ran well with a tireless late run.
2277=Named after a great ancient mathematician. Stuck in allowances until 3, he bloomed at 4 in 2013, winning 3 straight to open class, then the Asahi Challenge C. as favorite, from midfield. 2nd to Just a Way in the 2014 Nakayama Kinen, after a long break he was 7th in the Hong Kong Cup.
2278=The 2014 Derby winner. Almost unknown before his debut, he won the Radio Nikkei Hai at 2 as 7th choice, closing from midfield past blue-bloods like Deep Impact's son Satono Aladdin and brother Mont Charmant. In 2014 he was 2nd by a nose to To the World in the Yayoi Sho, then 4th to Isla Bonita in the Satsuki Sho despite the fastest finish from last. 3rd choice in the Derby, from gate 2 he raced forward for once, saved ground on the rail and won a straight duel with Isla Bonita. Trainer Hashiguchi, 4 times 2nd in the Derby incl. with Dance in the Dark, won it just before retiring. In autumn he won the Kobe Shimbun Hai; favorite in the Kikuka Sho, he was 9th, then flopped in the Japan Cup and Arima Kinen. At 4 he was 3rd in the Dubai Sheema Classic ahead of favorite Harp Star, but unplaced in the Takarazuka Kinen, Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen. Still slumping, his best was 5th in the Dubai Sheema Classic at 5 and AJCC at 6; he retired after the Japan Cup at 6 and went to stud.
2279=A speedster named after the new Tokyo-Osaka express Kodama (forerunner of the Shinkansen Kodama). He won the 1960 Satsuki Sho and Derby; trainer Takeda, who later had Shinzan, famously said, "Kodama cuts like a razor, Shinzan like a hatchet." Unbeaten in 3 at 3 (old age system) incl. the Hanshin 3yo S., at 4 he won an open race and the Spring S., then the Satsuki Sho by 6 lengths over 3yo champion Matsukaze O. His fame soared; the Derby drew record crowds and betting, and he won it easily as hot favorite, unbeaten in 2 classics. In autumn he lost 2 preps, was 5th in the Kikuka Sho and 6th in the Arima Kinen, winless that season, yet Horse of the Year. At 5 he stuck to 2000m or less, won 2 open races and the Takarazuka Kinen, and retired in glory. He sired Oka Sho winner Hide Kotobuki and some graded winners, and died at 20.
