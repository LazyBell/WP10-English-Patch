2874=A leading 2021 3yo filly. She won the Fairy S. early at 3, then was 3rd to Sodashi as 8th choice in the Oka Sho. 4th choice in the Oaks, she was 11th. She won the Shion S., then was 2nd choice behind Sodashi in the Shuka Sho and closed sharply from the back for 2nd to Akaitorino Musume. At 4, 2nd in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai, she was 2nd to Sodashi in the Victoria Mile - a crop 1-2 - then 5th in the Yasuda Kinen, retiring to breed.
2875=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Keisei Hai, leading at the 4th turn from a handy spot and pulling away. After 3 months off he was 6th in the Satsuki Sho and 8th in the Derby; 9th as 3rd choice in the St. Lite Kinen, 15th as 14th choice in the Kikuka Sho. At 4, 3rd in the Shirafuji S. (L) at 2000m, he dropped to miles: 5th in the Kochi S. (L), 4th in the Lord Derby CT, 3rd in the Kan'etsu S. (OP).
2876=Out of 2006 Oka Sho winner Kiss to Heaven, whose foals had disappointed until he won the Lord Derby CT (G3). 2nd in the Keisei Hai and NZT at 3, he kept falling short, then unplaced in 3 listed races late at 3 and at 4. Out of favor at 11th choice in the Lord Derby CT, he closed from the back with an otherworldly kick to win. 3rd in the Keio Hai SC, 5th in the Epsom C., then a long slump, until he charged to 2nd as 14th choice in the Keisei Hai AH at 6.
2877=A Funabashi horse who won the Kawasaki Kinen and Kashiwa Kinen. Blooming late at 3, he was 2nd to Omega Perfume in his first G1, the Tokyo Daishoten, late at 4. At 5 he beat Omega Perfume in the Kawasaki Kinen, won the Keisei Hai Grand Milers, then the Kashiwa Kinen - 3 straight, 2 G1s. The local hope was 7th to T O Keynes in the Champions C. The next year, 5th Kawasaki Kinen, 4th Kashiwa Kinen, 10th Tokyo Daishoten - out of rhythm.
2878=A foreign-bred son of British sire Kingman; 2021 NHK Mile C. winner. Unbeaten in 2, he was 2nd to Titleholder in the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, then skipped the Satsuki Sho for the NHK Mile C. 2nd choice behind 2yo champion Grenadier Guards, he won a duel with Songline by a nose from midfield - his first graded win a G1. 4th choice against older stars in the Yasuda Kinen, he held 3rd behind Danon Kingly and Gran Alegria. He beat Danon Kingly in the Mainichi Okan, then was 2nd to Gran Alegria, bidding to repeat, in the Mile CS - a fine 3yo flag-bearer. At 4, 8th in the Dubai Turf, he lost the Yasuda Kinen by a neck to Songline, then was 9th in the Sprinters S., 5th in the Mile CS and 9th in the Hong Kong Mile. At 5 he won the Milers C., 3rd in the Yasuda Kinen for a 3rd year, 3rd in the Mainichi Okan, then 7th as favorite in the Mile CS and retired.
2879=A debut winner at 2, he then struggled long in allowances, winning a 1-win race late at 3 and a 2-win race in January at 4. At 5 he stepped up to win the Nikkei Shinshun Hai - unplaced only 3 times in 16. 10th in the Hanshin Daishoten, he retired.
2880=Debuting in Hokkaido with 2 wins, he raced in the JRA: 1st in the Suzuran Sho (OP), 2nd in the Fukushima 2yo S. Far back in the Kyodo News Hai and Arlington C. at 3, he moved to the JRA that autumn and raced in dirt sprints. Winning the Hatsuharu S. in January at 5 to open class, at 6 he won the January S. and Yamato S. (both OP), then the Tokyo Sprint - 3 straight - and the Cluster C. 2nd in the Tokyo Hai, 5th in the JBC Sprint. At 7, 2nd in the Tokyo Sprint, Cluster C. and JBC Sprint - no win. At 8 he won the Tokyo Sprint again, then placed without winning, and after a 4th in the Capella S. moved to local racing.
2881=15th on his turf debut, he won easily on dirt next time and was always top five on dirt after. At 3 he won a 1-win race, was 2nd in the Fukuryu S. (OP), won a 2-win race, then the Leopard S. as favorite by 3 lengths. 5th as 2nd choice in the Miyako S., 10th in the Shiwasu S. (L), he then rested about a year.
2882=A debut winner, 5th in the Artemis S., then 10th in the Fairy S. and 5th in the Flower C., she won the Flora S. as 5th choice, breaking clear from a handy spot by a length. In the Oaks she led and faded, 14th; 11th in the Rose S., 15th in the Shuka Sho. At 4 only a 4th in the Kan'etsu S. (OP); kept in training at 5 but never ran, she retired to breed in September.
2883=Winning a 1-win race in her 9th start, she won the Hosu S. (L), then the Kanto Oaks as favorite, breaking clear and beating Cerasus Via by 2 lengths. A close 3rd in the Japan Dirt Derby, 3rd in the Ladies' Prelude, 10th in the Queen Sho. At 4, 2nd by a nose in the Marine S. and by a neck in the Elm S., then nearly a year off; 13th on return at 5, she retired to breed.
2884=Always top three in allowances, he reached open class early at 4 and won the Coral S. (L) and Muromachi S. (OP). 2nd in the Capella S., he won the Negishi S. at 5, then was 4th in the February S. and 2nd in Dubai's Golden Shaheen, and won the JBC Sprint easily as favorite - a G1. At 6, 6th in the February S., 2nd in the Golden Shaheen again, he won the Tokyo Hai but was 4th as favorite defending the JBC Sprint. At 7, the same route: February S. 2nd, Golden Shaheen 6th; 3rd in the Musashino S.
2885=From Leondeux's first crop, he gave his sire a quick graded win in the Hyogo Championship. Winning a maiden in his 4th start and a 1-win race, he was 2nd choice well behind hot favorite God Selection, but outdueled him by half a length. 5th in the Japan Dirt Derby and 15th in the Mile CS (Nambu Hai). After 11 months off, 15th in the Uzumasa S. and 8th in the Galaxy S. at 4.
2886=Winning a maiden and the Yukiusagi Sho in spring at 3, she won the Aoi S. as 13th choice, leading early and holding off Yokayoka by a nose. 3rd choice in the Keeneland C., she won by a head in a blanket finish - 4 straight. 16th as 2nd choice in the Keihan Hai; 4th in the Hakodate Sprint S. at 4, then all double-figure finishes, she retired to breed.
2887=A flagship son of Sea The Stars with 6 G1s, mainly European miles. Debuting late in June at 3, he won 4 straight incl. a graded race, then the Prix du Moulin in September, his first G1, and beat favorite Palace Pier by a neck in the Queen Elizabeth II S. At 4 he won the Lockinge S., Queen Anne S. and Sussex S., then stepped up to the International S. (about 2060m) and won by 6 lengths - 10 from 10. In his farewell Champion S. he lacked his usual kick, 4th, his only defeat. Highly prized, he went to stud in 2023.
2888=5th in the Hanshin JF (G1) and 3rd in the Oka Sho (G1), a classic name until spring at 3, she then turned to sprints, winning the Hakodate Sprint S. (G3), and at 4 the Silk Road S. and Keeneland C. (G3). In G1s, 2nd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 3rd in the Sprinters S. At 5, again 2nd and 3rd, just short of a G1; she won the Hanshin C. (G2), a 5th sprint graded win. At 6, curiously, the same: 2nd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, 3rd in the Sprinters S. - 3 years running.
2889=Half-sister of 2019 3yo filly star Shigeru Pink Diamond. She won her debut easily, then was 17th in the Hanshin JF. At 3 she won the Fillies' Revue, catching Yokayoka from midfield, then was 16th in the Oka Sho. As a sprinter, 4th in the Kitakyushu Kinen, 5th in the Keihan Hai, 4th in the Kyoto Himba S., she won the Kurama S. (OP). Unplaced in graded races after, she retired to breed after finishing last in the Michinoku S. (OP) at 5.
