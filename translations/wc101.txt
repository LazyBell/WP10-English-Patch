2475=A late-blooming star of America's 2016 3yo crop. Too late for the classics, he won 4 G1s incl. the BC Classic and Dubai World Cup, top of the dirt world. Debuting in April at 3, he won his 2nd start and 3 more, then in August led all the way in the G1 Travers S. by 13.5 lengths over Preakness winner Exaggerator and Belmont winner Creator. 2nd choice in the BC Classic, he outdueled champion California Chrome, rated the world's best at 134 lbs. At 4 he won the new Pegasus World C. easily, then in the Dubai World Cup stumbled at the start, raced last, swept wide and passed them all. Winless in 3 more, he retired; his first crop gave Kentucky Oaks winner Secret Oath, but illness ended his life in June 2020, with only 3 crops.
