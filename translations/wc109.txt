2654=Winning 4 straight at 4 to open class, he then struggled; his best was 5th in the Hankyu Hai at 6. Over a year later, at 7, he won the Tanabata Sho as 11th choice from midfield as favorites Sabre d'Or and Reiho Romance faded; with 4th choice Meiner Sage 2nd and 12th choice Power Pocket 3rd, the trifecta paid 2.56 million yen.
2655=A foreign-bred son of Speightstown, a dirt sprint star. Emerging at 4, he won 3 allowances around a trip to the Dubai Golden Shaheen, then the Procyon S. - Speightstown's first graded winner in Japan. Favorite at 2.0 in the JBC Sprint, he seemed to win from the front but was caught by Graceful Leap by a neck. At 5 he was 2nd in the Dubai Golden Shaheen and 8th in the BC Sprint; at 6, 2nd in Saudi Arabia, then won the Cluster C. and was 2nd in the JBC Sprint. At 7 he was 2nd to Copano Kicking in the Riyadh Dirt Sprint - a Japanese 1-2 - but 12th in the Golden Shaheen, and 5th in his 2nd BC Sprint, better than before.
2656=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Hakodate 2yo S. - Giant's Causeway's 4th graded winner in Japan. Waiting at the back, he spurted in the straight as favorite Nanyo Izayoi, closing from the same spot, faltered, and ran down the leaders. 5th as 3rd choice in the Keio Hai 2yo S. and 7th in the Asahi Hai FS. A sprinter at 3, 2nd in the Aoi S., he was 2nd choice back at beloved Hakodate in the Hakodate Sprint S., 2nd to front-running longshot Kaiser Melange.
2657=A late-blooming sprinter who matured at 5. Winning the Idaten S. (OP), he raced in the CBC Sho, Ibis Summer Dash and Kitakyushu Kinen, winning the Ibis Summer Dash as favorite and 2nd in the Kitakyushu Kinen - 2nd in the Summer Sprint Series. 4th as 10th choice in the G1 Sprinters S. At 6, after a poor spring, he won the Kitakyushu Kinen.
2658=A 2018 dirt name. 9th as 2nd choice in the Unicorn S., he led all the way in the Leopard S., holding off Hirabo Kurtash. Against older horses he won the Hakusan Daishoten by 5 lengths. At 4 he won the Nagoya Daishoten, was 2nd in the Antares S., won the Mercury C. and the Hakusan Daishoten again. A tendon injury followed; winless after returning at 6, he moved to local racing.
2659=Winning 2 classes when older, he was favorite in his first graded race, the March S., but closed from the back too late, 9th. Back in open class after a summer 16-million win, he raced 3rd and broke clear to win the Elm S. as 2nd choice.
2660=From Lord Kanaloa's 2nd crop, its first graded winner. Beaten a nose by Admire Mars on debut, he won a maiden at 1.1, then the Niigata 2yo S. as favorite after a straight duel with Unbroken and Stillness from midfield. At 3, 4th in the Mainichi Hai, he was 2nd as 14th choice in the NHK Mile C. - perhaps a fluke, as he was 14th in the Sekiya Kinen and 17th in the Capital S. (L). Unplaced at 4 until a 5th in the Shin'etsu S. (L), he won the Kyoto Kimpai at 5 as 12th choice, was 2nd to Hishi Iguazu in the Nakayama Kinen and won the Milers C. as 2nd choice. Then 10th in the Yasuda Kinen, 9th in the Mainichi Okan, 10th in the Mile CS, and unplaced until retiring after the Musashino S. at 6.
2661=By Harbinger, sire of Deirdre and Persian Knight - the first graded winner of his 5th crop, 5 straight crops with a graded winner. Suited to longer trips, at 2 he won the Sapporo 2yo S. and Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. and was 3rd in the Hopeful S. 17th in the Satsuki Sho, he was 5th as a longshot in the Derby. 2nd choice in the Kikuka Sho after a 5th in the St. Lite Kinen, he closed too late from the back, 9th. Older he was unplaced in the AJCC, Meguro Kinen and others, and far back on dirt at 5. Switched to jumps at 6, he won the Nakayama Daishogai (J-G1) that December - a second career.
2662=A hope from Lord Kanaloa's 2nd crop. Unbeaten in 3 - debut, Kokura 2yo S., Keio Hai 2yo S. - he was 4th in the Asahi Hai FS. 2nd in the Spring S. at 3, he was 13th in the Satsuki Sho and NHK Mile C., then turned to sprints, 2nd in the Centaur S. In the Keihan Hai in November he died of acute heart failure mid-race - a career cut short.
2663=Nearly always top three, he reached open class in summer at 3, then was favorite in the Keisei Hai AH under Lemaire, swung wide at the 4th turn and burst clear. 8th choice in the G1 Mile CS, he closed fastest from last for 5th. A fracture the next year meant a long break; back after 9 months in the Sekiya Kinen as favorite, he charged from the back to win.
2664=A new dirt star of 2018. A sharp closer from midfield, he won his way up and was 2nd to Le Vent Se Leve in the Japan Dirt Derby. Against older horses he won the Sirius S. as 2nd choice over Great Pearl and Sunrise Soar, then was 2nd in the JBC Classic and 5th in the Champions C. In the Tokyo Daishoten, one of a Big Three with Gold Dream and JBC winner Katy Brave, he ran them both down for his first G1. At 4 he won the Teio Sho; 2nd in the JBC Classic and 6th in the Champions C., he won the Tokyo Daishoten again. At 5 he won the Heian S., then was 2nd to Chrysoberyl in the Teio Sho and JBC Classic. Favorite at 1.3 in the Tokyo Daishoten without Chrysoberyl, he swung wide from 5th at the 4th turn and held off Funabashi longshot Casino Fountain by a neck - the first 3-time winner. At 6, 2nd in the Kawasaki Kinen and JBC Classic, he won a 4th straight Tokyo Daishoten the same way, holding off Clincher - an unprecedented feat. At 7 he won the Antares S., was 3rd in the Teio Sho and Miyako S., and went to stud.
2665=A mare who won 5 mile and sprint G1s in 2020-2021, ruling short races - with Oaks winner Loves Only You and Shuka Sho winner Chrono Genesis, part of a golden crop. A star from 2, she won her debut and the Saudi Arabia Royal C., then took on colts in the Asahi Hai FS as 1.5 favorite but was marked by Admire Mars, 3rd. At 3 she went straight to the Oka Sho and won, breaking clear from up front ahead of slightly favored Danon Fantasy. NHK Mile C. favorite for revenge on Admire Mars, she lacked her kick and was demoted to 5th for interference. Hoof trouble ruled out the Sprinters S.; back in the Hanshin C., she won by 5 lengths. At 4 she was 2nd by a nose in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, then crushed Almond Eye, bidding for an 8th G1, in the Yasuda Kinen - a changing of the guard. She won the Sprinters S. by 2 lengths from last, then the Mile CS: boxed in hopelessly, she passed all the leaders in the last 200m. At 5, aiming for a 3rd division at 2000m, she was 4th to Rei Papale on heavy ground in the Osaka Hai, and 3rd to 3yo Efforia in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) - beyond her trip. But she won the Victoria Mile easily, was a close 2nd to Danon Kingly in the Yasuda Kinen, and beat 3yo star Schnell Meister in her farewell Mile CS. A broodmare from 2022.
2666=Unbeaten in 4 incl. the Asahi Hai FS. With filly Gran Alegria favored, he was 2nd choice, shadowed her from a handy spot, drew level at the 4th turn and edged clear - the colts' pride intact. A classic fancy at 3, he was 4th in the Satsuki Sho, then focused on miles. In the NHK Mile C. rematch with Oka Sho winner Gran Alegria, the favorite, he broke clear from a handy spot to win - mile king of his crop. 9th as favorite in the Fuji S., he won the Hong Kong Mile. Older, 6th in the Yasuda Kinen and 3rd in the Swan S., he met Gran Alegria a 4th time in the Mile CS - now sprint queen, but beaten by him twice; she won, but he held 3rd from up front. In a 2nd Hong Kong Mile he was 3rd.
2667=Unbeaten in 2 as hot favorite, then 2nd by a nose in the Keio Hai 2yo S., a classic fancy, but 7th in the Fillies' Revue and 10th in the Oka Sho. Turning to sprints, she won the Opal S. (L), then the Silk Road S. at 4. 11th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, she closed brilliantly for 3rd as 10th choice in the Sprinters S., then was 12th in the Mile CS.
2668=Emerging early, 3rd in the Daily Hai 2yo S., he was 5th in the Shinzan Kinen, then won the Falcon S., waiting at the back and sweeping past the field. 7th in the NHK Mile C., unplaced in the Keeneland C. and Sprinters S. Older he tried everything - dirt, 2000m - always unplaced, retiring after the Yodo Tankyori S. at 7.
2669=Unbeaten in 2, 11th in the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S., he was 2nd in the Arlington C. and 3rd to Admire Mars in the NHK Mile C. at 3. Little after but the Toki S. (L) at 4, he revived at 5: 2nd in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai, Lord Derby CT and Chukyo Kinen, then won the Keisei Hai AH. 9th in the Mile CS.
2670=2nd on debut, she won 5 straight: a maiden, Canna S. (OP), Christmas Rose S. (OP), Marguerite S. (L) and the Aoi S. at 2.3, from a handy spot. 2nd against older horses in the Kitakyushu Kinen, she was 13th in the Sprinters S., then slumped (14th Silk Road S., 10th Kyoto Himba S.). At 5 she revived: 3rd as 12th choice in the Kokura Daishoten, a nose win in the Fukushima Himba S., and 4th to Gran Alegria from up front in the Victoria Mile. Unplaced until the next February, she retired to breed.
2671=5th in the Artemis S. and 2nd in the Fairy S. early, she was 8th in the Oka Sho, 13th in the Rose S., and scratched lame from the Shuka Sho. Her chance came late at 4: 3rd in the Mermaid S., 2nd in the Queen S., then 4th choice in a Fuchu Himba S. with G1 stars Primo Scene, Lucky Lilac and Crocosmia, she closed from last with an overwhelming kick. Unplaced in the QEII Cup and Arima Kinen, at 5 she was 2nd in the Hanshin Himba S., 3rd in the Queen S., and 4th as 13th choice in the Mile CS.
2672=By sprint sire Swept Overboard, he raced around a mile at 2-3 without winning, then tried 2500m+ at 4 and won 2 classes quickly, 2nd in the Diamond S., his first JRA graded race. In the Stayers S., with 3-time winner Albert scratched on the day, he was favorite, tracked in 4th and pulled away. Never placing again in long graded races, he switched to jumps at 8, moved to local racing, and ran in the Japan Cup from there (last).
