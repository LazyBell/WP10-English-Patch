433="The demon of Kyoto": all 8 of his wins came at Kyoto. He won his debut and the Shiragiku Sho, but injury cost him the spring classics; he was 3rd in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai and 10th in the Kikuka Sho. At 5 he won the Subaru S., then the Kyoto Kinen from the front for his first graded win, and the Kyoto Daishoten in autumn. In the Takarazuka Kinen at 6, as others swung wide off the torn-up inside, he alone dove to the rail, a thrilling 3rd.
