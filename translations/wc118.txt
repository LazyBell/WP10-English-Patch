2837=Heir to the white coat of grand-dam Shirayuki Hime and dam Buchiko, the first white horse to win a turf G1. She won a 7-runner debut easily from 2nd, then the Sapporo 2yo S. as 2nd choice, holding off Uberleben by a neck - the first JRA turf graded win by a white horse - and the Artemis S. easily as favorite. Favorite in the Hanshin JF, with a possible world-first G1 for a white horse at stake, she was boxed in at the top of the straight, split the pack and won a duel with Satono Reinas by a nose. At 3, a real classic contender, she went straight to the Oka Sho as co-favorite with Satono Reinas and beat her. Closely marked as Oaks favorite, she was 8th, then beat older stars Loves Only You and Blast Onepiece in the Sapporo Kinen. 10th as favorite in the Shuka Sho, she tried dirt in the Champions C. (12th). At 4, 3rd in the February S. on dirt, she won the Victoria Mile from up front for a 3rd G1, 2nd in the Fuchu Himba S., 3rd in the Mile CS. At 5 she lost the Victoria Mile by a head, then was 7th as 2nd choice in the Yasuda Kinen and retired.
