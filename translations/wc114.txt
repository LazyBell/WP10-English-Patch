2749=4th as favorite on debut, he won a maiden and a 5-million race, then was 3rd choice behind Vin de Garde and Agulhas in the Kisaragi Sho; tracking in 2nd, he passed leader Lance of Prana and pulled clear. 3rd choice in the NHK Mile C., he was bumped by Gran Alegria in the straight, 4th. Injured before the Derby, he returned only in June at 4 in the Yonago S. (L), placing a few times without winning until January at 7, then became a riding horse.
2750=Unbeaten in 2, 5th in the Hanshin JF, a classic name at 3, she won the Fillies' Revue as 3rd choice - a rare dead heat with No One after a fierce duel. 6th in the Oka Sho, 12th in the NHK Mile C., 13th in the Keisei Hai AH, she turned to 1400m and below: at once 2nd in the Shin'etsu S. (L) and Auro C. (L), and 2nd to Sound Kiara in the Kyoto Himba S. at 4. She won the Auro C. that autumn. Winless in 3 races at 5, she retired after a 7th in the Kyoto Himba S.
2751=Winning his first 2 at Kawasaki easily, a lead hope among 2019 2yo dirt horses, he was 3rd off a break in the Gold Junior, won the Heiwa Sho (S3), then caught JRA leader Iolite by a head in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun - a first G1 for his sire, dirt star Espoir City, in his 3rd crop. At 3 he kept falling short as a fancy (5th Keihin Hai, 3rd Fuyo Sho) and was 12th in the Totsuka Kinen, then dropped in trip to win the Aki no Kura (SP1). At 4, last of 12 in the Kurofune Sho, he placed steadily in local races.
2752=Named after a French ski resort; out of a 3-win JRA mare, by sprint king Lord Kanaloa. Rated for speed before his debut, he won it easily as favorite, then the Shinzan Kinen. 8th as 2nd choice in the Arlington C., 6th in the NHK Mile C., and 13th as 2nd choice in the Cassiopeia S. (L).
2753=Debuting on dirt, he won a 5-million race in summer at 3, then struggled in the 10-million class and switched to turf. Winning it in his 2nd turf race, then the Idaten S. (OP), he won his first graded race, the Ibis Summer Dash, at 1.9. 11th in the 1200m Keeneland C., he was 2nd in the Lumiere Autumn Dash (L). At 5 he won the Idaten S. again, then was 2nd defending the Ibis Summer Dash. At 6, 16th on dirt in the Chiba S. and unplaced in the Idaten S., he was 2nd again in the Ibis Summer Dash. At 7, 10th in it - his first time out of the top two; at 8, 16th in a 5th straight Ibis Summer Dash, he moved to local racing.
2754=Highly rated, he won his debut with Lemaire, then the Keisei Hai as 4th choice, dominant from 2nd. A classic hope, he was 7th as 2nd choice in the Yayoi Sho and 7th in the Satsuki Sho, closing from midfield but unable to catch the leaders. At middle trips he was 2nd in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai late at 3, 3rd in the AJCC at 4, and 2nd to Authority in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai - capable at longer trips too.
2755=He won his first graded race, the Aoba Sho, leading from gate 1 and winning a straight duel with favorite Run for the Roses - a Derby ticket. Leading boldly in a hot-paced Derby, he faded to 15th. Favorite in the St. Lite Kinen, he won easily - a classic name again - but a tendon injury ruled him out of the Kikuka Sho.
2756=Close in his first 3 races, he won his 4th. 5th of 8 in the Kisaragi Sho, he won the Yayoi Sho as 8th of 10, dominating from midfield. Far back in the Satsuki Sho, Derby, St. Lite Kinen and Kikuka Sho, he gradually improved at long trips: 4th in the Stayers S., 2nd in the Diamond S. and 3rd in the Hanshin Daishoten at 4, 8th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), then 5th as 16th choice in the Takarazuka Kinen. After heavy defeats in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai and Stayers S., and at 5 (16th, 6th, 14th), injury meant 1.5 years off; unplaced after returning at 6, he retired in summer at 7.
2757=A filly with a rare record: a dead heat in the 2019 Fillies' Revue. Winning a maiden, she was 12th choice, slipped up the inside from midfield and dueled Pourville to the line; the photo showed a dead heat. Far back in the Oka Sho and Oaks, then unplaced as a sprinter: 8th Sprinters S., 14th Swan S.
2758=Winning his debut and a 5-million race, 2nd in the Falcon S., a 3yo sprint name, he was 10th as 4th choice in the NHK Mile C., then beat older horses in the Chukyo Kinen as 3rd choice, edging clear by a nose in a 4-way finish - the first 3yo winner in 49 years.
2759=2nd as 4th choice on debut with a sharp late run, he won a maiden at 1.9 by 1 1/4 lengths. 5th choice behind Golconda and Darlington Hall in the Sapporo 2yo S., he burst clear from midfield and won with something in hand. In the classics, 4th in the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, 9th in the Satsuki Sho, 7th in the Derby, then a fierce battle for 3rd as 14th choice in the Kikuka Sho (5th past the post).
2760=Unable to win a maiden on turf, he switched to dirt and won 3 classes in a row, then was 4th as 3rd choice in his first graded race, the Nagoya Daishoten. At 5, 2nd in the Heian S. and Marine S. (OP), he won the Elm S. as 2nd choice with the fastest finish from midfield. Since: 12th in the Sirius S. and Champions C.
2761=A Mombetsu filly who won the Edelweiss Sho. After a debut win she won a 2yo open race and the Fleur Cup (H3) as heavy favorite, then as 7th choice among local stars in the Edelweiss Sho, drew level early from midfield and pulled clear. Out of form at 3, she revived in autumn: 2nd in the Salvia C. (OP), 3rd in the Rosita Kinen (S1).
2762=Unbeaten in 2, he closed sharply for 5th in the Hopeful S. A classic hope, 3rd in the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, he skipped the Satsuki Sho for the Aoba Sho, 3rd choice with Filio Allegro and Fly Like a Bird, and won by a neck, breaking clear wide from a handy spot. A fracture ruled out the Derby; back in autumn he won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, then was 14th in the Arima Kinen. Stronger at 4, he was 2nd as favorite in the Diamond S. after a match race with Grondioz, 10th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), then won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai again and split Derby winners Contrail and Shahryar for 2nd in the Japan Cup. At 5, on his first trip abroad, he led all the way in Saudi Arabia's Neom Turf C., then was 3rd in the Dubai Sheema Classic. Scratched lame from the Takarazuka Kinen with a fracture, he returned at 6 in the Elm S. (12th), suffered a career-ending injury and went to stud.
2763=Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Kyoto 2yo S., a 2019 2yo name. He won his debut as favorite, the Nojigiku S. at 1.1 by 5 lengths, then the Kyoto 2yo S. at 1.5, moving early from midfield and coasting home. Yutaka Take called him "a horse with a big future." At 3 he lost momentum: 4th as hot favorite in the Kyodo News Hai, 13th in the Satsuki Sho, 9th in the Derby, last as 5th choice in the Kobe Shimbun Hai. Older, his best was 2nd in the Miyakooji S. (L) at 4. At 5, after 2 dirt races, he went to France as Do Deuce's companion, raced twice there and retired.
2764=Winning a turf maiden, he struggled in 5-million races, then switched to dirt late at 3 and won 3 classes in a row. 6th in the March S., 4th in the Mercury C., he won the Sirius S. as 6th choice, joining the lead at the 4th turn and winning a duel with Anglaiphen. In G1s he was 7th in the JBC Classic and 5th in the Tokyo Daishoten. The next year, 2nd in the Saga Kinen, he won the Nagoya Daishoten, then was 3rd in the Hakusan Daishoten and 2nd in the Nagoya GP.
2765=Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Asahi Hai FS - seen as Triple Crown winner Contrail's chief rival. He won his debut by 2 lengths, then after 4 months off won the Saudi Arabia Royal C. in race and 2yo course records. Favorite over Keio Hai winner Taisei Vision in the Asahi Hai FS, he raced forward in a hot pace, was joined in the straight, then pulled away by 2.5 lengths. At 3, a classic lead player against Hopeful S. winner Contrail, he was 2nd to the unbeaten Triple Crown winner in the Satsuki Sho and Derby - a worthy rival. Skipping the Kikuka Sho, he won the Mainichi Okan easily as favorite against older horses. 2nd choice in the Mile CS against Gran Alegria, he closed sharply but was 5th. At 4, 5th in the Osaka Hai (Contrail 3rd), 8th in the Yasuda Kinen, 6th in the Mile CS, he was 3rd in the Hong Kong Mile, best of the Japanese. At 5, 3rd in the Yasuda Kinen, he won the Mainichi Okan, his first win in 2 years, then was 14th as 3rd choice in the Mile CS. Withdrawn from the Hong Kong Mile with a leg problem, he retired.
2766=The 2020 NHK Mile C. winner. Debuting in June at 2, 3rd in the Kokura 2yo S., he won the Momiji S. (OP), then was 8th in the Asahi Hai FS. At 3 he led all the way in the Crocus S. (L); favorite in the Falcon S., he was caught by longshot Shine Garnet, 2nd. In the NHK Mile C., with 2yo queen and Oka Sho runner-up Resistencia, Arlington C. winner Taisei Vision and unbeaten Mainichi Hai winner Satono Impresa fancied, he was 9th choice, but tracked in 2nd, passed Resistencia in the straight and held on - his first graded win a G1. 2nd in the Fuji S., he was 15th in the Mile CS. At 4, 3rd in the Silk Road S., he won the Keio Hai SC, but was 14th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and Yasuda Kinen and unplaced in 4 autumn races. At 5, 4th in Saudi Arabia's 1351 Turf Sprint, 5th in the Keio Hai SC, 3rd in the Hanshin C.; unplaced at 6, he moved to Australia in May.
2767=Struggling in turf allowances, he won the 10-million class only in spring at 4, then switched to dirt and won 3 straight: the Aki S. (3-win), Enif S. (L) and Uzumasa S. (OP). 11th as favorite in the Musashino S., he won the Tokai S. as 2nd choice behind Inti, racing handy and taking over. A fracture meant a long break; back in autumn, 4th in the Miyako S., 10th in the Champions C. At 6, 5th as 10th choice in the February S., then unplaced and winless. At 7 he won the Muromachi S. (OP), and moved to local racing after a 5th in the Negishi S. at 8.
