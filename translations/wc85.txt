1822=Symboli Kris S dirt colt; won 3 straight, then the Hakusan Daishoten by 6 lengths on heavy going.
1945=By South Vigorous, winner of 8 dirt sprint graded races incl. the JBC Sprint; her dam Haruwaka is related to Ten Point. Based at Funabashi, she was a close 4th to Manierisme in the Tokyo Princess Sho, 4th to JRA filly Colorful Days in the Kanto Oaks, and steady in Southern Kanto races. In the TCK Jo-o Hai at 4 she charged from wide to beat Colorful Days and others.
1970=A long-lasting Oi horse. At 5 he won 7 of 8 open races, and at 6 was 2nd in the G1 Kashiwa Kinen. Still without a graded title, he then won the Sakitama Hai, breaking well to lead and winning by 1.5 lengths.
1971=US-bred, by "Iron Horse" Giant's Causeway. Racing tough with his early speed from 2, he reached open class at 6 and led all the way in the Kokura Daishoten, his 8th graded try. Later he won over jumps too, incl. 2 jump open races late at 9.
1972=4th in the Kikuka Sho at 3 and 2nd in the Kyoto Daishoten at 4. A closer who often fell short, winless after spring at 4, he won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai at 7 on his 18th graded try. At 8 he still ran in the Takarazuka Kinen and Japan Cup.
1973=Fiery-tempered, he improved steadily once gelded, winning the Hakodate Kinen at 7 with the fastest finish, then the Niigata Kinen over Touch Me Not, 2012 Summer 2000 Series champion. In the Tenno Sho (Autumn) he was 17th to Eishin Flash.
1974=By Silent Happiness, 2-time graded winner incl. the Rose S. Stuck in allowances on turf until March at 4, he switched to dirt, reaching open class in his 4th dirt race and winning the March S. in his 7th. Since: Tokai S. 3rd, Shirakaba S. (OP) 1st, Nippon TV Hai 2nd.
1975=Half-brother of Spring S. winner Flying Apple (by Rahy), sold for over 100 million yen at the 2007 foal sale. On turf until 4, his best was 4th in the Radio Nikkei Sho. Switched to dirt at 5, he won the Sirius S. in only his 6th dirt race - helped by the weights, but beating Yamanin Kingly and Grape Brandy. In 2014 he won the Mercury C., then a tendon injury ended his career.
1976=Half-sister of 2011 Flower C. winner and Oka Sho 3rd Trend Hunter (by Manhattan Cafe). A late bloomer, she came good in spring at 5. Raced mainly on dirt, she won her first graded race, the Sparking Lady C., beating Miracle Legend, Clave Secreta and Red Claudia.
1977=4-time graded winner incl. the 2013 Kyoto Daishoten. Needing nearly a year to win, he reached open class at 4 and won the Niigata Daishoten, then the Kokura Daishoten at 5. That autumn he upset Gold Ship and Verxina in the Kyoto Daishoten. At 6 he was 4th in the Takarazuka Kinen and 5th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), and at 7 won the Meguro Kinen.
1978=By Silver Deputy, sire of 2-time US champion Silverbulletday. Raced on dirt after his 3rd start, a graded regular from 5, he peaked at 7 in 2012: 2 JRA-local graded wins incl. the Urawa Kinen, 2nd in the Diolite Kinen, 5th in the Kashiwa Kinen. A fracture after the Urawa Kinen ended his career; he became a riding horse.
1979=Reaching open class in autumn at 4, he won the December S. and Metropolitan S. After failing in graded races, he led at a slow pace to win the Diamond S. at 6 - at 190 to 1.
1980=Bred at Odo Farm, home of 1994 Tenno Sho (Autumn) winner Nehai Caesar. In open class from summer at 5, he finished far back in graded races. 12th choice at 167 to 1 in the Nikkei Sho at 6, he set off far clear and it worked, stunning Win Variation, Rulership and other stars. His owner named him after a wild cat that killed a viper with a punch. A fan favorite, he became a lead pony at Tokyo.
1981=Never out of the top five from his debut in January at 3, in his 2nd graded try, the Heian S. at 5, he beat Espoir City - a new dirt star. Leg trouble then held him back.
1982=By Meiner Love, who beat world-class miler Taiki Shuttle. She won 2 from debut incl. the Kokura 2yo S., then retired injured. As a dam she passed on early maturity and speed: JBC Sprint winner Dream Valentino, Hakodate 2yo S. runner-up Meiner Schokolade.
1983=From the first crop of 2005 Dubai World Cup winner Roses in May. Maturing like his sire, he beat Lord Kanaloa in the Hakodate Sprint S. at 5 for his first graded win, then was 3rd to Lord Kanaloa and Curren Chan in the Sprinters S. The next year he won the Silk Road S. in a blanket finish and closed for 2nd to Lord Kanaloa in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen. After a 6th in the Sprinters S. he switched to dirt sprints, at once 2nd in the JBC Sprint, then won the Hyogo Gold T. and others, and in November 2014 won the JBC Sprint for his long-sought G1. Winless in 2015, he was 2nd in the Kurofune Sho, Tokyo Hai and Hyogo Gold T., then won the Tokyo Hai at 9 in 2016.
1984=A late bloomer at 5, winner of the 2012 Tanabata Sho and 2013 Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. Open class only at the end of 4, in his 3rd graded try he won the Tanabata Sho by a nose as 14th choice. 3rd in the Niigata Kinen and 2nd in the Sapporo Kinen, he won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai (G2) at 6, then was 7th in the Hong Kong Vase.
1985=By great sprinter Durandal and, like him, a sprint-mile horse. His sire was named for Roland's holy sword in a medieval French epic, he for a sword of Celtic myth. He won the Chukyo Kinen in 2012 and 2013, both times sweeping past all from the back with Durandal's sharp kick.
1986=A summer horse, with most wins at Kokura. Debuting late in summer at 3, he rose steadily to open class by summer at 4 and won the Kokura Kinen at 5.
1987=A closer who rarely lost badly but often fell short, he reached open class in the Yasaka S. in autumn at 4, and was 2nd in the Fukushima Kinen with the fastest finish. Struggling against stronger rivals, he won the Kyoto Daishoten at 5 in a fast race, beating Oken Bruce Lee by a neck.
1988=After 4 JRA races he moved to Saga, won 9, and returned. Unable to run his race unless alone in front, he reached open class in spring at 6. 12th choice in his first graded race, the Procyon S., he boldly led and held on - at 119 to 1.
1989=Running well from 2, 3rd in the Kokura 2yo S., not just early-maturing: 2nd in the Falcon S. at 3, and at 4 he beat sprint stars A Shin Virgo and Epice Arome in the Kitakyushu Kinen. A "sprint closer," charging from last.
1990=Highly rated in training, carefully raced as he grew, with only 9 starts by summer at 4. 4th to Nakayama Knight in the All Comers, he beat Tenno Sho winner Beat Black and later Arima Kinen runner-up Ocean Blue in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. Since then, 2nd in the Meguro Kinen and 3rd in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, but a long break and no wins.
1991=A true Sakura Bakushin O, fast and forward on dirt. He led to win the Yoshun S. (16M) by 6 lengths in spring at 4, then the Summer Champion by 4 lengths. Kept waiting in the gate, he missed the break badly for 6th in the Tokyo Hai, then retired injured.
1992=A pure sprinter at 1200m or less, winner of the 2012 Keihan Hai and 2013 Ibis Summer Dash and Centaur S., and 2013 Summer Sprint Series champion. In G1s he was 3rd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 2nd in the Sprinters S. With Lord Kanaloa retired, 2014 seemed his chance, but he was 13th as heavy favorite in the Ocean S., 5th as 2nd choice in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, 2nd in the Centaur S. and 13th as favorite in the Sprinters S. At 6 he led in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen straight but was caught near the line by Hong Kong's Aerovelocity, 2nd. Winless in 4 more races at 7, he retired to stud. Famous for spinning wildly in the post parade, many fans longed to see him win a G1.
1993=He won the Ohara S. (16M) with the fastest finish, was 5th in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, then won the Kinko Sho from midfield. In the Arima Kinen, untested at top level, he was 10th choice, but under a perfect ride led briefly; Gold Ship swept past, but he was 2nd in his first G1. Like Silence Suzuka, he was immature and circled in his stall - eased by a big mirror, which went with him to Nakayama for the Arima Kinen. Hard to keep sound, he won the 2014 Nakayama Kimpai under top weight, with 9 horses within 0.5s.
1994=Winner of the 2013 Sirius S. At 2 he won 3 straight incl. the Hyogo Junior GP (Sonoda) easily. At 3 he was 3rd in the Unicorn S., Japan Dirt Derby and Leopard S., then won the Sirius S., waiting at the back and closing fastest. In spring at 3 he went to Dubai but didn't settle, 10th in the UAE Derby.
1995=Winning a Tokyo 1400m turf debut as favorite, she charged wide in the Artemis S. but lost by half a length to Collector Item. 4th choice in the Hanshin JF, she closed wide but faded, 7th. At 3 she raced forward in the Tulip Sho but couldn't catch Crofne Surprise, 3rd. In the Oka Sho, passed by Red Oval in the straight, she fought back to win - her first graded win a classic. With Deep Impact's kick, she seemed a possible heir to Gentildonna, but injury ended her 3yo season, and after returning short of her best she retired at the end of 2014.
1996=2nd G1 winner by US dual classic winner War Emblem. After a debut win at Hakodate in July, she was rested until autumn; 2nd to Sound Riana in the Fantasy S., she closed fastest among the leaders. In the Hanshin JF, with fancies collapsing, she ran down Crofne Surprise near the line from a handy spot - champion 2yo filly. At 3 she was 9th in the Tulip Sho, 5th in the Oka Sho, 9th in the Oaks and 11th in the Shuka Sho. At 4 she turned to sprints: close 3rd in the Hanshin Himba S., close 2nd in the Hakodate Sprint S., then held off Red Oval by a neck in the Keeneland C. After being whipped by a handler at the gate in the Keihan Hai, she lost form, retiring to breed after the Swan S. at 5.
1997=He won a Hanshin 1600m turf debut, weaving through the pack. Heavy favorite at 2.0 in the Daily Hai 2yo S., he pulled hard and was 11th. 3rd in the Wakagoma S., still keen, he then raced forward and burst clear to win the Kyodo News Hai in race-record time. Favorite in the Wakaba S., his temper struck again, 8th; in the Satsuki Sho he closed fastest from last but was only 9th.
1998=Best US foal of Henny Hughes before his import to Japan. 11 G1s incl. 2 Breeders' Cup Distaffs and 3 Zenyatta S. - a US star of the 2010s. At 2 she won the BC Juvenile Fillies; at 3 she won 4 G1s incl. the Santa Anita Oaks and BC Distaff, Eclipse champion 3yo filly. At 4 her only G1 was the Zenyatta S., but at 5 she was unbeaten in 5 with 3 G1s, champion older mare - the first to win G1s at 2, 3, 4 and 5, and the 2nd after Zenyatta herself to win the Zenyatta S. 3 times. At 6 she won the Vanity Mile, then after close 2nds won a 2nd BC Distaff and retired. Her first foal (by Uncle Mo) came in 2018.
1999=A bold front-runner who won the 2014 Takamatsunomiya Kinen. 13th in the Satsuki Sho at 3, he turned to sprints, winning the Swan S. well in autumn and holding 4th from the front in the Mile CS. At 4 he led all the way in the Hankyu Hai, then in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen beat later Sprinters S. winner Snow Dragon and favorite Straight Girl by 3 lengths for his first G1. 3rd choice in the Sprinters S., he raced from the back, 12th. 2nd in the Hanshin C. when able to race forward, he never won again.
