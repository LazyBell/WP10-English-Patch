3209=A leading member of the 2024 classic crop. A French-bred by Siyouni of the Nureyev line, his sire's first graded winner in Japan. Unbeaten in 2 from November at 2 incl. the Kyoto 2yo S. (G3), 2nd to Regaleira in the Hopeful S. (G1). At 3, 2nd in the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, 5th in the Satsuki Sho, 3rd in the Derby. In Europe, 3rd in the Irish Champion S., then 12th in the Arc. Back home, 2nd to Do Deuce in the Japan Cup, best of the 3yos. At 4 he won Saudi Arabia's Neom Turf C. (G2), then 7th Dubai Sheema Classic, 6th Irish Champion S., 8th Japan Cup, 14th Arima Kinen.
3210=Debuting at 3, he was nearly always top 3 until passing allowances in January at 4. In middle-distance graded races he struggled (7th Kinko Sho, 7th Niigata Daishoten, 6th Epsom C.), then won the October S. (L) and the Chunichi Shimbun Hai (G3) as 2nd choice, holding on from up front. At 5, 10th in the Kinko Sho, he won the Niigata Daishoten; 4th in the Mainichi Okan.
3211=Winner of the 2023 Capella S. On turf from debut, he won the 2-win class in summer at 4, then lost badly and switched to dirt at 5, quickly winning the 3-win class and Keiyo S. (L). 15th in the turf Hakodate Sprint S., back on dirt 5th in the Muromachi S. (OP), he led all the way in the Capella S.
3212=Champion 2yo filly of 2023 and winner of the 2025 Victoria Mile (G1). Unbeaten in her debut and the Niigata 2yo S. (G3), she was 3rd choice in a wide-open Hanshin JF; from midfield she split the field and held off 5th choice Stellenbosch inside and 2nd choice Corazon Beat outside by a neck. At 3, straight to the Oka Sho as favorite, 2nd to Stellenbosch; favorite in the NHK Mile C., 2nd to Jantar Mantar. She won the Keisei Hai AH (G3) as hot favorite, proving it no fluke. Racing abroad, 12th in Australia's Golden Eagle; at 4 she won Saudi Arabia's 1351 Turf Sprint (G2), then the Victoria Mile as favorite. In France she was favorite for the Prix Jacques le Marois (G1), 6th.
3213=A filly who won Funabashi's Marine C. at 4 in 2023. Winning her debut in October at 2, a 1-win race in August at 3, she won 2 more classes early at 4, then the Marine C. as hot favorite by 5 lengths from up front. She retired after it: 5 wins from 8, top 2 every time except a 9th on turf.
3214=Unplaced on turf early, he switched to dirt in summer at 3 and won 4 straight into open class. At 4 he won his first graded race, the Kakitsubata Kinen (JpnIII), in a tight finish, the Mercury C. (JpnIII) easily, and the Hakusan Daishoten (JpnIII) - 3 straight. In G1s: 5th JBC Classic, 2nd Champions C., 2nd Tokyo Daishoten. At 5, 4th Dubai World Cup, 2nd Teio Sho, then he won the JBC Classic - a G1-level win. In the Champions C. he was 2nd by a nose to champion Lemon Pop. At 6, 4th Saudi Cup, 7th Dubai World Cup; he won the Mile CS Nambu Hai (JpnI), then was 2nd in the Champions C. for a 3rd straight year.
3215=Winning his debut easily in August at 2, he led all the way in a 1-win race at 3, won the Fukuryu S. (OP), then led throughout in the Hyogo Championship, winning by 6 lengths. 3rd to Mick Fire in the Japan Dirt Derby, 2nd in the Urawa Kinen. At 4 he won the Heian S. (G3), then 14th in the Miyako S. and 10th in the Champions C.
3216=He won his first graded race, the Hokkaido Sprint C., at 6. Mostly at 1800m early without winning, he switched to sprints in spring at 4, climbed allowances fast and won the Tempozan S. (OP) at 5. At 6, 2nd in the Tokyo Sprint, he won the Hokkaido Sprint C. from up front. 9th in the JBC Sprint.
3217=Winner of the 2023 Kanto Oaks. Winning her debut by 5 lengths, then a 4th, she won a 1-win race in spring at 3. Only 3rd choice at Kawasaki's 2100m Kanto Oaks, having run only at a mile, she raced forward and held them off in the straight. 2nd in the Breeders' Gold C., 3rd in the Queen Sho.
3218=Winner of the 2023 Sparking Lady C. Talented early, 2nd in the Hyogo Junior GP at 2, she passed allowances in summer at 3 and turned to graded races at 4: 2nd in the Sparking Lady C. and the Marine C. at 5, then she won the Sparking Lady C. in a tight finish, avenging the year before. 3rd to Lemon Pop in the Mile CS Nambu Hai.
3219=An Oi horse who won the 2023 Southern Kanto Triple Crown: Haneda Hai, Tokyo Derby and Japan Dirt Derby. Unbeaten in 3 at 2, at 3 he won the Haneda Hai and Tokyo Derby by 6 lengths each, then beat the JRA runners in the Japan Dirt Derby - an unbeaten Triple Crown, only the 2nd after Toshin Blizzard 22 years earlier. With the 2024 overhaul of dirt racing (a 3yo dirt Triple Crown open to JRA horses), he was the last Southern Kanto Triple Crown winner. He won Morioka's Derby Grand Prix at 1.1-1 - 7 straight. Against older horses: 8th Tokyo Daishoten, 7th February S. at 4, 5th Kashiwa Kinen, 4th Mile CS Nambu Hai, then 13th in the Champions C., his first double-figure finish.
3220=Winner of the 2023 Summer Champion and Hyogo Gold Trophy. Debuting late in June at 3, he won 4 straight from his 2nd start through the classes. Unplaced in open races (10th Keiyo S., 6th Echigo S., 9th Cluster C.), he tried leading in the Summer Champion and never tired. After 3.5 months off, 20kg heavier, he won the Hyogo Gold Trophy, tracking the leader and taking over in the straight.
3221=Passing allowances at 4, she was 4th in her first graded race, the Empress Hai, won the Meitetsu Hai (L), then the Ladies' Prelude as 6th choice, catching hot favorite Grand Bridge. 3rd in the JBC Ladies' Classic, 9th in the Champions C. At 5 she won the Queen Sho and Sparking Lady C., then improved to 7th in the Champions C.
3222=Debuting in Hokkaido in 2023, she won the local Edelweiss Sho. After an easy debut win came 5th, 5th, 6th, 7th, then 2nd in the Blossom C. In the Edelweiss Sho, 10th choice of 14, she saved ground at the back and swept past them all in the straight.
3223=Winner of the 2023 JBC Ladies' Classic. Racing on turf at first, she ran in the Radio Nikkei Sho and Rose S. at 3, then 3rd in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai and 2nd in the Aichi Hai at 5, still winless. Switching to dirt that summer, she won the BSN Sho (L) at once, was 2nd in the Sirius S., then as JBC Ladies' Classic favorite led early and drew away. At 6, 3rd in the Kawasaki Kinen, a close 2nd in the Ladies' Prelude, then 7th defending the JBC Ladies' Classic, her last race.
3224=A dirt name of 2023-2024. Debuting at 3, he reached open class in February at 4, was 3rd in the Antares S. (G3), won the Sannomiya S. and Aso S. (OP), then the JBC Classic as 4th choice, surging clear from a handy spot past Meisho Hario and T O Keynes - a G1-level win. 5th Tokyo Daishoten, 5th February S. at 5, 4th Kashiwa Kinen, then he won the Teio Sho from up front for a 2nd G1-level win.
3225=A horse who rose to the top of the dirt world in 2024, Real Steel's first G1 winner, from his 2nd crop. Unbeaten in 3 at 2 incl. the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun (JpnI), at 3 he won the Saudi Derby (G3) and UAE Derby (G2), was 3rd in the Kentucky Derby, won the Japan Dirt Classic (JpnI), 3rd in the BC Classic, and won the Tokyo Daishoten - a star at home and abroad. At 4 he won the Saudi Cup (G1), 3rd in the Dubai World Cup, won the Nippon TV Hai (JpnII), then the BC Classic - the first Japan-trained winner. His earnings passed a record 2.9 billion yen by end-2025. Without a JRA start that year, he was the first dirt horse to be JRA Horse of the Year, also champion older colt and champion dirt horse.
3226=Debuting in 2023, he won the Hyogo Junior GP. 4th as favorite on a turf debut, he switched to dirt and won a maiden and the Platanus Sho (1-win). In the Hyogo Junior GP, a two-horse duel with Satono Phoenix, they broke clear and he won the long battle by a nose. 2nd, well behind Forever Young, in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun.
