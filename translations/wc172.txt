3714=Winner of 4 graded races incl. the 1969 Kinko Sho. He blossomed at 5 (old age system): 2nd in the Keio Hai Spring H. and Tanabata Sho, he won the Fukushima Kinen, then the Niigata Kinen. At 6 he won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, was 2nd in the Chukyo Kinen, and won the Kinko Sho. 13th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 7, his first double-figure finish, he retired after a 6th in an open race early at 8.
3715=Winner of the 1969 Hanshin Daishoten. First winning in his 8th start, in June at 4 (old age system), he ran in the Kikuka Sho still an allowance horse and was 3rd as 20th choice, then won the Hanshin Daishoten. Rarely in the top 5 in stakes as an older horse, he switched to jumps at 7 and won 4 straight incl. the spring Hanshin Shogai S., becoming favorite for the spring Nakayama Daishogai - but an unlucky incident in the paddock forced his withdrawal, and hurt, he never won again.
3716=Winner of the 1968 Hanshin Daishoten. Debuting at 4 (old age system), he won easily, then kept missing in allowances. Reaching open class in spring at 5, he was 2nd in the Hakodate Kinen; after 2nds in the Keihan Hai and autumn Kyoto Kinen, he won the Hanshin Daishoten. At 6, 10th in the spring Kyoto Kinen, 6th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), and he retired.
3717=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1972 Nihon Keizaisho. Winning his debut at 3 (old age system), 3rd in the Hokkaido 3yo S. (forerunner of the Sapporo 2yo S.), 2nd in the Asahi Hai 3yo S., he was noticed early. Struggling at 4, he won 2 allowances in autumn and the Kabutoyama Kinen. At 5 he won the Nihon Keizaisho and autumn Meguro Kinen; in Big Eight races, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and, at 6, 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring). He retired after a 6th in the Takarazuka Kinen.
3718=Winner of the 1970 All Comers and 1971 Nihon Keizaisho, owned by Kakuei Tanaka, later Prime Minister. At 4 (old age system), still an allowance horse, he was 19th in the Derby and 10th in the Kikuka Sho. Blossoming at 5, 3rd in the Sprinters S., he won the All Comers, then 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 7th in the Arima Kinen. He won the Nihon Keizaisho at 6 in record time, ran once at 7 and retired.
3719=Raced 20 times at 5 (old age system) in 1969, winning 3 graded races: Nihon Keizaisho, Keio Hai Autumn H. and All Comers. His first stakes win was the Keisei Hai early at 4, then 8th Satsuki Sho, 6th Derby, 7th Kikuka Sho - 21 starts that year too. After the 3 wins at 5, he was 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Winless at 6, but 2nd in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai and spring Kyoto Kinen, he retired that year.
3720=Winner of the 1967 Tanabata Sho and 1968 Nihon Keizaisho. Debuting at 4 (old age system) with 2 straight wins, he was 22nd in the Satsuki Sho and 23rd in the Derby, but 2nd in the Kabutoyama Kinen. At 5 he won 4 open races and the Tanabata Sho; at 6, 2nd in the Sprinters S., he won the Nihon Keizaisho in record time. 2nd in the Diamond S. and Nakayama Kinen at 7, he retired at 8.
3721=Winner of the 1971 All Comers and 1972 Fukushima Kinen. First winning late at 3 (old age system), he battled in allowances, reached open class in autumn at 4, was 5th in the St. Lite Kinen, then won the All Comers as 12th choice of 15. At 5, 4th in the Yasuda Kinen, 3rd in the Tanabata Sho, he won the Fukushima Kinen. In the Tenno Sho (Autumn) he broke down and was put down.
3722=A mare who won 4 graded races incl. the 1968 All Comers. 5th in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. (old age system), 2nd in the Oka Sho and 3rd in the Oaks at 4, she won the Keio Hai Autumn H. at 5, then the All Comers. 11th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn); at 6 she won the Himba Tokyo Times Hai (forerunner of the Fuchu Himba S.), at 7 the Kimpai (East), and retired. Over 18 years at stud she produced 8 daughters, all bred on, greatly expanding her family.
3723=Winner of the 1972 Mainichi Okan. Winning his debut at 3 (old age system) and 2 allowances at 4, he was 4th in the Satsuki Sho; 19th in the Derby, too far. 4th in the Kabutoyama Kinen, he won a 1800m open race at 5, his best trip, then the Mainichi Okan as 4th choice. 3rd in the All Comers, he left the turf.
3724=Winner of the 1971 Niigata Kinen and Mainichi Okan. Struggling long in allowances at 3 and 4 (old age system), he reached open class in his 19th start in autumn at 4, but was 16th and 11th in stakes. At 5 he matured, always top 3: 3rd in the Sekiya Kinen, then the Niigata Kinen and Mainichi Okan as a fancy. Unplaced in the Sprinters S. and All Comers, he retired.
3725=Winner of 4 graded races incl. the 1970 Mainichi Okan. 12th in the Satsuki Sho and 7th in the Derby at 4 (old age system), he improved in autumn: the Keio Hai Autumn H. as 5th choice, then the Mainichi Okan and St. Lite Kinen - 3 straight - and later the Lord Derby CT. Just one open win from 5, but 2nd in the AJCC and Nihon Keizaisho.
3726=Winner of the 1971 autumn Kyoto Kinen and 1972 Hollywood Turf Club Sho (forerunner of the Kyoto Daishoten). Winning his debut in summer at 4 (old age system), he won 6 of 9 allowances to spring at 6, never worse than 5th. 4th in his first stakes, the Kinko Sho, he won the autumn Kyoto Kinen. At 7, 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring), 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he won the Hollywood Turf Club Sho in record time and retired.
3727=Winner of the 1970 Naruo Kinen and Hollywood Turf Club Sho (forerunner of the Kyoto Daishoten). Passing allowances in autumn at 4 (old age system), he was 6th of 21 in the Kikuka Sho and 6th in the Tenno Sho (Spring) at 5. He won the Naruo Kinen (then 2400m), then the Hollywood Turf Club Sho, and after an open win at 6 left the turf.
3728=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1969 Hollywood Turf Club Sho (forerunner of the Kyoto Daishoten), and 3 times 2nd and once 3rd in the Tenno Sho - so close. A classic contender at 4 (old age system), 3rd in the Satsuki Sho, 8th in the Derby, he was scratched ill from the Kikuka Sho, then won the autumn Kyoto Kinen and Hanshin Daishoten. At 5 he was 2nd as Tenno Sho (Autumn) favorite; at 6 he won the Hollywood Turf Club Sho, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 6th in the Arima Kinen; at 7, 2nd in both Tenno Sho. After a 2nd in the Diamond S. at 8 he retired.
3729=Winner of the 1968 AJCC and Stayers S. Emerging in autumn at 4 (old age system), 3rd in the All Comers, 5th in the Arima Kinen, he won an open race and the AJCC early at 5, then the spring Stayers S. 5th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 2nd in the Arima Kinen - a fine stayer. Winless at 6, best 2nd in the CBC Sho and Aichi Hai, he retired at year-end.
3730=Winner of the 1972 spring Kyoto Kinen. After near-misses in allowances at 3 and 4 (old age system), he reached open class at 5 and at once was 2nd in the Naruo Kinen and 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Spring). Almost always top 5, he won the spring Kyoto Kinen at 6 - his only open win, but a familiar contender in graded races.
3731=Winner of 4 graded races incl. the 1971 spring Kyoto Kinen. After a long apprenticeship - 9 starts to win, then allowance struggles until late at 4 (old age system) - he blossomed at 5, winning the spring Meguro Kinen easily as 14th choice, then the Stayers S. At 6 he won the Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai and spring Kyoto Kinen, and was a close 2nd to Tomei in the Arima Kinen. At 7 he broke down in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and was put down.
3732=Winner of the 1970 autumn Kyoto Kinen. Debuting at 4 (old age system), he won a maiden and 3 allowances. At 5 he placed in stakes (2nd Nihon Keizai Shinshun Hai, 3rd Kitakyushu Kinen and Kokura Kinen), then won 2 allowances and the autumn Kyoto Kinen - 3 straight. Out of contention after, he retired in summer at 6.
3733=Winner of the 1970 spring Kyoto Kinen. Winning 3 straight allowances at 4 (old age system), he was 18th in the Satsuki Sho and 22nd in the Derby, then rested. Back at 5, 4th in the Swan S., he won the spring Kyoto Kinen, crushing fancied crop-mate Kinsen O and others. Poor in stakes after, he won one allowance at 6 and left the turf.
3734=Winner of the 1969 autumn Kyoto Kinen. He ran in the Kyoto Kinen 5 times, spring and autumn, from 5 to 7 (old age system): 7th in spring at 5, he won that autumn over younger Kinsen O and others; 3rd in spring and 6th in autumn at 6; last (10th) in autumn at 7. He was also 2nd in the Asahi Challenge C. and Hollywood Turf Club Sho.
3735=Winner of 3 graded races incl. the 1968 autumn Kyoto Kinen. He won 4 straight from late at 3 (old age system) to the Kisaragi Sho, was 2nd in the NHK Hai and 3rd in the Derby, then won the Hollywood Turf Club Sho in record time. At 5, 5th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), 3rd in the Takarazuka Kinen, he won the autumn Kyoto Kinen. 2nd in the spring Kyoto Kinen at 6, he retired after an 11th in the Swan S.
