3113=Struggling on dirt, he switched to turf in spring at 5 and was transformed, rising to win the Japan Cup. Leg trouble delayed his debut to spring at 3; he won his 5th start and a 1-win race in January at 4, then hit a wall, never top 2 in 8 tries. On turf he won the Awaji Tokubetsu (2-win) at once and the 3-win class, then in his first graded race, the Kyoto Daishoten, as 2nd choice, caught favorite Boccherini. In the Japan Cup, 3rd choice behind previous Derby winner Shahryar and 3yo Danon Beluga, 3rd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he came out best in the straight duel - a G1. 10th as 4th choice in the Arima Kinen; at 6, 13th in the Dubai World Cup, 8th Takarazuka Kinen, 7th defending the Japan Cup. Meant to race on, he was found to have tendon trouble and retired to stud.
