2841=By Deep Impact's son Mikki Isle; from his first crop (debuting 2020), his first graded winner. She won her debut as favorite, the Kokura 2yo S. after a duel with Mont Reve from midfield, and the Fantasy S. from up front - unbeaten in 3. 3rd choice in the Hanshin JF, she closed from the back for 4th. At 3 she dead-heated with Elizabeth Tower in the Tulip Sho at 1.6, but Yutaka Take flagged her temper, which left her last (18th) in the Oka Sho. Turning to sprints, she closed sharply for 4th in the Sprinters S. At 4 she won the Silk Road S. and Keio Hai SC, and the Centaur S. from up front by 2.5 lengths, but was 14th as favorite in the Sprinters S. and 5th as 2nd choice in the Hong Kong Sprint. At 5, pushed to favoritism in a wide-open Takamatsunomiya Kinen, she was 12th, then 15th in the Yasuda Kinen; 5th in the Sprinters S., she was last in the BC Filly & Mare Sprint.
2845=The first JRA graded winner by imported sire Makfi. Winning her debut and the Canna S. (OP), 3rd to Gold Chalice in the Chukyo 2yo S., she was 5th in the Marguerite S. (L) and 3rd in the Aoi S. at 3, then won the Ibis Summer Dash as favorite over Lion Boss and Mont Reve with the fastest finish. 8th in the Keihan Hai; at 4, 10th, 6th and 6th defending the Ibis Summer Dash. After a year off, she won it again on her return at 5 as 9th choice - 2 years after her first. 13th in the Sprinters S. and last on her dirt debut in the Capella S., she retired to breed.
2860=Winner of the 2021 Kikuka Sho, and older the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Takarazuka Kinen - a ruler of 2021-2022. He won his debut as favorite, then was 2nd to Danon the Kid in the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. and 4th in the Hopeful S. At 3 he won the Yayoi Sho Deep Impact Kinen, his sire Duramente's first graded winner. 2nd to Efforia in the Satsuki Sho, 6th from the front to Shahryar in the Derby, 13th as favorite in the St. Lite Kinen. In a Kikuka Sho without either classic winner, 4th choice, he led and pulled further clear - a classic title. From the wide outside draw he held on for 5th in the Arima Kinen. At 4 he won the Nikkei Sho at short odds, the Tenno Sho (Spring) by 7 lengths, and the Takarazuka Kinen over crop rival Efforia - best in training. 11th in the Arc, he was 9th to new star Equinox in the Arima Kinen. At 5 he won the Nikkei Sho, but was pulled up lame defending the Tenno Sho (Spring). 2nd in the All Comers, he held 5th from 2nd in the Japan Cup, then led and held 3rd in his farewell Arima Kinen, retiring to stud.
2861=Much noticed from his debut, he won a dirt debut at 1.7, then the Yamaboushi Sho by 7 lengths as 3rd choice, leading all the way. At 1.9 in the Hyogo Junior GP he led again and held on by half a length. Favorite in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun, hoping to give sire Mikki Isle a first G1, he was 7th. At 3, 2nd in the Shoryu S. (OP), unplaced in the Tango S. and NST Sho, he won the Muromachi S. (OP) from up front. At 4, sprinting in open races, he won the Tennozan S. and Osaka Sports Hai (both OP).
2862=2021 JRA Horse of the Year, winner of the Satsuki Sho, and at 3 the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen. By Epiphaneia, sire of Triple Crown filly Daring Tact the year before, much noticed, he won his debut as favorite and the Hyakunichiso Tokubetsu. At 3 he crushed later Derby winner Shahryar and others in the Kyodo News Hai, then won the Satsuki Sho from up front, 2nd choice behind Danon the Kid - unbeaten in 4. Derby favorite at 1.7, he led in the straight but was caught by Shahryar by a nose - his first defeat. Skipping the Kikuka Sho, he beat Triple Crown winner Contrail and sprint queen Gran Alegria in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), the first 3yo winner in 19 years since Symboli Kris S. Top of the fan vote in the Arima Kinen, against Chrono Genesis bidding for a 4th straight Grand Prix, he swept wide and ran them down - the Tenno Sho (Autumn)-Arima double at 3 like his grandsire Symboli Kris S, "best in training." Older he lost his shine: 9th as hot favorite in the Osaka Hai, 6th as favorite in the Takarazuka Kinen, 5th as 5th choice in the Arima Kinen. Pulled up in the Kyoto Kinen at 5 - checks found nothing - he retired as a precaution. First stud fee: 3 million yen.
2863=Unbeaten in 2, 3rd to Danon the Kid in the G1 Hopeful S., 2nd in the Kisaragi Sho, he went straight to the classics: 5th as 11th choice in the Satsuki Sho, 7th in the Derby. Injured in autumn, back at 4, he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai (G2), outdueling crop-mate and favorite Stella Veloce. A tendon injury cost 2 years; back in spring at 6, 3rd in the Kinko Sho and Niigata Daishoten, he won the Naruo Kinen. At 7 he won the Kyoto Kinen, 3rd in the Osaka Hai and All Comers - still going strong.
2864=By Deep Impact out of Apapane - both Triple Crown winners. In the 4th year of this mating came a star: the 2021 Shuka Sho winner. 7th on debut, she won a maiden and the Akamatsu Sho, then the Queen C. at 3 - 3 straight. 4th to Sodashi in the Oka Sho, 2nd choice in the Oaks, she was 2nd to Uberleben as Sodashi sank. Straight into the Shuka Sho, 4th choice behind Sodashi and improvers Fine Rouge and Andvaranaut, she won from up front - a G1. In the QEII Cup, billed vs. older Rei Papale, both faltered; she was 7th. Scratched at the gate with a leg problem before the Hanshin Himba S. at 4, she retired to breed.
2865=A Hokkaido mare who debuted at Mombetsu and won the Edelweiss Sho. Beaten a head on debut, she won a maiden by 4 lengths, then 3 straight as favorite, and the Edelweiss Sho as hot favorite against JRA horses for the first time, by 4 lengths from a handy spot. At 3 she won the Toritsuki Tokubetsu (Mombetsu) and Hayate Sprint (Morioka), then was last on her turf debut in the JRA's Keeneland C. Back home, she has lost badly.
2866=A Hokkaido horse, first winner of the JBC 2yo Yushun, new in 2020. At Mombetsu, with JRA horses Taisei Again and Luce d'Oro fancied, he was 6th choice, broke clear from a handy spot and held off local Transcendence. Last-to-10th in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun. At 3 he swept Hokkaido's local Triple Crown - Hokuto Hai, Hokkai Yushun and Okan Sho - then was 4th in Morioka's Derby Grand Prix. He later moved to Kawasaki and Sonoda, winning several local stakes there.

(L), he won the Kyoto 2yo S. as 3rd choice, creeping up from the back through the turns and winning a duel with Lagom. (L) and Kyoto 2yo S., he won the Kisaragi Sho at 3, holding off Yoho Lake from up front. 13th in the Satsuki Sho, 12th in the Derby and Niigata Kinen, he won the Andromeda S. 2869=The 2021 Derby winner; full brother of 2017 Satsuki Sho winner Al Ain. A debut winner at 2, he was 3rd to later Satsuki winner Efforia in the Kyodo News Hai, then won the Mainichi Hai (G3). In the Derby, 4th choice behind Efforia and Oka Sho runner-up Satono Reinas, he tracked Efforia from midfield and caught him by a nose. Favorite in the Kobe Shimbun Hai, 4th on heavy ground, he skipped the Kikuka Sho for the Japan Cup and was 3rd to Triple Crown winner Contrail - a worthy flag-bearer. At 4 he won the Dubai Sheema Classic (G1) by a neck, then was 4th of 5 in England's Prince of Wales's S. and 5th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), then closed sharply for 2nd to Vela Azul in the Japan Cup. At 5, 5th in Dubai, 11th in the Sapporo Kinen, 3rd in the BC Turf, 5th in the Arima Kinen. At 6, 2nd in Dubai, 3rd in the BC Turf, and a nose 2nd to Regaleira in the Arima Kinen, then retired. Only 7 of 74 mares conceived in his first stud year, and retirement was considered, but tests found his fertility normal and he stays at stud.
2870=Unbeaten in 2, outclassed at 3 (13th Aoba Sho, 10th St. Lite Kinen) but 3rd from the front in the Radio Nikkei Sho, he restarted, winning the 2-win class in autumn at 3 and the 3-win class in spring at 4. Back in graded company, he won the Epsom C. from up front. At 5 he won the AJCC; at 6, 3rd in Hong Kong's QEII Cup (G1), then the Sapporo Kinen (G2) - 3 graded wins.

2872=A blueblood by European great Frankel out of fast BC Filly & Mare Sprint winner Wavell Avenue. Ducking in on left-handed tracks in his first 2 starts, he was transformed going right-handed at Hanshin 1400m, winning in 1:20.4, 0.3s off the 2yo course record. 7th choice in the Asahi Hai FS, he sat 3rd as Mont Reve set a hot pace 5+ lengths clear, spurted before the 4th turn, caught him 200m out and held on in a Hanshin 1600m 2yo record 1:32.3 - a fine, bold ride by Kawada on fast ground. At 3, a mile hope, he was 2nd in the Falcon S., 3rd in the NHK Mile C. and Keisei Hai AH, then 13th in the Mile CS as crop-mates Schnell Meister and Danon the Kid held their own. He charged wide to win the Hanshin C., his first win in a year. At 4, 12th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 19th in England's Platinum Jubilee S., then 2nd in the Hanshin C. At 5, 7th as hot favorite in the Hankyu Hai, 5th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, a fracture; 6th in the Swan S., 2nd in his farewell Hanshin C., and to stud.
2873=Won the 2021 Sprinters S. at 3, showcasing his crop's strength. He won his debut as favorite, then the Shinzan Kinen at 3 as 4th choice, leading all the way. 4th as 2nd choice in the Arlington C. and 12th in the NHK Mile C., both fading from the front, he focused on sprints: 2nd to Fast Force in the CBC Sho, 2nd by a neck to Resistencia in the Centaur S. In the Sprinters S., 3rd choice behind Takamatsunomiya and Hong Kong Sprint winner Danon Smash and Resistencia, he broke clear from a handy spot and pulled away from Resistencia for a G1. In the Hong Kong Sprint he was brought down in another horse's fall and pulled up; the injury kept him out all of 4.
