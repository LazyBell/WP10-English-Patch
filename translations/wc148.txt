3329=A US filly who won 8 G1s from 1993 to 1995; her BC Distaff at 4 against great stablemate Inside Information was much talked about. Unbeaten from debut to the Frizette S. (G1) at 2, 3rd in the BC Juvenile Fillies. At 3 she won the Alabama S., Gazelle H. and Beldame S. - 3 straight G1s - then lost the BC Distaff by a neck, but was Eclipse champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won 4 straight G1s incl. the Apple Blossom H. and Hempstead H., then in the BC Distaff "clash of great mares" with stablemate Inside Information (5 G1s) and younger Serena's Song (8 G1s), she was 2nd as Inside Information romped. She ran once at 5 and retired. A fine broodmare too: 7 winners incl. Hollywood Derby (G1) winner Good Reward.
3330=A world traveller who won the Japan Cup, Dubai World Cup and Coronation C., and a top sire whose son Moon Ballad also won the Dubai World Cup. To the end of 1995 at 3 he had no stakes win, only G1 2nds in the Grand Prix de Paris and Eclipse S. At 4 he won the Gordon Richards S. (G3), then his first G1, the Canadian International S. in Canada. 2nd in the BC Turf, he came to Japan and won the Japan Cup over Helissio, Pentire, Bubble Gum Fellow and others - Eclipse champion turf male. At 5 he won the Dubai World Cup on his first dirt start, then 2 G1s in England, the Coronation C. and International S.; injured while preparing for the BC Turf, he retired. His first crop's Moon Ballad made it a father-son Dubai World Cup double; later came Papineau, Lahudood, Japan's Yasuda Kinen winner Asakusa Den'en and many more G1 winners.
3331=A US mare who won 11 G1s from 1994 to 1996. Debuting in May at 2 and winning her 3rd start, she won the Oak Leaf S. and Hollywood Starlet S. by year-end. At 3 she won the Las Virgenes S. and Santa Anita Oaks, then 16th against colts in the Kentucky Derby. She won the Black-Eyed Susan S., Mother Goose S., Haskell Invitational H. and Beldame S., and in the BC Distaff, a much-hyped big-three clash with older stars Inside Information and Heavenly Prize, was 5th - still 6 G1s, Eclipse champion 3yo filly. At 5 she won 3 more G1s, the Santa Monica H., Santa Maria H. and Hempstead H., with several G1 2nds, and retired. A great broodmare too: G1 winner Sophisticat and 3 other graded winners.
3332=Winner of 10 Australian G1s from 1995 to 1997 and sire of 11-time G1 winner Lonhro - a hero in Australia. At 2 in 1994/95 he won 3 of 5 with 2 seconds, Australian champion 2yo. At 3 in 1995/96 he beat older horses in the Cox Plate, then won 4 G1s in a single month late in the season - Australian Horse of the Year. At 4 in 1996/97 he ran unplaced more often, but improved to win 4 G1s and retired. His first crop produced Lonhro, who outdid him with 11 G1s; shuttled to France in 1998, he sired Laverock, winner of the Prix d'Ispahan and Gran Premio del Jockey Club.
3333=Sire of Curlin, English Channel and many more - Mr. Prospector's most celebrated sire son. North American champion sire in 2007 and 2008, he smashed the record for a year's progeny earnings. On the track he won 1 G1: debuting in April at 3, 2nd, then 3 straight wins; injured, he rested over a year, returned to win the Salvator Mile H. (G3) and Philip H. Iselin H. (G1), and retired after a 4th in the Woodward S. His first crop included Japan Cup Dirt winner Fleetstreet Dancer; in 2007 Curlin and English Channel won the BC Classic and BC Turf on the same day - his peak. Fabulous Strike, Furthest Land, Lookin at Lucky and many more G1 winners kept him a top sire.
3334=A great European miler who fought Spinning World in 1996, beating him all 4 times they met, and won 2 G1s. Debuting in October at 2, he won 2 straight incl. the Prix Thomas Bryon (G3). At 3 he won the Prix de Fontainebleau (G3), then the Poule d'Essai des Poulains, holding off Spinning World. 2nd by a head to Bijou d'Inde in the St. James's Palace S., he beat Spinning World decisively in the Prix du Moulin. 5th in the QEII S., he retired: 5 wins from 7, unbeaten in France, both losses in England. At stud in Ireland his best was US Citation H. (G1) winner Ashkal Way; little else of note.
3335=The 1996 Arc winner, familiar to Japanese fans as favorite in that year's Japan Cup. Debuting in March at 3, he won 3 straight to the Prix Lupin. 5th in the French Derby, he won the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud and Prix Niel, then led the Arc and drew away, winning by 5 lengths though Olivier Peslier stopped riding. Favorite in the Japan Cup, he was 3rd to Singspiel, but Cartier Horse of the Year. At 4 he won the Prix Ganay and Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud, then 3rd in the King George, 2nd in the Prix du Moulin, 6th defending the Arc, and retired. At stud in Japan he sired 2-time Meguro Kinen winner Pop Rock; shuttled to Australia, G1 winner Helenus. Below expectations, he was sold to Northern Ireland in 2005. As broodmare sire: mile G1 winners Sadamu Patek (Mile CS) and Jour Polaire (Victoria Mile).
3336=A great miler who won 5 mile G1s in 1996-1997, incl. the Prix Jacques le Marois, Prix du Moulin and BC Mile. A sire mostly in Australia, he is also broodmare sire of 2014 Oaks winner Nuovo Record. Unbeaten in 2 at 2 incl. a G3. At 3, 2nd to Ashkalani in the Poulains, he won the Irish 2000 Guineas, then the Prix Jacques le Marois, but was 2nd to Ashkalani again in the Moulin - beaten by him all 4 times they met. At 4 he won the Jacques le Marois and Moulin, then his farewell BC Mile. At stud in the US, he shuttled or was leased to Australia, New Zealand and Japan, later moving to Ireland. His stars include Australians Thorn Park and Special Harmony; in Japan, local runners like Haiseiko Kinen winner Silk Beat.
3337=Winner of the 1996 1000 Guineas and Champion S., Cartier champion 3yo filly. A full sister to Hector Protector, she was noticed before debut; from August at 2 she won 2 straight incl. the Fillies' Mile (G1). At 3 she won the Fred Darling S. (G3) and the 1000 Guineas - 4 straight. After 5 months off injured, she was 2nd in the QEII S., her first loss, then won the Champion S. easily on her first try at 10f - a 3rd G1. Cartier champion 3yo filly, rated 131, the top filly of the 1990s. At 4 she won 2 stakes, then 3rd in the Eclipse S. and 4th in the International S.; leg trouble ended her career. As a broodmare only Canadian G3 winner Rosberg stood out.
3338=From Unbridled's first crop, his leading sire son, siring many G1 winners such as Unbridled Elaine, Thorn Song and Forever Unbridled, extending grandsire Fappiano's line. Winning his debut easily in August at 2, he won the BC Juvenile, his sire's first G1 winner. At 3, twice 2nd in G2s, he won the Florida Derby and Wood Memorial, then was 5th, injured, in the Kentucky Derby. He won a listed race at 4 and retired. His first crop included BC Distaff winner Unbridled Elaine, making him a popular sire; over 100 stakes winners followed. As broodmare sire in Japan: Contrail, Suave Richard and more.
3339=A great Australian middle/long-distance horse of 1997-1998, who won both big races, the Melbourne Cup and Cox Plate, and was Horse of the Year twice. Winning his 3rd start in 1995/96 at 2, he won a G3 in the last race of 1996/97. Still inconsistent at 4 in 1997/98, he stepped up to 2400m and won the Caulfield Cup, his first G1, then the Melbourne Cup; later that season he won the Mercedes Classic, AJC Queen Elizabeth S. and Doomben C. - Australian Horse of the Year. At 5 in 1998/99, racing only in spring, he won 2 G1s, the Yalumba S. and Cox Plate - Horse of the Year again. Leg trouble kept him out nearly 2 years; back at 7 he ran twice below his best and retired. Australian Racing Hall of Fame, 2002.
3340=He won the 1998 BC Classic at 4, unbeaten in 6 that year, then sired 4 BC winners incl. Ghostzapper. Debuting late in May at 3, he won his 2nd start and 3 straight to the Jim Dandy S. (G2), but was well beaten in the Travers S. and Super Derby. Back in May at 4 after a rest, he was transformed: 3 straight incl. the Whitney H. (G1), 2 more stakes, then the BC Classic against what was called the greatest field ever - Skip Away, Silver Charm, Swain and more - pulling 3/4 length clear of a blanket finish. He retired after it. His first crop's Ghostzapper made it a father-son BC Classic double; Round Pond, Ginger Punch and Wilko also won BC races. He sired 15 G1 winners before being pensioned in 2019.
3341=A great European stayer of 1998-2000, full brother to Opera House. Debuting late in July at 3, he won a maiden in his 2nd start. At 4, 3rd in his first stakes, the Henry II S. (G3), he won the 20f Ascot Gold C. for a G1, plus the 14f Irish St Leger (G1) - Cartier champion stayer. At 5 he couldn't repeat in the Gold Cup, but won the Irish St Leger again and 3 G2s - champion stayer again. At 6 he won the Yorkshire C. (G2) and regained the Ascot Gold C. - just 2 runs, 2 wins, champion stayer for a 3rd year - and retired. Used mainly as a jumps sire, with no flat stars.
3342=A great sire of 3 Cartier Horses of the Year: Sea The Stars, Golden Horn and Ouija Board; Sea The Stars has greatly expanded his line. Not much noticed on the track, his main win was the Lockinge S. (G1) as an outsider. Winning his 2nd start at 2, he won just one allowance at 3. At 4, running as pacemaker for stablemate Kahal in the Lockinge, he led throughout and held on to win. Then the Jacques le Marois, QEII S. and BC Mile, all lost; at 5 he won 2 G2s and retired. At stud his first crop had Oaks and Irish Oaks winner Ouija Board, followed by Able One, Behkabad and Arc winners Sea The Stars and Golden Horn; champion sire in Britain and France in 2009 - far more acclaimed than as a racehorse.
