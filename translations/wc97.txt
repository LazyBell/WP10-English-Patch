2365=A Hokkaido-raced filly who won the 2015 Hokkaido 2yo Yushun, then joined the JRA. Moving to the JRA, she won Kawasaki's Kanto Oaks, then was 3rd in the Sparking Lady C., 2nd in the Breeders' Gold C. and Queen Sho. Steady in JRA-local races at 4, she retired without another win.

Beaten badly in graded races, he switched to sprint-to-middle open races from autumn at 4: 2nd in the Rigel S. and Yonago S. (OP) - always fancied in open races.
2369=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Niigata 2yo S., noticed early. 8th in the Satsuki Sho and 11th in the Derby, he was 2nd in the NHK Mile C. between them, suited to shorter trips. 2370=Winner of the 2015 Falcon S. Erratic - 3 wins, mostly unplaced otherwise - he was 14th of 18 in the Falcon S. Racing forward on the rail, he won a long duel with Active Minoru by a nose - even jockey Matsuda was surprised. Beaten badly in the NHK Mile C. and Hakodate Sprint S. after.
2371=He won Saga's Summer Champion in 2015. After a long time in allowances, he bloomed at 5, winning a 16-million and open race, then dirt graded races: the Summer Champion as favorite by 2.5 lengths from the front, 3rd in the Teletama Hai Oval Sprint, 2nd in the Nambu Hai, and 2nd in the JRA's Musashino S. In 2016 he was 6th in the February S. and won the Musashino S. in course-record time, his first JRA graded win. A Champions C. hope, he fractured a leg in training and was put down.
2372=The first winner of the Saudi Arabia Royal C., renamed from the Icho S. in 2015. After a maiden win as favorite, he broke clear early from midfield and held off favorite Immortal and others. At 3, 2nd in the Falcon S., he was 8th in the NHK Mile C. and 18th in the Derby. Falling short in open and G3 races, he moved to Australia at 4 - and thrived, 3rd in The Everest, the world's richest race.
2373=A mare who won the JBC Ladies' Classic in 2015 and 2016, a dirt specialist like her sire Kurofune. 2389=A speedster who won the spring and autumn mile G1s of 2015. With only a 5-million win by the end of 3, he grew fast at 4: winning 2 classes, he won the Lord Derby CT by 3 lengths as favorite, then the Yasuda Kinen as favorite, sitting handy, drawing level in the straight, pulling clear on the bridle, then kicking on.
