2708=A foreign-bred dirt sprinter. A front-runner at first, he was badly hampered at the start of the Fujinomori S. in October at 3, yet charged from last to win. He won the Autumn Leaf S. (OP), then the Capella S. with another devilish late run, and the Negishi S. at 4. Paired with Nanako Fujita, the first female JRA jockey in 16 years, he drew huge attention: 5th in his first G1, the February S., 2nd in the Tokyo Sprint, 3rd in the Cluster C., then won the Tokyo Hai. A neck 2nd to Bulldog Boss in the JBC Sprint, he won the Capella S. again - the first JRA graded win by a female jockey. At 5, 2nd in the Negishi S. and 3rd in the Summer Champion, he was 6th in the JBC Sprint, winless all year. At 6 in Saudi Arabia he missed the break yet charged to win the Riyadh Dirt Sprint; 5th in the Dubai Golden Shaheen, 3rd in the Summer Champion and 4th in the Tokyo Hai. At 7, 4th defending in Riyadh, he was injured before the Golden Shaheen and retired.

2725=Stuck in the 10-million class until the end of 3, he surged at 4: 2 classes, then the Niigata Daishoten, the Naruo Kinen as favorite and the Kokura Kinen as favorite - 5 straight that year. 2726=The 2019 Oaks winner and the first Japanese horse with 3 G1 wins abroad. With Oka Sho winner and sprint queen Gran Alegria and Shuka Sho winner and 3-time Grand Prix winner Chrono Genesis, the trio ruled 2019-2021. She won her debut easily under Lemaire and a 5-million race as favorite under Yasunari Iwata, closing powerfully. Not ready for the Oka Sho, she won the Wasurenagusa Sho (L) the same day easily. In the Oaks, narrowly favored over Chrono Genesis and Contra Check, she tracked them from midfield, burst clear and caught Curren Bouquet d'Or - in a record 0.8s faster than Gentildonna's. Missing the Shuka Sho, she was QEII Cup favorite but 3rd behind older Lucky Lilac and Crocosmia. At 4, 7th in the Victoria Mile, 2nd in the Naruo Kinen, 5th in the Fuchu Himba S., she was a close 3rd to Lucky Lilac in the QEII Cup, then 10th in her first mixed G1, the Arima Kinen. At 5 she won the Kyoto Kinen at short odds, was 3rd in the Dubai Sheema Classic behind Chrono Genesis, then won Hong Kong's QEII Cup, her first G1 abroad. 2nd in the Sapporo Kinen, she won the BC Filly & Mare Turf in America - the first Japanese BC winner - and in her farewell Hong Kong Cup won a duel with fellow Japanese Hishi Iguazu for a 3rd G1 abroad. She, Gran Alegria and Chrono Genesis all retired at the end of 2021; their foals' rivalry is eagerly awaited.
