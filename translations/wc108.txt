2632=He won the Urawa Kinen in autumn at 4. In graded races from spring at 3 without success (9th Unicorn S., 6th Leopard S.), he restarted in allowances and returned to open class at 4. 5th choice in the G2 Urawa Kinen, untested in open class, he raced forward, led in the straight and pulled away from Higashi Will Win to win by 6 lengths. From 5, a graded force, he was 3rd as 3rd choice in both the Saga Kinen and Diolite Kinen.
2633=A miler on turf or dirt who won the turf Yasuda Kinen and dirt February S. - with Soul Stirring, a flagship of Frankel's first crop in Japan. Fragile, he debuted only in June at 3, then won a maiden and 4 straight to open class. Placing but not winning in graded races (4th Hanshin C., 2nd Hankyu Hai and Milers C.), he lost the Azuchijo S. (OP) by a neck chasing prize money, then ran again a week later in the Yasuda Kinen. Against Osaka Hai winner Suave Richard, Mile CS winner Persian Knight and Milers C. winner Sungrazer, little noticed, he closed fastest from behind midfield - a huge upset, his first graded win a G1. Two winless years on, at 6 he tried dirt, winning the Negishi S., then the February S. as favorite over dirt stars like Inti, bursting clear from midfield - G1s on turf and dirt. 2nd in the Nambu Hai, he closed sharply for 5th in the Champions C.
2634=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Kyoto 2yo S., a 2017 2yo name. A Heart's Cry stayer, he won a 2000m debut, then the 2000m Kyoto 2yo S. as 2nd choice, passing favorite Time Flyer from a handy spot. A classic hope, he was 7th in the Kyodo News Hai, 6th in the Satsuki Sho and 14th in the Derby. 3rd in the St. Lite Kinen, he was 10th in the Kikuka Sho.
2635=A great Hong Kong miler with 8 G1s from 2017 to 2020. Starting in Australia, placed in G1s without a graded win, he moved to Hong Kong late at 3. Winless for a while at 4, at 5 he won his first graded race and the Hong Kong Mile, then the Queen's Silver Jubilee C. and Champions Mile - Hong Kong Horse of the Year. At 6 he won 4 G1s incl. a 2nd Hong Kong Mile, unbeaten in 8 that season, 10 straight in all. At 7 his only G1 was a 3rd straight Queen's Silver Jubilee C. Racing until early 8, he retired to Australia - 8 G1s at 1400-1600m, ruling Hong Kong's miles for 4 years.
2636=Unbeaten in 2, 4th in the Yayoi Sho at 3, he then stalled in allowances, always fancied but often 2nd or 3rd, winning a 16-million race only at 7. In open class, he won the Hakodate Kinen, breaking clear from up front, and was Fukushima Kinen favorite - a middle-distance force.
2637=He won 3 straight from a maiden to the Keisei Hai, a classic dark horse. In the Satsuki Sho, as the fancies sank, he was 3rd behind Epoca d'Oro and Sanrival - 3 longshots, trifecta 370,000 yen. He won the St. Lite Kinen in autumn, holding on with stamina from up front, but in a slow, sprint-finish Kikuka Sho he lacked speed, 9th. 4th in the AJCC at 4, a tendon injury followed; back at 5, 7th in the All Comers, he faded from the front in later races and retired.
2638=In open class only late at 5, but always well regarded. After a 2500m 16-million win, he was favorite in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai and won from up front under Mirco Demuro. Favorite again in the Meguro Kinen, he was a close 3rd to Win Tendaness, then won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai - a top stayer. At 7, 4th in the Kyoto Kinen, he was 3rd as a longshot in the Tenno Sho (Spring). A fracture cost a long break; back at 8 in the Naruo Kinen, as 10th choice he beat hot favorite Loves Only You by a nose.
2639=A leading Zenno Rob Roy colt. After a debut win, he led with ease in the Kisaragi Sho, waited for the pack in the straight, then kicked clear of Glory Vase and Red Leon, holding off Glory Vase's late charge. Injured after, he missed his 3yo season. Back at 4, 3rd in the Rokko S., he ran well in open races and won the listed Rakuyo S. at 6, his first win in 3 years. From 8 he raced locally.
2640=Best foal of Oken Bruce Lee, a rare sire of the Tony Bin line, he gave him a first graded win in the Kyodo News Hai. After a maiden and 5-million win, he raced forward while favorites Grail and Stay Foolish waited, led 200m out and held on. Far back in all 3 classics.
2641=Named after an ancient Greek coin; a 2018 3yo filly hope. Unplaced as favorite in the Fairy S., she won the Queen C., holding on from 2nd as the leaders collapsed in a hot pace. 3rd choice in the NHK Mile C., she was 14th.
2642=The first graded winner by imported sire Turtle Bowl. Slow to win at 3, he won a 16-million race early at 4, then the Kokura Daishoten in his first open race. Favorite in the Niigata Daishoten, Naruo Kinen and Kokura Kinen, he lost the first 2 narrowly, then won the Kokura Kinen easily from a handy spot. After a tendon injury and long break, he won the Nakayama Kimpai, but the injury recurred and he retired.
2643=Still a maiden in the 5-million class, she ran in the Fillies' Revue for an Oka Sho ticket and beat the fancies - Kobai S. winner Molto Allegro, unbeaten Encore Plus and Fukushima 2yo S. winner Anvar - none decisive, edging clear by half a length from a pack. Little rated, 10th choice in the Oka Sho, she fought well for 6th from up front.
2644=Winner of the Flower C. at 3, she was 13th as 7th choice in the Oaks, then won the Rose S., a Shuka Sho fancy. Almond Eye won the Triple Crown, but she closed well for 3rd. Against older mares in the QEII Cup, she was 6th, 2nd-best 3yo behind Normcore.
2645=Placing often on turf but not winning, he finally won a maiden and 5-million race on dirt - nearly 2 years in all. Back on turf late at 4, he won a 10-million race at once, was 3rd stepping up in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai, then won the Nikkei Sho 2 months later. A strong stayer, he ran in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Japan Cup, far back in both.
2646=Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Mainichi Hai, a classic fancy. Skipping the Satsuki Sho, he was 2nd choice in the Derby (5th), then beat older horses easily in the Niigata Kinen. Kikuka Sho favorite, with Derby winner Wagnerian absent and Satsuki winner Epoca d'Oro beaten in a trial, he couldn't match sharper finishers in a slow race, 4th. A great talent seemed set to end 3 without a crown, but as 3rd choice in the Arima Kinen he made a long run and held off older champion Rey de Oro by a neck for a G1. At 4, crop leader and Osaka Hai favorite, he was 6th, then 8th in the Meguro Kinen; he won the Sapporo Kinen, then was 11th of 12 in the Arc, and won the AJCC. Unplaced in the Osaka Hai, Takarazuka Kinen and Tenno Sho (Autumn), he was pulled up with atrial fibrillation in the Arima Kinen. At 7, 3rd in the Naruo Kinen and 5th in the Sapporo Kinen, he retired injured to become a riding horse - the first Arima Kinen winner, aside from deaths in training, not to go to stud.
2647=A steady performer in dirt open races, he won the Radio Nippon Sho in autumn at 5, then the March S. at 6 as 2nd choice, breaking clear from a handy spot and holding off Queen's Saturn by a nose.
2648=A specialist who raced only at a mile, a graded winner in spring at 5. Winning a 16-million race at 4, he dropped a class, won it again at 5, then in his first open race won the Lord Derby CT: boxed in at the top of the straight, he escaped through a narrow gap and passed gritty Campbell Junior. Unplaced in the Yasuda Kinen and Mile CS.
2649=Named after the Italian for "golden age." From Orfevre's first crop, his first male G1 winner. 2nd to Stelvio in the Spring S., he was 7th choice in the Satsuki Sho but led and held on for a G1, with longshots Sanrival and General Uno 2nd and 3rd (trifecta 370,000 yen). In the Derby he led and nearly won 2 classics, caught by Wagnerian, 2nd - clearly among his crop's best. 3rd choice in the Kikuka Sho, he raced forward but faded, 8th, blamed on the slow pace and the trip. At 4, troubled by nosebleeds, he raced only twice.
2650=3rd in the new G1 Hopeful S. at 2, he was 2nd choice in the Kyodo News Hai at 3 but 10th after a big weight loss. Then, as 7th choice, he broke clear from up front to win the Kyoto Shimbun Hai, with 11th choice Admire Alba 2nd (trifecta about 270,000 yen). Far back in the Derby and Kikuka Sho, he was 3rd in the Challenge C. Older, he ran well but kept falling short: four 2nds and five 3rds at 4-5. At 6, 4th in the AJCC and 2nd in the Kyoto Kinen, he rested with phlegmon, then had atrial fibrillation in the Sapporo Kinen; back, 5th, 7th, 4th, and 5th in the Hong Kong Vase. At 7 he tried the Red Sea Turf H. - his first 3000m since the Kikuka Sho - led from the start and pulled away, his first win in 3 years 9 months, then won the 3200m Dubai Gold C. too. 9th in the Takarazuka Kinen, 2nd in the Grand Prix de Deauville and 14th in the Arc, he retired injured to become a riding horse.
2651=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Phoenix Sho in summer at 2, she was 10th on dirt in the Shoryu S., then 3rd back on turf in the Tachibana S. (OP). 9th choice in the Aoi S., she led all the way; Love Kampu and Tour Vesoul dead-heated for 2nd. Beaten badly after, she switched to dirt in autumn at 3, won the Galaxy S., then the Kakitsubata Kinen, and later the Nagatsuki S. (OP) and 2nd in the JBC Ladies' Classic.
2652=In open class only at 5, after a 16-million win at Tokyo in a fast 2:22.9, he won his first open race, the G2 Meguro Kinen, as 9th choice, bursting clear from a handy spot. 8th in the Japan Cup, but very strong at long trips on left-handed tracks like Tokyo.
2653=He beat Kikuka Sho winner Fierement and runner-up Etario. He held off Etario by a nose from the front in a 5-million race in January at 3, then in the Radio Nikkei Sho broke clear from a handy spot and resisted Fierement's arrow-like charge. 14th as 5th choice in the Kikuka Sho, far behind both. Older, 9th in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai, he won the Nikkei Sho; 5th choice in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he faded from up front to 11th.
