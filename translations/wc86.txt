2000=Full brother of Leopard S. winner Boreas and Mainichi Hai runner-up Mount Shasta. A closer with the fastest finish in each of his first 3 races from a Tokyo 2000m turf debut, he ran down the classic fancies in the Yayoi Sho. 4th choice in the Satsuki Sho behind the Big Three - Logotype, Kodino and Epiphaneia - he closed from the back for 4th, proving his win no fluke. A Derby run was hoped for, but a tendon injury ruled him out.
2001=His uncle was 1990 Hanshin 3yo S. winner Ibuki Maikagura. 2nd in a Niigata debut at 2, he won 3 straight incl. the Falcon S., then closed for 2nd, on the same time as Meiner Hoouh, in the NHK Mile C. Mostly beaten badly after, he was 3rd in the Lord Derby CT at 5. Switched to jumps at 7, he was pulled up in his 3rd start and retired.
2002=Winner of the 2013 NHK Mile C. Racing only at 1600-1800m, at 3 he passed up a Satsuki Sho spot for the NHK Mile C. and won it as 10th choice, closing brilliantly wide. He tried the Derby, but at 15th the trip was too far, failing the NHK-Derby double of King Kamehameha and Deep Sky. A tendon injury cost him nearly 2 years; back at 5, his best was 2nd in the Port Island S. (OP). Winless to 7, he retired that November.
2003=Winner of the 2015 Milers C. and Sekiya Kinen, brother of sprint-mile stars like Ridill and Clarente - a pure miler with nearly all wins at a mile. 8th choice in the Milers C., he sat 3rd and ran down leader Sunrise Major. 2nd choice in the Sekiya Kinen, he led all the way for a 2nd graded win. Mostly far back in graded and G1 races after, he retired after the Takamatsunomiya Kinen at 7.
2004=With Espoir City and Smart Falcon, a flagship son of Gold Allure. Little noticed in 2014 with 1 graded win, he led all the way in his first G1, the February S., at 16th choice, upsetting Belshazzar and Hokko Tarumae. He won the Kashiwa Kinen by 2 lengths, proving himself, but was 2nd as favorite to Wonder Acute in the Teio Sho. In autumn he won the JBC Classic in record time, then stumbled at the start as favorite in the Champions C., 12th. At 5 he won the February S. and JBC Classic for a 5th dirt G1, but was 7th in a hot pace in the Champions C. and 4th to Sound True in the Tokyo Daishoten; still champion dirt horse. In 2016 he won 3 G1s in a row - Kashiwa Kinen, Teio Sho, Mile CS Nambu Hai - then was 5th as favorite in the JBC Classic and far back in the Champions C. At 7 he was 14th in the February S., seemingly past his peak, but won the Kashiwa Kinen with a new waiting style, then the Mile CS Nambu Hai. Going for an 11th JRA-local G1, beyond Hokko Tarumae, he was 2nd in the JBC Sprint and 3rd in the Champions C.
2005=From the 6th crop of Rosado, 5-time graded winner incl. the All Comers. She won a Tokyo 1600m debut as 10th choice, then the Fairy S. at 83.3 to 1, fighting back from up front - her sire's first JRA graded win. 2nd choice in the Anemone S., she held back and won a straight duel, unbeaten in 3. In the Oka Sho, on soft ground, she tracked handy but found nothing, 16th, far behind Ayusan.
2006=By On Fire, Deep Impact's younger brother; her uncles and aunts include Hanshin Himba S. winner Queen's Burn and Nagoya GP winner Wild Soldier. At 50.7 to 1 in her debut in November at 2, she won from midfield, then broke clear early to win the Daily Hai Queen C. at 3. After a long break she restarted at 4 in the 16-million class, won the Keeneland C. at 5 and was 3rd in the Sprinters S. Out of form at 6, she won the Fukushima Himba S. at 7. After finishing last in the QEII Cup she retired to breed.
2007=Winning her 2nd start at Kokura, she was 9th in the Kokura 2yo S. after a hot pace and trouble. She led to win the Rindo Sho, then at 89.9 to 1 in the Hanshin JF tracked in 2nd and held on for 2nd to Robe Tissage - a classic hope. In the Tulip Sho, ridden by Yutaka Take, she led all the way. Oka Sho favorite, she tried to pull clear again but tired, 4th.
2008=The standout 3yo filly of 2013. 10th in the Oka Sho, she won the Oaks easily as 9th choice in 2:25.2, 2nd-fastest ever after Gentildonna. In autumn, 4th in the Rose S., she beat fancied Denim and Ruby and Smart Layer in the Shuka Sho - 2 classics - then beat older mares in the QEII Cup from midfield for a 3rd G1. Against top colts when older she kept finishing far back; 2nd in the Victoria Mile was her last good run. Fans still await her revival.
2009=From the "Sakura" family; her uncle was Tenno Sho winner Sakura Chitose O and aunt QEII Cup winner Sakura Candle. She won a Tokyo 1600m debut, then the Flower C. by a nose over Ever Blossom. 6th choice in the Oaks, she was 14th to Meisho Mambo, and kept losing badly.
2010=2nd in the Tulip Sho at 3, she ran in the Oka Sho. A miler moving between open and allowance races, she won the Kyoto Kimpai at 6 from 3rd, then was 3rd in the Kyoto Himba S. and Hanshin Himba S. After a 12th in the Mile CS she retired to breed.
2011=A 2013 filly star. After the Flora S. she was 3rd in the Oaks, and after the Rose S. 4th in the Shuka Sho - G1 glory eluded her. 5th in the QEII Cup, she was 7th choice in the Japan Cup but closed in a devilish 33.2 to lose by a nose to Gentildonna. At 4 she was 3rd choice for the Dubai Sheema Classic, but was forced to lead and was 10th, as Gentildonna made history. Often closing too late after, at 5 she ran 2nd to Gold Ship in the 3000m Hanshin Daishoten, then closed fastest for 2nd on the same time in the Takarazuka Kinen.
2012=Foreign-bred half-brother of Argentina Kyowakoku Hai winner Taiki Eldorado, related to Mile CS winners Shinko Lovely and Taiki Marshal and Happy Path. A steady sprint-miler who loved Hakodate and Sapporo: in 4 Hakodate Sprint S. he won once and was 2nd twice, and won the UHB Hai and Sapporo Nikkan Sports Hai (OP). In the Mile CS at 5 he was 3rd to Zenno El Cid as 10th choice.
2013=A Warning sprinter who set up many longshots: 2nd as 11th choice in the Fuji S. at 5, he led to win the UHB Hai (OP) at 7 as 8th of 9, and was 3rd as 11th choice at 8. Best of all, as last choice of 16 he beat Bibi Garudan and Kinshasa no Kiseki in the Keeneland C. At 9 he was 2nd as 8th choice in the Hakodate Sprint S.
2014=A long-lasting mile-to-middle horse; ran 14 times at Kokura and won the 1995 Kokura Daishoten under Yutaka Take.
2015=Winning 3 straight from his 2nd start, he was 2nd to Shake Hands in the NZT, then won the Radio Tampa Sho by 2.5 lengths over classic runner Meiner Bridge and graded winner Go Go Nakayama. Unreliable with his temper, he was gelded; never winning a graded race again, he often ran well at a mile.
2016=A mile-to-middle horse with 5 open wins. In the February 1992 Kokura Daishoten he held off Nihon Pillow Brave and Mr. Spain.
2017=By Yamanin Ski, a stud on his pedigree. He won the 1994 Chunichi Shimbun Hai by a nose and was 2nd 3 years running in the Aichi Hai (then for home-bred sires).
2018=A gritty front-runner at local tracks who won the 2008 Fukushima Kinen. Very tough: 68 career races.
2019=A crop-mate of Narita Brian. He beat Derby runner-up Air Dublin in the St. Lite Kinen and was 4th in the Kikuka Sho. Older, his speed served him in graded races on any going.
2020=Needing 10 starts to win, he rose steadily to open class at 6, then won a blanket-finish 2009 Niigata Kinen at 7.
2021=49 career starts; he won the 1997 Kokura Daishoten in his 42nd race, then was 3rd as 9th choice in the Lord Derby CT and 2nd as 12th choice in the Keio Hai Spring C. His half-brother Osumi Jet won 7 dirt graded races in 51 starts.
2022=Racing mostly at a mile, he ran 47 times from 2. He won 4 open races and the 2002 Kitakyushu Kinen.
2023=Foreign-bred by Singspiel. Running in miles everywhere, he won the 2010 Kyoto Kimpai and was 2nd as 14th choice in the Fuji S.
2024=Crop-mate of Mejiro McQueen and Ines Fujin. At 7 he won the 1994 Niigata Daishoten over later Japan Cup winner Marvelous Crown.
2025=After 37 local races he joined the JRA, 5th in the 1990 Takarazuka Kinen and winner of the 1991 Chunichi Shimbun Hai at 7.
2026=A Tokai Teio colt and gritty front-runner. 2nd in the Asahi Challenge C. at 5, he won the Aichi Hai at 6.
2027=Peaking at 5, he won the 1997 Saga Kinen.
2028=A late bloomer, debuting in January at 3, he needed 4 starts to win and 9 more for a 2nd. Leaving the 16-million class in autumn at 5, he won the Kitakyushu Kinen, then ran 2nd in the Kokura Kinen, 4th in the Asahi Challenge C. and 2nd in the Kabutoyama Kinen, but was fatally injured in the Aichi Hai - a cruel end just as he bloomed.
2029=A sprinter who loved Kokura, winning the 2009 Kitakyushu Kinen and the Encore S. (OP) twice. A longshot specialist, with 4 wins at 8th choice or longer.
