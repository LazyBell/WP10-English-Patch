3315=In 1989 she became the 7th New York Filly Triple Crown (Triple Tiara) winner. Later imported to Japan as a broodmare, she produced filly Easy Mind by Easy Goer. At 2 she won 2 G1s, the BC Juvenile Fillies and Demoiselle S. - Eclipse champion 2yo filly. At 3 she won 4 straight to the Kentucky Oaks, then the Acorn S. and Mother Goose S.; in the CCA Oaks outsider Nite of Fun crossed first but was disqualified for interference, giving her the win and the Triple Crown by a whisker. She won the Alabama S. for 10 straight, then lost 3 against older mares, but was Eclipse champion 3yo filly. Beaten twice at 4, she retired. Imported to Japan in foal to Easy Goer, she produced Easy Mind, who won the Wasurenagusa Sho (OP) and carried on the female line.
3316=Winner of the 1990 Kentucky Derby and BC Classic, and a sire of many G1 winners incl. Grindstone, Unbridled's Song and Empire Maker - a firm pillar of the Mr. Prospector line. At 3 he won the Florida Derby, his first G1, then the Kentucky Derby over fancied Mister Frisky and Summer Squall. 2nd in the Preakness, 4th in the Belmont, he won the BC Classic in autumn - Eclipse champion 3yo. At 4 he was 3rd to Black Tie Affair in the BC Classic and retired. His first crop included Kentucky Derby winner Grindstone and BC Juvenile winner Unbridled's Song; then Preakness winner Red Bullet and Belmont winner Empire Maker. He died at 14 but left many promising sons.
3317=A Hong Kong gelding of 1990-1994, the first and only Hong Kong Triple Crown winner and 3 times Horse of the Year. Debuting late at 3, he won 6 straight incl. the Hong Kong Derby and Champions & Chater C. - Horse of the Year in 1990/91. In 1991/92 he won the Hong Kong Invitation C., Gold C. and QEII C. - Horse of the Year again. Only 2 wins in 1992/93, 11th in America's Arlington Million. In 1993/94 he swept the Stewards' C., Gold C. and Champions & Chater C. - the Triple Crown - Horse of the Year once more. Winless after, he raced until the 1995/96 season and retired to Australia.
3318=The 1991 Arc winner. His rivalry with crop-mate Generous, winner of the Derby and Irish Derby, drew attention; beaten in the Irish Derby, he got revenge in the Arc. Little noticed at first, he won the Prix Greffulhe (G3) at 3, then the French Derby easily - suddenly a top horse. In the Irish Derby he was outgunned by Generous, 2nd. After a break he won the Irish Champion S., then thoroughly beat Generous in the Arc. He was the new Cartier Award's champion 3yo colt over Generous. At 4, 3rd in the Prix Ganay, then injury ended his career. Dying at 10, he left just 4 crops, incl. G1 winners Compton Admiral and Execute.
3319=A mare who won the Prix de l'Abbaye in 1993 and 1994, Cartier Horse of the Year as a sprinter. Debuting late in August at 3, temperamental, she plodded through handicaps until 4. Trying stakes at 5, she kept placing, then won the King George S. (G3) in summer, the Nunthorpe S. for her first G1, 3rd in the Sprint C., and the Abbaye by 6 lengths - a stunning margin in a sprint. Her brilliance made her Cartier Horse of the Year. At 6 she won 4 stakes but was 8th in the July C. and 10th in the Nunthorpe; then she won the Abbaye again by 5. 14th in the BC Sprint in America. Not Horse of the Year this time, she was Cartier champion sprinter 2 years running, and retired to breed; of 8 foals, none won a stakes.
3320=The first filly to win the Canadian Triple Crown and the first Canadian-bred to win the BC Distaff - a legend in Canada. At 2 she won 3 of 5 and was 3rd in the BC Juvenile Fillies, Sovereign champion 2yo filly. At 3 she swept the fillies' races - Star Shoot S., Selene S., Canadian Oaks - then took on the colts: the Queen's Plate by 8 lengths, the Prince of Wales S. by 2 and the Breeders' S. by 8 - the Triple Crown. She also won the BC Distaff in the US; unbeaten that year, she was Sovereign Horse of the Year and Eclipse champion 3yo filly. Injured at 4, 1 win from 4, she retired. A fine broodmare: Canadian G1 winners Scatter the Gold and Dancethruthedawn, and sire Dance Brightly.
3321=Known as the sire of Satono Crown, also of French Oaks winner Sil Sila and Prix Vermeille winner My Emma. His half-sister Salsabil won the Oaks and Irish Derby. 2nd to Generous in the Derby, he won the St. James's Palace S. for his first G1, then was unplaced in the Eclipse S. and Champion S. and retired. His first crop's Sil Sila and My Emma made him a hot sire; later came Hong Kong Vase winner Indigenous, Sussex S. winner Soviet Song and Irish 1000 Guineas winner Bethrah, and from his final crop (born 2012) Satono Crown, making him known in Japan. He died in 2016.
3322=A key sire carrying the Bold Ruler line in a North America dominated by the Mr. Prospector and Northern Dancer lines, building it steadily with his sire Seattle Slew. Brilliant on the track, he won the Hollywood Futurity (G1) at 2 and the Santa Anita Derby at 3. A minor injury kept him out of the Kentucky Derby and Preakness, but he won the Belmont, then the BC Classic by 2 lengths - Eclipse Horse of the Year - and retired. His first crop had G1 winners Royal Indy and Runup the Colors; he was North American champion sire in 2003 and 2006, siring nearly 150 stakes winners. As broodmare sire: Super Saver, Mystic Guide and more - a great modern sire.
3323=A great US miler who won the BC Mile in 1992 and 1993. Starting on dirt, he won the Gotham S. (G3) early at 3, then stalled. Switched to turf in autumn at 3, he won an allowance by 10 lengths, was 2nd in a G3, and won the BC Mile in record time. At 4 he won 6 of 8 with 2 seconds and won the BC Mile again. At 5 he won the Caesars International H. for a 3rd G1, but bidding for an unprecedented 3rd BC Mile he was unplaced for the first time since his dirt days, and retired without an Eclipse Award. With low fertility he left few foals, but France's Prix Morny winner Orpen succeeded at stud.
3324=Winner of the 1992 Oaks and Irish Oaks, and favorite in that year's 12th Japan Cup - well known to Japanese fans. Debuting late in April at 3, she won 3 straight to the Oaks, then the Irish Oaks and Yorkshire Oaks, and was St Leger favorite over the colts. Favorite in the Arc too, she was headed near the line by Subotica, 2nd. Favorite in the Japan Cup, 6th to Tokai Teio, she was still Cartier Horse of the Year. At 4 she won only the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud from 5, 22nd in the Arc. Moving to the US at 5, she won an allowance, then faded and retired. A poor broodmare, with just one G3-winning filly.
3325=A notable Gone West sire son, whose first crop included G1 winner Xaar; in Japan he sired graded winners Zacharia and Rusel. Debuting in France in 1992, he won 4 straight unbeaten incl. 3 G1s - Prix Morny, Prix de la Salamandre and Dewhurst S. - Cartier champion 2yo colt. At 3, a close 2nd in a listed race, he won the 2000 Guineas by a wide margin in record time. Expected to dominate the miles, he was injured and 7th in the Sussex S., and retired. He was rated 130 in the International Classification. Besides Xaar, he sired Prix de l'Opera winner Zee Zee Top and St. James's Palace S. winner Zafeen.
3326=In 1993 she became the 8th New York Filly Triple Crown (Triple Tiara) winner. Temperamental, she was below par on the road, but unbeatable at home in New York. Debuting in July at 2, she won 3 straight incl. a G2; first past the post in the Spinaway S. (G1), she was demoted to 3rd for interference, then won the Matron S. for her first G1. At 3, after a G2 2nd, she won the Acorn S., Mother Goose S. and CCA Oaks, then the Alabama S. and Rare Perfume S. - 5 straight - but was 5th away in the BC Distaff. 5 wins from 7 with 4 G1s, but no Eclipse. At 4 she won 5 straight with 4 G1s; 9th in the BC Distaff, but Eclipse champion older mare. At 5 she won a G3 and retired injured. Bred to Storm Cat, Deputy Minister and others, she produced only one G3 winner.
3327=A great German sire, 4 times champion sire in Germany, siring Shirocco, the first German-bred BC winner, and many more at home and abroad. On the track he battled crop-mate Lando, winner of the 15th Japan Cup; 2-3 head to head, but he was German Horse of the Year at 3 over Lando. Racing until summer at 5, he won 12 of 23 incl. 3 G1s. His first crop included German Derby winner Samum, and he was champion sire by his 4th year. BC Turf winner Shirocco, Manduro, Getaway and over 100 stakes winners followed - far ahead of rival Lando at stud.
3328=She won the 1995 BC Distaff by 13.5 lengths, Eclipse champion older mare that year. Winning her debut at 2, at 3 she won 5 straight incl. 2 G1s, the Ashland S. and Acorn S.; 3rd in the Mother Goose S., she began another winning run. At 4 she won 5 straight incl. the Shuvee H. (G1); after one 2nd, the Ruffian H. and Spinster S. (G1s). In the BC Distaff, billed against stablemate and 8-time G1 winner Heavenly Prize and younger 8-time G1 winner Serena's Song, she left them nowhere, winning by a record 13.5 lengths, and retired. Many of her foals died young, but her daughter Smuggler won the Mother Goose S. and CCA Oaks.
