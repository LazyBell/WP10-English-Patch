3422=The 1982 Derby winner. Unbeaten in a brief career of 4, he was called by jockey Pat Eddery "the best horse I've ridden." Winning his debut easily in September at 2, he rested; at 3 he won the Ballymoss S. (G2) and Nijinsky S. (G2), a classic fancy. In the Derby he raced at the back, swung wide in the straight and produced an eye-opening finish, breaking a record that had stood half a century. Training problems ended his career after the Derby; just 3 wins that year, yet rated Europe's best at 134. He died young after one crop, which included G1 winner Pixie Erin.
3423=A Mr. Prospector sire son who, despite high hopes, sired few G1 winners but a steady stream of stakes winners. His best was Hollywood Gold C. (G1) winner Marquetry, whose sons Squirtle Squirt and Artax carry on the line. A brilliant racehorse, he was 1982 Eclipse Horse of the Year at 3 for 2 crushing G1 wins. After losing his opener, he won 3 allowances easily, then the Metropolitan H. against older horses by 7 lengths in record time. 5 days later he won the Belmont S. by 14 lengths. He won 2 more stakes, then hurt a leg and was 3rd in the Travers S. (G1), and retired.
3424=A leading sire son of Grey Sovereign-line Caro, siring many G1 winners like Star of Cozzene and Alphabet Soup; North American champion sire in 1996. In Japan he is known through Yasuda Kinen winner Admire Cozzene and Oaks winner Robe Decollete. A top miler, he won the 1985 BC Mile (G1). Debuting in May at 3 on dirt and often just missing, he switched to turf in summer at 4: 2nd United Nations H., 3rd Man o' War S., 3rd BC Mile. At 5 he won the Oceanport H. (G3), then his 2nd BC Mile in record time - Eclipse champion turf male - and retired.
3425=A speedster with his sire Mr. Prospector's pace, winner of the first BC Sprint (G1) in 1984. Sprinting on dirt from his debut at 3, he won his first 4 brilliantly. After 3 losses, at 4 he won 4 straight by 3 lengths (in record time), 7, 3 and 6. Last on his first turf start, a mile, he returned to dirt sprints and won 3 more. Favorite in the inaugural BC Sprint, he led and held on by a nose - his first stakes win a G1. Sadly he then fell gravely ill and died, passing on none of his speed.
3426=The 1984 French Oaks winner, from Northern Dancer's famed crop with Sadler's Wells, El Gran Senor and Secreto. Debuting late in April at 3, she won easily, then was 2nd in the Prix Saint-Alary (G1). 2nd choice in the French Oaks, she beat Saint-Alary winner Grise Mine. Back in autumn, she won the Prix de la Nonette (G3) and Prix Vermeille (G1) over Oaks winner Circus Plume. In the Arc she was 2nd, 2 lengths behind Sagace, ahead of All Along and Sadler's Wells. She retired to breed; no stars of her own, but descendants Shiva and Main Sequence extended her female line.
3427=Winner of the 1985 Eclipse S., Champion S. and BC Turf - hailed as Britain's greatest ever filly for beating the colts. Debuting in England in May at 2, she won her 2nd start, 2 wins from 6. At 3 she won the Nell Gwyn S. (G3) and the 1000 Guineas by 3 lengths, then was 2nd in the Coronation S. (G2) and Champion S. (G1). At 4 she blossomed: in the Eclipse S., Britain's top middle-distance race, she beat Rainbow Quest and others, the first filly to win it; then the Champion S. easily over Derby winner Slip Anchor and previous winner Palace Music; then the BC Turf in America as favorite, on her first US start and first 12f race. British Horse of the Year and Eclipse champion turf female, rated 135 by Timeform, second only to Slip Anchor's 136. Retired to breed, she produced nothing notable; later imported to Japan, she had no success there either.
3428=Winner of the 1984 Kentucky Derby and Belmont S. Debuting in July at 2 and winning his 2nd start, he won the Saratoga Special S. (G2), then the Futurity S. and Young America S. (G1s), but crop-mate Devil's Bag, "the second Secretariat," kept him in 2nd place. At 3 he won the Hutcheson S. (G3) and Florida Derby (G1); with favorite Devil's Bag retired just before, the Kentucky Derby was wide open, and he won by 3 1/4 lengths. Flat in the Preakness, 7th, he won the Belmont by 4 lengths - top of his crop. Just 8 days later he died suddenly. He never went to stud, but was Eclipse champion 3yo and helped his sire Seattle Slew to the North American sire title.
3429=Invincible in (then West) Germany from 1985 to 1987, and 5 times German champion sire. Modest at 2, 1 win from 5, he awoke at 3, winning from the start of the year incl. the German Derby and Aral-Pokal - unbeaten in 6, German Horse of the Year. At 4 he won France's Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud and 3 German G1s - Grosser Preis von Berlin, Aral-Pokal, Grosser Preis von Baden - unbeatable. In an Arc of historic strength with Dancing Brave and Shahrastani, he was 7th, but Horse of the Year again. At 5 he was 3rd in England's Coronation C. and 6th in the King George; he won the Grosser Preis von Baden, Horse of the Year for a 3rd year, and retired. At stud he sired Japan Cup winner Lando, German Derby winner Borgia and many more - still one of Germany's great sires.
3430=An Irish horse who moved to the US at 4 and blossomed; in 1987 at 5 he won 6 G1s incl. the BC Turf - Eclipse champion turf male. Unbeaten in 3 from October at 2, he was 7th in the Derby and 2nd in the Irish Derby, then kept losing. Moved to the US in summer at 4, he was 2nd in the Oak Tree Invitational H. (G1) and BC Turf. At 5 he won the Hialeah Turf C., his first G1, then 4 straight G2s and G1s; 3rd in the Arlington Million, he won the Turf Classic S., Man o' War S. and BC Turf - America's turf champion - and retired. A great sire too, with Zagreb, Royal Anthem and many G1 winners; in Japan, sire of Hishi Amazon and Taiki Eldorado.
3431=A gelding who dominated Australia and New Zealand in 1986-1987, known in Japan from 2 Japan Cup trips. Modest at 2, he improved at 3, winning 7 straight incl. the New Zealand Derby. At 4 he won 3 straight Australian G1s to the Cox Plate; a Japan Cup fancy, an infection ruled him out. He won the Australian C. (G1) back home - Horse of the Year in both countries. Resting much of 5, he won only the Air New Zealand S. (G1). At 6, 3rd in the Cox Plate, he tried the Japan Cup again but, past his peak, was 8th behind the Pay the Butler-Tamamo Cross duel. Winless after, he retired at 7 and later entered New Zealand's racing Hall of Fame.
3432=Winner of the 1986 Derby and Irish Derby - in the Derby he beat Dancing Brave, the only horse to beat him in Europe. Unraced after a 2nd on his only start at 2, at 3 he won the Classic Trial S. (G3) and Dante S. (G2), becoming a Derby fancy. With Dancing Brave hot favorite, he broke clear early from up front and held off Dancing Brave's charge from last by half a length. He won the Irish Derby by 8 lengths as favorite, but was 4th to Dancing Brave in the King George and in the Arc, outclassed. He retired to stud in the US; poor as a sire, he moved to Ireland and Japan, siring only a few graded winners.
3433=A US star of 1986-1987, winner of 3 G1s - Kentucky Derby, Hollywood Gold C. and BC Classic - and Eclipse Horse of the Year, but a poor sire; reports that he was slaughtered in Japan caused outrage. Debuting in September at 2, he won his 4th start, overshadowed by rival Snow Chief. At 3 he struck back in the Kentucky Derby as 9th choice, with a powerful finish for his first G1. 2nd in the Preakness, 3rd in the Belmont; champion 3yo went to 3-time G1 winner Snow Chief. At 4, after near-misses, he won the Hollywood Gold C., then 4 straight incl. the BC Classic - Horse of the Year. Winless in 6 at 5, he retired. Siring few stakes winners, he was imported to Japan in 1994; still unsuccessful, he was disposed of (slaughtered) after his 2002 season, as later investigation revealed.
3434=She won the 1987 French Oaks (G1) easily, beating great filly Miesque, and won 5 G1s in all incl. the Phoenix Champion S. and Champion S. - one of the great mares of the 1980s. First winning in spring at 3, she won 4 straight to the Prix Saint-Alary (G1), then the French Oaks by 4 lengths from English and French 1000 Guineas winner Miesque. After about a year off, at 4 she won the Phoenix Champion S. (now Irish Champion S.) and the Champion S. - only the 2nd to win both. 3rd in the BC Turf in America. At 5 she won the Prix d'Ispahan for a 5th G1, and retired after a 3rd in the Eclipse S. Not a successful broodmare; her colt Manshood, unraced, went to stud on pedigree.
3435=A late bloomer who in 1990 at 5 won 4 US G1s incl. the Metropolitan H. and Hollywood Gold C. Debuting in August at 2 and winning his 2nd start, he then lost for 2 years until autumn at 4. After a 3rd win that December, he took off: 4 straight incl. 2 G2s, the Pimlico Special H. for his first G1, then the Metropolitan H., Hollywood Gold C. and Whitney H. - beating Easy Goer in the Metropolitan and Sunday Silence in the Gold Cup, the best in training. 6th in the Woodward S., he retired injured - Eclipse Horse of the Year. At stud in the US, his farm went bankrupt after one season and he was imported to Japan, siring local graded winners like Onward Meteor and Sakino Tenryu.
