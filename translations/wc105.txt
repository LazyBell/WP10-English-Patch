2571=Her grand-dam was 1997 2-classic filly Cheers Grace; she was the family's first graded winner. Struggling in allowances, she blossomed mid-4, passing 3 classes in a row. Beaten badly in open class, she won the Breeders' Gold C. in her 4th try, leading early in the straight and holding off favorite Queen Mambo.
2572=Winning 3 classes in a row in summer at 4, he won the Kitakyushu Kinen, tracking in 2nd from a fine start and breaking clear - 4 straight wins and his first graded win, with longshots 2nd and 3rd for a trifecta over 1 million yen. 4th choice in the Sprinters S., he faded to 15th. At 5 he won the Hankyu Hai. After an 18th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 9th in the Hakodate Sprint S., he was 3rd as 11th choice in the Keihan Hai, then led all the way on heavy ground in the Hanshin C. as 11th choice, a 3rd graded win. At 6, 6th defending the Hankyu Hai and 4th in the Hanshin Himba S., he retired.
2573=Without a graded win, she won the G1 Victoria Mile as 8th choice - a finish that works on testing ground. Her first graded race came when older: in the Hanshin Himba S. on heavy going she held 3rd from up front, well clear of 4th behind Mikki Queen. In the Victoria Mile on soft ground she held 3rd again; as favorites Mikki Queen and Rouge Buck sank, 3 longshots filled the places (trifecta 910,000 yen). A year later, in another rain-softened Victoria Mile, she won a straight duel with Red Avance from midfield and held off favorite Lys Gracieux by a nose - her first graded win a G1. 6th against males in the Mile CS.
2574=3rd in the Fantasy S. at 2, talented early. 2nd in the Anemone S. (OP) at 3, she was 6th in the Oka Sho as 14th choice, closing fastest from the back, then 4th in the Oaks as 9th choice, again fastest. In autumn she won a 10-million race and the Shion S., then the Shuka Sho as 3rd choice with an otherworldly finish from the back - a G1. Older, 3rd in the Dubai Turf, she won the Queen S. and Fuchu Himba S., and was 2nd in the Hong Kong Cup. At 5 she toured: 4th in the Dubai Turf, 6th in Hong Kong's QEII Cup, 6th in England's Prince of Wales's S., then won the Nassau S., 4th in the Irish Champion S. and 3rd in the Champion S. In Hong Kong's Vase she closed too late, 4th. With Mozu Katchan and Persian Knight, a flagship of import sire Harbinger.
2575=A summer improver who won a 5-million race at 3, then the Rose S. as 8th choice, a Shuka Sho fancy. A sharp closer, she swept past Satsuki Sho runner Fandina and Oaks runner-up Mozu Katchan in the Rose S., then was 4th in the Shuka Sho. Struggling at 4, she soared after switching to dirt in summer: 3rd in the Sparking Lady C., then the Breeders' Gold C., and 2nd as favorite in the G1 JBC Ladies' Classic.
2576=Winner of the Kanto Oaks and Ladies' Prelude, a force among dirt fillies. Winning 2 to open class in spring at 3, she was 3rd in the Hyogo Championship, then won the Kanto Oaks as favorite by 4 lengths. 12th on turf in the Rose S., she won the Ladies' Prelude on dirt by 8 lengths. At 4 she was 2nd in the Marine C. and Heian S., 3rd in the Breeders' Gold C., and 4th as 2nd choice, a length behind Ange Desir, in the JBC Ladies' Classic.
2577=Very strong in fillies' dirt races: the JBC Ladies' Classic and 3 graded wins. On turf at first, she switched to dirt after a 13th in the Fillies' Revue at 3 and was 2nd at once in the Kanto Oaks. She won the Sparking Lady C. as 2nd choice from a handy spot, then was 3rd in the Ladies' Prelude and 2nd in the Queen Sho. At 4 she timed her run perfectly to win the Empress Hai, then led for the first time to win the Marine C. 4th in the Ladies' Prelude, she won the JBC Ladies' Classic as 6th choice by a head after a straight duel with favorite Rabbit Run.
2578=Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Asahi Hai FS, a leader of the 2017 2yo crop. He won his debut easily from a handy spot, then after 3.5 months off won the Saudi Arabia Royal C., 2nd choice behind Lemaire's Stelvio. Favorite in the Asahi Hai FS, from the rail he raced forward and closed fastest to beat Stelvio by 3.5 lengths in 1:33.3, the fastest since the race moved to Hanshin in 2014. Top classic fancy, he won the Yayoi Sho, then missed the Satsuki Sho with leg trouble. Hot Derby favorite over Satsuki winner Epoca d'Oro and unbeaten Blast Onepiece, he couldn't hold on from up front, 6th. Back at 4 in the Kinko Sho against G1 winners Al Ain, Persian Knight and Lys Gracieux, he won easily, then the Milers C. In the Yasuda Kinen, billed vs. Triple Crown filly Almond Eye, trouble left her 3rd and him last. He was 2nd to her in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 2nd in the Mile CS. At 5, 3rd in Hong Kong's QE S., 13th in the Yasuda Kinen, 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), he was 4th in the Hong Kong Cup as fellow Japanese Normcore and Win Bright ran 1-2.
2579=He won the Hakodate 2yo S. as favorite, a 2017 2yo name. Ridden by Hamanaka from his debut, he won a maiden easily, then caught leader Win Gelbera from a handy spot in the Hakodate 2yo S. 2nd to Tower of London in the Keio Hai 2yo S., he raced forward like Danon Premium in the Asahi Hai FS but faded, perhaps at his limit, 7th.
2580=US-bred, by Stormy Atlantic. Winning his 3rd start by a distance, he won the Kokura 2yo S. under Yutaka Take, sitting 2nd-3rd and breaking clear early. 3rd to Tower of London in the Keio Hai 2yo S. He gave his sire a first graded win.
2581=The 2018 Derby winner, who gave Yuichi Fukunaga his long-sought Derby. Highly rated, he won his debut, the Nojigiku S. (OP) and Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. At 3 he was 2nd to 2yo champion Danon Premium in the Yayoi Sho; with Danon Premium out, he was Satsuki Sho favorite but was outmaneuvered by the leaders and closed in vain, 7th. With Danon Premium back for the Derby, he was 5th choice, but moved up early, broke clear and held off Satsuki winner Epoca d'Oro by half a length - Fukunaga's bold ride paid off. He won the Kobe Shimbun Hai, then rested, unwell, instead of the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 4 he was 3rd in the Osaka Hai, 4th in the Sapporo Kinen, 5th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 3rd in the Japan Cup. At 5 he raced only twice; at 6, 5th in the Kyoto Kinen, then 12th, 6th and last (18th) in the Japan Cup. Falling ill, he was treated intensively but died suddenly of multiple organ failure at the end of the year.
2582=Brother of Derby and Tenno Sho (Autumn) winner Rey de Oro, said to be even more gifted. He won his debut at 1.2, but a fracture followed and a long break. Back in May at 3, he won 2 classes and was 2nd in his first graded race, the St. Lite Kinen. After a dry spell he won the Epsom C. at 4, then closed powerfully for 2nd in the Fuji S., but was 15th in the Mile CS. 3rd in the Lord Derby CT, then beaten badly, he retired.
2583=From Lord Kanaloa's first crop, his first male G1 winner. 2nd to Danon Premium in the Saudi Arabia Royal C. and Asahi Hai FS, he was among his crop's best. At 3 he won the Spring S.; 2nd choice in the Satsuki Sho, he couldn't catch the leaders, 4th, then 8th in the Derby, ending his classic bid. Against older horses he was 2nd in the Mainichi Okan with a sharp finish from midfield. In the Mile CS, with Yasuda Kinen winner Mozu Ascot, Mainichi Okan winner Aerolithe and defending champion Persian Knight, he was 5th choice, best of the 3yos, broke clear from up front and held off Persian Knight. Later 3rd in the Nakayama Kinen at 4 and 2nd in the Keio Hai SC and Swan S. at 5, he retired injured after the Kyoto Kimpai at 7; meant to be a riding horse, he later went to stud.
2584=A new dirt star who, under Mirco Demuro, won 3 straight G1s: Japan Dirt Derby, Mile CS Nambu Hai and Champions C. He won his debut easily as favorite, the Platanus Sho at 1.4, and, unbeaten, the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun at 1.8, moving up at the 4th turn and winning without the whip. Favorite at 1.9 in the Champions C., he won easily, following Almond Eye's Japan Cup the week before - the 3yo crop's strength. Leg trouble kept him out all of 2019; back in spring 2020, 5th in the Kashiwa Kinen and 10th in the Teio Sho, he retired.

At 5, 3rd in the Ocean S., he was 12th as favorite in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 8th as favorite in the Keio Hai SC; in the Hong Kong Sprint, as Danon Smash won brilliantly, he was 13th of 14.

2587=A hope from Orfevre's first crop, she won the Osaka Hai and became the 4th to win the QEII Cup twice, giving her sire big titles.
