2610=A local horse owned by singer Saburo Kitajima (via Ono Shoji) who won the Tokyo Hai. Debuting in the JRA, he reached open class only at 6; after the Keiyo S. (OP) he kept losing, and moved to Oi at 7. He at once won the After 5 Star Sho, then the Tokyo Hai as 7th choice, and was 5th in his first G1, the JBC Sprint. At 8 he improved further: 2nd in the Tokyo Sprint and Sakitama Hai, 3 straight wins incl. the Tokyo Hai, 3rd in the JBC Sprint. At 9, 9th in the Negishi S., he won the Tokyo Sprint, was 5th in the Kashiwa Kinen and 3rd in the Sakitama Hai, and retired after winning the After 5 Star Sho.
2611=A Mombetsu filly who won the Edelweiss Sho. Winning her debut in summer at 2 and the local Lily Cup, she was narrowly 2nd in the Floral Cup, then won the Edelweiss Sho, tracking in 3rd, spurting early and winning a duel with fellow Hokkaido filly Graviola, far clear of the rest.
2612=An Oi dirt sprinter who raced almost only at Oi; his first graded win was a G1, the JBC Sprint. 4th as a longshot in the Tokyo Sprint at 4, 2nd in it 2 years later, he was 5th in the Tokyo Hai, then won the JBC Sprint as 8th choice with a sharp finish from midfield. In his first JRA race, the Capella S., a crushing 59kg left him 8th.
2613=Reaching open class in January at 4, he then had a long road: 5th Meguro Kinen, 4th Argentina Kyowakoku Hai, 3rd Stayers S. - close but no win, except the Sapporo Nikkei OP at 5. Fading from 6, late at 7 he won the Stayers S. as 6th choice, beating 2015-2017 winner Albert from a handy spot.
2614=By Spiecule, a minority Sunday Silence-line sire with 8 crops, in Korea since 2014 - his leading JRA-local graded winner. Winning allowances in autumn at 4, she was 2nd in the G1 JBC Ladies' Classic, then led all the way in the Queen Sho at 2.0. She won the Ladies' Prelude at 5 and Empress Hai at 6, and at 7 the Breeders' Gold C. and 3rd in the Ladies' Prelude.
2615=He won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai late at 4. 3rd in the Keisei Hai and Kyodo News Hai early, out of the top three only twice in his career, a graded win was expected, but he was 3rd as favorite in the Niigata Daishoten and 12th as favorite in the Sekiya Kinen. In the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, 2nd choice, he led 200m out from midfield and held off favorite Mikki Rocket.
2616=Emerging early, 2nd in the Yayoi Sho, 16th in the Satsuki Sho, he was 4th as 14th choice in the Derby. Winless for a while despite 2nds in the Fukushima Kinen and Kyoto Kimpai, he won the Hakodate Kinen in summer at 5, leading all the way under veteran Katsuharu Tanaka. Dropping in trip, he was 3rd in the Swan S. and 4th in the G1 Mile CS, then far back in the Hanshin C., Hankyu Hai and Lord Derby CT. After a break, 4th in the Lord Derby CT and Sekiya Kinen at 7, he retired after the Silk Road S. at 8.
2617=Winning a 5-million race at 3, she was 2nd in her first graded race, the Leopard S., then 3rd in the Empress Hai, 2nd in the Queen Sho and Sparking Lady C. Moving to Oi at 6, she led all the way to win the Marine C. by 2 lengths - a graded winner at last - and the Queen Sho. At 7 she won 3 straight: Sparking Lady C., Sparking Summer C. and Nippon TV Hai, but was 8th in the Kashiwa Kinen and 10th in the JBC Ladies' Classic. At 8 she won the Sakitama Hai, 2nd in the Empress Hai and Marine C.
2618=He won his debut easily, then was 2nd in the Koyamaki Sho and Kisaragi Sho. Fragile, he raced 4 times at 3, winning only a 16-million race. Stronger at 4, he won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai as favorite from midfield, then was 2nd to Fierement in the Tenno Sho (Spring). 6th as favorite in the Kyoto Daishoten, he skipped Japan's G1s and won the Hong Kong Vase, his first G1. At 5, 17th in the Takarazuka Kinen, his first heavy defeat, he won the Kyoto Daishoten, then was 5th, close behind 3 Triple Crown winners, in the Japan Cup. At 6, 4th in the Kinko Sho, he was 2nd to Loves Only You in Hong Kong's QEII Cup, Japanese runners 1st-4th; 3rd in the All Comers, he won the Hong Kong Vase again. At 7, 8th in the Dubai Sheema Classic, 6th in the Sapporo Kinen, 3rd defending the Vase, he retired to stud.
2619=A spring 3yo hope, 4th in the Kyodo News Hai and 7th in the Spring S., he was 6th choice in the Aoba Sho and finally showed his class, bursting clear from a handy spot and beating Etario by 2 lengths. 7th in the Derby, he was unplaced in the Sapporo Kinen and Kobe Shimbun Hai. Older, often top five but never top three, he retired after the Fukushima Minpo Hai at 7.
2620=The first graded winner by Scat Daddy, of the Storm Bird line. After dirt wins in his debut and a 5-million race, his first open race was the turf Falcon S.; 3rd choice, he burst clear from a handy spot. 7th choice in the NHK Mile C., he held 4th from 2nd. At 1200-1400m he was 2nd in the Hanshin C. late at 3. 7th as favorite in the Hankyu Hai at 4, he won the Takamatsunomiya Kinen as 3rd choice behind Danon Smash and Mozu Superflare, breaking clear early from 4th-5th and winning by half a length. 4th in the Sprinters S., he was 6th as favorite on dirt in the JBC Sprint. At 5 he was 11th in the Yasuda Kinen, 4th in the Sprinters S. and 12th in the JBC Sprint, and retired to stud, carrying a rare line.
2621=Out of famed sprinter Believe, bred in America to top sires each year - her first graded-winning foal. After an easy debut win, he won the Daily Hai 2yo S. under Atzeni, replacing the injured Yutaka Take, with a perfect late run - unbeaten in 2. With Take back, he was 2nd to Time Flyer in the Hopeful S. At 3 he faded: 3rd in the Yayoi Sho, 9th in the Satsuki Sho, 17th in the Derby. On shorter trips he won only listed races at 5 and 6, then at 7 won the Ocean S., and after defeats in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and Kitakyushu Kinen, won the Sprinters S. as 8th choice by a neck in a blanket finish - a G1, and a mother-son double with Believe. After a 12th in the Hong Kong Sprint he retired to stud.
2622=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Fantasy S. Slow away on debut, she won from midfield with a brilliant kick, praised by jockey Moreira. In the Fantasy S. she broke well, waited at the back, then charged wide with a finish from another dimension. A fracture found after ruled out the Hanshin JF.
2623=After a long road, he won his first graded race, the Niigata Daishoten, at 7. 4th in the Stayers S. late at 3 and 2nd in the Kyoto Kinen, he slumped long, improving only at 6: 3rd in the Kinko Sho, 4th in the Naruo Kinen. In the Niigata Daishoten at 7, under a top weight, he closed sharply from midfield to win.
2624=In open class when older, he raced in mile graded races like the Fuji S., Kyoto Kimpai and Tokyo Shimbun Hai, and was Chukyo Kinen favorite at 5 (3rd to Win Ganion). At 6 he won the Kyoto Kimpai from the back past fancied Red Ansel and Kruger. Mostly unplaced after, incl. G1s, he switched to jumps at 9.
2625=Often top five as a longshot in fillies' graded races - "her day will come." 4th choice in the Turquoise S. late at 4, she was a close 5th to Miss Panthere, then won the Aichi Hai at 5 from a handy spot. Far back in the Victoria Mile and QEII Cup.
2626=Winning a maiden in his 2nd start, then 4th and 2nd in 5-million races, he was gelded. Back, he rose quickly, 5th to Satono Arthur in his first open race, the Rakuyo S., then won his first graded race, the Keio Hai SC, as 4th choice after a straight duel with Campbell Junior and Satono Ares.
2627=2nd in the Daily Hai 2yo S. and 5th in the Kisaragi Sho, he won the NZT by a head over favorite K A Knotech. Overlooked as 8th choice in the NHK Mile C., NZT winners having flopped lately, he was 8th. 4th as 16th choice in the Mile CS, mostly unplaced after, he was forgotten - then in the next Swan S. he led for once and held off Admire Mars, Stelvio and others, a stunning 2nd graded win. 17th in the Mile CS, 5th in the Hakodate Sprint S. the next year, he went to jumps, then became a riding horse.
2628=Emerging early, 3rd in the Daily Hai 2yo S. and 4th in the Asahi Hai FS, he was NZT favorite at 3 but was caught by Katsuji's charge by a head. In the NHK Mile C., with Tower of London and Gibeon fancied and NZT horses overlooked, he raced last, swung wide in the straight and accelerated furiously 200m out to pass Gibeon and Red Veyron - a G1. Autumn disappointed: 5th in the Mainichi Okan, 11th in the Mile CS, far behind Katsuji. Unplaced all of 4, he revived at 5: 4th in the Lord Derby CT, 5th as 11th choice in the Yasuda Kinen, 3rd in the Fuji S.
2629=He faced Dee Majesty in the Satsuki Sho and Satono Diamond in the Kikuka Sho, racing until 7. At 4, 3rd in the AJCC and 2nd in the Nikkei Sho, he then slumped with a tendon injury. Near retirement at 7, as last choice of 16 in the Diamond S., he won by a nose over Meisho Tengen - also his sire Dream Journey's first graded winner. After the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Meguro Kinen, the tendon injury recurred and he retired.
2630=A dirt sprinter who won the Capella S. late at 3. In the UAE in spring at 3, he was 7th in the Mahab Al Shimaal (G3) and 11th in the Dubai Golden Shaheen. Restarting in allowances, he won a 16-million race in November, then the Capella S. as 4th choice, breaking clear from midfield and holding off 9yo Snow Dragon.
2631=A filly who beat later Kikuka Sho runner-up Etario in a 5-million race in spring at 3, then won the Flora S. as favorite, charging from the back with a finish from another dimension past Normcore and Pioneer Bio. 6th as 3rd choice in the Oaks, and 6th as favorite in the Rose S.
