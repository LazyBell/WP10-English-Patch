904=An Australian sprinter, winner of 7 G1s from 2004 to 2009 - a gelding who came to Japan in 2006 and won the Sprinters S. Debuting late, in April at 4, he won 7 straight to his first G1. In 2006, at 6-7, he contested the Global Sprint Challenge: he won the Lightning S. at home, then the King's Stand S. He was bought for just A$1,250 (about 110,000 yen) by owner-trainer Joe Janiak, who worked as a baker in the morning, trainer by day and taxi driver at night to make ends meet; the horse's big earnings let him become a full-time trainer.
