2730=In open class only in autumn at 5, he was 2nd in the Muromachi S. (OP) and 5th as 10th choice in the Capella S. He won the Polaris S. and Tempozan S. (both OP), was 3rd in the Procyon S., then won the Miyako S. as 7th choice, sweeping early from the back to lead.
2731=From the first crop of Kizuna (at stud from 2015). A neck 2nd on debut, he won a maiden as favorite, then led all the way in the Hakodate 2yo S., his sire's first graded win. 2nd in the Keio Hai 2yo S., 7th in the Asahi Hai FS. A sprinter at 3, he won the Aoi S., then 5th in the Centaur S. and 16th in the Sprinters S. His fiery temper sometimes cost him, so he was gelded late at 3. At 4, 3rd to Contra Check in the Ocean S., he won the Hakodate Sprint S., leading and just holding the late surge. After unplaced runs, 3rd again in the Ocean S., then last (16th) twice, he retired.
2732=From the now-rare Northern Dancer male line, best son of Turtle Bowl. A debut winner, 2nd in the Hakodate 2yo S., he won the Keio Hai 2yo S., was 2nd to Salios in the Asahi Hai FS, and won the Arlington C. at 3. Racing handy for once as 2nd choice in the NHK Mile C., he was 4th; 5th in the Fuji S., then 14th in the Mile CS, his first heavy defeat. At 5, 4th in the Kyoto Kimpai and CBC Sho, 12th in the Sprinters S., he was 2nd in the Keihan Hai.
2733=From grand-dam Shirayuki Hime's white-coated family, the first white horse to win a JRA graded race, and a graded winner on both turf and dirt. A turf maiden winner, 8th in the Ivy S. (OP), he switched to dirt, winning a 5-million race in spring at 3, then won his first graded race, the Leopard S. (G3), as 10th choice, catching favorite Dell Mar Louvre. At 4, 2nd in the Pollux S. and Sobu S. (both OP), he swept from the back to win the Brazil C. (L), and at 5 the Sleipnir S. (OP). Back on turf at 6, 5th as 13th choice in the Nikkei Sho (G2), 15th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he won the Hakodate Kinen (G3) - JRA graded wins on turf and dirt. Over 2 winless years later, at 8, he won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai (G2) as 10th choice under top weight 58.5kg - sheer grit. Injured in the Meguro Kinen (G2) at 9, he retired.

2737=Winless at 3 - 5th Flora S., 16th Oaks, 2nd Shion S., 16th Shuka Sho - she blossomed older: 4th in the Aichi Hai, then the Nakayama Himba S. as 3rd choice behind crop-mates Espoir and Contra Check, and the Fukushima Himba S. 2740=A new dirt star of 2019 who shot to the top. Full brother of Japan Dirt Derby winner Chrysolite, with siblings like Marialite and Liar Fail. He won his debut and a 5-million race as favorite, the Hyogo Championship at 1.1 and the Japan Dirt Derby at 1.2 for a G1. After the Nippon TV Hai, all eyes were on how the 3yo would fare against Gold Dream and February S. winner Inti in the Champions C. - he raced handy, went toe to toe and held off Gold Dream: dirt champion. At 4 he lost for the first time in the Saudi Cup, then won the Teio Sho and JBC Classic, unbeatable at home. At 1.4 in the Champions C., he lacked his kick and was 4th behind Chuwa Wizard and others he had beaten - his first home defeat. Injured, back in autumn at 5, he was 6th in the Nippon TV Hai and retired with leg trouble.
