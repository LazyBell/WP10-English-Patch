2547=A 2017 3yo dirt force. Last (16th) in a turf debut, he switched to dirt and was nearly always top two, winning through the classes. In his first open race, the Hyogo Championship, he burst clear from a handy spot, then was 3rd in the G1 Japan Dirt Derby. Perhaps tired, he was 12th in the Leopard S., 4th in the Hakusan Daishoten and 11th in the Miyako S.
2548=Unable to pass the 10-million class with the JRA by 5, he blossomed after moving to Hyogo: 4 quick wins, 6th in the Kurofune Sho, the Tokai Oka Sho (OP), then the Kakitsubata Kinen. 2nd choice behind hot JRA favorite Chocolat Blanc, he led all the way and pulled away with the fastest finish.
2549=Owned, like Kitasan Black, by singer Saburo Kitajima (via his office, Ono Shoji). A dirt sprinter at 1200-1400m, he won allowances only at 5, then placed in open races, and won the Tokyo Sprint as favorite, leading and holding on by a head.
2550=A Normandy Thoroughbred Racing club horse at just 19,000 yen a share - 7.6 million yen for 400 shares - who earned over 100 million yen, winning the Kurofune Sho and Valentine S. (OP). Winning allowances in summer at 4, 12th in the Musashino S., he kept placing in open races, then at 5 won the Valentine S. and Kurofune Sho, and was 5th in the G1 Kashiwa Kinen.
2551=Slow through allowances, he then surged: fresh into open class, he went straight to the G1 Kawasaki Kinen and won as 5th choice, leading all the way and pulling clear in the straight. Winless for a while, at 6 he won the Urawa Kinen by 4 lengths over 3yo Grimm, on a graded winning streak, and stars Chrysolite and Apollo Kentucky.
2552=By Gold Allure, from a dirt family - her dam's brother Testa Matta won the February S. and Japan Dirt Derby. Improving late at 3, she won 2 allowances, then the TCK Jo-o Hai and Empress Hai early at 4. In autumn she was 4th as 3rd choice in the JBC Ladies' Classic.
2553=A record-setting mare: 2 Arcs, 3 King Georges, and the Arc and BC Turf in the same year. At 3 she won the English and Irish Oaks, romped in the King George and won the Arc as favorite, Cartier Horse of the Year. Injured at 4, she returned in the G3 September S., then won a 2nd Arc (7th horse to do so) and the BC Turf - the first to win both in a year. At 5 she won the King George and bid for a 3rd Arc, but was 2nd to Waldgeist. At 6 she won a 3rd King George, then in her farewell Arc finished 6th, her first time out of the top three. Her first foal (by Kingman) came in 2022, and a filly by Dubawi in 2023.
2554=A late-blooming sprinter in open class at 7, graded winner at 9 in the Keeneland C. After 2 wins from debut, leg trouble meant a long break; gelded, he returned in summer at 5, steadily placing in allowances. From 7 he ran in the Keeneland C. 3 years running and won at the 3rd try at 9, closing brilliantly from last as 12th of 13.
2555=He won the Ibis Summer Dash at 7, then was 2nd in the Centaur S. - Summer Sprint Series champion. Long in allowances, he won the 10-million class only at 7, then stepped up from the 16-million class to win the Ibis Summer Dash as 8th choice, unleashing a powerful late surge from midfield.
2556=He won the Naruo Kinen at 6. Debuting late in spring at 3, he reached open class in just 7 starts over 3 years, always fancied. 3rd choice in the Naruo Kinen, his 2nd open race, he led as usual, saved energy, and held off favorite Smart Layer by a neck.
2557=A late-blooming middle-distance horse who won the Hakodate Kinen at 6. Winning allowances at 5 so strongly that he was 2nd choice in his first open race, the Tanabata Sho (8th), he then placed steadily: 5th Niigata Kinen, 4th AJCC, 5th Kinko Sho. In the Hakodate Kinen he crept up from midfield on the back straight, joined the leaders at the 4th turn and burst clear.
2558=Blooming at 6, he was 2nd in his first graded race, the Nakayama Kinen, ahead of favored Ambitious and Real Steel. 13th in the G1 Osaka Hai and 9th in the Hakodate Kinen, he was transformed in the Sapporo Kinen, closing sharply from midfield. He tried the Tenno Sho (Autumn), won by his uncle Sakura Chitose O, raising "Sakura" hopes, but was 8th. 3rd in the Nikkei Sho 2 years running, he never won again and retired to stud at 8.
2559=After 45 starts, he became a graded winner at 6 in the Sirius S. Though he ran in the Sapporo 2yo S. and Kikuka Sho, he was a dirt specialist. Winning the Aso S. and Radio Nippon Sho (both OP) at 5, he moved to dirt graded races but never made the top five - until the Sirius S. (G3), where as 11th choice he swept past the leaders from midfield for a stunning upset.
2560=Debuting in graded races at 5 in summer 2017, he topped the Summer Mile Series at once. After 3 allowance wins at 4 he struggled in open class (14th in the Shin'etsu S.), then won the Tanigawadake S. (OP) when out of favor, the Paradise S. (OP), and the Chukyo Kinen by 2.5 lengths - 3 straight. 2nd in the Sekiya Kinen, he shared the series title with Gran Silk.
2561=A middle-distance horse at about 2000m who races forward and holds on. Winning the 10-million class at 5, he stepped up for the Tanabata Sho (6th), then won the Kokura Kinen as 4th choice on a light weight, beating favored Strong Titan and San Martin, and the Niigata Kinen next - 2017 Summer 2000 Series champion.
2562=After 23 allowance starts, he thrived in open class: 4th in his first graded race, the Sirius S., he won the Brazil C. and Betelgeuse S. (both OP), was 4th in the G1 Kawasaki Kinen, then won the Mercury C. as favorite, catching joint fancy Pionero by a neck. At 6 he won it again, pulling away from rival Phoenix Mark. At 7 he won the Kawasaki Kinen, beating G1 winners Katy Brave, All Blush and Apollo Kentucky for his long-sought G1.
2563=He won the Summer Champion in summer at 5. It took 2.5 years to reach open class, rising steadily and almost never out of the top five. 5th in the Akhal-Teke S. (OP), he won the Summer Champion as 2nd choice from up front, holding off Tamuro Miracle.
2564=At 5 he won the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and Sprinters S., ruling the sprints. In allowances at 3-4 but always fancied, he reached open class in summer at 4 and was favorite in the Kitakyushu Kinen (5th), then won the Centaur S. At 5 he won the Silk Road S. and Takamatsunomiya Kinen, then was 4th in Hong Kong's Chairman's Sprint Prize (G1). He won the Centaur S. as favorite, then the Sprinters S. as favorite from midfield, catching Love Kampu and Line Spirit - a spring-autumn sprint G1 double.
2565=He won 3 classes early at 4 and the Ritto S. (OP) - 4 straight - then was favorite in his first graded race, the Cluster C., 3rd behind local Bulldog Boss. In the Oval Sprint he led all the way, holding off Bulldog Boss for revenge.
