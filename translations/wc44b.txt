567=At first a plain front-runner relying only on speed, she needed 6 starts to win. But once she learned to settle, she unleashed a fearsome kick, winning the Yonsai Himba Tokubetsu (East) and charging into 3rd in the Oaks. Outclassed against colts, she was astonishing when things fell right, winning the Tokyo Shimbun Hai with a furious finish. Retired in spring at 4, she produced Derby 4th Kyowa Splendor (01: Sunday Silence).
569=She won an Oka Sho shared at the top of the betting with Admire Groove, then in the Oaks, 2nd choice behind her rival, unleashed the field's fastest 33.5 finish for a double. In autumn, as heavy favorite, she was 5th in the Rose S., clouding her Triple Crown path. But in the last leg, the Shuka Sho, she won a tight duel with Admire Groove by three-quarters of a length - only the second fillies' Triple Crown winner, after Mejiro Ramonu. Going for a 4th crown in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, she ran superbly but lost to Admire Groove's grit by a nose. As an older mare she struggled, and the Shuka Sho was her last win. She died in August '07, leaving just one colt (by King Kamehameha), Judah. He debuted as favorite in March '10 but was 4th; he won twice after moving to local racing, then was crushed on returning to the JRA and retired.
574=She held off Air Deja Vu in the Queen C. for her first graded win, but was 6th as 2nd choice in the Oka Sho. Skipping the Oaks, she never won again, but was strong against fillies: 2nd in the Nakayama Himba S. and Queen S., 3rd in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup.
575=2 wins from 13, but one was the graded Queen S. In the classics she ran well in every race: 3rd in the Oka Sho, 2nd in the Oaks, 3rd in the Shuka Sho. As a dam she produced American JCC winner Air Shady and Shuka Sho winner Air Messiah. Her half-brother is Air Shakur, 2000 Satsuki Sho and Kikuka Sho winner.
582=She won her debut as favorite, then the TV Tokyo Sho Sansai Himba S. and Queen C., 3 straight. But she fractured a leg during the Queen C., losing the spring. Back, she was 13th as 3rd choice in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, then won the Christmas S. Going for a third graded win in the next Tokyo Shimbun Hai, she fractured again on sloppy going. She won 2 open races later, but never regained her old burst. No notable offspring.
584=Debuting late, in April at 3, she won in her 2nd start and, after one race, won 4 straight. She was 3rd in the Niigata Kinen and 2nd in the Himba Tokyo Times Hai. Her talent then bloomed over long distances: 3rd to Kashima Wing in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai at 4, she won the Diamond S. early the next year, her first graded race. In the Nakayama Kinen she was 9th, her first finish outside the top five, and retired.
585=She won in her 3rd start, then ran the next week in the Kokura 2yo S. Last in the betting, over 100 to 1, she charged from the back in the straight to win her first graded race. She was crushed in the Fantasy S., but as 7th choice in the Hanshin JF she again swooped down the straight to win. From 3 she slumped badly, finishing in double figures in the classics and almost every race - an early bloomer who shone for a moment at 2.
588=She won the Tulip Sho and Rose S. and ran well in the classics: 2nd in the Oka Sho, 4th in the Oaks, 4th in the Shuka Sho, 5th in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup, then won the Aichi Hai against older mares. At 4 she was 4th in the Yasuda Kinen and 5th in the Queen Elizabeth II Cup. In '08 she won the Kyoto Himba S., her first win in about a year but fractured a leg. While resting she developed colic, and thrashing in pain suffered a new open fracture. Beyond saving, she was put down, to much regret.
589=From the first crop of double classic winner Neo Universe, he won the 2009 Japanese Derby like his sire. Unbeaten in 4 from his debut, including the Yayoi Sho, he was clear Satsuki Sho favorite but finished 14th as the fast pace collapsed the leaders, him included - a shock. Seeking redemption in the Derby, he raced forward on heavy going and pulled away in the straight to win by 4 lengths. Leg trouble then kept him from racing properly; 2nd in the Sapporo Kinen at 4, he rested again, and after 2 more years off was 14th in the Sapporo Kinen, his last race. He went to stud from 2014.
592=With early speed typical of a Danzig x Mr. Prospector cross, and versatility, she came from behind to win a 5-million race as 8th choice, then led all the way to win the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun despite a loose horse pestering her. At 3 she aimed for the Oka Sho via the Fillies' Revue, but was last in both, then raced only on dirt. She won the Cluster C. in August at 4 and raced until August at 5.
568=A late-maturing sprinter, stuck in allowance races until spring at 5. From June she won twice and was 3rd in the Centaur S., and at 6 beat favorites like Scarlet Bouquet in the Swan S. for her first graded win.
587=Unbeaten in her first 4, including the Edelweiss Sho and Hokkaido 2yo Yushun, she then lost race after race as if a different horse. Moving to local Oi at 4 and America at 5, she won the Dahlia H., her first win in 3 years.
