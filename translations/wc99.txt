2420=Winner of the Artemis S., talented early. 2nd choice in the Hanshin JF, she was 7th to Major Emblem. At 3 she flopped in all 3 fillies' classics (10th, 9th, 9th) and was 11th in the QEII Cup. Older she suddenly improved: 4th in the Fukushima Himba S., then 2nd as 11th choice in the Victoria Mile (trifecta 910,000 yen). A long slump followed, with a 3rd in a fillies' graded race. In spring at 6, as 4th of 10, she closed from the back to win the Fukushima Himba S., and at 7, when forgotten again, she won the Aichi Hai by a neck as 9th choice.
2421=A star of the 2015 debut crop, ridden throughout by Christophe Lemaire, who gained a full JRA license that year. She won her debut and a 5-million race as favorite, lost to Denko Ange's late charge in the Artemis S., then led all the way to win the Hanshin JF, top of her crop and champion 2yo filly. She won the Queen C. by 5 lengths, but was jostled in the Oka Sho, 4th. In the NHK Mile C., at her best trip, she held off Lord Quest and others for a G1. Injured after, she never recovered and retired at 4.
2422=The 2016 Oaks winner, hailed as the best of her crop, but injured just before the Shuka Sho. Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Tulip Sho, she was 2nd choice in the Oka Sho behind 2yo queen Major Emblem; it became a duel with 3rd choice Jeweler, who won by a nose. With Jeweler then injured, she was clear Oaks favorite and won. In the Rose S. she beat a returning Jeweler easily, Shuka Sho favorite, but a tendon injury before the race ended her career.
2423=Steady from 2, he beat later 3-time graded winner Smart Odin in the Hagi S., but missed the spring classics. Focusing on miles, he was 2nd to Air Spinel in the Kyoto Kimpai at 4, then beat him in the Tokyo Shimbun Hai for his first graded win. After the Milers C. he was last (18th) in the Yasuda Kinen, and 12th as 4th choice in the Keisei Hai AH.
2424=Leading all the way to win his debut and the Chukyo 2yo S., a star 2yo, he was 1.5 favorite in the Kokura 2yo S.; slightly slow away, he still burst clear with superb speed. 5th in the Asahi Hai FS and 12th in the NHK Mile C., from summer at 3 he was 2nd in the Hakodate Sprint S. and Keeneland C. and 4th in the Sprinters S. 7th choice against G1 winners like Isla Bonita in the Hanshin C., he shot clear for his first graded win since Kokura. At 6 he tried dirt in the Chiba S. (OP) and won, his first win in 2 years.
2425=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Sapporo 2yo S. After an easy debut win as favorite, he was a fancy in the Sapporo 2yo S. and won a photo after a straight duel with favorite Prophet. 5th in the Hopeful S. and Yayoi Sho, little else. Missing 4 injured and dropping 2 classes, he won on his return at 5 as 7th choice, rose to open class again by year end and was 2nd in the Stayers S. Hopes were high at 6, but after poor runs in the Manyo S. and Hanshin Daishoten, he broke down in the Meguro Kinen and was put down - unusually precocious for a Heart's Cry.
2426=A rare Tochigi-bred. Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Keio Hai 2yo S.: 2nd choice, he led at the 4th turn and edged clear in a long straight drive to win by 1 1/4 lengths. That was his peak; 11th in the Asahi Hai FS, he did little after.
2427=A flagship of the 2016 crop, Kikuka Sho winner, one of a Big Three with Dee Majesty and Makahiki. Prized from a foal, he won his debut easily as favorite and was unbeaten in 3 to the Kisaragi Sho. Satsuki Sho favorite over Leondeux and Makahiki, he was hampered in the straight, 3rd. In the Derby he just failed to catch Makahiki, 2nd. With Makahiki in France, autumn was a duel with Dee Majesty: he won the Kobe Shimbun Hai and Kikuka Sho as favorite, dominant from a handy spot - Deep Impact's first Kikuka Sho winner. Favorite over Kitasan Black in the Arima Kinen, he caught leaders Kitasan Black and Gold Actor near the line for the Grand Prix. In 2017, aiming at the Arc, he won the Hanshin Daishoten, but in the Tenno Sho (Spring) rematch couldn't catch leader Kitasan Black, 3rd. In France, after the Prix Foy, he struggled on Chantilly's heavy turf in the Arc, 15th. Apart from the Kyoto Daishoten at 5 he never recaptured his best, and retired to stud.
2428=The 2016 Derby winner, one of a Big Three with Dee Majesty and Satono Diamond. Unbeaten in 3, he closed from last but couldn't catch Dee Majesty in the Satsuki Sho, 2nd; in the Derby, from midfield, he ran him down to win. In France from August, he won the Prix Niel, then in the Arc, with betting newly allowed in Japan, a hot pace drained him, 14th. Older he fell short: 4th in the Osaka Hai, 5th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 4th in the Japan Cup. As Rey de Oro's younger crop rose, he rarely made the top five at 5, and at 6, a Derby winner at double-figure odds at times, he still popped up with a 4th in the Osaka Hai and Japan Cup. At 7, rivals long retired, he kept running in G1s (11th Osaka Hai, 9th Japan Cup). At 8, 8th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), he suddenly won the Kyoto Daishoten - his first win in 5 years - catching favorite Aristoteles near the line. Then the Japan Cup became a clash of 4 Derby winners with Wagnerian, Contrail and Shahryar; 14th, but ahead of Wagnerian. After 3 races at 9, all far back, he retired to stud.
2429=A talent hailed after Dee Majesty and Makahiki. Winning the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S. in his 3rd start, at 3 he won the Mainichi Hai and Kyoto Shimbun Hai; 6th in the Derby. Turning to miles, he went winless against older horses until the Hankyu Hai at 4, a 4th graded win, charging powerfully from last.
2430=No G1, but 2nd in the QEII Cup 3 years running - a memorable mare. 3rd in the Sapporo 2yo S. and Artemis S. at 2, she missed the spring classics but was 2nd in the Rose S. and 6th in the Shuka Sho. At 4 she led all the way in the Fuchu Himba S. as 5th choice, holding off favorites Vivlos and Admire Lead. 9th choice in the QEII Cup, she nearly led all the way, 2nd. At 5 she was 2nd again as 9th choice; at 6, 3rd in the Victoria Mile as a longshot, and - as everyone half expected - 2nd in a 3rd QEII Cup. In her farewell, her first Arima Kinen, she tracked leader Aerolithe in 3rd but, perhaps beyond her trip, was last. Even among quirky Stay Gold runners she excelled in big races; may she produce a star.
2431=A great mare who won the QEII Cup at 4 and the Takarazuka Kinen against top males at 5. Blooming at 4, she won 2 allowances, was 2nd as favorite in the Mermaid S. and 5th in the All Comers. In the QEII Cup, against favorite Nuovo Record, defending champion Lachesis and 3yo star Rouge Buck, Ebina timed his move perfectly to win. 4th in the Arima Kinen, at 5 she was 2nd in the Meguro Kinen and 8th choice in a Takarazuka Kinen packed with G1 winners - Duramente, Kitasan Black, Lovely Day - but closed brilliantly from midfield to hold off Duramente for a 2nd G1. Favorite to defend the QEII Cup, she was 6th.
2432=Emerging early, 2nd in the Sapporo 2yo S., he won the Keisei Hai the next January, racing 4th-5th and pulling away. Overlooked in the classics: 11th as 10th choice in the Satsuki Sho, 17th as 18th in the Derby, unplaced in the St. Lite Kinen and Fukushima Kinen. At 4, 3rd in the Shirafuji S. (OP), he dropped to the 16-million class.
2433=Winner of the 2015 Hopeful S. Named after comet Hartley 2, observed by NASA's Deep Impact probe - fitting for a Deep Impact colt. After a debut win as favorite, he was 3rd choice in the Hopeful S. behind hot favorite Lord Quest, but drew level at the 4th turn and outkicked him. Beaten badly in the Kyodo News Hai at 3, leg trouble followed; he recovered enough to race but never regained his power, retiring at 6 after just 8 starts - a talent unfulfilled.
2434=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Kyoto 2yo S., noticed early. He won as favorite on Kikuka Sho day, the "legendary debut" race that produced Buena Vista, Rose Kingdom and Victoire Pisa; Yutaka Take was excited. In the Kyoto 2yo S. he tracked in 2nd and won a straight duel with Respect Earth. A classic fancy, he was 7th in the Spring S. and 15th in the Satsuki Sho, then stayed in allowances, returning to open class in autumn at 5. Improving with 3 top-five runs in Hokkaido at 6, he won the Kyoto Daishoten as 11th choice, sitting 3rd and passing leader Danburite - his first graded win in 4 years.
2435=A super horse named by Maaya Morinaga, introduced in Winning Post 8 2016. By her beloved Kizuna out of 2-time Arc winner Treve, he storms home to win the Arc his sire couldn't - a son fulfilling his father's dream. The name "Mirai" (future) holds the wish to see that future.
2436=A super horse named by the University of Tokyo Horsemen Club, introduced in Winning Post 8 2016. Heir to his sire Durandal, whose kick was likened to Shinzan's "hatchet." His dam 1 Carat adds even more sparkle. Late-normal maturity with lasting growth like his sire, he always makes players fear a charge from off the screen. His name is a 16th-century German greatsword (inspired by Durandal, a French holy sword).
2437=A super horse named by the University of Tokyo Horsemen Club, introduced in Winning Post 8 2016. A classic dirt horse of North American blood on both sides, inbred 3x4 to Deputy Minister, he races forward and keeps going. After ruling Japan's dirt races he aims for the BC Classic his sire Casino Drive never won. His name means "Watch me, Father."
2438=A super horse named by the University of Tokyo Horsemen Club, introduced in Winning Post 8 2016. Mejiro McQueen on the sire's side; Shinzan, Maruzensky and other greats of modern Japanese racing on the dam's. As her name suggests, she is flexible; early-normal maturity, a threat in the fillies' classics. The name is a double meaning: "UT + Rise" (University of Tokyo and her dam's name) and "utilize."

2442=Winning his debut in summer at 2, he then spent years in allowances, rising and dropping, before an open win early at 5. Often closing too late, he won the Capella S. late at 6 for his first graded win. Winless at 7.
