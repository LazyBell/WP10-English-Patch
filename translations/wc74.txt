1597=A regular near the top in allowances, he used his light weight to lead all the way against stronger horses in the '08 Tanabata Sho, winning the '08 Summer 2000 Series. 1601=In 2004 he won the US double unbeaten, but was 2nd in the Belmont S., narrowly missing the Triple Crown. Winning his first 5 with ease, he won his first graded race, the Arkansas Derby, then the Kentucky Derby. Heavy favorite at 1.7 in the Preakness, he won by a record 11.5 lengths, hailed as "the second coming of Secretariat." In the Belmont the Triple Crown seemed certain at 1.35, but just as he looked the winner, outsider Birdstone caught him near the line. He retired after it, Eclipse champion 3yo colt, and went to stud, siring some stakes winners but no stars. Dance Partner famously went from Japan to be bred to him, but the foal, Smarty Dance, won just once.
1602=Up-and-down in form, but talented enough to beat later G1 winner Tall Poppy. Just short of classic class, he came good in winter at 3, winning an open special and then the Chunichi Shimbun Hai. Struggling after, he switched to dirt and won his first dirt race, the Sirius S., with authority from 2nd, and was a close 2nd in it the next year.
1603=A famous stayer who won the 4000m G1 Gold C. 4 times. Winning his first 3, he was an English Derby fancy, but injury cost him his 3yo season. Back in spring at 4, racing mainly over 12 furlongs+, he won his first G1, the Coronation C.
