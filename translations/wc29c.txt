281=The horse who rewrote Japanese thoroughbred bloodlines from the ground up. He produced many heirs such as Special Week and Deep Impact, nearly turning Japan's bloodlines all Sunday Silence. He was also a superb racehorse, winning the Kentucky Derby and Preakness S. He lost the Belmont S. to rival Easy Goer, but won the Breeders' Cup Classic in autumn and was Eclipse Horse of the Year. After retiring at 4 he was little valued for his pedigree in America, so he was exported to Japan for stud. His first crop gave Fuji Kiseki and Genuine, the next Bubble Gum Fellow and Dance in the Dark - a flood of G1 winners. He kept siring G1 winners every year until his last, sparking a "Sunday Silence storm" that swept the JRA's G1s. His sire sons - Fuji Kiseki, Dance in the Dark, Stay Gold, Special Week, Agnes Tachyon, Heart's Cry, Deep Impact and more - are an elite group, and successful descendants are countless. His descendants have also spread abroad, and his influence is now worldwide.
