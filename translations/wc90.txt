2154=Winner of the 1981 QEII Cup. Overshadowed by Brocade and Tenmon in spring, she grew fast in autumn, winning the Sapphire S. and Kobe Shimbun Hai, then the QEII Cup, sharing champion 3yo filly with Tenmon. She tried the Arima Kinen, Tenno Sho (Spring) and Takarazuka Kinen without success, retiring at 4.
2155=The 1981 Oaks winner. Though she retired after it, she beat 2-classic colt Katsu Top Ace and classic regular San'ei Solon - hailed as the best filly since Tesco Gaby. Debuting in July at 3 (old age system), she beat San'ei Solon in the Shiragiku Sho, then Katsu Top Ace and others in the Asahi Hai 3yo S. At 4 she beat colts in the Keisei Hai, but struggled on heavy ground for 2nd to Brocade in the Oka Sho. Her camp said "the Oaks is hers," and from midfield she burst clear to win easily. Badly injured in a typhoon while resting that summer, she retired, and the injury limited her as a broodmare.

Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Hanshin 4yo Himba Tokubetsu, she was Oka Sho favorite; on a quagmire, she broke clear from a handy spot to win easily, having won on heavy ground before. 13th to Tenmon in the Oaks, beyond her trip, she then fought a classic duel with older Oka Sho winner Hagino Top Lady in the Tomoe Sho, losing a photo. Turning to sprints, 2nd in the CBC Sho, she beat defending champion Sakura Shingeki in the Sprinters S. At 6 she was 2nd in the Milers C., Yasuda Kinen and Hakodate Kinen. No notable foals.

2165=The best runner by little-known sire Yellapa. 13th in the Derby and 6th in the Kikuka Sho at 4 (old age system), he was 3rd in the Hanshin Daishoten, 2nd in the Sankei Osaka Hai and won the Kinko Sho at 5. 2167=Winner of the 1982 QEII Cup. A super blueblood given a treasured name by Chiyoda Farm and trained by filly specialist Yukio Inaba. 2168=Winner of the 1981 Hanshin 3yo S. (old name). With heavy favorite Saruno King scratched, she won by 2.5 lengths as 5th choice. Injured the next year, she came back in the CBC Sho late at 3, 14th, and retired.

2180=A mare who won the 1971 Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen, Horse of the Year. Small and plain as a foal - "like a mouse" - she sold for half the average price and struggled to find a stable. She retired as Horse of the Year; her son Tenmei won the Tenno Sho, the first mother-son Tenno Sho double.
