1370=Lacking a finishing punch, he often just lost, but kept adding wins and reached open class at 5. A graded regular, he won the Meguro Kinen and Fukushima Kinen, placed in others and raced until 10.
1371=A speedy middle-distance horse who set a Japanese 2000m turf record of 1:57.5 in the Australia T. Hot-tempered, he was demoted for veering in his 2nd start and injured jockey Mikio Matsunaga by acting up in training. At 3 he won the Shinzan Kinen and was 3rd to Taiki Fortune in the NHK Mile C. He won the Kinko Sho at 4, then slumped, running poorly in the Naruo Kinen, Takarazuka Kinen and Kyoto Daishoten, and retired. Prized for his pedigree and speed at stud, he sired no good runners.
1372=Half-brother of 4-time US G1 winner Thunder Gulch. He won 9 on dirt. In the Unicorn S. he crossed the line first but was demoted to 10th. At 4 he won the Procyon S. and Kashiwa Kinen, adding 2 more graded wins later.
1373=Winner of 14 G1s, a European record, and the BC Mile 3 years running. Beaten by later French fillies' Triple Crown winner Zarkava in the French 1000 Guineas and Oaks, she then focused on miles, winning the Prix Rothschild, Prix du Moulin and more. At 3 she won the BC Mile in America; at 4 she won the Jacques le Marois by 6 lengths and the BC Mile again. At 5 she won 5 G1s incl. a 3rd BC Mile, Cartier Horse of the Year. Racing on at 6, she won the Prix d'Ispahan and Prix Rothschild for her 14th G1, but going for an unprecedented 4th BC Mile was 3rd to Court Vision, and retired. 17 wins from 27; jockey Olivier Peslier, her rider throughout, named her among the best he ever rode.
