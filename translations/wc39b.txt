457=Small at around 420 kg, she won her debut by 9 lengths, then the Sazanka Sho and Elfin S. After a 4th in the Tulip Sho, she beat London Bridge in the Oka Sho for her first classic. The longer trip told in the Oaks, where she was 3rd, but in autumn she won the Shuka Sho for a fillies' double. As an older mare she struggled against colts, but won the Queen Elizabeth II Cup at 5, her first win in 2 years, her class showing. Her half-brother Kizuna, 15 years younger, won the 2013 Japanese Derby.
460=She won the Suzuran Sho by 6 lengths and the Hakodate Sansai S. by 5 - called a sure classic bet. But her temper was the miscalculation. At 3 she refused to enter the gate for the Sumire Sho, had to retake a training exam and missed the Oka Sho. After the Yonsai Himba Tokubetsu (East) she made her long-sought Oaks, finishing 3rd. Winless at 3, she slumped in spring at 4 too. But teamed with master Yukio Okabe, she tried a late charge for the first time in the Keio Hai Autumn H. and won in a time equal to the then world record. She lost the Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen, but at 5 won the Sprinters S. (G2) and Keio Hai Spring C.. In the Yasuda Kinen she dueled Nippo Teio, losing by just 0.2 seconds in 2nd. After a 4th in Tamamo Cross's Tenno Sho (Autumn), she retired. As a dam she produced Stage Champ (90: Real Shadai).

450=She won 3 straight including the Sapporo Sansai S., then had two long breaks. In autumn of the next year she won the Autumn Sprint S. and Himba Tokyo Times Hai, then was 5th in the Mile Championship despite a slow start.
