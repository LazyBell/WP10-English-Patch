2110=All 7 wins at 1200-1400m on dirt, charging wide from the back - a rare closer among dirt sprinters. In the Capella S. late at 4, on fast wet ground behind a blistering 32.7 first 600m, he set a JRA record of 1:08.7.
2111=Winning the Mikuni Tokubetsu in September at 4, he won 5 in 7 months to the Sobu S., then struggled in open class. 3rd in the Azuma Kofuji OP at 6, he won the Tanabata Sho by racing wide on the better ground. Like his half-sister Nifty Niece, he kept improving.
2112=His sire Grindstone (1996) and grandsire Unbridled (1990) both won the Kentucky Derby. With that power and speed he shone early on dirt; in summer at 3 he led all the way in Asahikawa's Grand Chariot C. by 6 lengths, the 3rd another 7 back.
2113=Related to Tenno Sho winner Hokuto Boy, she showed early promise but, nervous, stalled for a while. Maturing in summer at 4, she was 2nd in the Hakodate Kinen, won the UHB Hai by 3.5 lengths over Legacy World, then charged wide to win the Fuchu Himba S.
2114=11th choice in the Mainichi Hai at 3, he ran 2nd from the front and raced boldly in the Derby. At 4 he went up the muddy rail to win the Hankyu Hai, and led for 2nd as 14th choice in the Takamatsunomiya Hai - dangerous when forgotten.
2115=His dam Solo Singer ran in the Oka Sho. Under trainer Yamauchi, who also trained his dam, he won the Kyodo News Hai and was 4th in the Satsuki Sho. From 4 he turned to dirt like his sire Brian's Time, 2nd in 2 open races, and raced at Kasamatsu until 8.
2116=Crop-mate of Tokai Teio. He won the Kyodo News Hai 4yo S. and ran in all 3 classics, 3rd in the Satsuki Sho and Derby. A sharp closer but tricky; if he pulled, his kick misfired, so he tested his riders. Tough too: 22 starts in 17 months.
2117=At 3 and 4 he regularly closed fastest from the back. Slow to win but rock-steady, top five 16 straight. 4th in a 10-million race, he ran again a week later in the Aichi Hai (home-bred sires only) and, under Aichi jockey Minoru Yoshida, won by a nose - a 2-class jump to a graded win.
2118=With the JRA until June at 5, he won 5 on dirt. Moving to local racing, he won stakes at Morioka, Kaminoyama and Mizusawa, beating later G1 winner Toho Emperor. Moving to Kochi at 9, he raced 20+ times a year from 10 to 15: 27 wins from 233.
2119=By Rodrigo de Triano, a 2000 Guineas winner, out of a Southern Kanto Oka Sho winner - but via his broodmare sire Alanas, 3rd in the 3100m Grand Prix de Paris, 5 of his 7 wins came at 2400m+. In the Diamond S. at 6, under Olivier Peslier, he raced handy on the rail and won like a stayer.
2120="Bakushin O for sprints, Sekai O for middle trips." Same owner, age and sire as Sakura Bakushin O, debuting a day apart. Long outshone, he was then 3rd in the Yayoi Sho and NHK Hai and won the Valentine S. (OP) from the 9-million class.
2121=A local specialist at 1800-2000m on turf, he won the Fukushima Minpo Hai at 4, then blossomed at 6 with Shoichi Osaki. He won the Joetsu S., was 2nd in the Toki S., then avoided the rain-damaged rail to win the Niigata Kinen from wide.
2122=Debuting at Sapporo at 2, 6th at best in 4 turf races, he switched to dirt in December and won at once. All 5 wins came at 1600-1800m on dirt. In the Grand Chariot C. at 3 he beat Kasamatsu's Fujino Condor and later G1 winner Mugamuchu.
2123=Around 1990 Mill George runners like Inari One, Rosita and Osaichi George shone. Winning his debut in autumn at 2, he emerged at 3 and won the Kobe Shimbun Hai. Older, he often placed in graded races: 2nd as 10th choice in the Keihan Hai, 2nd as 11th in the Kinko Sho - a longshot favorite.
2124=He won a Fukushima 1000m turf debut in June at 2 in record time, by 4 lengths. A turf sprinter at 2 and 3, after winning the dirt Happy End C. late at 3 he starred in dirt sprints, winning the Hokkaido Sprint C. at Sapporo as favorite over Nobo True and Shadow Scape.
2125=Her first 4 wins came on dry dirt, but from summer at 5 she handled turf sprints and gained speed with age. At 6, after an 18th as favorite in the Ibis Summer Dash, she closed powerfully to win the Kitakyushu Kinen. As a dam, by Deep Impact she produced graded winners Mikki Glory and Katsuji.
2126=Placed from 3, e.g. 3rd on the same time as Silva Cockpit in the Kisaragi Sho, but often lacked a kick. A 2600m win at Kokura at 4 turned him into a stayer: in the Good Luck H. he matched the Arima Kinen time on the same day and course, then won the Diamond S.
2127=5th, 0.5s behind Dance in the Dark, in the Kikuka Sho, a prized talent, but after the Naruo Kinen (8th) a fracture cost him 2 years 10 months. Back, he was 4th as 14th choice in the Meguro Kinen at 7, then won the Tanabata Sho, held at Tokyo that year.
2128=A powerful closer at 1800-2000m on turf, best on long straights - Tokyo, and old Niigata's outer course. In the Niigata Kinen at 6 he closed fastest in 34.4, rare then - a striking run.
2129=Tough from June at 2: 12 starts at 3, 11 at 4, 10 at 5. A middle-distance front-runner, at 6 she led all the way in the Mermaid S. over Bella Leia and Little Amapola.
2130=Early-maturing, she won her June debut at 2 by 4 lengths over later classic runner-up Dantsu Flame. Beaten by Tenzan Desert in the Lavender Sho, she held off Gary Mucho in the Hakodate 3yo S.
2131=She won her first 4 incl. the Fantasy S. Favorite in the Hanshin JF, she was 4th to Tamuro Cherry in a blanket finish, then 3rd in the Fillies' Revue. Injured, she returned after 1.5 years to win the Niigata Nippo Sho, but retired after another injury. She was ridden by Naosuke Sugai, later trainer of Just a Way and Gold Ship.
2132=A Special Week front-running miler. In open class in autumn at 4, he held off Kyoei Storm by a nose in the Keisei Hai AH. He raced on locally after.
2133=He ran down $1.1-million buy Star El Dorado in the Hakodate 2yo S. Often placing in turf sprints without winning, he won the Rokko Island S. (16M) late at 4, 2 years on.
2134=He led to win a dirt allowance by 5 lengths as 11th choice, then the Chunichi Sports Sho 4yo S. by 3 lengths as 13th choice - both under front-running master Nakadate. A sprint front-runner, he also won the Fukushima Minyu C. (OP).
2135=Winning first in local racing, she returned to the JRA and turned to long turf races at 4, running on gamely. After a 4th in the Keihan Hai at 5, she won the Shinshun S. (16M) from last and the Kyoto Himba S.
2136=He beat later graded winner Sunrise Jaeger in the Kyushu Sports Hai at 5, then went early to win the Kokura Kinen on 52kg. His half-brother Aratama Summons was 3rd in it, and his nephew Aratama Universe 2nd in the Kokura Summer Jump.
2137=By Kingmambo, sire of El Condor Pasa and American Boss. He led to win at 4, then was 4th as favorite to later G1 winner Tsurumaru Boy in the Chukyo Kinen, the only front-runner in the top five. 6th choice in the Niigata Daishoten, he led and held everyone off.
2138=5th on debut, he ran twice more at 2-week gaps, winning his 3rd start, then again 2 weeks later ran in the Hakodate 2yo S. As 9th choice he caught leader Toho Asuka - his camp's will to win it. At 3 he won the Fukushima Minyu C. (OP), on turf or dirt.
2139=A late-blooming dirt closer. In his first graded race, the Sirius S. at 5, he was 2nd, then won the Musashino S. A handsome grey, he became a lead pony after retiring.
2140=A very late bloomer: 13 starts to win, a 2nd win in his 43rd. After a 4th win in his 52nd, he won the Kabutoyama Kinen as 9th choice on 50kg. He ran 62 times.
2141=Half-brother of Flower C. winner Shonan Talent. Winning his 2nd start, he used his speed to win 3 straight incl. the Kyodo News Hai. 3rd in the Spring S., he was 3rd choice in the Satsuki Sho but missed the break and finished far back. Losing form, he became a closer and won a 16-million race at 5, 2 years on, then was 2nd as 9th choice in the Swan S. with the fastest finish.
2142=Without a classic despite a 2nd, 4th and 2nd, he made up for it by winning the Dubai Turf the next year, his first G1. Highly rated from his debut, a top classic fancy, he was 2nd to Duramente's charge in the Satsuki Sho. In the Derby he chased Duramente from behind but was 4th, also passed by Satono Rasen and Satono Crown. 2nd choice in the Kikuka Sho without Duramente, he lost by a neck to Kitasan Black. In the Dubai Turf he raced handy and broke clear in the straight, held off all under star jockey Ryan Moore. 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 5th in the Japan Cup, the next year he won the Mainichi Okan but was 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). At 7, 3rd in the Dubai Turf, he was beaten badly in the Yasuda Kinen and retired injured to stud. He has sired Leben Stil, Chikappa and Forever Young, who won 3 local G1s and raced abroad: 3rd in the Kentucky Derby and BC Classic, and in 2025 winner of the Saudi Cup, his earnings 2nd all-time, ahead of Equinox and 9-crown Almond Eye (as of June 2025). Expect his foals to shine on turf and dirt.
2143=By Sunday Silence, then all-conquering. 3rd with the fastest finish on debut, he won 3 straight incl. 2 open races. Out of form in spring, he won the Radio Tampa Sho in July with his old kick, and was 2nd to Tokai Point in the Nakayama Kinen in 33.9.
2144=Winner of the 1980 Tenno Sho (Spring). Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Chukyo 3yo S. (old age system), injury then kept him out long. Back in autumn at 4, he was 5th as favorite in the Kobe Shimbun Hai, 8th in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai and 6th in the Kikuka Sho. At 5 he won an open race by 8 lengths, then as 3rd choice won a duel with Mejiro Transam in the Tenno Sho (Spring). After a 10th in the Takarazuka Kinen he retired; his best foal was Shinzan Kinen winner Nichido Thunder.
2145=Winner of the 2009 Procyon S. Fragile, with only 6 starts by the end of 5, he won his way up to open class in January at 6, then won the Procyon S. - Agnes Tachyon's first dirt graded winner. Leg trouble kept him from big races, and he retired in May at 7.
2146=Winner of the 2008 Keihan Hai. Raced on dirt due to leg trouble, she emerged on turf in autumn at 4: 2nd in the Kyoraku S., then won the Keihan Hai as 4th choice. 15th as favorite in the Silk Road S., she mostly finished far back in turf sprints after, and became a broodmare in 2011.
2147=Early to shine, she won her 2nd start, was 3rd in the Phoenix Sho (OP), then won the Kokura 3yo S. (old name) and the TV Tokyo 3yo Himba S. (now Fairy S.). 2149=Won the 2004 Keisei Hai, beating heavy favorite and later Derby winner King Kamehameha - a win many still rate. Favorite in the Yayoi Sho, he was 5th to Cosmo Bulk, then flopped in the Satsuki Sho and Derby. A crop with Daiwa Major, Company and Heart's Cry was hard luck.
2150=A late star by Tosho Boy. 2151=Won the 2003 Fairy S., then a 2yo race. A classic fancy, she won the Elfin S. and was 2nd in the Fillies' Revue, but 11th in the Oka Sho, and never won on the flat again. 2152=Winner of the 2001 Nakayama Kimpai. 2nd in the 4yo Himba Tokubetsu at 3, she was 13th in the Oaks and Shuka Sho, then improved in open races and won the Nakayama Kimpai at 4 - longshots filled the top 3 in a rare upset. Little in 15 more races; a broodmare from 2003.
