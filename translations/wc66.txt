1290=The 1977 Derby winner. 1292=Crushed in the Satsuki Sho (13th) and Derby (18th), he was transformed after a break, winning the Kobe Shimbun Hai and Kyoto Shimbun Hai. 2nd choice for the Kikuka Sho, he was 5th on heavy ground, then never won again.

1298=Winning 3 straight to the Queen C., she was 11th in the Oaks. After an 8th in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and 3rd in the Rose S., she was 7th choice for the Queen Elizabeth Cup, but closed sharply for a narrow 2nd to Mejiro Ramonu.

1302=A famed stayer who won the Stayers S. and Diamond S., both over 3000m, 2 years running. Favorite in his first G1, the '89 Tenno Sho (Spring), he was 3rd to Inari One, and retired without a G1.
