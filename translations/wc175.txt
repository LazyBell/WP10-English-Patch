3786=Winner of the 2025 Lord Derby CT (G3). Unbeaten in 2, he was 6th in the Yayoi Sho and 11th in the Aoba Sho, then dropped to the mile and won 2 classes into open class. (OP).

(G3), formerly the Arlington C., renamed when its partner track changed from Arlington Park to Churchill Downs. 3rd as 9th choice in the Asahi Hai FS late at 2, 3rd in the Kisaragi Sho at 3, he won the Churchill Downs C. as 2nd choice, holding off favorite Arte Veloce. 5th in the NHK Mile C., fading from up front; 3rd in the Swan S., then he won the Rigel S. (L).
3788=Winner of the 2025 NZT (G2). Winning his 2nd start, he lost 2 close calls in a 1-win race, then stepped up as 2nd choice in the NZT and caught favorite Admire Zoom with a sharp late run from the back. 11th as 2nd choice in the NHK Mile C.
3789=Winner of the 2025 Hanshin Himba S. (G2). Noticed early - 2nd in the Artemis S., favorite in the Hanshin JF at 2 - she was 13th in the Oaks at 3. Back in allowances, still in the 3-win class at 4, she won the Hanshin Himba S., holding on from up front in a 3-way photo. 13th in the Victoria Mile, 7th in the QEII Cup.
3790=Winner of the 2025 Fukushima Himba S. (G3). She won 3 straight at 3 from a maiden through the 2-win class, then after a 2nd the 3-win class at 4. Favorite in her first stakes, the Fukushima Himba S., she moved early from midfield and won by 2 lengths. 7th in the Victoria Mile and Ireland T. (G2), then she retired.
3791=True to his name, a long-serving runner who won the Kokura Daishoten (G3) and Milers C. (G2) at 7 in 2025. Starting on dirt and stuck in the 2-win class, he switched to turf in spring at 4, won 2 straight into open class, and ran in mile-to-2000m stakes. He won the December S. (L) late at 5, was 2nd in the Kokura Daishoten at 6, then at 7 won it, and the Milers C. too. In G1s: 13th Yasuda Kinen, 15th Mile CS.
3792=A 2025 3yo dirt star. Winning his 2nd start at 2 and a 1-win race easily at 3, he won the Unicorn S. (G3) as 3rd choice, holding off hot favorite Clay King by 3/4 length. 3rd choice in the Tokyo Derby (JpnI), he was 5th, behind fancied JRA runners Natural Rise and Clay King and 2 local horses.
3793=From Saturnalia's first crop, winner of the 2025 Kyoto Shimbun Hai (G2), giving his sire a graded title. Debuting in November at 2 and winning his 2nd start, 4th in the Kisaragi Sho, he won the Kyoto Shimbun Hai as 5th choice in commanding style from up front. 3rd in the Derby; 2nd in the Kobe Shimbun Hai, but 14th as 3rd choice in the Kikuka Sho.
3794=Winner of the 2025 Niigata Daishoten (G3). Always in good company - 6th in the Hopeful S. at 2, 3rd in the Yayoi Sho and 14th in the Satsuki Sho at 3 - he turned to middle distances: 2nd in the Radio Nikkei Sho, then at 4 the Fukushima Minpo Hai (L) and the Niigata Daishoten as 8th choice, leading all the way. 5th in the Fukushima Kinen.
3795=Winner of the 2025 Aoi S. (G3). Winning her July debut at 2 and a 1-win race at 3, 6th in the Marguerite S. (L), she was 15th choice of 16 in the Aoi S., but surged from midfield and alone broke clear of a blanket finish - a trifecta of 1.89 million yen. 3rd in the Kitakyushu Kinen.
3796=A stayer who won his first stakes, the 2025 Meguro Kinen (G2). Unbeaten in 2, 4th in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai at 3, he won the 2-win class, then was 3rd to Urban Chic in the Kikuka Sho. 3797=A filly who won the 2025 Hakodate Sprint S. (G3). Winning just a dirt maiden early, she switched to turf in summer at 3 and quickly climbed to open class.
