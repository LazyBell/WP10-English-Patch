1748=Daiwa Major's sister and queen of the greatest fillies' crop. She beat Vodka in the Oka Sho; a fever ruled out the Oaks, but in autumn she won the Rose S., Shuka Sho and Queen Elizabeth II Cup. A world tour from the February S. was planned the next year, but leg trouble ended her career. Always racing in G1s, never out of the top two - surely a leading candidate for the greatest mare ever.
