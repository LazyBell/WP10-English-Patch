2339=The 2015 Oka Sho winner. 2nd in the Artemis S. and Hanshin JF at 2, she was 5th choice in the Oka Sho, led from the start at a slow pace and won by 4 lengths with plenty left. At 6 she was still 2nd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 5th in the Sprinters S., and 5th in the February S. and JBC Sprint on dirt. Fading at 7, she was 5th in the Sprinters S. and 4th in the Hanshin C. In 2020 she went to Ireland to breed.

Favorite in the Oka Sho and Oaks, her best was 2nd in the Oaks, but her potential was clear. Straight into the QEII Cup in autumn, 4th; voted into the Arima Kinen, she was 10th. At 4 she took on males, winning the Epsom C. and Mainichi Okan, 3rd choice in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). Without a G1, she won the All Comers in autumn at 5, a match for any male.

2342=A super blueblood out of G1 winner To the Victory, with graded-winning siblings. Missing the spring classics, she won 2 in summer and was 3rd in the Rose S. A bit short in G1s like the Shuka Sho and Victoria Mile, she won the Nakayama Himba S. at 5, racing forward and holding on.

2345=With Mirco Demuro aboard he won the Satsuki Sho and Derby with overwhelming power, ranked with Deep Impact and Orfevre. A late foal of Admire Groove, from a G1 line - grand-dam Air Groove, great-grand-dam Dyna Carl. Slow away and 2nd on debut, he won next by nearly a second, then lost narrowly in the Kyodo News Hai. 3rd choice in the Satsuki Sho, he swung wide from the back at the 4th turn and blew past favorites Satono Crown and Real Steel. Back home he was a close 2nd to Marialite in the Takarazuka Kinen, but injury struck again and he retired to stud. He sired G1 winners Titleholder, Stars on Earth, Liberty Island, Dura Erede, Lugal and Durezza, but died in 2021 at just 9 with only 5 crops. In 2023 those foals made him leading sire.

2350=The 1969 Oka Sho winner.
