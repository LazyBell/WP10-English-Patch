2589=The mare who dominated 2018-2020, hailed the best in training. At 3 she became the 5th fillies' Triple Crown winner, then raced only in G1s, winning a JRA-record 9 turf G1s - the star who sent her sire, new stallion Lord Kanaloa, soaring. She won the Shinzan Kinen in January at 3, then went straight to the Oka Sho; unbeaten 2yo queen Lucky Lilac was favored, but she charged from the back to steal the show. Oaks favorite at 1.7, she broke clear from handy and won with an otherworldly finish. Despite going straight to the Shuka Sho, she was 1.3 favorite and won from midfield as if the others had stopped - the Triple Crown. Skipping the QEII Cup for the Japan Cup, at 1.4 she crushed Kikuka winner Kiseki and Osaka Hai winner Suave Richard. In spring she won the Dubai Turf as favorite with the world watching, then, hampered at the start, was 3rd in the Yasuda Kinen with a stunning finish. In autumn she beat younger star Saturnalia easily in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), a 6th G1. A fever ruled out the Hong Kong Cup; in the Arima Kinen she faded, her first time out of the top three. Was she declining? At 5 she won the Victoria Mile by a street - 7 turf G1s, equaling the record - then was 2nd to sprint queen Gran Alegria in the Yasuda Kinen. That autumn she won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) again for an unprecedented 8th G1 - beyond Symboli Rudolf, Deep Impact and Gentildonna. With 2 unbeaten Triple Crown winners, Contrail and Daring Tact, set for the Japan Cup, she chose it as her farewell - the best in training vs. 2 unbeaten Triple Crown winners. The 3 fought out the finish as fans hoped, and the senior star won, setting records for G1 wins and earnings (as of 2020). Her first foal, by Epiphaneia, arrived in 2022, followed by foals by Maurice, Kitasan Black and Equinox - the last hailed as the "15-crown baby." Great things are awaited.
