(G2) as 8th choice, edging Meiner Ticket by a neck in a two-horse duel. 12th as 4th choice in the Asahi Hai FS; 4th as Falcon S. favorite at 3. 9th choice in the NHK Mile C., he won a blanket finish by a head, then the Keeneland C. (G3). In Australia's Golden Eagle he was 5th of 16.
3597=6th on debut in September at 2, she won a maiden, then the Daily Hai 2yo S. (G2) as favorite, joining the lead at the 4th turn from midfield and holding off Dragon Boost by half a length - a precious graded win for Yuichi Fukunaga's stable in its first year. 11th in the Hanshin JF.
3598=A 2000m stakes regular who finally won the Fukushima Kinen (G3) at 7. At 4 he won 3 straight allowances and the Cepheus S. (OP); his best in stakes were 3rds in the Fukushima Kinen at 4 and 5 and the Kinko Sho at 6. At 7, at his 3rd try, he won the Fukushima Kinen.
3599=The 2025 Derby winner, from Kitasan Black's 4th crop - his 3rd JRA G1 winner after Equinox and Sol Oriens. Unbeaten in 3 at 2 incl. the Hopeful S. (G1), he was the classic favorite. Straight to the Satsuki Sho at 3 as 1.5-1 favorite, he was caught by Museum Mile, 2nd. Derby favorite again, he broke clear and never let anyone get near him. In France he won the Prix du Prince d'Orange (G3), then was the best-fancied of 3 Japanese in the Arc, but faded to 14th. Back home, 2nd choice behind crop-mate and Tenno Sho winner Masquerade Ball, 4th in the Japan Cup.
3600=A 2025 classic standout. Unbeaten in 3 at 2 incl. the Kyoto 2yo S. (G3), all as favorite. A classic contender at 3, 11th in the Satsuki Sho, he was 5th to Croix du Nord in the Derby. He won the Kobe Shimbun Hai (G2) with a brilliant late run, then was 2nd to Energico as 2nd choice in the Kikuka Sho.
3601=Winning his 3rd start, he won 3 sprints straight: the Fukushima 2yo S. (OP), Chukyo 2yo S. (OP) and Marguerite S. (L) at 3. Winless in stakes (3rd Aoi S., 10th Centaur S., 5th Keihan Hai), at 4 he was 2nd in the Ocean S. and 3rd in the Hakodate Sprint S., then won the Keihan Hai (G3) as favorite.
3602=Winning a maiden in his 8th start, he took 1.5 years to clear the allowances, in autumn at 4. In staying stakes: 4th Stayers S., then at 5, 3rd Diamond S., 2nd Hanshin Daishoten, 5th Tenno Sho (Spring). In Australia, 13th in the Caulfield Cup, then 2nd by a nose in the Melbourne Cup.
3603=Winning a newcomer race in September at 2, she won the Edelweiss Sho (JpnIII), leading in the straight and drawing clear. 2nd choice in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun (JpnI), she moved early to lead at the 4th turn and held off Happy Man - the first filly dirt champion in 8 years. At 3, 7th in Saudi Arabia's Saudi Derby (G3), then on her first turf start in the Hakodate Sprint S. (G3) she collapsed in the race with cardiac arrest, ending her short life.
3604=Debuting at Mombetsu with 3 wins and 3 seconds from 6, he was 5th choice of 10 in the JBC 2yo Yushun (JpnIII), but saving ground at the back, kicked from a perfect spot and won by 3 lengths as the leaders faded - a first graded title for his sire Le Vent Se Leve's first crop. 3rd to Myriad Love in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun, 4th in the Keihin Hai at 3, he then swept the Hokkaido Triple Crown - Hokuto Hai, Hokkai Yushun, Okan Sho - all as favorite, only the 8th to do so. 10th in the Japan Dirt Classic (JpnI).
3605=Winning a maiden through the 2-win class at 3 and the 3-win class early at 4, he won his 2nd open race, the Radio Nippon Sho (OP). 2nd as 11th choice in his first stakes, the Miyako S., he won the Urawa Kinen (JpnII), leading at the top of the straight and drawing away. At 5 he won the Heian S. (G3), 2nd in the Teio Sho and 3rd in the Tokyo Daishoten.
3606=Debuting in June at 2, he won a 1200m dirt newcomer race, bursting clear from midfield. 4th in the Yamaboshi Sho (1-win), he won the Hyogo Junior GP as favorite, catching the leader at the 4th turn and pulling away with a superior finish.
3607=At 3 he won the Wakaba S. (L), but was 16th in the Satsuki Sho and 15th in the Derby. On dirt he won the Green Channel C. (L); back on turf from autumn at 5, he won the Andromeda S. (L), then the Chunichi Shimbun Hai (G3) as 3rd choice, leading all the way.
3608=Winning a dirt maiden, she was 13th on turf in the Queen C. (G3), then climbed 3 dirt classes. Favorite in her first dirt stakes, the Capella S. (G3), late at 3, she broke clear from midfield and held off late closers like Kurojishi Joe. At 4, 3rd in Saudi Arabia's Riyadh Dirt Sprint (G2), 5th Tokyo Sprint, 4th Oval Sprint.
3609=Unbeaten in 2, 4th in the Asahi Hai FS - talented early. On dirt from 3, he reached open class in January at 4 and won listed races, but not stakes. 2nd in the Kashiwa Kinen at 6 and 7, he won the JBC Sprint in autumn at 7 - his first graded win a G1.
3610=Debuting in January at 3, she won a newcomer race and raced at a mile, reaching open class with the Hakata S. (3-win) in June at 4. In her first stakes, the Queen S. (G3), she held on for a close 3rd; she won the Cassiopeia S. (L), then the Turquoise S. (G3) under top weight. At 5, 2nd in the Hanshin Himba S., 4th in the Victoria Mile, she won the Queen S. as favorite, then 6th in America's BC Mile; her retirement was then announced.
3611=Winner of the 2024 Asahi Hai FS (G1). Winning his 2nd start, he was 5th choice in the Asahi Hai FS, sat 2nd, led at the 4th turn and drew 2.5 lengths clear with a stunning finish. Top mile hope at 3, he was 1.7-1 NZT favorite but caught by a neck by Immigrant Song; NHK Mile C. favorite, he found nothing, 14th. 6th as 2nd choice in the Swan S.
3612=Rated the greatest sprinter in British racing history: 14 wins and 3 seconds from 17, incl. 2 July Cups and 2 Nunthorpe S. Rivals so feared him that most of his races had just 3 runners. 2nd on his April debut at 2, he won 5 by year-end, 4 easily - Timeform's top 2yo at 133. At 3, after one run, he was 2nd in the 2000 Guineas on his first try at a mile, then focused on sprints, winning 4 straight incl. the King's Stand S. and July C. - Timeform's best again at 138. At 4 he won 4 of 5, repeating in the July C., King George S. and Nunthorpe S., and retired. At stud he sired many sprinters, but mostly fillies, so his male line died out; his name survives in dam lines.
3613=Winner of England's stayers' triple crown (Gold C., Goodwood C., Doncaster C.), called Britain's greatest stayer of the 20th century. Losing his first 3 from October at 2, he won the Classic Trial S. in spring at 3, then took the staying path: the Royal Standard S., Princess of Wales's S. and St. George S. 2nd to Black Tarquin in the St Leger, he then showed his true class: the Jockey Club S. and King George VI S., then at 4 the Ormonde S. and Corporation S., and the stayers' triple crown, the first in 70 years - 7 straight - and retired. A fine sire, with St Leger winner Alcide and Fillies' Triple Crown winner Meld; Alcide's son Remand, imported to Japan, sired Opec Horse, Agnes Lady and more.
3614=Debuting in England, then moving to America, he won the Washington DC International twice - the only horse ever to do so. In England he won 3 of 6, but temperament let him down in the classics: 7th in the 2000 Guineas, 12th in the Derby. Moving to the US in autumn at 3, he gradually blossomed, winning the Suburban H. in summer at 4, the Saratoga H., and the turf Washington DC International. At 5 he won the Widener H., Gulfstream Park H. and Metropolitan H., then the Aqueduct H. and a 2nd Washington DC International - US champion handicap male - and retired. Not a great sire, but 12 stakes winners incl. Arc-winning filly San San; daughter Too Bald produced Capote, carrying his blood on.
3615=Rated among France's best racehorses of the 20th century. Winning his June debut at 2 easily, he swept the Prix Robert Papin, Prix Morny and Grand Criterium, sometimes called France's 2yo triple crown - unbeaten in 4, top-weighted at 63kg in France's Free Handicap. At 3 he won the Prix de Sevres, the Poule d'Essai des Poulains and Prix Lupin; illness kept him out of both Derbys, but back in autumn he won the Prix Royal-Oak, then the Arc easily - unbeaten again, rated 68kg, far above rival Admiral Drake. At 4, after more wins, he got loose in the streets while travelling and was 5th in the Ascot Gold C., his first defeat; 4th in the Arc, he retired. At stud, taken from France to Germany during WWII and returned after the war, he still sired Vieux Manoir, a successful sire, founding the Brantome line.
