3492=A British sprinter who won 3 sprint G1s in Europe and Dubai in 2019, Cartier champion sprinter. Winning his debut in June at 2, he won the Gimcrack S. (G2) in his 4th start, 2nd in the Middle Park S. and 3rd in the Dewhurst S. At 3 he won 2 G3s but was 3rd in the Commonwealth C. and 4th in the Sprint C. - not quite there. At 4 he won the King's Stand S. (G1), and at 5 he peaked: 2 G2s in Dubai, then the Al Quoz Sprint, and back home the King's Stand S. and Diamond Jubilee S. - top of European sprinting. He retired, champion sprinter on his first-half record alone. His first crop has G1 winners Irish 2000 Guineas winner Rosallion and BC Juvenile Turf Sprint winner Big Evs - a fine start.
3493=From Frankel's first crop, one of his best, winning 4 G1s incl. the Champion S. in 2017 and 2018, and sire of Arc winner Ace Impact. Winning his only start at 2, after 6 months off he won his opener at 3, then 3rd in the Derby and 2nd in the Irish Derby. He won the Great Voltigeur S. (G2), Prix Niel (G2) and Champion S. (G1) - 3 straight. At 4 he won the Prix Ganay and Coronation C. - 5 straight; 2nd in the Prince of Wales's S., he won the Champion S. again for a 4th G1 and retired. His first crop had French Derby and Arc winner Ace Impact, making him France's champion sire in 2023.
3494=The 2018 French Derby winner, an Irish-bred, Irish-trained son of Deep Impact. Winning his debut at Saint-Cloud in September at 2, after 6 months off he was 2nd in the Prix La Force (G3), then won the Prix Greffulhe (G2). 2nd choice in the French Derby, he broke clear in the straight and held on by half a length. 3rd in the Prix Guillaume d'Ornano (G2); 5th in the Irish Champion S. and 9th in the Arc. At 4, 2nd in the Prix Ganay and Prix d'Ispahan, he switched to the mile, 3rd in the Prix Messidor and 6th in the Jacques le Marois, and retired. His 2nd crop has 2-time British Champions Fillies & Mares S. winner Kalpana - spreading Deep Impact's blood in Europe.
3495=Winner of the 2018 2000 Guineas (G1), a Japan-bred, Irish-trained son of Deep Impact - the first Japan-bred winner of an English classic. Winning his debut in August at 2 and the Beresford S. (G2), he won the Racing Post T. (G1) as favorite - next year's top classic hope. Straight to the 2000 Guineas at 3, 2nd choice behind new star Masar, he beat him - unbeaten in 4, a classic. Hot favorite for the Derbys, he was 4th in the Derby and 3rd in the Irish Derby, then 2nd in the Eclipse S., 4th in the International S. and 2nd in the Irish Champion S. Tendon trouble ended his career.
3496=Winner of 4 G1s in 2018 incl. the Eclipse S. and International S., Cartier Horse of the Year. Unbeaten in 3 from August at 2 incl. the Royal Lodge S. (G2), he lost the Racing Post T. by a neck. At 3, 5th in the 2000 Guineas and 3rd in the Derby, then a storming run: the Eclipse S., International S., Irish Champion S. and QEII S. - 4 straight G1s from a mile to 10f. Last in the BC Classic on his first dirt start, but Cartier Horse of the Year and champion 3yo colt. Retired to stud that year, his first crop's Dubai Mile won the Criterium de Saint-Cloud (G1).
3497=Winner of 3 G1s in 2020 incl. the Eclipse S., Cartier Horse of the Year. One of the best by Dubawi, Dubai Millennium's only sire son, he was hyped before his debut. Debuting in September at 2, he won the Autumn S. (G3) in his 3rd start. Injury cost him spring at 3; he won only the Prix du Prince d'Orange (G3) on return. At 4 he won the Grosser Preis von Baden, his first G1, but was 10th in the Arc, his only time unplaced. At 5 he won the Dubai Millennium S. (G3), then the Coronation C., Eclipse S. and International S. - 3 straight G1s. 2nd to Magical in the Irish Champion S., he was Horse of the Year, retired that year, and has stood in Ireland since 2021.
3498=A US mare who won 7 of 8 incl. 3 G1s in 2019, Eclipse champion older mare. Winless from October at 2, she won 3 straight stakes at 3 to the Santa Anita Oaks (G1). Beaten by Monomoy Girl and Eskimo Kisses in the fillies' classics, she won the Cotillion S. (G1), and was 3rd to Monomoy Girl in the BC Distaff. At 4, with rival Monomoy Girl out injured, she ruled: the Apple Blossom H., Ogden Phipps S. and Personal Ensign S. (G1s), her only loss a 2nd in the BC Distaff - champion older mare. At 5 she won the world's richest race, the Saudi Cup, doubling her earnings; 2nd in the Personal Ensign S., she was found to have a fracture and retired.
3499=A US filly who won 7 G1s from 2018 to 2020, Eclipse champion 3yo filly and champion older mare: 14 wins from 17, all 3 losses 2nds. Unbeaten in 3, 2nd in the Golden Rod S. (G2) at 2. At 3 she won the Ashland S., Kentucky Oaks, Acorn S. and CCA Oaks - 4 straight G1s; first past the post in the Cotillion S., she was demoted to 2nd, then won the BC Distaff - undisputed champion. Injured, she missed all of 4. Back in spring at 5 she won 2, then the La Troienne S. and BC Distaff - unbeaten in 4, champion older mare. At 6 she won the Bayakoa S. (G3), lost the Apple Blossom H. by a nose, and retired with a fracture.
3500=A US turf filly who excelled at a mile, Eclipse champion turf female of 2020. Unbeaten in 3 from September at 2 incl. the BC Juvenile Fillies Turf (G1). At 3 she won the Appalachian S. and Lake Placid S. (G2s) and the 9f Queen Elizabeth II Challenge C. (G1). At 4 she won the Jenny Wiley S. and Just a Game S. (G1s), but 4th to Uni in the First Lady S.; Uni, also a 2-time G1 winner, took the Eclipse. At 5 she won the Jenny Wiley S. and Diana S. (G1s), then in her farewell BC Filly & Mare Turf was 2nd by a neck to Audarya - champion turf female at last.
3501=An Australian mare of 2018-2022, a stayer who won 11 G1s incl. the Melbourne Cup and Caulfield Cup. Debuting at 2 in 2017/18, she won the Ethereal S. (G3) in 2018/19, then the Vinery Stud S. and Australian Oaks (G1s). Only the Tancred S. in 2019/20, then a huge 2020/21: the Winx S., Turnbull S., Caulfield Cup, Chipping Norton S. and Ranvet S. - 5 G1s, Australian Horse of the Year. After 3 more G1s in 2021/22 she moved to France for its rich staying races, but best 3rd in the Prix Foy (G2), no G1 impact; her retirement was announced at the end of 2022.
3502=Cartier champion 2yo colt of 2018 and champion 3yo colt of 2019, a central figure in European racing. Winning his debut in August at 2, he won the Solario S. (G3), Champagne S. (G2) and Dewhurst S. (G1) - unbeaten in 4, champion 2yo. Aimed at the 2000 Guineas and Derby at 3, a leg issue held him back; 2nd in the Dante S. (G2) and Irish 2000 Guineas, 3rd in the St. James's Palace S. Dropping to 1400m, he won the Prix Jean Prat (G1), then beat older horses in the Sussex S. (G1). Aimed at the BC Mile, he was found to have a fracture and retired to stud.
3503=A great European miler of 2020-2021, Cartier champion 3yo colt in 2020 and champion older horse in 2021. Watched from debut, he won his first 2 in England from August at 2; at 3 a handicap, then the St. James's Palace S. and Jacques le Marois (G1s) - unbeaten in 5. 3rd in the QEII S., his first loss, but champion 3yo colt. At 4 he won the bet365 Mile (G2), then the Lockinge S., Queen Anne S. and Jacques le Marois - 3 straight mile G1s. 2nd again in the QEII S., he was still champion older horse, and retired to stud in England.
3504=Winner of the 2020 Kentucky Derby (G1) and BC Classic (G1), Eclipse Horse of the Year - a top earner who helped his sire Into Mischief to the North American title. Winning his debut in November at 2, at 3 he won the Sham S. (G3) and San Felipe S. (G2). 2nd in the Santa Anita Derby after a slow start, his first loss, he won the Haskell S. (G1). In a Kentucky Derby moved to autumn by Covid, he beat Belmont winner Tiz the Law (the Belmont, delayed 2 weeks, was run first). 2nd in the Preakness, he won the BC Classic over favorite Tiz the Law again, and retired to stud that year.
3505=A US filly who won 5 G1s in 2020-2021, Eclipse champion female sprinter of 2020. Winning her debut in March at 3, she won 3 straight to the Acorn S. (G1) by 18 3/4 lengths, then the Test S. (G1) by 7. Crossing 3rd in the Kentucky Oaks, she was disqualified for a drug test. She won the BC Filly & Mare Sprint in record time - champion female sprinter. At 4 she won 4 straight incl. the Derby City Distaff S. and Ballerina H. for a 5th G1 - unbeatable in filly sprints - but was 3rd to Ce Ce in her 2nd BC Filly & Mare Sprint, and retired.
3506=Winner of 4 straight G1s in 2021 - Poulains, French Derby, Eclipse S. and Irish Champion S. - Cartier Horse of the Year. Named after St. Mark's Basilica in Venice. French-bred, he debuted in Ireland and won a maiden in his 3rd start; 3rd in the Vincent O'Brien National S. (G1), he won the Dewhurst S. (G1) to end the year. At 3 he won the Poulains, the French Derby as favorite by 1 3/4 lengths, the Eclipse S. as favorite over older horses by 3.5 lengths, and the Irish Champion S., a tight 4-runner race - 5 straight G1s. Cartier Horse of the Year and champion 3yo colt, he retired to stud in Ireland that year.
