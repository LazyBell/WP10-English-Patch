2784=The 3rd unbeaten Triple Crown winner, after Deep Impact. With Daring Tact also winning the fillies' Triple Crown unbeaten, 2020 was the "double triple." Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Hopeful S., he was the classic focus: in the Satsuki Sho he beat Asahi Hai FS winner Salios by half a length after a duel, then in the Derby led early in the straight and crushed him. In autumn he won the Kobe Shimbun Hai without the whip. A week after Daring Tact's triumph, under even more pressure, he was shadowed all the way by summer improver Aristoteles in the Kikuka Sho and won a long, grinding duel by a neck - the 8th Triple Crown winner, unbeaten like his sire Deep Impact. His camp and Daring Tact's agreed on a Japan Cup clash of unbeaten Triple Crown winners; 8-G1 champion Almond Eye joined - the race of the century. The senior star won, Contrail 2nd and Daring Tact 3rd, thrilling fans. Older, in the Osaka Hai against sprint queen Gran Alegria, he was 3rd behind runaway longshot Rei Papale. In the Tenno Sho (Autumn) he was 2nd to 3yo Satsuki winner Efforia - ahead of Gran Alegria both times, but doubts arose. In his farewell Japan Cup, billed vs. 3yo Derby winner Shahryar, he won as decisively as in his Triple Crown year - a 5th G1. Retiring to stud as Deep Impact's heir, his first crop is eagerly awaited.
