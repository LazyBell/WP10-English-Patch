3436=Winner of the 1988 Preakness S. and Belmont S.; his Belmont win by 14 3/4 lengths recalled his sire Secretariat. Until early at 3 he was a Louisiana local star, winning 5 of 7 with 2 seconds. Winning the Lexington S. (G2), he went national as a Kentucky Derby contender; 3rd behind Winning Colors and Forty Niner, he beat both in the Preakness, then crushed the Belmont field - best of his crop. Injury then ended his career; Eclipse champion 3yo, he went to stud. Hoped to be Secretariat's heir, he sired just one G1 winner and died at 13. In Japan he is known as the sire of 1993 Rose S. (G2) winner Star Ballerina.
3437=A late bloomer who won the 1991 BC Classic (G1) at 5. Raced hard early - 8 starts at 2, 16 at 3 - without standing out, he won the Sheridan S. (G3) in autumn at 3, then was 8th in the BC Sprint. At 4 he improved, winning 3 G2s and G3s, and was 3rd in the BC Sprint. At 5 he switched from sprints to middle distances in June and won 3 straight stakes, then the Philip H. Iselin H. for his first G1, a G2, and the BC Classic - 6 straight. He retired as Eclipse Horse of the Year. His first crop included 2-time G1 winner Formal Gold, plus Washington Color, a 3-time graded winner in Japan; later imported to Japan, he sired JBC Sprint winner Fujino Wave and many local stars.
3438=A US sprint filly of 1989-1991 with 24 wins from 31, never worse than 4th - a long-time force. At 2 she won 3 of 5. At 3 she won 8 straight incl. the Test S. (G1) and Prioress S. (G2); 2nd by a neck in the BC Sprint, she was still Eclipse champion sprinter. At 4 she won 7 of 9, then in the BC Sprint fought a fierce duel with hot favorite Dayjur, who jumped a shadow from the stands, and she won. At 5 she won 5 of 7, but was injured before the BC Sprint and retired. As a broodmare, 9 winners incl. 2 stakes winners.
3439=An Australian gelding who won 7 G1s from 1990 to 1992 and was 3rd to Golden Pheasant in the 11th Japan Cup. Sprinting early, he won the Royal Sovereign S. (G3) at 3, also 2nd in 3 G1s. At 4 he won the George Main S. (G1) at a mile, plus 2 more mile G1s and 2 sprint G1s - dominant at shorter trips. At 5 he tried middle distances, winning the 2000m Caulfield S. (G1); 12th of 14 in the 2040m Cox Plate, he went further still in the Japan Cup, and as hot favorite Mejiro McQueen faltered, finished 3rd behind Golden Pheasant and Magic Night. After a long rest he won the Warwick S. (G1) at 6 and retired.
3440=A European stayer of the early 1990s, racing mostly at 14f and beyond, winner of the Irish St Leger and Australia's Melbourne Cup. Debuting late in autumn at 4, he won over hurdles too. 5th in his first G1, the Irish St Leger, at 5; after 6th in the Champion Hurdle Challenge T. (G1) at 6, he focused on the flat. That autumn he won the Irish St Leger, then the Melbourne Cup - the first winner from outside Oceania. At 7 he won the Irish St Leger again, 7th as Melbourne Cup favorite. At 8, 4th and 3rd in the same races, and he retired. A gelding, he lived in Ireland until 27.
3441=Winner of the 1990 fillies' double in England, and the first filly in 90 years to win the Irish Derby (G1) - a great mare of 1990s Europe. Winning her debut easily in September at 2, after a 2nd she won the Prix Marcel Boussac (G1). At 3 she won the Fred Darling S. (G3), then the 1000 Guineas as favorite, and the Oaks by 5 lengths. Instead of the Irish Oaks she took on Derby winner Quest for Fame in the Irish Derby and beat him. She won the Prix Vermeille, then was Arc favorite over In the Wings and Belmez, but flat, 10th, and retired. She died at 9, but produced 3 stakes winners.
3442=A mare who debuted in Argentina, then moved to the US and won 10 G1s incl. the BC Distaff. Debuting at 3 in 1990/91, she won a G1 and 2 G2s. Moving to the US in late 1991, she won 2 G2s, then in 1992 5 straight G1s incl. the Santa Maria H., Santa Margarita Invitational H. and Apple Blossom H. After 2 losses she won the BC Distaff easily - 7 wins from 9 with 6 G1s, Eclipse champion older mare. In 1993 she won the Apple Blossom H., Milady H. and Spinster S. - champion older mare again. In 1994 the Santa Margarita Invitational H., a 10th G1. Racing in 1995, she won 1 of 6 and retired. Few foals.
3443=A speedster called "America's best sprinter" in 1990-1991, the first to be Eclipse champion sprinter twice running. Debuting in October at 2, he won 2 of 3. At 3, ignoring the classics, he won 5 straight sprint stakes; 2nd by a neck to that year's Horse of the Year Criminal Type in the Metropolitan H. (G1), he won 3 more incl. the Jerome H. (G1) by 13 lengths - champion sprinter. At 4 he won the Carter H. and Vosburgh S. (G1s); hurt just after the start of his farewell BC Sprint, he was 9th, but champion sprinter again. At stud in the US, and in Japan for 3 years from 1999, he sired no superstar but over 30 stakes winners incl. 2002 Hong Kong Horse of the Year Electronic Unicorn.
3444=A great British sprinter who in 1991 became the first European-trained BC Sprint (G1) winner. First winning at 3, he won the Nunthorpe S. (G1) that summer for his first stakes, then was 2nd in the Sprint C. and Prix de l'Abbaye (both G1). In America, on his first dirt start as 9th choice, he crushed US stars like Housebuster and Pleasant Tap in the BC Sprint - Cartier champion sprinter. At 4 he won the Sprint C. (G1) and King's Stand S. (G2) from 6, then 4th defending the BC Sprint, and retired. No stars at stud, but many winners.
3445=A French horse who blossomed after moving to the US; in 1993 he won 5 G1s and was Eclipse Horse of the Year. He also ran in that year's Japan Cup, famously 2nd after jockey Kent Desormeaux misjudged the finish line. Racing in France to the end of 3, he won 3 of 8 with just a G3. In the US at 4 he won only an allowance from 4. At 5 he emerged: a G2, then 3 straight G1s - San Luis Rey S., San Juan Capistrano H., Eddie Read H. - a G2 2nd, then the Oak Tree Invitational S. and BC Turf, top US turf horse. 2nd to Legacy World in the Japan Cup, he was Horse of the Year and retired after it. At stud in Japan he sired almost no stars.
3446=A durable US mare of 1991-1994 who won 9 G1s, Eclipse champion turf female in 1992 and 1993. Debuting in June at 2, she won 3 of 7 incl. 2 G3s. At 3 she won 2 more G3s and the Matriarch S., her first G1. At 4 she won the Beverly Hills S. and Ramona H., 2 seconds, then the Matriarch again - champion turf female. At 5 she won the Beverly Hills H., Ramona H. and Beverly D. S. - 3 straight G1s; 9th in the BC Mile, she won a 3rd Matriarch - champion again. At 6 she won a 3rd Ramona H. for a 9th G1, and retired after a 2nd in the Beverly D. S. Kidney disease took her early with just 2 foals, but she entered the US Hall of Fame.
3447=Winner of both the 1992 2000 Guineas and Irish 2000 Guineas, with 5 G1s at a mile to 10f. Debuting in July at 2, he won 5 straight to the Middle Park S. (G1), but crop-mate Arazi, Cartier Horse of the Year at 2, took the honors. At 3, beaten in the Greenham S. (G3), he won both 2000 Guineas, then the International S. and Champion S. - 5 G1s. Last in the BC Classic on his first dirt start, he retired to stud in Japan. His 2nd crop had Oaks winner Erimo Excel; graded winners Ibuki Yamano O and Grace Namura followed.
3448=A star miler in Europe and America in 1993-1994; his 1994 BC Mile (G1) made him Cartier Horse of the Year with just one G1 that year. Unbeaten in 2 from October at 2, at 3 he was 2nd in the 2000 Guineas and won the Irish 2000 Guineas. 5th in the Derby and Eclipse S., less effective further, he focused on the mile: 4th Prix du Moulin, 2nd QEII S., 5th BC Mile - winless but suited. At 4 he won the Queen Anne S. (G2), then kept placing, until a 2nd BC Mile try; thoroughly prepared after the year before, he won by 3 lengths and retired. A successful sire with over 80 stakes winners.
3449=Winner of the 1993 German Derby, Grosser Preis von Baden and Grosser Preis des Jockey Club; at 5 in 1995 he won the 15th Japan Cup, beating Narita Brian and Hishi Amazon. Debuting in August at 2, he won 2 listed races from 3. At 3, 5th, 6th and 7th, he won the German Derby as 12th choice, then the Grosser Preis von Baden. At 4 he won the Hansa-Preis (G2), Baden again and the Jockey Club race; 8th in the Arc, but German Horse of the Year. At 5 he won the Gran Premio di Milano and Merck Finck-Bank Preis (G1s), 4th in the Arc, then the Japan Cup, holding off Hishi Amazon's charge - Horse of the Year again. Retired to stud that year, he sired G1 winners Paolini, Intendant and more.
