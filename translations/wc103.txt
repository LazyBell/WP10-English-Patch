2521=Winner of 5 dirt G1s by spring at 6, a leader of dirt racing in 2017-2019. Unbeaten in 3, 2nd in his first graded race, the Hyogo Championship, he won the Unicorn S., catching gritty leader and favorite Strong Barows. Favorite in the Japan Dirt Derby, he was 3rd; 2nd choice in the Champions C., he faded to 12th. But at 4, never out of the top two at Tokyo 1600m, he won the February S. from midfield, his long-sought G1. A dry spell ended with a surge from the Champions C. - the 3rd horse to sweep both JRA dirt G1s in a year. At 5 he won the Kashiwa Kinen and Teio Sho, 2nd in every other race. At 6, 2nd in the February S., he won the Kashiwa Kinen again, then lost narrowly to 3yo star Chrysoberyl in the Champions C., still the senior leader. At 7, fading with an unplaced run, he was 2nd to Chuwa Wizard in the Champions C., ahead of Chrysoberyl - revenge.
2522=Brother of 2-time Victoria Mile winner Verxina, sister of Shuka Sho winner Vivlos - a blue-blooded family owned by ex-baseball player Kazuhiro Sasaki. He won the Japan Cup at 5. Missing the classics, he won 3 allowances in autumn at 3, then the Hanshin Daishoten at 4, and was 3rd to Kitasan Black as 3rd choice in the Tenno Sho (Spring), his first G1. He won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai and was 3rd to Kitasan Black again in the Japan Cup. At 5, 2nd in the Hanshin Daishoten and to Kitasan Black in the Tenno Sho (Spring), ahead of Satono Diamond. In his 2nd Japan Cup, 5th choice behind Kitasan Black and younger Derby winner Rey de Oro, he tracked Kitasan Black and took over in the last 100m to win. 3rd in the Arima Kinen, at 6 he was 2nd in the Tenno Sho (Spring) and 3rd in the Arima Kinen again. At 7 he raced abroad: 2nd in the Dubai Sheema Classic, 6th in the King George like his sire Heart's Cry, and 8th in the International S., the first Japanese runner since Zenno Rob Roy. In a 4th Arima Kinen, at a career-long 135.4 to 1, he ran on under old partner Fukunaga for 6th and retired.
2523=Winner of the Fairy S., the first fillies' graded race of the year, leading all the way as 3rd choice. In a strong crop with Major Emblem and Jeweler, she was 9th in the Oka Sho, then turned to dirt, 2nd in the Onuma S. (OP) and 3rd behind Amour Briller in the Breeders' Gold C.
2524=A Roses in May runner from Mombetsu who won the Hyogo Junior GP, then was 3rd against JRA horses in the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun.
2525=The 2016 2yo champion. 2nd on debut at Sapporo, he won a Nakayama maiden and the Begonia Sho, then as 6th choice in a strong Asahi Hai FS unleashed a Deep Impact-like kick to win - jockey Shii's first G1 since the 2010 Takamatsunomiya Kinen (Kinshasa no Kiseki). Favorite in the Spring S. at 3, he was 4th, then 11th in the Satsuki Sho. Winless in graded races since, he won the Tomoe Sho (OP) and was 2nd in the Capital S. (OP).
2526=Winning his 3rd start, steady, he won the Spring S. in his 6th race with a powerful finish from midfield. A "grey Stay Gold colt" like Gold Ship, he was expected to shine, but was 8th in the Satsuki Sho and 15th in the Derby. Jockey Matsuoka still rated him; in autumn he broke clear from up front to win the Fukushima Kinen. Older he won the Nakayama Kinen, but flopped in G1s like the Osaka Hai and Mile CS. At 5 he won the Nakayama Kimpai and Nakayama Kinen - 5 graded wins - then the QEII Cup in Hong Kong, beating crop-mates Lys Gracieux and Deirdre. Unplaced in the All Comers and Tenno Sho (Autumn), he won a fierce duel in the Hong Kong Cup - Hong Kong G1s in spring and autumn. At 6, defending the Hong Kong Cup, he burst clear from midfield but was caught by fellow Japanese Normcore, 2nd.
2527=Out of Fortune Word, a filly who ran in the Asahi Hai FS. Hoped to shine on turf, he stalled in 3 races, then won 5 straight on dirt and his first graded race, the Heian S. A chip fracture cost a year; back in the Antares S. at 2.2, he moved up at the 4th turn and pulled clear. Favorite in the Heian S. and Sirius S., he was 5th and 11th.
2528=A late-blooming Neo Universe colt: 6 starts to win, 20 to pass the 10-million class. Then he bloomed, winning 2 to open class and the Niigata Daishoten. A tendon injury cost 2 years; back at 7 he lost badly, and from 8 has raced on dirt, out of the top five but for a 2nd in the Azuma Kofuji Sho.
2529=A Manhattan Cafe colt with a sharp kick from up front; half-brother of Hanshin Himba S. winner Smart Layer. Emerging with a 4th in the Mainichi Hai, he was 2nd choice in the Kyoto Shimbun Hai and narrowly caught favorite Satono Chronicle from midfield, earning a Derby spot - but a fracture of the right radius was found, and he had to rest.
2530=He won 3 straight at 3 incl. the Aoba Sho, a Derby fancy. Winning a maiden only in March at 3 and a 5-million race easily, he was Aoba Sho favorite and surged from last through the 3rd-4th turns, leaving everyone behind. Derby favorite, he couldn't catch Rey de Oro and Suave Richard, 3rd. Leg trouble meant 2 years off; with a 2019 return planned, he broke his pelvis and retired without racing again.
2531=By imported sire Harbinger, she won the QEII Cup at 3 - with crop-mates Deirdre and Persian Knight, 3 G1 winners for Harbinger in a year. Winning a maiden at 3, she won a 5-million race and the Flora S. as 12th choice, charging from midfield. 6th choice in the Oaks, she was 2nd to Soul Stirring, well clear of 3rd. 7th in the Rose S. and 3rd in the Shuka Sho, she won a star-studded QEII Cup - Vivlos, Mikki Queen, Rouge Buck, Shuka Sho winner Deirdre - racing forward and holding off Mikki Queen. At 4 she was 6th in the Dubai Sheema Classic, 3rd in the Sapporo Kinen, then 3rd as favorite in the QEII Cup behind Lys Gracieux.
2532=A Deep Sky colt who won the 2017 Antares S. Slow to open class, he was 2nd as 12th choice in the Tokai S. at 5, won the Aldebaran S. (OP), was 4th in the Nagoya Daishoten, then won the Antares S.
2533=From the first crop of Jo Cappuccino, NHK Mile C. winner as 10th choice. 5th in the Arlington C. and 6th in the Falcon S., he won the NZT as 12th choice from a handy spot, with 8th choice Mason Junior 2nd from the front - a big upset. Bidding to match his sire in the NHK Mile C., he was 12th, then last in the Derby and Mile CS.
2534=A big colt, hyped from his debut and popular for his distinctive face, but slow to rise and missing the classics. Winning steadily when older, he reached open class, and at 5 in the Lord Derby CT tracked in 2nd and held off Campbell Junior by half a length - his first graded win.
2535=Needing 7 starts to win, he improved with each drop in trip, quickly reaching open class in turf sprints. 2nd in his first graded race, the Silk Road S., after leading briefly, he went to the Takamatsunomiya Kinen; with Oka Sho winner Let's Go Donki and Sprinters S. winner Red Falx fancied, he alone found another gear and won. Then he faded: 4th as favorite in the Hakodate Sprint S., 11th as 3rd choice in the Sprinters S., far back in the Swan S. At 5, 2nd in the Silk Road S., 6th in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, he won the Hakodate Sprint S. At 6, 15th in the Silk Road S., he was 2nd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and 3rd in the CBC Sho, then unplaced. At 7 an occasional 5th; at 8, 3rd in the Keeneland C., he retired to stud after the Hanshin C.
2536=A blueblood named after a rare dessert wine. Unwell, he debuted only in June at 3, then rose steadily and was 2nd as 2nd choice in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai at 4, his first graded race. He won the Nikkei Sho over Arima Kinen winner Gold Actor and Satsuki winner Dee Majesty - a spring star. 9th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), tired, he was 4th as 2nd choice in the Takarazuka Kinen. Unplaced in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), Japan Cup and Arima Kinen, at 6 he won the AJCC and Hanshin Daishoten, but fractured a leg after a workout and was put down.
2537=A filly who ran in the Satsuki Sho as favorite - a unique record. Debuting in January at 3, she won her debut by 9 lengths, the Tsubaki Sho and the Flower C. by 5, all from up front with speed. Skipping the fillies' classics, she went for the Satsuki Sho, bidding to be the first filly winner in 69 years, but didn't quicken, 7th. 6th in the Rose S., she was 13th as 2nd choice in the Shuka Sho.
2538=A Victoire Pisa sprinter. After winning his debut and the Kurumi Sho, he was 4th as 2nd choice in the Keio Hai 2yo S. - a strong race, won by later Asahi Hai runner-up Mondo Cannon with later Oka Sho winner Reine Minoru 2nd - and won the Falcon S. the next year. Laminitis followed, and he died that November; the Falcon S. was his last race.
2539=A quirky Lohengrin filly with a sharp kick. She won the Fillies' Revue in her 4th start and closed for 4th in the Oka Sho, dispelling stamina doubts. Favorite in the NHK Mile C., she lacked her kick, 17th.
2540=A rare Fastnet Rock runner in Japan, with big weight swings. 502kg at his debut at 3, he grew to 522kg in summer at 4 and won fast to open class. +10kg, he was 14th in the Keihan Hai; -8kg the next year, he won the Ocean S. 3rd choice in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen at +14kg, he was 10th.
2541=Steady and versatile, but long breaks kept him from winning much. In his first graded race, the Hankyu Hai, his 21st start, he closed brilliantly to win as 7th choice.
2542=A dirt sprint closer with a brilliant finish. Often falling short from the back, he reached open class in summer at 4 and was 4th in the Champions C. He won the Negishi S. as favorite, then was 3rd as favorite in the February S. 5th in Dubai's Godolphin Mile, he has been 2nd in the Procyon S. and 4th in the Nambu Hai since.
2543=A Bago colt who won the Keisei Hai with a steady finish; 2nd to later Derby winner Rey de Oro in the Habotan Sho. 6th as 3rd choice in the Yayoi Sho and 14th as 16th choice in the Satsuki Sho, he suffered a suspensory injury and never returned, retired in summer at 4.
2544=A Black Tide filly like Kitasan Black. She won the Fairy S. in her 3rd start as 10th choice with the fastest finish, and the Anemone S. too, but her kick misfired in the Oka Sho.
2545=Named after the son of an acquaintance of his owner. A mudlark, he was 2nd to later Satsuki Sho winner Al Ain in the Senryo Sho, then won the Shinzan Kinen on heavy ground with a finish from another dimension - his sire Reach the Crown's first graded winner. 14th in the NHK Mile C. and 12th in the Derby, he ran steadily in open races without winning, then dropped to win the Tarumi S. (16M). 8th in the Kokura Kinen, he raced in open and listed races without a win, switched to dirt at 8, and moved to local racing after the Sobu S. (OP) at 9.
