1573=For trainer Kunihide Matsuda, the ultimate horse was one who could win both the NHK Mile C. and the Derby - the brutal 2-week route stablemates Kurofune and Tanino Gimlet had tried, each winning just one. He won the Kobe Shimbun Hai flawlessly in autumn, and was to run in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) instead of the Kikuka Sho, where, given his power and rivals, he seemed sure to follow Bubble Gum Fellow and Symboli Kris S as a 3yo winner. In 2012 Lord Kanaloa won a G1 abroad, and later came dirt king Hokko Tarumae, double classic winner Duramente, Rey de Oro and Lovely Day - a leading non-Sunday Silence sire. He retired from stud in 2019 with ill health and died that year, aged 18. Through many heirs like Lord Kanaloa, his blood will keep spreading.
