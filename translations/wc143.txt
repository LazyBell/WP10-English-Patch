3257=An American filly star of 1963-1964, out of great mare Cosmah and half-sister to Halo, sire of Sunday Silence. Debuting at 2 in 1963, she won 7 straight - 7 of 8 that year, Daily Racing Form champion 2yo filly. At 3 she won the Arlington Classic S., the first filly winner in 17 years, and beat veteran Old Hat in the Maskette H., winning 10 of 14 - US champion 3yo filly and DRF champion handicap mare. She won 3 more at 4 and 3 at 5, a long career. Rarely in foal, she left only 4 foals, 1 stakes winner, and could not carry on the great female line of her granddam Almahmoud and dam Cosmah.
3258=A sire son left in America by legendary Ribot, hugely successful racing and at stud - called Ribot's best heir. Inconsistent at 2, he blossomed at 3: after 3 straight wins, 3rd in the Kentucky Derby, he won the Preakness, beating favored Lucky Debonair. In autumn he did the unusual for a US classic winner and ran in the Arc; on his first trip abroad and first turf start, he was a fine 6th to Sea-Bird in a field of 20 called the strongest ever. 9 wins from 13, US champion 3yo. He raced until autumn at 4. At stud he sired Hoist the Flag, Run the Gantlet, Bowl Game and more; Hoist the Flag sired Alleged, carrying on the line.
3259=An Argentine great, unbeaten in 6 incl. the Argentine Triple Crown, then the Gran Premio Carlos Pellegrini against older horses - an unbeaten quadruple crown. Moving to the US, he won the Coronado H. and an exhibition race, but was hampered and 2nd in the Citation H., his first loss; a fracture found after ended his career. At stud he sired 3-time Eclipse Horse of the Year Forego, plus Thatch, Formidable, Posse and more, successful sires who carried his line. His daughter Special, dam of Nureyev and granddam of Sadler's Wells, spread his blood worldwide through dams.
3260=The 1967 Preakness and Belmont winner. With Buckpasser and Dr. Fager he formed a big three, and their 1967 Woodward S. clash is legendary. Winning 3 of 4 from autumn at 2, he was classic favorite at 3; worked up in the Kentucky Derby, he was 3rd to outsider Proud Clarion, then won the Preakness and Belmont as favorite. That autumn came the Woodward: older superstar Buckpasser, crop-mate Dr. Fager from a separate path, and Damascus - the clash of the century. He beat both by 10 lengths, a legend; Horse of the Year. At 4 he won 6, but Horse of the Year went to Dr. Fager at his peak, and he retired to stud. Hugely successful, he sired Private Account, Desert Wine, Ogygian and more - a rare carrier of the Teddy male line.
3261=The 1967 2000 Guineas and Derby winner, one of Ballymoss's best. Debuting in autumn at 2, unplaced first, he won 2 straight, then the 2000 Guineas and Derby at 3 - 4 straight. Aimed at the Triple Crown in the St Leger, his preparation failed; 3rd to Reform in the Champion S. instead. At 4 he improved, winning the Coronation S., Coronation C., Prince of Wales's S. and Eclipse S., then the King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. as hot favorite - Britain's best older horse. Injury then ended his career. Not a great sire, but his best was Oaks and St Leger winner Dunfermline.
3262=A brilliant American filly by Bold Ruler, honored 3 years running: champion 3yo filly, then champion handicap mare at 4 and 5. Debuting late at 3, she had only a maiden win by spring. That summer she won the Princess S., then the Test S. and Alabama S.; though she missed after, no one stood out and she was champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won the Santa Maria H., Santa Margarita H. and Wilshire H., and was a close 2nd to peak Dr. Fager against males in the Californian S. 7 wins from 14, champion handicap mare; at 5, 5 from 13, champion again, and she retired. She died young with just 2 foals, but one, Cellini, won England's Dewhurst S.
3263=Winner of the 1968 2000 Guineas and Derby, plus the Champion S. and America's Washington DC International - Sir Gaylord's best, a success racing and at stud. Winning 3 of 4 at 2, at 3 he won the 2000 Guineas Trial, 2000 Guineas and Derby. Then a surprise 2nd in the Irish Derby, 3rd to previous dual classic winner Royal Palace in the Eclipse S., 2nd to Vaguely Noble in the Arc, before winning the Champion S. In the Washington DC International he beat previous winner Fort Marcy and Japan's Takeshiba O. He retired to stud in the US, siring 94 stakes winners incl. Arc winner Ivanjica, and was champion broodmare sire in Britain and Ireland in 1983.
3264=In 1968 she became the first winner of the New York Filly Triple Crown, said to be as good as famed US fillies Ruffian and Go for Wand. Very small, she was rated low early and won just 2 of 15 by the end of 2. At 3 she was transformed: an allowance, the Prioress S. and La Troienne S., then the Kentucky Oaks as favorite by 4.5 lengths. In the Triple Crown she won the Acorn S. by 6, Mother Goose S. by 10 and CCA Oaks by 12 - rivals gave up, saying no one could beat her. 2 more wins; undisputed champion 3yo filly. At 4, handicap weights bore down on her small frame; she won her opener narrowly, then broke down in the Santa Margarita H. Surgery saved her at first, but laminitis followed and she was put down.
3265=The 1968 Arc winner. As favorite, he crushed dual classic winner Sir Ivor, Irish Derby winner Ribero, German Triple Crown winner Luciano and French Oaks winner Roseliere - a performance compared to Sea-Bird's. Winning 2 of 4 at 2 in England, he moved to France at 3 under a new owner. Not entered for the classics, he aimed at the Arc, winning the Prix de Guiche, Prix du Lys and Prix de Chantilly. In a stacked Arc he beat Sir Ivor by 3 lengths, earning a Timeform rating of 140. He retired after it. A great sire too, with champion mare Dahlia, Exceller, Empery and more, he was champion sire in Britain and Ireland in 1973 and 1974. Gay Mecene and Mississipian, imported to Japan as sires, are also among his best.
3266=One of the most successful of the Herod line, he spread it worldwide through Arc winner Sagace, Yawa, No Lute and many more. Racing in France, he won the Prix Jacques le Marois and Prix Lupin; the French Derby and Arc were a bit far for him. He shone at stud: top first-season sire in 1973, he went on to be French champion sire 4 times. Sagace sired BC Classic winner Arcangues, and Dandy Lute, imported to Japan, led to Bizen Nishiki and Daitaku Helios. His male line has faded, but Lorenzaccio, Klairon's other leading son, keeps the Herod line alive.
3267=Raise a Native is best known for Mr. Prospector, but Majestic Prince is also a key sire son carrying his line today. His best include Belmont winner Coastal and Swaps S. winner Majestic Light. Majestic Light, also sire of Nishino Flower, was grandsire of Maria's Mon, whose sons Monarchos and Super Saver succeeded at stud, building a branch second only to the Mr. Prospector line. A fine racehorse, he won the 1969 Kentucky Derby and Preakness unbeaten, but racing with a leg problem in the Belmont he was 2nd to Arts and Letters, just missing the Triple Crown.
3268=A 1969 US classic star who won the Belmont S. Battling rival Majestic Prince, he lost the first 2 classics but won the last. Modest at 2 (2 of 6), he improved at 3, winning the Everglades S. and Blue Grass S. 2nd by a neck to Majestic Prince in the Kentucky Derby and by a head in the Preakness, he won the Belmont, denying Majestic Prince an unbeaten Triple Crown. With his rival retired injured, he dominated, winning the Jim Dandy S., Travers S., Woodward S. and Jockey Club Gold C. - Horse of the Year. Injured in spring at 4, he retired. At stud he sired Preakness winner Codex.
3269=A Chilean horse who moved to the US and was 1972 Eclipse champion turf horse. Good on turf and dirt, he was hugely popular in his California base; "cougar" means puma, so he was nicknamed "Big Cat." In Chile he won 5 of 12 by the end of 3 (late June, being southern-born). Moving to the US at 4, he won on both surfaces; in 1972, late 5 into early 6, he won 4 of 9 with 3 seconds and 2 thirds - champion turf horse. In 1973 he won 3 incl. the Santa Anita H. and Century H., and retired. At stud he sired 24 stakes winners incl. Kentucky Derby winner Gato Del Sol. His male line faded, but he is broodmare sire of sire Charlot.
3270=A standout from the rare Himyar line, he succeeded racing and at stud, keeping that line alive. Winning a maiden at 2, he improved at 3, winning the Derby Trial S., then, skipping the Kentucky Derby, the Withers S. and Arlington Classic S. at a mile. At 4 he won 4 of 5 incl. the Los Angeles H. and Autumn Days H. At 5 he peaked: after a 2nd, he won 6 straight handicaps incl. the San Carlos H. and San Pasqual H., the Santa Anita H. over Cougar in his first try at 10f, and the Hollywood Gold C. under 134 lb (60.5kg). Horse of the Year, he retired at year-end. At stud he sired later North American champion sire Broad Brush, French Derby winner Youth and 54 stakes winners in all.
3271=Dr. Fager's half-sister, who ruled US sprinting in 1969-1970, champion sprinter twice - a match for her brother. Watched from debut, she won 2 at 2, then shone at 3: the Jasmine S., Prioress S., and the Comely S. over later New York Filly Triple Crown winner Shuvee. Skipping the Triple Crown for sprints, she won the Miss Woodford S., Test S. and Fall Highweight H., then the Vosburgh H. over Shuvee and top older mare Gamely - champion sprinter. At 4 she won the Correction H., Hempstead H. and Regret H., then the Fall Highweight H. under 140 lb and the Interborough H. under 142 lb - astonishing strength. Champion handicap mare went to Shuvee, but she retired as champion sprinter again. A fine broodmare: 4 of her 5 foals won stakes; one, Great Above, sired Holy Bull, carrying on Ta Wee's blood.
3272=In 1969 she became the 2nd winner of the New York Filly Triple Crown. Behind contemporaries Gamely, Ta Wee and Gallant Bloom, she raced long and set the North American earnings record for a mare. Struggling early, she won her 7th start in August at 2, then the Frizette S. and Selima S. Triple Crown favorite at 3, she won the Acorn S. by 3/4 length, Mother Goose S. by 2.5 and CCA Oaks by 3 - the 2nd ever. She also won the Alabama S. and Ladies H., but lost twice to Ta Wee and 3 times to Gallant Bloom, who took the titles.
