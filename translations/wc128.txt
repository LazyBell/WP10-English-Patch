At 7, 2nd as 5th choice in the Kashiwa Kinen; at 8, 8th and 5th, he moved to local racing.

2998=Passing the allowance ranks only in January at 5 with the Buzen S., she won the Marine C. in her first open race. 6th as favorite in the Sparking Lady C., 2nd to Reine Blanche in the Ladies' Prelude, she won the JBC Ladies' Classic as favorite, bursting clear from midfield by 2.5 lengths over Madras Check. 2999=The 2022 Satsuki Sho winner. His sire Drefong, an American of Storm Cat's line, went to stud in Japan; from his first crop (debuting 2021), he was the first G1 winner. Unbeaten in 2, he was 5th to Do Deuce in the Asahi Hai FS (G1) and 2nd in the Kyodo News Hai at 3. 5th choice in the Satsuki Sho, he moved up from a handy spot at the 4th turn and pulled away from favorites Equinox and Danon Beluga - a classic, and his sire's first G1. Then 7th in the Derby, 9th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), 6th in the Hong Kong Cup. At 4 on dirt: 4th in the Saudi Cup, then 11th, 9th, 15th. Back on turf at 5: 3rd Nakayama Kinen, 5th Osaka Hai, 2nd Sapporo Kinen, 5th in the BC Mile. At 6 in Australia, 18th and 13th; a fracture ended his career.

3002=Winner of 5 G1s incl. the 2022 Derby - with crop-mate Equinox, one of the best of his era. Debuting in September at 2, unbeaten in 3, he won the Asahi Hai FS (G1). Classic favorite, 3rd to Geoglyph in the Satsuki Sho, he beat Equinox in the Derby - top of his crop. In the Arc he couldn't show his best, 19th. At 4 he was scratched in Dubai with a leg problem, then well beaten by Equinox in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Japan Cup - a slump. With Equinox retired, he won the Arima Kinen for a 3rd G1, back on top: reunited with Yutaka Take, back from injury, he raced at the back, crept forward from 600m out, was 2nd into the straight and passed Titleholder with the fastest finish - a masterful ride. At 5, 5th in the Dubai Turf and 6th in the Takarazuka Kinen, he won the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Japan Cup in autumn - best in training. Bidding for the autumn triple in the Arima Kinen, he was withdrawn lame and retired. Will his foals inherit his rare talent?
