2673=Unbeaten in 2, a classic fancy, she was 11th in the Flower C. and restarted in allowances. Winning 2 classes, she was 2nd in the Kyoto Himba S. at 4, then won it at 5 as 9th choice, running on from a handy spot past favorite Miss Panthere and others. 3rd in the Shin'etsu S. (L) that autumn.
2674=3rd in the Spring S., he was 13th to Epoca d'Oro in the Satsuki Sho. Winning 3 allowances by spring at 4, he was 2nd in the Hakodate Kinen at once, then mostly unplaced, his only placing a 2nd in the Tomoe Sho (OP) at 6. Two races later he won the Niigata Kinen as 12th choice, charging from the back by half a length.
2675=Debuting late, in December at 2, he won his debut and a 5-million race, then was 2nd to Blast Onepiece in the Mainichi Hai and to K A Knotech in the NHK Mile C., and rested instead of the Derby. 13th in the St. Lite Kinen, he won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai as favorite after a fierce duel. Older he mostly disappointed, top five only in the Lord Derby CT and Naruo Kinen. Then at 6, last choice of 10 in the February Kinko Sho, he led all the way and held off hot favorite Daring Tact by a neck - his first win in 2 years 4 months. At 7, 5th in the Kinko Sho and 4th in the Naruo Kinen, he retired after the Chunichi Shimbun Hai.
2676=A rising star of 2019 who won both mile G1s at 4. Noticed from his debut, he won it and a 5-million race, but was 3rd in the Mainichi Hai and 4th in the Arlington C., restarting in allowances. Talented, he soon won 2 classes, then the Tokyo Shimbun Hai as favorite at 4; Yuichi Fukunaga said he "still has room to grow." In the Yasuda Kinen, with Almond Eye (5 straight G1s) and Milers C. winner Danon Premium dominating the betting, he was 4th choice far behind, but spurted from a handy spot and passed leader Aerolithe for his first G1. In the Mile CS, 3rd choice behind Danon Premium and 3yo Danon Kingly, he sat handy and pulled away in the straight - the 7th to win both mile G1s in a year, the first since Maurice in 2015. Like Maurice he went for the Hong Kong Mile, 2nd choice behind local hero Beauty Generation, but was blocked in the straight, 7th. At 5 he won the Milers C., then was 2nd choice behind Almond Eye in the Yasuda Kinen; Gran Alegria split them, he was 3rd. Straight into the Mile CS, he seemed to have it won but was caught near the line by Gran Alegria, 2nd - ceding the mile crown to a new star. At 1.5 in the Hanshin C., his first 1400m turf race since his debut, he couldn't keep up early and closed fastest for 3rd.
2677=A miler who was 2nd in the Arlington C. at 3. 6th as 4th choice in the NHK Mile C., he grew in autumn, won the Rigel S. (OP) by 4 lengths, then the Kyoto Kimpai as favorite from a handy spot. 3rd in the Milers C., a fracture followed; 9th in the Sekiya Kinen after 2.5 years off, he retired but went to stud for his precious Kurofune blood.
2678=A sprinter who won the CBC Sho at 5. Top two in his first 6 starts, at 3 he was 2nd in the Ibis Summer Dash and Centaur S., and a neck 2nd to Fine Needle as 11th choice in the G1 Sprinters S. Then a slump with heavy defeats, until the CBC Sho at 5 as 13th choice: he led from a fine start and held everyone off, with 11th choice Anvar 2nd - quinella 138,600 yen. Perhaps a one-off; heavy defeats resumed.
2679=A top stayer: Kikuka Sho and 2 Tenno Sho (Spring). In a crop led by fillies like Almond Eye and Lucky Lilac, he and miler Indy Champ flew the colts' flag. Debuting at 3, unbeaten in 2, he skipped the classics with fragility and was 2nd as heavy favorite in the Radio Nikkei Sho. Straight into the Kikuka Sho, 7th choice after the break, he was given little chance - but Lemaire, fresh from a Shuka Sho win, was aboard. Settled in midfield, he spurted through the pack at the 4th turn and caught Etario by a nose - a classic in only his 4th start. At 4, 2nd in the AJCC, he became Tenno Sho (Spring) favorite after AJCC and Hanshin winner Shaketra's sudden death, broke clear early and beat Glory Vase by a neck. 3rd in the Sapporo Kinen, he was most fancied of 3 Japanese in the Arc but last on heavy ground. In an Arima Kinen with 11 G1 winners he was 4th to Lys Gracieux. At 5 he won the Tenno Sho (Spring) again as heavy favorite, by a nose over Stiffelio, then went straight to the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and was 2nd, half a length behind Almond Eye.
2680=A talent who stunned on debut. Favorite in the Sapporo 2yo S., he lost a tight finish, 3rd. In the Kyoto 2yo S. at 2.8 he waited at the back, moved up through the 3rd-4th turns, drew level at the top of the straight and won a duel with Breaking Dawn. At 3, 3rd in the Kyodo News Hai, he ran well as a longshot in the classics: 5th in the Satsuki Sho, 6th in the Derby.
2681=Steady in trials at 3 - 4th in the Tulip Sho and Flora S., 2nd in the Rose S. - she was 4th to Almond Eye in the Shuka Sho. Older, 2nd in the Epsom C. and top five in the Sekiya Kinen and Fukushima Himba S., she won the Fuchu Himba S. at 5 as 7th of 8, from a handy spot as favorites like Loves Only You collapsed. In the QEII Cup she lost by a head to Lucky Lilac, then in her farewell Arima Kinen, as 11th choice, charged wide with the fastest finish for 2nd - a devilish late kick fans won't forget.
2682=As crop-mate Duramente's first foals debuted, he raced on, a late bloomer who won his first graded race at 8. On turf until spring at 5, stuck in the 10-million class, he switched to dirt and won 2 classes quickly, then was 2nd in the Sirius S. and 2nd to Le Vent Se Leve in the Champions C. 3rd in the Miyako S., 2nd in the Diolite Kinen, he won the Antares S. at 8, then was 2nd in the Elm S., 3rd in the Urawa Kinen and Tokyo Daishoten. At 9: 2nd Sirius S., 3rd Tokyo Daishoten again. At 10, 7th in the Antares S., he retired when unwell before the Heian S.
2683=Winning 3 straight by wide margins in summer at 3, she was a Shuka Sho fancy and led boldly for 2nd to Almond Eye. Older, 14th in the Nakayama Himba S., she won the Hanshin Himba S., then the Queen S. as favorite from a handy spot.
2684=Winning only a maiden on dirt, he switched to turf when older and showed stamina at once: 3 wins to open class, then 2nd in the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai. After a 6th and 5th, he won the Argentina Kyowakoku Hai at 5 as 2nd choice, racing forward and holding on. His later highlight was 4th in the Hanshin Daishoten at 6.
2685=Debuting late in February at 3, he won easily, then grew steadily, winning the Nagoya GP late at 3 over Grimm and Mitsuba. Blooming older, 2nd in the Tokai S., he won the Diolite Kinen and Heian S., was 2nd in the Teio Sho, then won the JBC Classic at 1.6, dueling Omega Perfume and edging him by a nose. Then rival Chrysoberyl arrived: 4th in the Champions C. behind him, he won the Kawasaki Kinen easily without him, but was 3rd to him in the Teio Sho and JBC Classic. After a 6th in the Teio Sho and a fracture, he was 3rd in the JBC Classic and 2nd to younger T O Keynes defending the Champions C. At 7 he won the Kawasaki Kinen, was 3rd in the Dubai World Cup and 2nd to Meisho Hario in the Teio Sho, retiring a top horse.
