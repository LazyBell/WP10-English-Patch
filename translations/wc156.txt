3450=She won the 1993 BC Distaff (G1), Eclipse champion 3yo filly; her fierce duel with runner-up Paseana was The Blood-Horse's "Race of the Year." She began unbeaten in 4 incl. a stakes, then stopped winning, and moved to a California stable in spring at 3. She won the Hollywood Oaks (G1) at once, 4 straight incl. a listed race and G2. Favorite in the BC Distaff, she beat 9-time G1 winner Paseana by a nose after a fierce duel. At 4 she won the Gamely H. for a 3rd G1; 6th defending the BC Distaff, she retired. As a broodmare she produced BC Mile (G1) winner War Chant; her daughter Spinning Wildcat, imported to Japan, produced Danon Smash.
3451=A durable Australian gelding from 1992 to 1998, winning 8 G1s from 1000m to 2500m. At 2 in 1992/93 he won his 2nd start, then the QTC Sires' Produce S. and Castlemaine S. (G1s around a mile). At 3 he won the Caulfield Guineas and Australian Guineas (mile G1s) and the 2500m Victoria Derby and 2400m AJC Derby. At 4 he won the 1000m Lightning S. (G1). Winless in 4 at 5, but 2nd in the Cox Plate. At 6, mainly sprinting, he won the Lightning S. again, a 1200m G2 and a listed race. Fading at 7, he won the Chirnside S. (G2) and retired at season's end.
3452=A British 10f specialist who won 5 G1s in 1995-1996 incl. 2 Eclipse S. and 2 International S. Debuting late in July at 3, he won his 4th start and 3 straight, then joined Godolphin and won 3 more in Dubai early at 4. In England he won the Eclipse S., his first stakes win a G1, then the International S. Last in the BC Classic on dirt, and last in the Dubai World Cup at 5, he focused on turf, winning the Prix d'Ispahan, Eclipse S. and International S. - 3 straight G1s. 2nd in the Champion S., his only loss at his best trip, he was Cartier champion older horse and retired. At stud he sired G1 winners Cavalryman and Cutlass Bay and many more.
3453=A great miler who won 4 mile G1s in Europe and America in 1995 and was Cartier Horse of the Year over Triple Crown winner Lammtarra. 3rd on debut in September at 2, she then kept winning: the Weld Park S. (G3), Athasi S. (L), and the Irish 1000 Guineas in spring at 3. In England she won the Coronation S. in record time; in France the Prix du Moulin against older horses and colts as favorite. 2nd in the QEII S., her only mile defeat, she bounced back in the BC Mile, breaking clear to win by 2 lengths, 7 more to 3rd, and retired. The Cartier vote split with Lammtarra, but her strong wins in 4 countries carried the day. She left no stars and died at 11.
3454=A star European sprinter of 1996, Cartier champion sprinter. Debuting late in July at 3, he won his 2nd and 3rd starts, then kept losing. At 4 he was transformed: 3 straight incl. the Prix de Saint-Georges (G3), the Prix du Gros-Chene (G2), then the July C. as 2nd choice, leading throughout, and the Prix Maurice de Gheest (G1). In the Abbaye he was caught late by Kistena, 2nd by a neck, ending 6 straight. 6 wins and a 2nd from 7 - champion sprinter - and he retired. His first crop had French Derby winner Anabaa Blue, then G1 winners Goldikova, Yell and many more - a great success.
3455=Winner of the King George VI & Queen Elizabeth Diamond S. in 1997 and 1998, who also ran well in the Dubai World Cup and BC Classic - top class on turf and dirt. Debuting in France in May at 3, he won 5 straight to the Grand Prix de Deauville (G2), then 3rd to Lammtarra in his first G1, the Arc. At 4 he won the Coronation C., then 4th to Helissio in the Arc and 3rd to Pilsudski in the BC Turf. Moving to a British stable at 5, he won the King George over Pilsudski, but was 7th to Peintre Celebre in the Arc. At 6 he won the King George again and the Irish Champion S. - 4 G1s - and on dirt was 2nd in the Dubai World Cup and 3rd in the BC Classic. Cartier champion older horse. At stud only 13 stakes winners - below hopes.
3456=Winner of the 1995 Kentucky Derby and Belmont S., and a successful sire of dual classic winner Point Given; North American champion sire in 2001. Debuting in September at 2, he won 2 of 6 incl. the Remsen S. (G2). At 3 he won the Florida Derby; 4th in the Blue Grass S., he then won the Kentucky Derby, was 3rd in the Preakness and won the Belmont - best of his crop. He won the Travers S. easily for a 4th G1; 5th to 10-race winner Cigar in the Jockey Club Gold C., but Eclipse champion 3yo. His first crop had BC Distaff winner Spain, his 2nd 6-time G1 winner Point Given. Leased to Japan for a year with no stars, though in-utero import Iide Kenshin won the Zen-Nihon 2yo Yushun.
3457=A miracle horse who won the 1996 BC Mile (G1), spent ages injured, and won it again 2 years later. Unbeaten in 3 from September at 2, he won the Best Turn S. (G3) at 3, 2nd in the Illinois Derby (G2), won the Jersey Derby (G2), then was last in the BC Sprint; 4 wins from 10 with 3 stakes. At 4 he won a G3 and listed race, then the BC Mile on turf over Spinning World and Mark of Esteem - suddenly a star. A leg injury kept him out about 2 years; back in autumn at 6, after an allowance, he won a fierce duel in the BC Mile - an amazing comeback.
3458=Winner of both the Cox Plate and Melbourne Cup in 1996/97, Australian Horse of the Year. At 2 in 1994/95 he won 1 of 4. Emerging at 3, he won the Expressway S. (G2) and soon the Australian C. (G1), but rival Octagonal blocked him: 2nd, 3rd, 2nd in G1s. At 4 he finally beat Octagonal in the Cox Plate, then won the Melbourne Cup by 2 1/4 lengths - only the 4th to win both in a season. Coming to the Japan Cup, he fell ill and was scratched - as his sire Sky Chase had been in 1988. Back home he won the C F Orr S. for a 4th G1 and retired.
3459=Winner of the 1998 French Derby and Irish Derby, Cartier Horse of the Year. Debuting at 3, he won the Prix La Force (G3) in his 3rd start, then the French Derby as 6th choice, outfighting Croco Rouge, and the Irish Derby as favorite by 4.5 lengths - top of his crop. Aimed at the Arc, he was 3rd to Sagamix in the Prix Niel and 8th to Sagamix in the Arc. In a wide-open Cartier vote he still took Horse of the Year and champion 3yo colt. At 4, placings in G1s (2nd Ganay, 3rd Coronation C., 3rd Saint-Cloud) and a win in the Prix Gontaut-Biron (G3), then he retired to stud in Japan. Siring only 2-time graded winner Admire Monarch, he was exported to France after 4 years.
3460=Winner of the 1999 Kentucky Derby and Preakness. Once running in claiming races, he rose to the top and became Eclipse Horse of the Year. Struggling early, he won his 6th start at 2; still losing at 3, he got his 2nd win in a claiming race. Believing in him, his camp ran him in the Kentucky Derby, and he won as 11th choice, stunning everyone; 5th choice in the Preakness, he won by 1.5 lengths. Triple Crown favorite in the Belmont, he was injured in the race, 3rd, and retired. After 3 years at stud he was imported to Japan, siring Wonder Acute, a 3-time dirt G1 winner.
3461=The 2001 Arc winner. Gifted but injured through much of 3 and 4, he romped in the Arc as if making up for lost time. Winning 2 of 3 from August at 2, he won 2 stakes at 3, then was 2nd by a length to Sinndar in the Derby and 4th to Giant's Causeway in the Eclipse S., injured in the race. After a year off, he returned in summer at 4, won a listed race easily, the International S. by 7 lengths, and the Arc by a joint-record 6 lengths. On dirt in the BC Classic he lost by a nose to Tiznow. At 5, 3rd in the Dubai World Cup, then 2nd in a G3, and he retired. At stud: G1 winners Presvis and Sakhee's Secret.
3462=Winner of the 2003 BC Classic and 2004 Dubai World Cup - no Eclipse Award, but for a time the best on dirt. Debuting late in May at 3, he couldn't keep up and was pulled up; after a long rest he first won in February at 4, then climbed through allowances and claimers, winning the Goodwood BC H. (G2) that autumn. At 5 he won the Goodwood again, then the BC Classic for his first G1. At 6 he beat Medaglia d'Oro in a duel in the Dubai World Cup - top of the dirt world. He won the Pacific Classic S., then was 3rd to Ghostzapper in the BC Classic and retired. Few stars at stud, but 2 G1 winners: Shared Account and Whitmore.
3463=He blossomed after moving from England to the US; with 3 straight G1s incl. the Jockey Club Gold C., he was 2003 Eclipse Horse of the Year. Debuting in England in April at 3, he won his 2nd start, then struggled - no future in 7 European turf starts, so he moved to the US. He won 3 straight, the New Orleans H. (G2) in March at 4, then 3 more incl. the Pimlico Special H. (G1). A narrow 2nd in the Stephen Foster H., he won the Suburban H., Woodward S. and Jockey Club Gold C., pulling clear each time - Horse of the Year - and retired. A steady sire with some G1 winners, if short of an ideal heir to A.P. Indy.
