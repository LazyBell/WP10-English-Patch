3356=A US winner of 7 G1s from 2005 to 2007 incl. 3 straight Hollywood Gold Cups. A cheap horse once sold in a claiming race, he earned a record $5.26 million for a claimer - a dream horse. At 2 he won a maiden in his 5th try. At 3, after 2 allowance wins, he was claimed by new owners. At 4 he blossomed, winning the Californian S., then the Hollywood Gold C. for his first G1. At 5 he won 4 G1s: Santa Anita H., Charles Whittingham Memorial H., Hollywood Gold C. and Pacific Classic S. At 6 he won the Santa Anita H. and Hollywood Gold C. - 7 G1s. He raced until July at 7 and retired injured; a comeback a year later ended in a heavy loss, and he retired for good. He became a lead pony and entered the US Hall of Fame in 2015.
3357=Raced in Germany until 3 in 2005, then in France from 4; in his prime at 5 he won 3 G1s: Prix d'Ispahan, Prince of Wales's S. and Prix Jacques le Marois. Unbeaten in 4 in Germany to autumn at 3, he was 4th in his first G1, Italy's Premio Roma. In France at 4 he won his opener, then kept placing to year-end. At 5 he blossomed: 2 straight wins to the Ispahan, his first G1, then the Prince of Wales's S., Jacques le Marois and Prix Foy - 5 straight. Having won at 2400m, he was aimed at the Arc, but a fracture ended his career. At stud he sired 5 G1 winners incl. Prix Royal-Oak winners Vazirabad and Ireseine and Criterium de Saint-Cloud winner Mandaean.
3358=The 2005 Derby winner, one of Montjeu's best, and sire of great mare Treve, 2-time Arc winner. Unbeaten in 2 at 2 incl. the Racing Post T. (G1). At 3 he won the Dante S., then the Derby by 5 lengths, unbeaten. Hot favorite in the Eclipse S., he was caught by Oratory, 2nd, and 2nd to him again in the Irish Champion S. In the Arc he was 5th to fellow Montjeu son Hurricane Run, and retired. Disappointing at stud at first, he shot to fame when Treve, from his 4th crop, won the French Oaks, Prix Vermeille, Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud and 2 Arcs. With few other stars, his standing is slipping.
3359=The only star and sire son of Dubai Millennium, who died after a single crop. His first crop produced 9 G1 winners incl. Monterosso and Prince Bishop - beyond all hopes. A top racehorse too, he won 3 straight from June at 2 incl. the National S. (G1). At 3, 5th in the 2000 Guineas, he won the Irish 2000 Guineas, was 3rd in the Derby, and won the Prix Jacques le Marois. 2nd in the QEII S., he retired as a precaution after injury. G1 winners came yearly: Tattersalls Gold C. winner Al Kazeem, Hong Kong Cup winner Akeed Mofeed, Australian Srikandi and more. Sire sons are emerging, like Makfi, imported to Japan, sire of All at Once, Sirius Colt and French 2000 Guineas winner Make Believe.
3360=The 2006 Arc winner, well known in Japan for beating Japan's great hope Deep Impact. Debuting late in April at 3, he unseated his rider; 2nd next time, he won his 3rd start, then the Prix du Lys (G3) and Grand Prix de Paris, his first G1. In autumn he won the Prix Niel by half a length, then the Arc, billed as a 3-way clash between favorite Deep Impact, bidding to be the first Japan-trained winner, previous winner Hurricane Run and BC Turf winner Shirocco. He passed Deep Impact, who had broken clear, and held off outsider Pride - 5 straight. 5 wins from 7; Cartier Horse of the Year went to Ouija Board, but he was French Horse of the Year. Injury kept him off the track at 4 and he retired. His best foals were Criterium de Saint-Cloud winner Epiculis and Australian G1 winner Spillway - below hopes. He died in 2022.
3361=Winner of 4 mile G1s in 2008 incl. the 2000 Guineas and Irish 2000 Guineas, in fierce rivalry with crop-mates New Approach (Derby) and Raven's Pass (BC Classic). Debuting in May at 2, he won 2 straight incl. a stakes, 2nd in the Phoenix S. (G1), and 3rd to New Approach in the Futurity S. (G2). At 3 he got revenge, beating New Approach by a nose in the 2000 Guineas and by 1 3/4 lengths in the Irish 2000 Guineas. At a mile he beat Raven's Pass in the St. James's Palace S. and Sussex S. - 4 straight G1s. 4th in France's Prix du Moulin, 2nd to Raven's Pass in the QEII S., and 2nd to him again in his farewell BC Classic, showing he could handle a new trip and surface (synthetic that year). His first crop had G1 winners George Vancouver and Pedro the Great, his next Sudirman - a fine start.
3362=Winner of 6 G1s from 2009 to 2013 incl. 3 straight Coronation Cups, the BC Turf and Dubai Sheema Classic. Unbeaten in 3 from August at 2 incl. the Racing Post T. (G1) - Cartier champion 2yo, a future star. At 3, 6th in the 2000 Guineas, his preparation faltered and he rested to year-end. At 4 he won the Ormonde S. (G3), then the Coronation C. and BC Turf. At 5 he won the Coronation C. again, 3rd in the King George and International S. At 6 he won the Dubai Sheema Classic and a 3rd Coronation C. - 6 G1s. Preparing for the King George he suffered a severe injury; he survived 3 operations, but colic left him beyond help and he was put down.
3363=A US winner of 6 G1s from 2011 to 2013 incl. 2 BC Ladies' Classics, Eclipse champion mare 3 years running. The biggest star left in the US by Empire Maker before his export to Japan; her success made the sale controversial. Winning her debut in October at 2, at 3 she won the Alabama S. and BC Ladies' Classic - Eclipse champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won the Beldame Invitational S. and a 2nd BC Ladies' Classic - champion older mare. At 5 she won the Delaware H. and Personal Ensign H.; bidding for a 3rd in the renamed BC Distaff, she was 4th to Beholder, but champion older mare again. She retired to breed but died at 9, leaving one foal by Galileo, Delta's Royalty, a broodmare since 2021 - will she carry on her dam's blood?
3364=A great Hong Kong sprinter who won 4 sprint G1s from 2014 to 2016, the first foreign-trained winner of the Takamatsunomiya Kinen. New Zealand-bred, he first raced there as Naisoso Warrior, winning 1 of 1, then moved to Hong Kong in the 2012/13 season, late at 4. At 5 he started winning handicaps; at 6 he won the Hong Kong Sprint, his first G1, then in Japan beat Straight Girl and Mikki Isle in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, and Singapore's KrisFlyer International Sprint - 3 G1s. Without a G1 at 7, he raced on at 8 and won the Hong Kong Sprint again. After an 8th in the Centenary Sprint C. (G2), he retired.
3365=A rare successful sire from the now-minor Grey Sovereign line, prized in America as an outcross to the dominant Mr. Prospector and Northern Dancer lines. Winning his debut in August at 2, he won the Champagne S. (G1) and BC Juvenile - near-unanimous Eclipse champion 2yo. At 3, after a 3rd in the Wood Memorial, a leg problem ended classic plans; back, he won the Kelso H. (G2) and retired at 3. His first crop had Kentucky Derby winner Nyquist, making him North America's champion 2yo sire; then Hollywood Derby winners Mo Town and Mo Forza, BC Turf Sprint winner Golden Pal and Belmont winner Mo Donegal - on a roll.
3366=One of Dubawi's best, winner of 4 G1s in 2015-2016 incl. the King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. and Dubai Sheema Classic. Debuting in July at 2, he won just a maiden from 3. Still missing at 3, he won the Great Voltigeur S. (G2) that summer. At 4 he won the King George, his first G1, then the Prix Foy and, at 5, the Dubai City of Gold, then the Dubai Sheema Classic, the Coronation C. and International S. - 6 straight. Favorite in the Arc, 5th to Found. At 6, 3rd defending the Sheema Classic, he retired. At stud in England from 2018, his foals disappointed and he was switched to jumps breeding.
3367=From Sea The Stars' first crop, one of his best; he won the German Derby by 11 lengths and sired a father-son German Derby winner, Fantastic Moon. Winning his debut by 4 lengths in September at 2, his only start that year, at 3 he won the Fruhjahrs-Preis (G3) and Union-Rennen (G2), then the German Derby as favorite, veering wide at the top of the straight yet still storming clear. Aimed at the Arc, 2nd in the Grosser Preis von Baden, he was found injured and retired. At stud from 2015, his 2nd crop had Coronation S. winner Alpine Star, followed by German Derby winner Fantastic Moon and German Oaks winner Muskoka - a steady sire.
3368=A US winner of 6 G1s incl. the 2015 BC Mile. Debuting in September at 2, she won her 2nd start and a stakes in her 4th - talented early. Beaten at 3, her best a 2nd in the San Clemente H. (G2), winless in 4. At 4 she soared: 3 straight incl. the Just a Game S. (G1), then the First Lady S. and BC Mile against the colts - Eclipse champion turf female. At 5 she won 3 straight incl. the Jenny Wiley S., the Distaff Turf Mile S. (G2), then the Queen Anne S. and Woodbine Mile S. - 8 straight, 6 G1s. 2nd in the First Lady S. and in the BC Mile, she retired, and has bred in Ireland since 2017.
3369=The 2015 Derby and Arc winner, denying great mare Treve a 3rd Arc. Winning his only start at 2, at 3 he won a listed race and 3 straight to the Derby, flying from the back in the straight. Skipping the Irish Derby, he beat older horses in the Eclipse S., then lost the International S. by a neck to filly crop-mate Arabian Queen, his first defeat, but won the Irish Champion S. In the Arc, with favorite Treve bidding for a historic 3rd, he overpowered them all. In his farewell BC Turf he was half a length behind Found, whom he had beaten in the Irish Champion S. Cartier Horse of the Year, he retired to stud.
