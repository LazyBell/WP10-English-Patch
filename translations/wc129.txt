3013=A Hokkaido filly who debuted in 2021. Running 2nd, 2nd, 1st, 2nd, 1st, she won the Lily C., then the Edelweiss Sho as 3rd choice in her first graded race, waiting in midfield in a hot pace and catching gritty Historic Nova by a neck. 7th in the JBC 2yo Yushun, her first 1800m race, she won the Tokyo 2yo Yushun Himba by 4 lengths as 5th choice. At 3, racing in Southern Kanto stakes, she won 4 of 4 apart from a 3rd in the Kanto Oaks - a bright future.
3014=An Irish filly by Deep Impact who won the 2021 Oaks, Irish Oaks and Yorkshire Oaks - Cartier champion 3yo filly - but was put down after a fracture the next year. With 1 win from 7 at 2, she won the Musidora S. at 3, then the Oaks by a record 16 lengths; Dettori said, "I've won many classics, but never one so easy." She won the Irish Oaks by 8.5 and the Yorkshire Oaks by 4, top of Europe's 3yo fillies. In autumn, 2nd in the Prix Vermeille, 6th in the Arc and 3rd in the British Champions Fillies & Mares S. Early at 4 she fractured her pelvis in her stall and, despite treatment, was put down.
3015=Winning her debut in October at 2, she rested, then was hampered and 10th in the Fairy S. at 3. In the Flora S., 5th choice, she broke clear from a handy spot - a perfect win. 9th in the Oaks, 13th straight into the Shuka Sho.
3016=She won her debut by 3 lengths despite a slow start, then after a break was 2nd choice behind later 2-classic filly Stars on Earth in the Queen C. in February at 3, and beat her by a neck - a classic hope. 11th in the Oka Sho, 5th in the Oaks, 9th straight into the Shuka Sho.
3017=Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Kyodo News Hai (G3), he was 2nd choice behind Do Deuce in the Satsuki Sho (G1), 4th, then 4th as Derby favorite. Skipping the Kikuka Sho, he was 3rd to fellow 3yo Equinox in the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and 5th in the Japan Cup, best of the 3yos. At 4, 2nd in the Dubai Turf, 4th in the Sapporo Kinen and Tenno Sho (Autumn), then 6th in the Japan Cup, his first time unplaced. At 5, 3rd in the Dubai Turf, then 14th and 9th.
3018=A 2022 3yo filly star who won the Shuka Sho (G1). From King Kamehameha's final crop, she completed graded winners in all 14 of his crops. Early on she placed in graded races (5th Niigata 2yo S., 3rd Saudi Arabia Royal C., 5th Daily Hai 2yo S.). At 3 she won the Flower C. (G3), skipped the Oka Sho, and was 2nd to Stars on Earth as 10th choice in the Oaks, holding on from up front. She won the Shion S. (G3), then was 3rd choice behind 2-classic filly Stars on Earth and Namur in the Shuka Sho; racing forward as usual, she broke clear and held them off - a G1. 14th in the QEII Cup, 12th in the Victoria Mile at 4; after a long injury break, 8th in the Osaka Hai and 9th in the Victoria Mile at 5, then she won the QEII Cup from a handy spot - a 2nd G1.
3019=Winning a maiden in his 5th start, 13th in the Aoba Sho, he won a 1-win race and was 4th to World Premiere as 16th choice in the Kikuka Sho. Restarting in allowances, he won the 2-win class only at 5, then 15th stepping up in the Tenno Sho (Spring), and won the Stayers S. that December - a true stayer. At 6: 11th, 12th, 5th, 7th, then 3rd defending the Stayers S. Meant to race on, he retired injured and became a riding horse.
3020=A legendary US sprinter, 6th in The Blood-Horse's top 100 US racehorses of the 20th century: 18 wins from 22, still hailed as the greatest sprinter ever for his raw speed. Winning easily from his debut, at 3 he beat crop-mate and later dual classic winner Damascus. Their rivalry was fierce: he faded from the front to 3rd in the Woodward S., beat Damascus in the Suburban H. at 4, then faded to 2nd in the Brooklyn H. In the Washington Park H. that summer he ran a dirt mile in an astonishing 1:32.2 - then a world record on any surface, still the world dirt record. Horse of the Year, he retired the next year after a record 1:20.2 for 7 furlongs in the Vosburgh H. Champion sire in 1971, his male line is gone, but he lives on as Fappiano's broodmare sire.
3021=Unbeaten in 2, favorite in the Hanshin JF (G1), she was 4th to Circle of Life. At 3 she won the Tulip Sho (G2) as favorite, a classic focus, but was 10th as Oka Sho favorite. 3rd in the Oaks, she lost the Shuka Sho by half a length to Stunning Rose and was 5th in the QEII Cup, 2nd-best 3yo. At 4, 7th in the Victoria Mile, 16th in the Yasuda Kinen, she won the Fuji S. (G2), then the Mile CS as 5th choice, finding a finish from another dimension at the back and sweeping past them all - a G1. 3rd in the Hong Kong Mile, 2nd in the Dubai Turf and Yasuda Kinen at 5. In the Mile CS she felt something in a leg, was eased home last, and retired.
3022=Debuting at 3, he won a maiden and 1-win race quickly, then kept placing until the 3-win class in autumn at 4. 4th in the Chunichi Shimbun Hai, at 5 he was 2nd in the Naruo Kinen and 3rd in the Tanabata Sho, then won the Chunichi Shimbun Hai as 8th choice, leading all the way; with longshots 2nd and 3rd, the trifecta paid 2.36 million yen. Winless in graded races at 6, he switched to jumps in December.
3023=Winner of the 2021 QEII Cup. With 19 starts, no graded win and no G1 runs, she was 10th choice - a stunning win, trifecta 3.39 million yen. Winning her 2nd start at 2, she needed 8 more for a 1-win race, then improved at 4, winning 2 more classes. 7th in her first open race, the Fuchu Himba S., she ran in the QEII Cup, where crop-mate and Osaka Hai winner Rei Papale, Shuka Sho winner Akaitorino Musume and All Comers 1-2 Win Marilyn and Win Kitos were fancied. Modest in results but nearly always fastest home, she unleashed her otherworldly kick as the fancies moved early and faded, and won easily. Winless after, apart from a 3rd in the Kinko Sho and 4th in the QEII Cup at 5, she retired after a 15th in the Arima Kinen.
3024=Winner of 6 G1s in 2022-2023 incl. the Tenno Sho (Autumn) and Arima Kinen - best in the world, and the first crop of Kitasan Black, sending his sire's fame sky-high. Unbeaten in 2 incl. the Tokyo Sports Hai 2yo S., he went straight to the classics: 2nd to Geoglyph in the Satsuki Sho and to Do Deuce in the Derby. In autumn he beat older horses incl. previous Derby winner Shahryar in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), then was Arima Kinen favorite over champion Titleholder and won easily from midfield. At 4 he romped in the Dubai Sheema Classic, earning the world's top rating, won the Takarazuka Kinen by a neck, and the Tenno Sho (Autumn) in a stunning record 1:55.2. In his farewell Japan Cup, billed vs. 3yo Triple Crown filly Liberty Island, the two broke clear but he drew away to win by 4 lengths - 6 G1s. His first stud fee: 20 million yen. Can he surpass his sire?

At 5, 3rd in the Nakayama Kinen, 10th in the Victoria Mile, 4th in the Chukyo Kinen and Keisei Hai AH, she won the Turquoise S. again by a neck, closing wide. At 6, mostly unplaced, but 3rd in the Keisei Hai AH.
