2392=Winner of 3 mile G3s. Winning his debut as 6th choice and 3rd in the Junior C. (OP), he stepped up for the Arlington C. as 9th choice. Handy at the 4th turn, he seemed to stall, then in the last 100m suddenly accelerated and shot clear. Raw but talented, he was 3rd in the Sekiya Kinen and Keisei Hai AH against older horses. Winless after, he dropped a class, then won 3 straight: a 16-million race, the Sekiya Kinen and Fuji S. A Mile CS fancy, he was 16th. 3rd in the Milers C. at 5, he faded and was retired after the Keisei Hai AH at 7.
2393=A 5-time graded winner at a mile to 2000m. His first was the NZT in spring at 3: 7th choice, he burst clear from midfield and held off favorite Gran Silk. He won the Fukushima Kinen that autumn, the Nakayama Kimpai and, late that year, the Kinko Sho. A G1 contender, he was 4th in the Arima Kinen and 3rd in the Osaka Hai. In 2017, with the Kinko Sho moved to March, he won it again - twice in 3 months, a rare record.
2394=A blueblood with graded-winning siblings like Reve d'Essor and Apres Midi. Winning 2 at 3, he won the Aoba Sho as favorite from midfield in a 3-way straight duel. 4th choice in the Derby, he was 9th behind Duramente's runaway. He won the Nikkei Shinshun Hai the next year, but was 10th in the Tenno Sho (Spring), and did little in the Nikkei Shinshun Hai and Hanshin Daishoten after.
2395=A 2015 dirt star who won the Japan Dirt Derby. Noticed on dirt in allowances, he won the Unicorn S. with a powerful finish, then swept past the field from midfield to win the Japan Dirt Derby by 2.5 lengths. Against older horses he won the Musashino S. and was 2nd in the Champions C. At 4 he fell short: 2nd in the February S., 4th in the Kashiwa Kinen, 2nd in the Teio Sho, then unplaced from autumn, and lost favor. At 6 he revived, winning the Negishi S. and February S. for a 2nd G1, then made the top five in the Kashiwa Kinen and Mile CS Nambu Hai. Far back at 7 in the February S. and Godolphin Mile, he moved to Oi - and it worked: closing steadily, he was 2nd to JRA stars in the Tokyo Daishoten. Still racing at 8, with more dirt graded success hoped for.
2396=A middle-distance horse who won the Osaka Hai (G2) and Radio Nikkei Sho. Unbeaten in 2, he was 3rd in the Kyodo News Hai and Mainichi Hai, then won the Principal S. for a Derby spot, but chose the Radio Nikkei Sho for his trip. Hot favorite, he spurted from midfield and pulled away. 5th against older horses in the Tenno Sho (Autumn), at 4 he won the Osaka Hai (G2), was 2nd in the Mainichi Okan and 4th in the Tenno Sho (Autumn). The next year the Osaka Hai became a G1; he was 5th.
2397=He won the 2015 Kobe Shimbun Hai, rated top of his crop that autumn. Racing mainly on dirt at first, 2nd in the Hyogo Championship, he switched to turf in summer at 3, won a 16-million race easily under Christophe Lemaire, then led all the way in the Kobe Shimbun Hai. Kikuka Sho favorite, unable to lead, he was 3rd. More fancied than Kikuka winner Kitasan Black in the Arima Kinen, he broke down in the race and was last.
2398=Debuting in May at 3, he won 4 straight from his debut to the 16-million class. 3rd in his first graded race, the Musashino S., he won the Negishi S., then the February S. as 2nd choice, breaking clear mid-straight and holding off favorite Nonkono Yume - on the road to dirt kingship. Then a long drought: 2nd in the Nippon TV Hai and Sakitama Hai, but no win for 2 years until the Coral S. (OP) in March at 6. He then won the Korea Sprint, a G1 abroad, and was 4th in the JBC Sprint and February S. Trying turf at 7 in the Centaur S. and Swan S., unplaced in both, he retired.
2399=Rarely beaten badly from 2, he was 2nd in the Aoba Sho for a Derby spot. Steady in long graded races but never quite winning, he rested after a 4th in the Tenno Sho (Spring). After 9 months off, perhaps short of his trip, he closed strongly to win the AJCC. A spring G1 force was expected, but injuries kept him out.
2400=2nd in the NZT and NHK Mile C. favorite, talented early, but stuck on 2nds and 3rds, always fancied. At 5, on his 10th graded try, he won the Keisei Hai AH, sweeping from midfield to overwhelm the leaders - sharing the Summer Mile Series title with Win Ganion.
2401=A mile-to-middle graded star who won the Kokura Daishoten and Tanabata Sho at 4. In open class in spring at 3, he was beaten badly in the Kobe Shimbun Hai and Kikuka Sho, then won the Kokura Daishoten. That summer he won the Tanabata Sho - the first 4yo winner in 17 years.
2402=Winner of the 2015 Fairy S. Struggling to win a 5-million race, she led for the first time in the Fairy S. and won easily as 11th choice. 16th choice in the Oka Sho, she led and held 5th, but was 13th in the Oaks and 15th in the Shuka Sho; her only later highlight was 4th in the Queen S.
2403=Winner of the 2015 Queen C. Winning her debut as 6th choice and a 5-million race, she was 2nd choice in the Queen C., swung wide at the 4th turn and beat later 2-classic winner Mikki Queen and favorite Roca. 7th in the Oka Sho and far back in the Oaks and Shuka Sho, she never came close to Mikki Queen again.
2404=A leading 3yo filly without a classic, she grew stronger and won the QEII Cup. Unbeaten in 2, she won the Fillies' Revue as favorite, 3 from 3. 3rd choice in the Oka Sho, she was 4th behind Let's Go Donki's runaway, best of the fancies. 9th in the Oaks, she was 2nd as 5th choice in the Shuka Sho, closing to a neck of Mikki Queen. Fancied but beaten in the QEII Cup and Victoria Mile, she bloomed in autumn at 4: winning the Fuchu Himba S., she beat Marialite, Mikki Queen and others as 3rd choice in the QEII Cup for a G1. Little at 5, but in her farewell Arima Kinen, ridden forward by Lemaire, she was 2nd to Kitasan Black as 8th choice.
2405=Winner of the 2015 Swan S. Unbeaten in 3 incl. the Flower C., a leading 3yo filly, her camp chose the NHK Mile C. over the fillies' classics; 2nd to Clarity Sky, she was exceptional for a filly. In autumn she beat older horses and colts in the Swan S., then was 5th as 5th choice in the Mile CS after a tight finish. Older she was 3rd in the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, then faded: 6th in the Swan S., 17th in the Kyoto Himba S., and retired to breed.
2406=Winner of the 2015 Oaks and Shuka Sho. By Deep Impact out of a 3-time French graded winner, sold for 100 million yen at the Select Sale. Lacking prize money, she was balloted out of the Oka Sho, won the Wasurenagusa Sho the same day, and in the Oaks crushed favorite Rouge Buck and Oka Sho winner Let's Go Donki, closing from midfield with her sire's power - the best of her crop. After the Rose S. she won the Shuka Sho as favorite, bursting clear of the pack. Skipping the QEII Cup for the Japan Cup, 3rd choice against older males, she was 8th; champion 3yo filly. At 4 she was 2nd in the Victoria Mile, 3rd in the QEII Cup and 5th to Satono Diamond in the Arima Kinen. At 5, a puzzling 7th in the Victoria Mile, she was 3rd in the Takarazuka Kinen, then in the QEII Cup, going for a 3rd G1, closed fastest but just missed, 3rd to 3yo Mozu Katchan - a changing of the guard.
2407=Winner of the 2015 Rose S. With Oaks winner Mikki Queen hot favorite, she was 7th choice, but charged from the back and outran Mikki Queen's late run. Joint favorite with her in the Shuka Sho, she was 6th, then 3rd in the QEII Cup, best of the 3yos. Older, 2nd in the Kyoto Kinen, she took on males, but was 9th in the Osaka Hai, 6th in the Meguro Kinen and 12th in the Takarazuka Kinen, then 8th in the QEII Cup. At 5, 9th in the Kinko Sho and 13th in the Hanshin Himba S., she retired after a 17th in the QEII Cup.
2408=A King Kamehameha filly with a sharp finish. 3rd in the Flora S. and Shuka Sho at 3, often at the mercy of the pace, she finally won the Aichi Hai at 5, then the Mermaid S. as 3rd choice, breaking clear from a handy spot past Tosen Victory and other fancies. 9th against males in the Kyoto Daishoten, but a force in fillies' graded races.
2409=Of Sakura Bakushin O's blood, he won the Takamatsunomiya Kinen and became sprint king. Debuting late, he won 5 straight to open class, then placed in sprint graded races (2nd in the Kitakyushu Kinen and Keihan Hai) before winning the Takamatsunomiya Kinen, tracking joint favorite Mikki Isle and running him down. In autumn he led all the way in the Centaur S., and a spring-autumn sprint double seemed sure, but from the rail he raced too cautiously, was swallowed up, 12th - a shock from a horse only twice out of the top two. Thrown off, he was 10th as favorite in the Hong Kong Sprint. Injured, he returned in the next Sprinters S., 6th as 8th choice, his old fire gone, and retired to stud.
2410=A middle-distance dirt star. In open class in summer at 4, he raced in graded races from 5, winning the Saga Kinen. After a 6th and 9th, he beat Oi star Keiai Leone in the Mercury C., tracking in 4th and alone finding a different gear as the leaders held on.
2411=A miler who won the Epsom C. at 5. 4th as favorite in the Shinzan Kinen at 3, talented but short of wins, he restarted in allowances, won 3 classes quickly, then the Rigel S. (OP). A fancy in 1400-1600m graded races, he finally won the Epsom C. at 5.
2412=Emerging at 4, he won 3 straight - a 16-million race, the Coral S. (OP) and Nagoya's Kakitsubata Kinen. That summer he won the Procyon S. as favorite, leading from 2nd at the top of the straight. After poor runs, he was 3rd choice in the Capella S. but led all the way to win by 1 3/4 lengths. From 5 he slumped, unplaced except a 2nd in the Nambu Hai, until 7, when he won the Ritto S. (L) and returned to the graded mix; at 8 in May he won the Sakitama Hai.

(OP) at 3, she then stayed in allowances until mid-5. 2nd in the Kokura Nikkei OP, she was 11th choice in the Fuchu Himba S., swung wide from midfield and flew past all as favorite Smart Layer and others dueled. 2414=Winner of the 2015 Keisei Hai. Winning steadily from his debut, his first open race was the Keisei Hai, closing wide from midfield. Beaten at Nakayama in the Spring S. and Satsuki Sho, he was 2nd against older horses in the Kokura Kinen, 5th in the St. Lite Kinen, 6th in the Kikuka Sho and 6th in the Kinko Sho - close to another graded win.
2415=Dominant among US females in 2015-2017: 13 wins and 2 2nds from 15, 9 G1s. Most wins were runaways; her losses were by a nose and a neck. At 2 she won her debut, then the Del Mar Debutante S., Chandelier S. and BC Juvenile Fillies. At 3 she won the Santa Anita Oaks, missed the Kentucky Oaks with a fever, then romped in the CCA Oaks, Alabama S. and Cotillion S. Against older mares in the BC Distaff she lost by a nose to Beholder, her first defeat, yet was unanimous champion 3yo filly. At 4 she won 2 more G1s, then after a neck 2nd in the Personal Ensign S., leg trouble ended her career. A broodmare from 2018; her first foal (by Arrogate) came in 2019.

G1s proved hard: 9th in the Champions C. and February S.; after a 5th, 4th and 9th, he retired after a 10th in the Musashino S.
2417=He won his debut and a 5-million race as favorite, then a fracture kept him out until early 4.
