3645=France's best 3yo of 1961, who also won England's King George VI & Queen Elizabeth S. and succeeded at stud. Debuting in September at 2, he won his 2nd start, then the Prix de la Salamandre and Grand Criterium - French champion 2yo. At 3, 2nd in his opener, he won the Poulains, Prix Lupin and French Derby - unbeatable at home. In the King George he outfought previous dual classic winner St. Paddy. In autumn he won the Prix Foy, was 2nd in the Arc, and retired. At stud he sired Irish Derby winner Prince Regent and Italian Derby winner Ruysdael; Ruysdael, imported to Japan, sired Tenno Sho winner Tenmei.
3646=The first horse to win both the 2000 Guineas and Irish 2000 Guineas, in 1969. Debuting in June at 2, he won the Imperial S. and Middle Park S., rated 131 in the Free Handicap. At 3, 2nd in the Greenham S., he won the 2000 Guineas by 2.5 lengths, then the Irish 2000 Guineas, and beat Habitat by half a length in an epic St. James's Palace S. Unwell, he rested; 2nd on return in the Peter Hastings S., he was 7th to Habitat in France's Prix du Moulin and retired. His best foal was Take A Reef, a top English 3yo; moved to Australia, he sired nothing notable.
3647=Winner of the 1964 Derby and Irish Derby - only the 2nd to win both, the first in 57 years. Beaten on debut at 2, he won the Irish National S. by 8 lengths, becoming a classic fancy. At 3 he won the Irish 2000 Guineas, then the Derby and Irish Derby, all as favorite. In the King George, outsider Nasram led all the way and he was 2nd; 2nd again in the Arc, lacking a finishing punch, he retired with record earnings for an Anglo-Irish horse of 153,646 pounds. He died at 9 after just 5 crops, which included Irish St Leger winner Reindeer and Irish Oaks winner Santa Tina - a fair success.
3648=The 1970 Arc winner, famed for denying Nijinsky Europe's grand slam. Debuting in August at 2, he won 1 of 4. At 3 he blossomed: 3rd in the Prix Lupin, he won the French Derby. In the Prix Royal-Oak he was promoted to 1st after first-past-the-post Hallez was demoted, but the run itself was a clear defeat, so he was 4th choice in the Arc. Hot favorite at 1.4-1 was unbeaten English Triple Crown winner Nijinsky, seen as a certainty; in a straight duel, he edged ahead by a head - famed as the first horse to beat Nijinsky. He retired after it. At stud: Prix Royal-Oak winner Henri le Balafre, Italian Oaks winner Marmolada and others.
3649=Winner of the 1902 English Fillies' Triple Crown plus the 2000 Guineas, 4th in the Derby - the only horse ever to run in all 5 English classics. Her punishing schedule began at 3: the 1000 Guineas 2 days after the 2000 Guineas; the next month the Oaks 2 days after the Derby, plus 3 more races that month incl. the St. James's Palace S. In autumn she won the St Leger - 4 English classics - and 2 days later was 2nd in the Park Hill S. At 4 she won 5 incl. the Jockey Club S. and Champion S., racing until spring at 5. Her own foals did little, but daughter Maid of the Mist was a fine broodmare, and the family flourished; 6 generations on came Speed Symboli, winner of the Tenno Sho (Spring) and Arima Kinen.
3650=A US horse who won 33 of 89 from 1935 at 2 to 1940 at 7. Unremarkable at first, he improved and finally beat Triple Crown winner War Admiral to become the best - a symbol of hope for Depression-era America, hero of books and films. At 2, raced as a form of training, he ran 35 times, needing 18 to win and winning just 5. At 3, unsettled by a stable change, he started winning, 9 from 23. At 4 he blossomed, winning 7 straight coast to coast under ever-heavier weights, then 3 more - champion handicap male. At 5 the hoped-for clash with War Admiral, a year younger, came in the Pimlico Special match race; he won by 4 lengths - Horse of the Year and the best. Racing until 7, he won his long-sought Santa Anita H. and retired. Not a successful sire, he entered the Hall of Fame in 1958 and ranks 25th in The Blood-Horse's top 100 of the 20th century.
3651=The 1951 French Derby winner and a great sire of Pharamond, Moutiers and many European Oaks winners; French champion sire in 1966, spreading the Prince Rose line worldwide. In Japan his imported sons Pharamond, Moutiers and Diatome sired stars like Kaburaya O, Tanino Moutiers and Kushiro King. His male line has faded, but he lives on as Sea-Bird's broodmare sire. On the track he won 8 of 9 to spring at 3: 2nd in his 3rd start, he won the Grand Criterium; at 3, aiming at 2000m+, he won the Prix de Guiche, Prix Greffulhe and Prix Hocquart, then the French Derby and the long Grand Prix de Paris. Aimed at the Arc, he retired injured.
3652=Unbeaten winner of the 1884 Derby, rated with contemporary St. Simon among the best of the 19th century. At 2 he won 3 minor races. Lowly rated for the Derby on his first run at 3, he dead-heated with Harvester for the win and shot to fame, then won the Gold Vase, Cesarewitch H., Newmarket Free H. and Jockey Club C. - suddenly the best. At 4 he won 4 of 5, his first defeat after 11, incl. the Ascot Gold C. and Jockey Club C. At 5 he met younger Derby and St Leger winner Melton in the Jockey Club C., beating him by 8 lengths for a 3rd straight win, and retired. At stud he sired only a few German classic winners, a stark contrast to great sire St. Simon.
3653=Winner of the 1960 Derby and St Leger, and sire of 6th Japan Cup winner Jupiter Island. Debuting in August at 2, he won his 2nd start. At 3, 6th in the 2000 Guineas, he won the Dante S., then the Derby as 3rd choice by 3 lengths, and the St Leger easily as hot favorite. At 4 he won 4 incl. the Eclipse S. and Jockey Club S.; 2nd in the King George and Champion S., his earnings were 2nd-highest for a British-trained horse, and he retired with 9 wins from 14. At stud: Jupiter Island, Eclipse S. winner Connaught, Irish St Leger winner Parnell and more.
3654=A great 19th-century sire who reshaped the Thoroughbred, his line producing many sires - Prince Rose, Ribot, Bois Roussel, Wild Risk and more - forming the powerful St. Simon line. Unbeatable on the track in England in 1883-1884, he won all 10; not entered for the classics, he won the Ascot Gold C. and Goodwood C. by 20 lengths - in a class of his own. At stud he sired Triple Crown winner Diamond Jubilee, Fillies' Triple Crown winner La Fleche and many more, champion sire in Britain and Ireland 9 times; in 1900 his foals swept all 5 English classics. His male line is declining, but through dams he produced Nearco (inbred to him 5x4x4x5); it is said no horse today lacks his blood.
3655=A US star of 1959-1962 who at 4 in 1961 beat champion Kelso in the Washington DC International - US champion turf male. Blossoming late at 3, he won 5 straight from the Arlington Classic S. to the United Nations H., beating previous Horse of the Year Sword Dancer. At 4, just 2 wins from 15 to September, he then won the Hollywood Gold C., Knickerbocker H. and Washington DC International, beating Kelso, later 5-time Horse of the Year, into 2nd. At 5, 2 wins from 17, and he retired. At stud he steadily sired stakes winners like T. V. Commercial and Quack, US champion sire in 1974.
3656=Winner of the Arc in 1950 and 1951, and French champion sire in 1962 and 1965. Winning his debut at 2, after a run he won 3 straight incl. the Grand Criterium and Prix de la Foret, then the Poulains and Prix Lupin at 3 - 6 straight. 2nd by a short head to Scratch in the French Derby, he won England's Queen Elizabeth S. and the Arc, by then Europe's championship - 5 wins and a 2nd from 6 at 3. At 4 he won the Prix Ganay, Coronation C. and Arc from 4 and retired. At stud: French Derby winner Reliance and King George winner Match; Tanerko became a sire too, and his descendants Relko and Fidion, imported to Japan,, succeeded, Fidion siring Mejiro Durren, Mejiro Thomas and more.
3657=A great English horse of the mid-19th century, 15 wins from 16, winner of the 1849 Derby and St Leger. At 4 he won the Ascot Gold C. by 8 lengths, but was 2nd to Voltigeur in the Doncaster C., his only defeat. Unsatisfied, his camp challenged Voltigeur to a rematch; the next year's match race was billed the race of the century, and he won a fierce duel to gain revenge, then retired. At stud he sired Derby winner Ellington and Oaks winner Brown Duchess; via son Dollar, 8 generations on came Tourbillon, then Partholon, who greatly influenced Japan. As broodmare sire of Galopin, St. Simon's sire, his influence spread worldwide.
3658=Unbeaten in 7 in England at 2 in 1911, mostly by wide margins - called the greatest 2yo in British history. Odd white blotches on his coat earned him the nickname "the Spotted Wonder." His only close call, the National Breeders' S., where he was left badly at the start yet won by a neck, became a legend of his brilliance. Hot classic favorite at 3, he never raced again due to injury. His best foals were 2000 Guineas winner Tetratema and speedy filly Mumtaz Mahal. Disliking stud duty, he sired few foals, yet with them alone was champion sire in 1919. Tetratema succeeded at stud, and via his son Theft came Tokino Minoru and many stars in Japan.
3659=One of Australia's historic greats, winning 10 of 12, mostly sprints, from 1956 to 1960. Debuting at 2 in 1956/57, he won his first 4 incl. the inaugural Golden Slipper S., the top 2yo race; his sire Star Kingdom's foals then won it 5 years running. He also won the AJC Champagne S. and, in 1957/58 at 3, the Canterbury Guineas - races of today's G1 standard. Injured and 4th in the Hill S., he was off for about 2 years; back at 5 in 1959/60 he won 3 straight incl. the Lightning S. and Futurity S., and retired. A successful sire of Golden Slipper winner Eskimo Prince and many more.
3660=A dominant English miler of 1946-1947, rated 144 by Timeform, called the best miler in British racing history. Unbeaten in 4 at 2, all easily, he won an allowance and the 2000 Guineas at 3 by a record 8 lengths; jockey Gordon Richards said he would have won by 20 had he not eased him. A Derby certainty, he was 4th, perhaps over too far, then won the St. James's Palace S. and Knight's Royal S. - unbeaten in 8 at a mile - and retired. At stud in England and America he sired Kentucky Derby winner Tomy Lee and many more; King of the Tudors and Tudor Melody succeeded as sires, and in Japan his line through Tudenham produced Hospitality and Sarno King.
